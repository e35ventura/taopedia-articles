# Burn Cost

Burn Cost is the TAO amount recycled when a new subnet is created. The
[Glossary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#burn-cost) defines it as the required
amount of TAO to be recycled for subnet creation.

## What Burn Cost Represents

The [Create a Subnet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/create-a-subnet#burn-cost)
documentation places Burn Cost in the subnet-creation flow. In Taopedia terms, it names the TAO
amount associated with creating a subnet, while the subnet's task and evaluation rules are separate
design choices.

## Recycling, Not a Price Quote

The [Glossary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#burn-cost) frames Burn Cost as TAO
that is recycled. That makes the term different from a spot quote, a value table, or a forecast
about future subnet demand.

## Dynamic Subnet-Creation Context

The [Create a Subnet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/create-a-subnet#burn-cost) page
describes Burn Cost as dynamic in relation to subnet creation. This article keeps that point at the
concept level and does not reproduce any point-in-time amount.

## Interpretation Boundaries

The [Create a Subnet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/create-a-subnet#burn-cost) reference
is the right place for operational detail. A Taopedia article should treat Burn Cost as a glossary
term, not as a live value table, subnet-creation walkthrough, or fee-optimization guide.
