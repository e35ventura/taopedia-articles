# Subnet Limit Reached

`SubnetLimitReached` means a subnet-registration call arrived when the chain already occupies every
allowed subnet slot and no eligible subnet can be pruned to make room. In `do_register_network`, the
runtime compares the current non-root subnet count against `get_max_subnets()` and returns this
error when `get_network_to_prune()` finds no candidate
([Subtensor source: subnet.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/subnet.rs),
[Subnet Deregistration](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-deregistration)).

The check runs after mechanism, registration-window, and rate-limit gates and before lock-cost,
pruning, and subnet initialization
([Subtensor source: subnet.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/subnet.rs)).

## What It Means

Subnet registration is capped by the network-wide `SubnetLimit` storage value, read through
`get_max_subnets()` in `misc.rs`. In step 5 of `do_register_network`, the runtime counts active
non-root subnets from `NetworksAdded` and, when that count is already at the limit, asks
`get_network_to_prune()` whether an existing subnet can be dissolved to recycle its netuid
([Subtensor source: subnet.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/subnet.rs),
[Subtensor source: misc.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/utils/misc.rs)).

When no prune candidate exists, the call stops with `SubnetLimitReached`. When a candidate does
exist, registration continues and the selected subnet is dissolved later in the same extrinsic
([Subtensor source: subnet.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/subnet.rs)).

`get_network_to_prune()` in `root.rs` scans non-root subnets, skips any still inside the network
immunity period, and selects the lowest moving alpha price. If prices tie, the earliest registration
timestamp wins
([Subtensor source: root.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/coinbase/root.rs),
[Subnet Deregistration: Selection criteria](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-deregistration#selection-criteria)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when the chain is at its subnet cap and every existing subnet is ineligible for
automatic pruning. The subnet-deregistration guide describes one common case: when all occupied
subnets are still within the network immunity period, `get_network_to_prune()` returns nothing and
registration fails with `SubnetLimitReached`
([Subnet Deregistration: All subnets immune](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-deregistration#all-subnets-immune)).

It can also appear when the cap is reached but no non-immune subnet satisfies the prune-selection
rules at submission time. That is a capacity failure on the add-network path, not a balance or
timing failure on the same extrinsic
([Subnet Deregistration: Trigger](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-deregistration#trigger)).

When debugging, rule out earlier gates on the same extrinsic. `MechanismDoesNotExist` means the
submitted mechanism index is not supported on the registration path. `NetworkTxRateLimitExceeded`
means the network-wide subnet-creation cooldown has not elapsed. `CannotAffordLockCost` is the later
rejection when the coldkey cannot fund the current lock cost after capacity logic has already passed
([Subtensor Standard Errors: MechanismDoesNotExist](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#mechanismdoesnotexist),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NetworkTxRateLimitExceeded](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#networktxratelimitexceeded)).

The practical meaning is narrow: no subnet slot is available without an eligible prune target.

## Distinction from Cannot Afford Lock Cost

`SubnetLimitReached` and `CannotAffordLockCost` are both raised in `do_register_network`, but at
different stages. `SubnetLimitReached` is the step-5 capacity rejection when the subnet count is at
the limit and no prune candidate exists. `CannotAffordLockCost` is the step-6 affordability check
after capacity logic has already passed or identified a recycle netuid
([Subtensor source: subnet.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/subnet.rs)).

- **SubnetLimitReached** — no subnet slot is available without a prune candidate.
- **CannotAffordLockCost** — the coldkey cannot fund the lock cost.

## Distinction from Network Tx Rate Limit Exceeded

`SubnetLimitReached` and `NetworkTxRateLimitExceeded` both block subnet creation, but they enforce
different rules. `NetworkTxRateLimitExceeded` is the timing gate on how often a new subnet can be
created. `SubnetLimitReached` is the capacity gate on how many subnets may exist at once when no
eligible subnet can be pruned
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NetworkTxRateLimitExceeded](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#networktxratelimitexceeded),
[Subnet Deregistration: Rate limiting](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-deregistration#rate-limiting)).

- **SubnetLimitReached** — subnet cap reached with no prune candidate.
- **NetworkTxRateLimitExceeded** — subnet-creation cooldown has not elapsed.

## Distinction from No Neuron Id Available

`SubnetLimitReached` and `NoNeuronIdAvailable` both signal capacity exhaustion, but at different
scopes. `SubnetLimitReached` applies to creating a new subnet on the add-network path.
`NoNeuronIdAvailable` applies to registering a neuron on an existing subnet when no UID slot is free
or pruneable
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NoNeuronIdAvailable](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#noneuronidavailable),
[Subtensor source: registration.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/registration.rs)).

- **SubnetLimitReached** — the network has no available subnet slot.
- **NoNeuronIdAvailable** — the targeted subnet has no available neuron UID.

## Distinction from Subnet Not Exists

`SubnetLimitReached` and `SubnetNotExists` both involve subnet presence, but in opposite directions.
`SubnetLimitReached` means the chain cannot add another subnet because the cap is full and no prune
target is eligible. `SubnetNotExists` means a call targeted a netuid that is not present
([Subtensor Standard Errors: SubnetNotExists](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#subnetnotexists)).

- **SubnetLimitReached** — subnet creation blocked by network capacity.
- **SubnetNotExists** — the named subnet does not exist.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the capacity check in `do_register_network`. It
does not quote a live subnet count, immunity countdown, or prune candidate for any network, and it
does not describe token liquidation after a subnet is dissolved; those are documented separately
([Subnet Deregistration](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-deregistration),
[Create a Subnet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/create-a-subnet)).
