CookingTAO is Bittensor Subnet 122.

## What CookingTAO Provides

The on-chain description states "Democratizing Bittensor Mining." The project website is
[cookingtao.com](https://cookingtao.com/). Public project materials describe CookingTAO as
infrastructure for deploying, managing, and monetizing miners across the Bittensor ecosystem.

## Platform Context

The [CookingTAO website](https://cookingtao.com/) presents the project as a miner-deployment and
management platform. Its feature descriptions emphasize no-code miner deployment, wallet
authentication, real-time monitoring, multi-subnet support, and a marketplace for miner code. That
source material supports a platform-level description of CookingTAO, not a detailed claim about a
published Subnet 122 scoring algorithm.

CookingTAO's public website explains the user-facing product as a way to browse subnets, launch
miners, monitor performance, and handle deployment complexity. It also describes developer-facing
features such as submitting miner logic and sharing code through a marketplace. Those are product
capabilities around Bittensor mining, rather than a complete specification of how validators on
Subnet 122 score miner outputs.

The public materials therefore support a mining-access and deployment-layer description. The
website's language focuses on lowering the operational barrier to mining across Bittensor, while the
available public sources do not describe a subnet-specific reward mechanism. Its listed use cases
also distinguish ordinary users from developers, reinforcing that the public product is about
access, deployment, and miner-code distribution.

## Public Materials Boundary

The public materials support several concrete claims: CookingTAO is presented as "Democratizing
Bittensor"; it provides miner deployment and management infrastructure; it includes wallet
connection, monitoring, multi-subnet support, and code-marketplace concepts; and it is associated
with Subnet 122 through public subnet listings. The same materials do not describe a validator
benchmark, challenge format, reward formula, or miner scoring protocol for this subnet.

For readers, that means CookingTAO can be described as a Bittensor mining platform while the
subnet-specific miner and validator mechanism remains unspecified in public materials.

## Miner and Validator Roles

Subnet 122 operates under the standard Bittensor two-role structure. Miners supply a capability to
the network, and validators evaluate those contributions and assign weights. Those weights feed into
[[Yuma Consensus]], which converts validator assessments into emissions that reward the strongest
performers.
