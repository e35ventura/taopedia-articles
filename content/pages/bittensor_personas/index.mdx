# Bittensor Personas

[Bittensor personas](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/introduction#bittensor-personas) are the
role labels Bittensor documentation uses for the main ways people work with the network. The list
groups miners, validators, subnet creators, stakers, and a separate offline testing role for a
blockchain operator.

## Role Map

The [Bittensor personas](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/introduction#bittensor-personas)
section is a map of roles, not a single protocol object. It helps readers separate who produces
subnet work, who evaluates that work, who defines subnet incentives, who supports validators with
TAO stake, and who runs an isolated chain during offline testing.

## Subnet Roles

In Bittensor's [subnet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-subnets) model, miners
produce useful work and validators measure that work against subnet-specific standards. Subnet
creators sit beside those roles by managing the incentive mechanisms that define the work and
evaluation expected inside a subnet.

## Staking Role

The [Bittensor personas](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/introduction#bittensor-personas)
section also names stakers as TAO holders who support validators through staking. That role belongs
to the support side of validator selection and incentive alignment, not to miner production or
validator evaluation itself.

## Offline Operator Boundary

The blockchain-operator persona is limited to offline testing in the
[Bittensor personas](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/introduction#bittensor-personas) section.
It refers to running a local instance of Subtensor as an independent chain, separate from the
Bittensor main network and test network.

## Scope of the Term

Bittensor personas are a reader-facing role taxonomy. The term does not provide wallet guidance,
staking instructions, role counts, subnet membership records, or a guide to joining any specific
subnet. For platform context, see the
[Bittensor platform components](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/introduction#components-of-the-bittensor-platform)
overview.
