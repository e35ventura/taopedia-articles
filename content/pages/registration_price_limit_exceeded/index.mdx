# Registration Price Limit Exceeded

`RegistrationPriceLimitExceeded` means a `register_limit` call named a maximum acceptable
registration burn that is below the subnet's current burn cost at submission time. The Subtensor API
errors reference summarizes it as "Registration Price Limit Exceeded"
([Subtensor API Errors: RegistrationPriceLimitExceeded](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#registrationpricelimitexceeded)).

Neuron registration on a subnet recycles TAO through a dynamic burn price that rises after
successful registrations and decays over time
([Understanding Neurons](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/neurons),
[Subtensor source: registration.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/registration.rs)).

## What It Means

`register_limit` registers a hotkey on a subnet only when the on-chain burn cost for that netuid is
at or below the caller's `limit_price` cap. Before entering the shared registration flow, the
runtime reads the current burn with `get_burn(netuid)` and compares it against the submitted limit.
`RegistrationPriceLimitExceeded` means the live burn cost is higher than the cap
([Subtensor source: registration.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/registration.rs),
[Subtensor source: dispatches.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/macros/dispatches.rs)).

`limit_price` uses the same balance units as the subnet burn price. The check is a price-protection
rejection on the `register_limit` path, not proof that the coldkey lacks enough TAO to pay the burn
or that the hotkey is already registered.

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a coldkey submits `register_limit` with a `limit_price` below the subnet's
current registration burn. That can happen when demand pushed the burn up between quote and
submission, when automation reused an old cap after another registration succeeded on the subnet, or
when the client set a conservative maximum to avoid paying more than intended
([Understanding Neurons](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/neurons),
[Neuron Precompile: registerLimit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/evm-tutorials/neuron-precompile#registerlimit)).

When debugging, compare against other registration rejections on related calls.
`NotEnoughBalanceToStake` means the coldkey cannot fund the current burn even when no cap is set.
`HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet` means the hotkey already holds a UID on the subnet.
`SubNetRegistrationDisabled` means registration is turned off on the netuid
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeyalreadyregisteredinsubnet),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: SubNetRegistrationDisabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#subnetregistrationdisabled)).

The practical meaning is narrow: raise `limit_price`, wait for the burn to decay, or use an uncapped
registration path if paying the live burn is acceptable.

## Distinction from Not Enough Balance To Stake

`RegistrationPriceLimitExceeded` and `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` both involve registration burn
pricing, but they test different conditions. `RegistrationPriceLimitExceeded` is raised in
`do_register_limit` when the live burn exceeds the caller's cap. `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` is raised
later in `do_register` when the coldkey cannot remove enough free balance to pay the burn
([Subtensor source: registration.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/registration.rs)).

- **RegistrationPriceLimitExceeded** — the live burn cost exceeds `limit_price`.
- **NotEnoughBalanceToStake** — the coldkey cannot fund the registration burn.

## Distinction from Hotkey Already Registered In Subnet

`RegistrationPriceLimitExceeded` and `HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet` both block registration, but
at different stages. `RegistrationPriceLimitExceeded` is the burn-cap check on `register_limit`
before the shared flow begins. `HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet` means the hotkey already occupies a
UID on the subnet
([Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeyalreadyregisteredinsubnet)).

- **RegistrationPriceLimitExceeded** — the submitted burn cap is too low for the current price.
- **HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet** — the hotkey already has a UID on the subnet.

## Distinction from Subnet Not Exists

`RegistrationPriceLimitExceeded` and `SubnetNotExists` both appear on `register_limit`, but at
different steps. `SubnetNotExists` means the submitted netuid does not name a live subnet.
`RegistrationPriceLimitExceeded` means the subnet exists but its current burn exceeds the cap
([Subtensor source: registration.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/registration.rs)).

- **RegistrationPriceLimitExceeded** — burn cost exceeds `limit_price`.
- **SubnetNotExists** — the submitted netuid is not a live subnet.

## Distinction from Registration Not Permitted On Root Subnet

`RegistrationPriceLimitExceeded` and `RegistrationNotPermittedOnRootSubnet` both block
`register_limit`, but for different reasons. `RegistrationNotPermittedOnRootSubnet` means the call
targeted the Root Subnet, which does not use this burn-registration path.
`RegistrationPriceLimitExceeded` applies on ordinary mining subnets when the live burn exceeds the
submitted cap
([Registration Not Permitted On Root Subnet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#registrationnotpermittedonrootsubnet)).

- **RegistrationPriceLimitExceeded** — the burn cap is below the live registration cost.
- **RegistrationNotPermittedOnRootSubnet** — the call targeted the Root Subnet.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the burn-cap comparison on the `register_limit`
path. It does not report the live registration burn on any particular netuid; that value is subnet
chain state readers can query at observation time
([Subnet Hyperparameters: View hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#view-hyperparameters),
[Bittensor Networks](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/bittensor-networks)).
