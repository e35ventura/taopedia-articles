# Slippage Too High

`SlippageTooHigh` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised on staking paths when a submitted
amount would exceed the caller's slippage ceiling and the extrinsic does not allow partial
execution. The standard-errors reference describes it as "Slippage is too high"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#slippagetoohigh)).

## What It Means

Add-stake and remove-stake extrinsics accept a `max_amount` cap and an `allow_partial` flag so
callers can bound how much TAO or alpha the pool conversion may consume. In `stake_utils.rs`,
`validate_add_stake` and `validate_remove_stake` compare the submitted amount against that cap when
`allow_partial` is false and return `SlippageTooHigh` when the amount is larger than `max_amount`
([Understanding Slippage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/slippage),
[Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

On add-stake paths, this check runs after the minimum-stake floor passes and before the coldkey
balance and pool-simulation steps. When `allow_partial` is true instead, the runtime caps the stake
amount to `max_amount` rather than returning this error
([Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

On remove-stake paths, this check runs after zero-amount and minimum-remainder validation against
the pool simulation, and only when `allow_partial` is false
([Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a wallet or integration names a stake or unstake amount above the slippage
ceiling it supplied, with partial execution turned off. That commonly happens when live pool pricing
moves between quote time and submission, when `max_amount` is set too tightly for the intended trade
size, or when automation reuses a stale cap
([Understanding Slippage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/slippage),
[Managing Your Stakes](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-sdk),
[Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

For a reader, the practical meaning is narrow: the named amount exceeds the caller's own slippage
limit. The problem is not that the amount is below the minimum stake increment, that free balance
cannot fund the call, or that the subnet pool cannot simulate the swap
([Amount Too Low](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#amounttoolow),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: InsufficientLiquidity](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#insufficientliquidity)).

## Distinction from Amount Too Low

`SlippageTooHigh` and `AmountTooLow` both appear during staking validation, but they guard different
limits. `AmountTooLow` rejects amounts below `DefaultMinStake` rules, including zero unstake amounts
and partial unstakes that would leave a remainder below the floor. `SlippageTooHigh` rejects amounts
above the caller-supplied `max_amount` when partial execution is disabled
([Amount Too Low](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#amounttoolow),
[Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

- **AmountTooLow** — the stake amount is below the minimum allowed increment.
- **SlippageTooHigh** — the stake amount exceeds the caller's slippage cap.

## Distinction from Insufficient Liquidity

`SlippageTooHigh` and `InsufficientLiquidity` both relate to subnet pool conversions, but at
different stages. `SlippageTooHigh` is the caller's ceiling check on the submitted amount before the
runtime proceeds with balance and pool execution steps on add-stake, or before lock checks on
remove-stake. `InsufficientLiquidity` means the pool could not simulate or complete the swap needed
for the action
([Insufficient Liquidity](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#insufficientliquidity),
[Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

- **SlippageTooHigh** — the named amount exceeds the caller's slippage cap.
- **InsufficientLiquidity** — the pool could not support the swap.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the `max_amount` checks in `validate_add_stake`
and `validate_remove_stake`. It does not describe every move-stake or order-swap path that can raise
other slippage-related rejections, or how to size `max_amount` against live reserve levels; those
are documented separately ([Understanding Slippage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/slippage),
[Managing Your Stakes](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-sdk)).
