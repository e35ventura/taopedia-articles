# Slippage Too High

`SlippageTooHigh` means a price-protected staking call on netuid 1 or another named subnet asked for
more than the submitted limit price allows while partial execution was disabled. The Subtensor API
errors reference summarizes it as "Transaction slippage is above your limit"
([Subtensor API Errors: SlippageTooHigh](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#slippagetoohigh),
[Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#slippagetoohigh)).

## What It Means

Staking moves value through each subnet's TAO and alpha reserves
([Staking and Delegation](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)).

`add_stake_limit` and `remove_stake_limit` let a coldkey name a limit price and choose whether the
call may partially fill when pool movement would exceed that band
([Staking Implementation](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/navigating-subtensor/swap-stake),
[Price Protection](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/price-protection)).

`SlippageTooHigh` is the strict-mode rejection on those limit variants. The runtime first computes
the largest stake or unstake amount that still fits the submitted limit price, then compares the
caller's requested size against that cap. When partial fills are disabled and the request is larger,
the extrinsic fails with this error instead of trimming the amount
([Staking Implementation](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/navigating-subtensor/swap-stake),
[Understanding Slippage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/slippage)).

This is a price-protection rejection, not proof that the coldkey lacks balance for an ordinary
add-stake call or that the subnet pool has no liquidity at any size.

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a coldkey submits `add_stake_limit` or `remove_stake_limit` with strict
fill-or-kill protection and names an amount larger than the limit price currently permits on the
subnet. That can happen when the limit price is tighter than the live pool ratio, when a client
reuses a quote from an earlier block, or when the caller intentionally disallows partial fills so
the trade must execute fully within the band
([Price Protection](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/price-protection),
[Understanding Slippage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/slippage)).

On netuid 1, readers can compare a proposed limit against live subnet pool context through the
subnet documentation and storage query patterns described for hyperparameters and reserves before
resubmitting a protected stake call
([Subnet Hyperparameters: View hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#view-hyperparameters)).

When debugging, compare against other limit-path rejections on the same call. `ZeroMaxStakeAmount`
means no executable amount remains within the submitted limit price at all. `InsufficientLiquidity`
means the pool swap behind the action could not simulate or complete. `AmountTooLow` means the
submitted amount is below the minimum stake increment
([Subtensor Standard Errors: ZeroMaxStakeAmount](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#zeromaxstakeamount),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: InsufficientLiquidity](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#insufficientliquidity),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: AmountTooLow](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#amounttoolow)).

The practical meaning is narrow: lower the requested amount, relax the limit price, allow partial
fills, or wait for pool conditions that fit the submitted protection.

## Distinction from Zero Max Stake Amount

`SlippageTooHigh` and `ZeroMaxStakeAmount` both involve price protection on limit staking paths, but
they reject different outcomes. `ZeroMaxStakeAmount` means the limit-price search found no
executable stake amount on the subnet. `SlippageTooHigh` means some amount could execute within the
band, but the caller requested more than that maximum while partial fills were disabled
([Subtensor Standard Errors: ZeroMaxStakeAmount](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#zeromaxstakeamount)).

- **SlippageTooHigh** — strict protection rejected an oversized request.
- **ZeroMaxStakeAmount** — no amount can execute within the limit price.

## Distinction from Insufficient Liquidity

`SlippageTooHigh` and `InsufficientLiquidity` both can appear when a pool-backed conversion fails,
but at different checks. `InsufficientLiquidity` means the swap simulation or execution could not
complete in the pool. `SlippageTooHigh` means strict price protection blocked the requested size
even though a smaller amount might still fit the limit
([Subtensor Standard Errors: InsufficientLiquidity](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#insufficientliquidity)).

- **SlippageTooHigh** — strict limit protection blocked the requested size.
- **InsufficientLiquidity** — the pool could not support the swap behind the action.

## Distinction from Not Enough Balance To Stake

`SlippageTooHigh` and `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` both appear on add-stake paths, but they test
different conditions. `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` means the coldkey cannot fund the TAO amount the
call would withdraw. `SlippageTooHigh` means the requested add-stake size exceeds what the submitted
limit price allows under strict protection
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake)).

- **SlippageTooHigh** — strict price protection rejected the requested add-stake size.
- **NotEnoughBalanceToStake** — the coldkey cannot fund the withdrawal amount.

## Distinction from Not Enough Stake To Withdraw

`SlippageTooHigh` and `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` both appear on remove-stake paths, but they test
different conditions. `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` means the hotkey-coldkey pair does not hold enough
staked alpha for the removal. `SlippageTooHigh` means the requested unstake size exceeds what the
submitted limit price allows under strict protection
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughstaketowithdraw)).

- **SlippageTooHigh** — strict price protection rejected the requested unstake size.
- **NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw** — the pair lacks enough staked alpha for the request.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the strict price-protection gate it represents.
It does not quote a live limit price, pool ratio, or maximum executable amount on netuid 1 or any
other subnet; those values are subnet chain state readers can query for the selected netuid
([Bittensor Networks](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/bittensor-networks)).
