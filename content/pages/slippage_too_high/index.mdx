# Slippage Too High

`SlippageTooHigh` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised on limit staking paths. The
standard-errors reference describes it as "Transaction slippage is above your limit"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#slippagetoohigh)).

## What It Means

Limit stake and unstake calls let a wallet cap how much of a requested swap the chain may execute.
In `stake_utils.rs`, `validate_add_stake` and `validate_remove_stake` return `SlippageTooHigh` when
`allow_partial` is false and the amount that would execute exceeds the caller's `max_amount` ceiling
([Understanding Slippage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/slippage),
[Slippage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#slippage),
[Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

On add-stake limit paths, `do_add_stake_limit` derives `max_amount` from the submitted `limit_price`
through `get_max_amount_add` before calling `validate_add_stake`
([Subtensor source: add_stake.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/add_stake.rs),
[Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

When `allow_partial` is true, the validation path caps the executable amount instead of returning
this error
([Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a wallet submits a limit stake or unstake with partial execution disabled
and the pool conversion would require executing more than the caller's `max_amount` protection
allows. That can happen when `limit_price` is tighter than the live pool price, when the requested
TAO or alpha amount is larger than the slippage ceiling permits, or when market movement between
simulation and submission widens the gap
([Understanding Slippage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/slippage),
[Managing Your Stakes](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-sdk),
[Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

For a reader, the practical meaning is narrow: the swap would breach the caller's slippage limit
with partial execution turned off. The problem is not that the amount is below the minimum stake
increment, that the coldkey lacks free balance, or that the pool cannot simulate the swap
([Amount Too Low](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#amounttoolow),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: InsufficientLiquidity](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#insufficientliquidity)).

## Distinction from Amount Too Low

`SlippageTooHigh` and `AmountTooLow` both appear in staking validation, but they test opposite
bounds. `SlippageTooHigh` is the upper ceiling enforced when `allow_partial` is false and the
executable amount would exceed `max_amount`. `AmountTooLow` is the lower floor tied to
`DefaultMinStake` and zero unstake rejection
([Amount Too Low](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#amounttoolow),
[Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

- **SlippageTooHigh** — the swap would exceed the caller's slippage limit.
- **AmountTooLow** — the stake amount is below the minimum allowed increment.

## Distinction from Insufficient Liquidity

`SlippageTooHigh` and `InsufficientLiquidity` both involve subnet pool swaps, but they reject
different failures. `SlippageTooHigh` means the caller's slippage protection blocked execution
because the amount exceeded `max_amount` with partial execution disabled. `InsufficientLiquidity`
means the swap simulation or execution could not complete in the pool
([Subtensor Standard Errors: InsufficientLiquidity](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#insufficientliquidity),
[Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

- **SlippageTooHigh** — the caller's slippage limit was breached.
- **InsufficientLiquidity** — the pool could not support the swap.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the `max_amount` check that raises it in
`validate_add_stake` and `validate_remove_stake`. It does not describe every move-stake or
order-swap path that can also raise `SlippageTooHigh`, or how to choose `limit_price` values for a
specific wallet workflow; those are documented separately
([Understanding Slippage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/slippage),
[Managing Your Stakes](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-sdk),
[Subtensor source: order_swap.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/order_swap.rs)).
