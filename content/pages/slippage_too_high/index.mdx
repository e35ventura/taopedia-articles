# Slippage Too High

`SlippageTooHigh` means a limit stake or unstake call would convert more through the subnet pool
than the wallet's slippage protection allows. The standard-errors reference describes it as
"Transaction slippage is above your limit"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#slippagetoohigh)).

## What It Means

Bittensor staking and unstaking can move value through subnet pool reserves, where the effective
conversion price shifts as the trade size changes
([Understanding Slippage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/slippage),
[Slippage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#slippage)).

Limit stake and unstake calls let a wallet name a slippage ceiling before the swap runs. When
partial fills are disabled, `SlippageTooHigh` is the rejection signal that the conversion would need
to execute beyond that ceiling, so the extrinsic stops instead of accepting worse pool terms
([Managing Your Stakes](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-sdk),
[Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#slippagetoohigh)).

When partial fills are enabled, the chain may execute a smaller amount that fits the cap rather than
returning this error
([Managing Your Stakes](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-sdk)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a wallet submits a limit stake or unstake with a tight slippage cap and
without allowing partial execution. Common causes include a `limit_price` that no longer matches the
pool after market movement, a requested TAO or alpha amount larger than the cap permits, or a client
that disables partial fills while still naming an aggressive limit
([Understanding Slippage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/slippage),
[Managing Your Stakes](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-sdk)).

For a reader, the practical meaning is narrow: the swap would breach the wallet's slippage limit.
The problem is not that the amount is below the minimum stake increment, that free balance cannot
fund the call, or that the pool cannot complete the conversion at all
([Amount Too Low](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#amounttoolow),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: InsufficientLiquidity](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#insufficientliquidity)).

## Distinction from Amount Too Low

`SlippageTooHigh` and `AmountTooLow` both appear around staking amounts, but they reject opposite
problems. `SlippageTooHigh` means the swap would exceed the caller's upper slippage cap on a limit
call with partial fills disabled. `AmountTooLow` means the named amount is below the chain's minimum
stake increment or is zero on an unstake path
([Amount Too Low](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#amounttoolow),
[Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#slippagetoohigh)).

- **SlippageTooHigh** — the swap would exceed the caller's slippage limit.
- **AmountTooLow** — the stake amount is below the minimum allowed increment.

## Distinction from Insufficient Liquidity

`SlippageTooHigh` and `InsufficientLiquidity` both involve subnet pool swaps, but they signal
different failures. `SlippageTooHigh` is the wallet's slippage protection rejecting a conversion
that would execute past its cap. `InsufficientLiquidity` means the pool could not support the swap
simulation or execution
([Subtensor Standard Errors: InsufficientLiquidity](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#insufficientliquidity),
[Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#slippagetoohigh)).

- **SlippageTooHigh** — the caller's slippage limit was breached.
- **InsufficientLiquidity** — the pool could not support the swap.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label on limit stake and unstake paths. It does not
explain how to pick `limit_price` values for a specific wallet workflow or every other extrinsic
that can surface slippage-related rejections; those are documented separately
([Understanding Slippage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/slippage),
[Managing Your Stakes](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-sdk)).
