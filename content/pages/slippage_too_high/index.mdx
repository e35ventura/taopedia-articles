# Slippage Too High

`SlippageTooHigh` means the submitted stake or unstake amount exceeds the slippage ceiling the
caller supplied, and the extrinsic does not allow partial execution. The standard-errors reference
describes it as "Slippage is too high"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#slippagetoohigh)).

Add-stake and remove-stake calls can include a maximum-amount cap and a partial-execution flag so
wallets bound how much TAO or alpha a subnet pool conversion may consume
([Understanding Slippage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/slippage),
[Managing Your Stakes](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-sdk)).

## What It Means

Slippage protection lets a caller set the worst amount it is willing to accept from a pool swap.
When partial execution is disabled, naming an amount above that cap raises `SlippageTooHigh` instead
of filling only part of the request
([Understanding Slippage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/slippage),
[Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#slippagetoohigh)).

For debugging, treat this as a caller-side slippage limit failure, not as proof that the amount is
below the minimum stake increment, that the coldkey lacks free balance to fund an add-stake call, or
that the subnet pool cannot support the swap
([Subtensor Standard Errors: AmountTooLow](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#amounttoolow),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: InsufficientLiquidity](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#insufficientliquidity)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a wallet or integration names a stake or unstake amount above the slippage
ceiling it supplied, with partial execution turned off. That commonly happens when live pool pricing
moves between quote time and submission, when the cap is set too tightly for the intended trade
size, or when automation reuses a stale limit
([Understanding Slippage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/slippage),
[Managing Your Stakes](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-sdk)).

When partial execution is enabled, the runtime can cap the filled amount to the supplied limit
rather than returning this error
([Understanding Slippage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/slippage)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the named amount exceeds the caller's own slippage limit.

## Distinction from Amount Too Low

`SlippageTooHigh` and `AmountTooLow` both appear during staking validation, but they guard different
limits. `AmountTooLow` rejects amounts below the chain's minimum staking increment.
`SlippageTooHigh` rejects amounts above the caller-supplied slippage cap when partial execution is
disabled
([Subtensor Standard Errors: AmountTooLow](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#amounttoolow),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: SlippageTooHigh](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#slippagetoohigh)).

- **AmountTooLow** — the stake amount is below the minimum allowed increment.
- **SlippageTooHigh** — the stake amount exceeds the caller's slippage cap.

## Distinction from Insufficient Liquidity

`SlippageTooHigh` and `InsufficientLiquidity` both relate to subnet pool conversions, but they
reject different conditions. `SlippageTooHigh` means the caller's own slippage ceiling was exceeded.
`InsufficientLiquidity` means the pool could not simulate or complete the swap needed for the action
([Subtensor Standard Errors: InsufficientLiquidity](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#insufficientliquidity),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: SlippageTooHigh](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#slippagetoohigh)).

- **SlippageTooHigh** — the named amount exceeds the caller's slippage cap.
- **InsufficientLiquidity** — the pool could not support the swap.

## Distinction from Not Enough Balance To Stake

`SlippageTooHigh` and `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` both can appear on add-stake calls, but they compare
different conditions. `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` means the signing coldkey does not have enough free
TAO to fund the requested amount. `SlippageTooHigh` means the named amount exceeds the caller's
slippage cap with partial execution disabled
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: SlippageTooHigh](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#slippagetoohigh)).

- **NotEnoughBalanceToStake** — not enough free TAO to add stake.
- **SlippageTooHigh** — the named amount exceeds the caller's slippage cap.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the caller slippage-cap condition that raises
it. It does not describe how to size slippage limits against live pool reserves, or every staking
path that can return other swap-related rejections; those are separate topics
([Understanding Slippage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/slippage),
[Managing Your Stakes](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-sdk)).
