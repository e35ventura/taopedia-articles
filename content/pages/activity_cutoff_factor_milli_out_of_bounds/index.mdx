# Activity Cutoff Factor Milli Out Of Bounds

`ActivityCutoffFactorMilliOutOfBounds` means an activity cutoff factor update on netuid 1 or another
named subnet submitted a per-mille value outside the documented allowed band. The standard-errors
reference describes it as "The supplied activity-cutoff factor is outside the allowed range"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#activitycutofffactormillioutofbounds)).

Activity cutoff factor expresses validator inactivity tolerance in per-mille epochs so the effective
window scales with tempo
([Activity Cutoff Factor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#activitycutofffactor),
[Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters)).

## What It Means

The subnet hyperparameter reference documents `ActivityCutoffFactor` as a `u32` measured in
per-mille epochs, with an allowed range from 1,000 to 50,000 per-mille. The constants reference
lists the same floor and ceiling as `minActivityCutoffFactorMilli` at 1,000 and
`maxActivityCutoffFactorMilli` at 50,000
([Subnet Hyperparameters: ActivityCutoffFactor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#activitycutofffactor),
[Subtensor Constants: minActivityCutoffFactorMilli](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/constants#minactivitycutofffactormilli-u32)).

`ActivityCutoffFactorMilliOutOfBounds` means that owner-path update rejected the submitted factor
because it fell below 1,000 or above 50,000 per-mille
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#activitycutofffactormillioutofbounds)).

This is a value-range gate on the activity cutoff factor setter. The failure concerns the submitted
per-mille count, not whether the caller lacks permission or whether the admin freeze window is
active.

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a subnet owner submits an activity cutoff factor below one tempo or above
fifty tempos in per-mille units. That can happen when a client uses the wrong scale, copies an
outdated extrinsic, or submits a value outside the documented 1,000 to 50,000 band
([Subnet Hyperparameters: ActivityCutoffFactor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#activitycutofffactor)).

When debugging on netuid 1, readers can read the live factor with
`btcli subnet hyperparameters --netuid 1 --network finney` before resubmitting an update
([Subnet Hyperparameters: View hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#view-hyperparameters)).

When debugging, rule out authorization and timing failures first. `BadOrigin` and `NotSubnetOwner`
mean the signer lacks subnet-owner permission. `AdminActionProhibitedDuringWeightsWindow` means the
call arrived during the epoch-end admin freeze
([Subtensor Standard Errors: BadOrigin](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#badorigin),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NotSubnetOwner](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#notsubnetowner),
[Admin Action Prohibited During Weights Window](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#adminactionprohibitedduringweightswindow)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the submitted activity cutoff factor is outside the allowed
per-mille range.

## Distinction from Activity Cutoff Too Low

`ActivityCutoffFactorMilliOutOfBounds` and `ActivityCutoffTooLow` both involve inactivity tolerance
settings, but on different hyperparameters. `ActivityCutoffFactorMilliOutOfBounds` applies to the
live `ActivityCutoffFactor` per-mille setter. `ActivityCutoffTooLow` applies to the legacy
fixed-block `ActivityCutoff` map and its `MinActivityCutoff` floor
([Subtensor Standard Errors: ActivityCutoffTooLow](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#activitycutofftoolow),
[Activity Cutoff](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#activitycutoff)).

- **ActivityCutoffFactorMilliOutOfBounds** — the submitted per-mille factor is outside 1,000 to
  50,000.
- **ActivityCutoffTooLow** — the legacy activity cutoff block count is below the minimum floor.

## Distinction from Admin Action Prohibited During Weights Window

`ActivityCutoffFactorMilliOutOfBounds` and `AdminActionProhibitedDuringWeightsWindow` both appear on
subnet hyperparameter updates, but they reject different problems.
`ActivityCutoffFactorMilliOutOfBounds` means the submitted per-mille value is outside the documented
band. `AdminActionProhibitedDuringWeightsWindow` means the signer was otherwise permitted, but the
admin freeze window was active
([Admin Action Prohibited During Weights Window](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#adminactionprohibitedduringweightswindow)).

- **ActivityCutoffFactorMilliOutOfBounds** — the submitted factor is outside the allowed range.
- **AdminActionProhibitedDuringWeightsWindow** — the call arrived during the admin freeze window.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the 1,000 to 50,000 per-mille gate it
represents. It does not report the live activity cutoff factor on a particular netuid; that value is
subnet chain state readers can query for the selected netuid
([Subnet Hyperparameters: View hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#view-hyperparameters),
[Bittensor Networks](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/bittensor-networks)).
