# Axon

An axon is the endpoint-side component in Bittensor's Axon-dendrite communication model. The
official [Glossary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#axon) describes axons as
receiving incoming synapse objects and as the advertised entry point commonly associated with a
subnet miner. The
[Axon reference](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/python-api/html/autoapi/bittensor/core/axon/) is
the official technical reference for the same Bittensor component.

## Relationship to Dendrites

Axon and [[dendrite|Dendrite]] are paired terms. A dendrite starts an exchange with an axon
endpoint, and the axon is the endpoint that receives the incoming synapse object. The
[Glossary entry for Dendrite](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#dendrite) frames
dendrites as client instances that communicate with axons through the Axon-dendrite model.

## Subnet Communication Context

The term is useful because subnet validation depends on miner-validator interaction. The glossary
frames an axon as an entry point commonly associated with a subnet miner so subnet validators can
communicate with that miner. At Taopedia level, the important distinction is conceptual: axon names
the endpoint-side communication component, while miner and validator remain the subnet roles.
