# Validator Registration

Validator registration is how a participant enters a Bittensor subnet as a validator. The
[Validating: Validator registration](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators#validator-registration)
reference describes registration as registering a hotkey with a subnet to receive a UID on that
subnet so the validator can take part in evaluation and validation work there.

## Distinction from UID Slot

The glossary describes register as purchasing a UID slot through a dynamic registration burn in TAO.
Validator registration names that entry process when the participant entering the slot will validate
on the subnet ([Glossary: Register](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#register),
[Glossary: UID Slot](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#uid-slot),
[Validator Registration](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators#validator-registration)).
Registration is the paid entry step; the UID slot is the numbered position inside the subnet that
entry grants once it succeeds.

- **Validator registration** — paid entry process for a validating participant.
- **UID slot** — numbered position granted inside a subnet after entry.

## Distinction from Validator Deregistration

Validator registration gives a subnet validator a UID slot after registering a hotkey with that
subnet. Validator deregistration removes a low-performing validator slot when registration pressure
requires replacement, while the subnet itself continues
([Validator Deregistration](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators#validator-deregistration),
[Glossary: Register](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#register),
[Subnet Deregistration](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-deregistration)).

- **Validator registration** — entry into a subnet validator slot.
- **Validator deregistration** — removal of a low-performing validator slot.

## Distinction from Burn Cost

Validator registration is the validator-specific subnet entry process that grants a UID slot. Burn
cost is the dynamic TAO amount paid to register on a subnet
([Validating: Validator registration](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators#validator-registration),
[Glossary: Burn Cost](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#burn-cost)).

- **Validator registration** — paid entry process for a validating participant.
- **Burn cost** — dynamic TAO price for that registration.

Entry-process vocabulary and registration-price vocabulary answer different questions on the same
subnet path ([Glossary: Register](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#register)).

## Distinction from Yuma Consensus

Validator registration obtains a validator UID slot on a selected netuid. Yuma Consensus converts
validator weight submissions into emission shares each tempo once validators are active
([Glossary: Register](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#register),
[Yuma Consensus](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus)).

- **Validator registration** — obtaining a validator UID slot on a netuid.
- **Yuma Consensus** — mechanism that turns validator weights into emission shares.

Entry vocabulary names slot acquisition; Yuma vocabulary names settlement after validators operate
([Emission](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions)).

## Distinction from Validator Permit

Validator registration names the paid entry process that grants a validator UID slot on a subnet.
Validator permit records whether that subnet UID may submit counting weights after entry
([Validating: Validator registration](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators#validator-registration),
[Glossary: Validator Permit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-permit)).

- **Validator registration** — paid entry into a subnet validator slot.
- **Validator permit** — flag recording whether subnet weights may count.

Entry vocabulary names how a hotkey obtains a slot; permit vocabulary names weight-submission rights
([Glossary: Subnet Validator](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#subnet-validator)).

## Distinction from Registration Cost

Validator registration names the validator-specific subnet entry process. Registration cost is the
dynamic TAO burn paid at entry time
([Validating: Validator registration](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators#validator-registration),
[Glossary: Burn Cost](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#burn-cost)).

- **Validator registration** — entry process connecting a validator hotkey to a subnet UID slot.
- **Registration cost** — dynamic TAO amount paid to complete that entry.

Process vocabulary names the admission step; cost vocabulary names the live burn price
([Glossary: Register](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#register)).

## Reader Boundary

Validator registration should not be read as a recommendation to join any particular subnet, as a
command tutorial, or as a guarantee of emissions. It is the concept term for how a validator enters
a subnet UID slot and begins participating in subnet validation vocabulary
([Validating: Validator registration](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators#validator-registration)).

Current chain values, wallet state, subnet-specific outcomes, or production measurements belong to
the relevant network, block, account, or tool output at the time of observation.
