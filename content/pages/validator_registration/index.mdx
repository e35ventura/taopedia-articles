# Validator Registration

Validator registration is how a participant enters a Bittensor subnet as a validator. The official
[Validating: Validator registration](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators#validator-registration)
documentation describes registration as registering a hotkey with a subnet to receive a UID on that
subnet so the validator can take part in evaluation and validation work there.

## Why Registration Matters

Subnets organize participants around UID slots. The
[Glossary: UID Slot](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#uid-slot) defines a slot as
the position a subnet miner or validator occupies inside a subnet. Without registration, a hotkey is
not part of that subnet's validator set.

For a reader, validator registration is the entry point into subnet validation vocabulary. It does
not describe validator quality, delegated stake totals, or which subnet to join.

## Validator Role After Registration

A [subnet validator](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#subnet-validator) evaluates
miner work inside a subnet and participates in the subnet's consensus and emissions picture.
Official validating documentation treats registration as the step that connects a validator hotkey
to that role through a subnet UID slot.

This article does not describe validator setup, weight submission commands, or live permit tables.
Those details belong in official validating references and live subnet data.

References:
[Validating: Validator registration](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators#validator-registration),
[Understanding Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-subnets)

## Registration Cost Context

Validator registration uses the same broad register vocabulary as other subnet participants. The
[Glossary: Register](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#register) describes subnet
entry as purchasing a UID slot through a dynamic registration burn in TAO. Official validating
documentation points readers to current registration cost references rather than fixed values.

Validator registration should not be read as a price quote. It names the paid entry step that
connects a validator hotkey to a subnet UID slot.

## Permit and Lifecycle Context

Registration is only the entry step. A validator also needs validation-permit context to perform
validation work inside a subnet. The
[Glossary: Validator Permit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-permit)
marks whether a participant has validation rights, and
[Validator Deregistration](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators#validator-deregistration)
describes how a full subnet can later remove a low-performing validator when registration pressure
requires replacement.

For readers, registration starts validator participation; permit status and deregistration describe
later lifecycle rules around that slot.

## Relationship to Register

Validator registration and register are related but different scopes in Bittensor subnet vocabulary.
The [Glossary: Register](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#register) describes
subnet entry as purchasing a UID slot through a dynamic registration burn in TAO, while
[Validating: Validator registration](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators#validator-registration)
describes that entry process applied to a subnet validator who will take part in evaluation and
validation work.

For readers, register is the general subnet-entry term, while validator registration names the
validator-specific case of that process.

References: [Glossary: Register](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#register),
[Validating: Validator registration](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators#validator-registration)

## Relationship to UID Slot

Validator registration and a UID slot are related but different parts of the subnet participation
lifecycle. The [Glossary: Register](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#register)
describes the subnet-entry process, while the
[Glossary: UID Slot](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#uid-slot) describes the
participant position inside a subnet that entry grants.

For readers, validator registration names the validator-specific entry process, while a UID slot
names the position a validator occupies after that entry succeeds.

References: [Glossary: UID Slot](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#uid-slot),
[Validating: Validator registration](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators#validator-registration)

## Relationship to Netuid

Validator registration and a netuid are related but different parts of Bittensor subnet vocabulary.
Register describes the subnet-entry process that grants a UID slot, while a netuid selects which
subnet that entry targets. The
[Glossary: Netuid](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#netuid) places netuid at the
subnet-selection level, and
[Validating: Validator registration](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators#validator-registration)
describes validator entry into a selected subnet.

For readers, netuid names which subnet market the validator is entering, while validator
registration names the validator-specific entry process inside that subnet context.

References: [Glossary: Netuid](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#netuid),
[Validating: Validator registration](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators#validator-registration)

## Relationship to Validator Permit

Validator registration and a validator permit are related but different parts of validator
participation after entry. Validator registration names the subnet-entry process that grants a UID
slot, while the
[Glossary: Validator Permit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-permit)
describes whether a participant has validation rights inside a subnet.

For readers, validator registration names how a validator enters a subnet, while validator permit
names whether that participant can perform validation work inside the subnet after entry.

References:
[Validating: Validator registration](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators#validator-registration),
[Glossary: Validator Permit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-permit)

## Reader Boundary

Validator registration should not be read as a recommendation to join any particular subnet, as a
command tutorial, or as a guarantee of emissions. It is the concept term for how a validator enters
a subnet UID slot and begins participating in subnet validation vocabulary.

## Relationship to Subtensor

Validator registration and Subtensor are related but different parts of Bittensor chain vocabulary.
Subtensor names Bittensor's layer 1 blockchain and system of record, while validator registration
names the subnet-entry process that places a validator inside a UID slot on that chain layer. The
[Glossary: Subtensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#subtensor) describes the
blockchain layer, and
[Validating: Validator registration](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators#validator-registration)
describes validator entry into a subnet UID slot.

For readers, Subtensor names the chain layer, while validator registration names the
validator-specific entry process recorded on that layer.

References: [Glossary: Subtensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#subtensor),
[Validating: Validator registration](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators#validator-registration)

## Relationship to Extrinsics

Validator registration and extrinsics are related but different parts of Bittensor chain action
vocabulary. Extrinsics name signed transactions and calls submitted to Subtensor, while validator
registration names the subnet-entry outcome that a successful register action can grant to a
validator. The [Glossary: Extrinsics](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#extrinsics)
describes signed transactions submitted to the chain, and the
[Glossary: Register](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#register) describes subnet
entry as purchasing a UID slot through a dynamic registration burn in TAO.

For readers, extrinsics name the submitted chain actions, while validator registration names the
validator-specific entry process those register actions implement.

References: [Glossary: Extrinsics](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#extrinsics),
[Glossary: Register](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#register)

## Relationship to Mempool

Validator registration and the mempool are related but different stages of Bittensor chain activity.
The mempool names pending transactions before block inclusion, while validator registration names
the subnet-entry outcome after a register action is accepted and processed on chain. The
[Glossary: Mempool](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#mempool) describes the
pending-transaction context, and
[Validating: Validator registration](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators#validator-registration)
describes validator entry into a subnet UID slot.

For readers, the mempool belongs to the pre-inclusion waiting stage, while validator registration
names the entry result once a register action succeeds on chain.

References: [Glossary: Mempool](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#mempool),
[Validating: Validator registration](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators#validator-registration)

## Relationship to Transaction Fees

Validator registration and transaction fees are related but different parts of Bittensor chain
vocabulary. Transaction fees name the costs attached to state-changing actions on Subtensor, while
validator registration names the subnet-entry process that includes a dynamic registration burn in
TAO under the register vocabulary. The
[Transaction Fees in Bittensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/fees) documentation describes
fee-bearing actions, and the
[Glossary: Register](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#register) describes subnet
entry as purchasing a UID slot through a dynamic registration burn in TAO.

For readers, transaction fees belong to the cost model for chain actions, while validator
registration names the paid entry process that can grant a validator a subnet UID slot.

References: [Transaction Fees in Bittensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/fees),
[Glossary: Register](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#register)
