# Hotkey

A hotkey is the operational key in Bittensor wallet design, used for daily network interactions such
as staking, mining, and validation. It is controlled by the coldkey, which is the ownership root for
TAO and stake ([Glossary: Hotkey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#hotkey),
[Wallets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/wallets)).

Hotkey is operational key vocabulary. It is not the ownership root and not the recovery material for
wallet assets. Those roles belong to the coldkey and seed phrase respectively.

## Coldkey and Hotkey Boundary

Bittensor wallet design separates the coldkey from the hotkey. The coldkey is the ownership root
that controls TAO and stake. The hotkey is the operational key used for active network participation
([Wallets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/wallets),
[Working with Keys](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/working-with-keys)).

This boundary keeps high-value key material separate from operational risk. A hotkey handles
frequent actions; the coldkey authorizes ownership transfers and major stake changes.

## Operational Role

Hotkeys handle the operational side of subnet participation. Miners and validators register to
subnets using hotkeys. Staking and unstaking actions are signed with hotkey authority in the
operational flow ([Working with Keys](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/working-with-keys)).

That operational scope is what distinguishes a hotkey from a coldkey. The hotkey acts; the coldkey
owns. A hotkey can be rotated without transferring underlying wallet ownership.

## Child Hotkeys

Child hotkeys are a related concept that extend operational key management. A parent hotkey can
delegate specific operational permissions to child hotkeys, which can then act within defined subnet
scopes ([Wallets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/wallets)).

This keeps hotkey vocabulary flexible for advanced delegation patterns without changing the core
meaning. A hotkey remains an operational key; child hotkeys are scoped delegations of that
operational role.

## Reader Boundary

Hotkey is concept vocabulary for the operational key role in Bittensor wallet design. It should not
be read as ownership material, a recovery mechanism, or a balance container
([Glossary: Hotkey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#hotkey)).

A coldkey holds ownership authority over wallet assets. A seed phrase recovers coldkey access. A
hotkey handles operational network interactions. The three terms answer different questions about
the same wallet system ([Wallets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/wallets)).
