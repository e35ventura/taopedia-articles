# Max Weight Limit

Max weight limit is a per-subnet hyperparameter, stored on chain as `MaxWeightsLimit`, that caps how
large any single weight a validator assigns may be, measured as a fraction of that validator's total
weight. It is enforced when a validator calls `set_weights`
([Subtensor source: subnets/weights.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/weights.rs)).

## What It Controls

A validator scores other neurons by submitting a vector of weights. Max weight limit bounds the
concentration of that vector: it sets the maximum share the largest single weight may hold once the
vector is normalized. It does not change how many UIDs a validator must score or how the resulting
weights feed consensus; it only limits how much weight one target can receive relative to the rest
([Subtensor source: subnets/weights.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/weights.rs)).

The value is a `u16` interpreted as a fraction of `u16::MAX`. A limit of `u16::MAX` corresponds to a
full share of `1.0`, so the largest weight may be the entire vector; a smaller value lowers the
allowed share. The stored default is `u16::MAX`, which means the cap has no practical effect until a
subnet owner lowers it
([Subtensor source: pallets/subtensor/src/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/lib.rs)).

## How It Is Enforced

When a validator submits weights, the runtime normalizes the submitted vector and then compares its
largest normalized entry against the limit expressed as a fraction of `u16::MAX`. The check passes
when the largest normalized weight is less than or equal to that fraction; it fails, and the call is
rejected with `MaxWeightExceeded`, only when the largest normalized weight is strictly greater than
the limit. A vector whose largest weight is exactly equal to the limit passes
([Subtensor source: subnets/weights.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/weights.rs),
[Subtensor source: epoch/math.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/epoch/math.rs)).

Two cases skip the cap. A self-weight, where a validator submits a single weight on its own UID, is
allowed to exceed the limit. And when the limit is set to `u16::MAX`, the check returns early and
always passes, so no concentration bound applies
([Subtensor source: subnets/weights.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/weights.rs)).

## Distinction from Max Weight Exceeded

Max weight limit is the hyperparameter; `MaxWeightExceeded` is the error raised when a weight
submission violates it. The limit defines the allowed ceiling on the largest normalized weight, and
the error is the rejection returned from `set_weights` when a submitted vector's largest weight is
above that ceiling
([Subtensor source: subnets/weights.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/weights.rs)).

- **Max weight limit** — the per-subnet cap on the largest normalized weight.
- **Max weight exceeded** — the error returned when a submission's largest weight is above the cap.

## Distinction from Min Allowed Weights

Max weight limit and min allowed weights both constrain a validator's weight vector, but on
different axes. Min allowed weights sets how many UIDs the vector must include. Max weight limit
bounds how large the single biggest weight may be once the vector is normalized. One is a floor on
breadth; the other is a ceiling on concentration
([Subtensor source: subnets/weights.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/weights.rs)).

- **Min allowed weights** — the minimum number of UIDs a weight vector must include.
- **Max weight limit** — the maximum share the largest single weight may hold.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the max weight limit hyperparameter and the concentration check it drives on
`set_weights`. It does not document the full weight-setting path, how normalized weights are later
combined during consensus, or the live value configured on any particular subnet; each subnet stores
its own `MaxWeightsLimit`
([Subtensor source: subnets/weights.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/weights.rs)).
