# Validator Take

Validator take is the share of delegated-stake emissions that a validator keeps before the remaining
emissions flow to stakers
([Glossary: Validator Take %](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-take-),
[Emission: Distribution](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions#distribution)).

The term is a distribution concept. It describes a split inside delegated-stake emissions after the
validator-side amount already exists, not the consensus step that produced that amount.

## Development Stage Context

The
[Introduction to Bittensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/introduction#subnet-development)
describes subnet development as moving from localnet to testnet and then mainnet. For validator take
on a subnet such as netuid 1, that sequence changes how readers should interpret delegated-stake
commission splits and staker distribution examples.

In localnet, validator-take examples can be exercised in an isolated environment. Local commission
settings reflect local chain configuration rather than production validator economics.

On testnet, validator take can be observed in a shared, non-production network. Testnet validator
commission and staker splits are separate from mainnet delegation state
([Emission: Distribution](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions#distribution)).

On mainnet, validator take is the live documented split between validators and their stakers on the
production Bittensor network
([Glossary: Validator Take %](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-take-)).

The [Bittensor Networks](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/bittensor-networks) reference
separates mainnet, testnet, and localnet. A validator-take example from one environment should not
be read as representing production commission settings on another network.

## Distinction from Subnet Owner Take

Validator take is the commission a validator keeps from delegated-stake dividends. Subnet owner take
is the documented share of subnet alpha emissions routed to the subnet owner
([Glossary: Validator Take %](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-take-),
[Emission: Distribution](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions#distribution)).

- **Validator take** — commission on validator–delegator dividend split.
- **Subnet owner take** — owner share from subnet alpha emissions.

Commission vocabulary and owner-cut vocabulary answer different distribution questions
([Delegation](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)).

## Distinction from Child Hotkeys

Validator take is the commission rate on validation dividends. A child hotkey is the authorized key
a parent delegate uses to validate with re-delegated stake
([Glossary: Validator Take %](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-take-),
[Child Hotkeys](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys)).

- **Validator take** — fee applied when dividends split between validator and stakers.
- **Child hotkey** — worker identity validating under delegated stake.

Commission vocabulary and delegation-key vocabulary answer different validator-side questions
([Yuma Consensus: Validator emissions](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus#validator-emissions)).

## Distinction from Dividends

Validator take is the share of validator dividends kept by the validator hotkey. Dividends are the
validator-side reward stream produced from subnet emissions before that split
([Glossary: Validator Take %](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-take-),
[Glossary: Dividends](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#emission)).

Take names the validator-kept portion at distribution time; dividends name the broader
validator-side payout category the take rate applies to
([Emission: Distribution](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions#distribution)).

## Distinction from Childkey Take

A child hotkey carries its own take rate, the childkey take, which applies at a different point in
the dividend flow. Validator take is the commission a validator keeps from emissions tied to
delegated stake before the remainder reaches delegators
([Glossary: Validator Take %](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-take-),
[Child Hotkeys](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys)).

Validator take governs the split between a validator and its delegators. Childkey take governs the
split between a parent hotkey and a child hotkey validating on its behalf.

## Distinction from Validator Permit

Validator take is the commission on delegated-stake dividends. A validator permit is the on-chain
right to submit counting weights and take part in consensus
([Glossary: Validator Take %](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-take-),
[Glossary: Validator Permit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-permit)).

A UID needs the permit before it earns validation dividends; the take only applies once those
dividends exist. Setting take does not grant a permit.

## Distinction from Effective Stake

Effective stake is the combined staking total behind a delegate hotkey. Validator take is the
commission rate applied when delegated-emission returns are split at distribution
([Glossary: Effective Stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#effective-stake),
[Glossary: Validator Take %](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-take-)).

Two validators with the same effective stake can leave different nominator outcomes if their take
rates differ. Amount vocabulary and commission vocabulary answer different reader questions.

## Distinction from Nominator

A nominator delegates TAO to a validator hotkey. Validator take names what share of
delegated-emission returns the validator keeps before the remainder flows to those nominators
([Glossary: Nominator](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominator),
[Glossary: Validator Take %](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-take-)).

Role vocabulary names who supplies support; take vocabulary names how delegated returns are split
between validator and stakers at tempo distribution
([Delegation](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)).

## Distinction from Delegation Rewards

Validator take is the commission a validator keeps from delegated-emission returns before nominators
receive the remainder, while delegation rewards are what remains for delegated stake after that
split
([Glossary: Validator Take %](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-take-),
[Staking and delegation overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)).

Take names the cut taken off the top; delegation rewards name the portion that reaches the nominator
afterward.

## Distinction from Emission Split

Validator take is not a fourth headline share in the emission split. It is the cut a validator keeps
from delegated stake inside the validator-and-staker bucket after that bucket is credited
([Glossary: Validator Take %](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-take-),
[Emission: Distribution](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions#distribution)).

The emission split names the role proportions credited at tempo end; validator take is applied
inside one of those buckets, not as a separate slice of the split.

## Distinction from Yuma Consensus

Validator take names the configurable commission a validator keeps from delegated-stake emissions.
Yuma Consensus is the separate mechanism that produces the validator-side emission pool from which
that take is applied during distribution each tempo
([Glossary: Validator Take %](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-take-),
[Yuma Consensus](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus),
[Emission](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions)).

- **Validator take** — commission on delegated-stake validator emissions.
- **Yuma Consensus** — mechanism that turns validator weights into emission shares. Readers should
  keep commission vocabulary separate from settlement vocabulary. Validator take sets what share a
  validator keeps from delegated emissions; Yuma produces the validator-side pool that take is
  applied to
  ([Glossary: Validator Take](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-take-),
  [Yuma Consensus](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus#validator-emissions)).

Take is a distribution rule on an existing emission pool. Yuma creates that pool from stake-weighted
weight aggregation each tempo ([Emission](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions)).

- **Validator dividend** — validator-side share before take is applied.

## Reader Boundary

Validator take should be read as reference vocabulary for emission distribution. It is not a
recommendation, validator quality score, or complete explanation of staking outcomes
([Glossary: Validator Take %](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-take-),
[Yuma Consensus: Validator emissions](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus#validator-emissions)).

The narrow meaning is the validator-kept share before remaining delegated-stake emissions flow to
stakers.
