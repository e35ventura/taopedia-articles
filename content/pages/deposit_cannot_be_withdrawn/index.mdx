# Deposit Cannot Be Withdrawn

`DepositCannotBeWithdrawn` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised by the `withdraw`
extrinsic in the crowdloan pallet. The Subtensor API error reference describes it as "The deposit
cannot be withdrawn from the crowdloan"
([Subtensor API: Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors)).

## What It Means

When the [crowdloan](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans) creator calls `withdraw`,
the extrinsic handles their case differently from ordinary contributors. The creator's required
deposit is reserved within their contribution, so before returning any funds, the deposit amount is
subtracted first. `DepositCannotBeWithdrawn` fires when subtracting the deposit leaves no
withdrawable amount remaining — meaning the creator's entire contribution equals their deposit, so
there is nothing to return yet
([Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

This check only applies to the crowdloan creator. Ordinary contributors go through a separate path
in `withdraw` that doesn't involve the deposit, and they never encounter this error
([Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

## Distinction from No Contribution

`DepositCannotBeWithdrawn` and `NoContribution` are both raised inside `withdraw`, but at different
stages. `NoContribution` is checked earlier and fires when the caller has no recorded contribution
at all. `DepositCannotBeWithdrawn` is checked after that confirmation, in the creator-specific
branch, when the contribution exists but is fully consumed by the locked deposit
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NoContribution](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

- **NoContribution** — caller has no contribution on record at all.
- **DepositCannotBeWithdrawn** — creator has a contribution, but it is fully locked as the deposit.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the deposit-subtraction check that raises it.
It does not describe how crowdloan deposits are set or the rest of the withdrawal flow; those are
documented separately ([Crowdloans](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans)).
