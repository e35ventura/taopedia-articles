# Deposit Cannot Be Withdrawn

`DepositCannotBeWithdrawn` is a crowdloan pallet error raised by the `withdraw` extrinsic on the
creator path. The pallet defines it as "The deposit cannot be withdrawn from the crowdloan"
([Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

## What It Means

During an active [crowdloan](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans), contributors can
call `withdraw` to reclaim funds before finalization. For the crowdloan creator, the pallet keeps
the initial deposit locked and allows withdrawal only from amounts above that deposit. After
subtracting the locked deposit from the creator's recorded contribution, `withdraw` returns
`DepositCannotBeWithdrawn` when nothing remains to withdraw
([Crowdloans: Crowdloan Lifecycle](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans#crowdloan-lifecycle),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

This is a creator-deposit retention rejection on the withdraw path, not proof that the campaign was
finalized, that the caller has no contribution record at all, or that a non-creator account lacks
withdrawable funds.

## Why It Appears

This error appears when the crowdloan creator calls `withdraw` while their recorded contribution
equals the locked deposit or otherwise leaves no amount above the deposit after retention. That can
happen when a client treats the full creator contribution as withdrawable, retries withdrawal after
earlier partial withdrawals, or confuses creator deposit recovery with the `dissolve` cleanup path
([Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

When debugging, compare against other rejections on the same call. `AlreadyFinalized` means the
campaign already completed finalization. `NoContribution` means the caller's recorded contribution
is zero before the creator deposit retention step runs
([Already Finalized](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#alreadyfinalized),
[No Contribution](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#nocontribution),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

The practical meaning is narrow: only amounts above the locked creator deposit are withdrawable
during an active campaign; deposit recovery follows the documented dissolution path instead.

## Distinction from No Contribution

`DepositCannotBeWithdrawn` and `NoContribution` both appear on `withdraw`, but at different checks.
`NoContribution` means the caller's recorded contribution is zero. `DepositCannotBeWithdrawn` means
the creator does have a contribution record, but after retaining the deposit no withdrawable amount
remains ([No Contribution](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#nocontribution),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

- **DepositCannotBeWithdrawn** — only the locked creator deposit would be withdrawn.
- **NoContribution** — the caller has no recorded contribution to withdraw.

## Distinction from Already Finalized

`DepositCannotBeWithdrawn` and `AlreadyFinalized` both block `withdraw`, but at different checks.
`AlreadyFinalized` means the campaign already completed finalization. `DepositCannotBeWithdrawn`
means the campaign is still non-finalized but the creator has no excess above the locked deposit to
withdraw
([Already Finalized](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#alreadyfinalized),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

- **DepositCannotBeWithdrawn** — the creator has no withdrawable amount above the deposit.
- **AlreadyFinalized** — the campaign was already finalized.

## Distinction from Deposit Too Low

`DepositCannotBeWithdrawn` and `DepositTooLow` both involve the creator deposit, but on different
extrinsics. `DepositTooLow` is raised on `create` when the submitted initial deposit is below the
runtime minimum. `DepositCannotBeWithdrawn` is raised on `withdraw` when the creator tries to pull
funds but only the locked deposit would move
([Deposit Too Low](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#deposittoolow),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

- **DepositCannotBeWithdrawn** — the creator cannot withdraw the locked deposit during the campaign.
- **DepositTooLow** — the submitted create deposit is below the runtime minimum.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the creator deposit retention check on
`withdraw`. It does not report a particular crowdloan's live deposit or contribution amounts on any
network; those are campaign state readers can query separately
([Crowdloans](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans),
[Bittensor Networks](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/bittensor-networks)).
