# Zero Share In Batch

`ZeroShareInBatch` is a Subtensor standard error raised during batched limit-order execution when
any order's pro-rata share of the pool output rounds down to zero in integer arithmetic. The pallet
defines it as "An order's pro-rata share in the batch rounded down to zero. The whole batch is
rejected so the order's input is never consumed without delivering any output (conservation), and
the order stays retryable in a differently-composed batch"
([Subtensor source: limit-orders/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/limit-orders/src/lib.rs)).

## What It Means

`execute_batched_orders` pools all buy and sell inputs, sends them through the subnet's liquidity
pool in a single swap, then distributes the resulting output back to each order's signer
proportionally. For buyers, each signer's alpha share is computed as
`total_alpha × signer_net / total_buy_net`, floored to a whole unit. For sellers, each signer's
TAO payout is computed similarly and then reduced by the relayer fee. When any result floors to
zero, the pallet raises `ZeroShareInBatch` and rolls back the entire batch — including the
up-front asset collection and the pool swap — rather than transferring nothing to that signer while
still marking their order as terminal
([Subtensor source: limit-orders/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/limit-orders/src/lib.rs)).

This rollback is intentional conservation: the order stays open and retryable in a batch composed
differently, so no signer loses input without receiving output
([Subtensor source: limit-orders/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/limit-orders/src/lib.rs)).

## Why It Appears

The error appears when the total pool output is small relative to the number of orders in the
batch, causing at least one order's fractional share to floor to zero in 64-bit integer arithmetic.
This is most likely when a batch contains many orders with very different sizes, an order's net
amount is very small compared to the total, or the pool returns a small output due to liquidity
conditions at execution time
([Subtensor source: limit-orders/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/limit-orders/src/lib.rs)).

## Distinction from Duplicate Order In Batch

`ZeroShareInBatch` and `DuplicateOrderInBatch` are both hard-fail errors that reject the entire
batch, but they fire at different stages. `DuplicateOrderInBatch` fires during the pre-execution
validation pass when the same order ID appears twice in the batch's input list.
`ZeroShareInBatch` fires during the distribution phase, after the pool swap has already been
attempted, when integer rounding produces a zero payout for at least one signer
([Subtensor Standard Errors: DuplicateOrderInBatch](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors),
[Subtensor source: limit-orders/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/limit-orders/src/lib.rs)).

- **DuplicateOrderInBatch** — an order ID appears more than once in the batch input.
- **ZeroShareInBatch** — an order's pro-rata output share rounds to zero during distribution.

## Distinction from Swap Returned Zero

`ZeroShareInBatch` and `SwapReturnedZero` both involve a zero-output outcome in batched order
execution, but they detect it at different points.`SwapReturnedZero` fires when the pool swap
itself returns zero total output. `ZeroShareInBatch` fires when the swap returns a non-zero total
but the distribution arithmetic floors at least one individual signer's share to zero
([Subtensor Standard Errors: SwapReturnedZero](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors),
[Subtensor source: limit-orders/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/limit-orders/src/lib.rs)).

- **SwapReturnedZero** — the pool swap produced zero total output for the batch.
- **ZeroShareInBatch** — the swap produced non-zero output but one signer's share rounded to zero.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the integer floor check that raises it. It
does not describe how batched orders are composed, how fees are calculated, or how liquidity pool
pricing works
([Subtensor source: limit-orders/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/limit-orders/src/lib.rs)).
