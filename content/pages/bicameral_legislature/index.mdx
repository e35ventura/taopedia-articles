# Bicameral Legislature

Bicameral legislature describes the two-body governance structure used in Bittensor proposal review.
The [Glossary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#bicameral-legislature) defines it
as a two-tier system comprising the Triumvirate and the Senate for proposal approval.

## Two Governance Bodies

The Triumvirate and Senate have separate governance roles. The official
[Governance Overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/governance) describes the Triumvirate as the
body that creates proposals and the Senate as the body that approves or rejects them. The
[Senate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/governance/senate) documentation provides the Senate-side
context for reviewing proposals.

## Reader Boundary

This term names the governance structure, not the status of any specific proposal or the membership
of either body. For proposal records, readers should use the official governance references rather
than treating a glossary article as live governance state.

## Relationship to Multiple Mechanisms

Bicameral legislature names Bittensor's two-body governance review for proposals. The
[Glossary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#subnet-creator) notes that subnet
creators can configure multiple incentive mechanisms to distribute emissions across different types
of work or evaluation criteria.

For readers, bicameral legislature still names governance structure. Approved subnet design changes
may enable more than one incentive mechanism in a subnet market.

References:
[Multiple Incentive Mechanisms Within Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-multiple-mech-subnets),
[Glossary: Subnet Creator](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#subnet-creator)

## Relationship to Senate

Bicameral legislature and the Senate address related but different parts of Bittensor governance
vocabulary. Bicameral legislature names the two-body review structure, while the Senate names the
approval body inside that structure. The
[Glossary: Bicameral Legislature](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#bicameral-legislature)
describes the Triumvirate-and-Senate system, and the
[Glossary: Senate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#senate) describes the review
body formed from elected delegates.

For readers, bicameral legislature answers how governance is split into two bodies, while the Senate
answers which body approves or rejects Triumvirate proposals.

References:
[Glossary: Bicameral Legislature](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#bicameral-legislature),
[Glossary: Senate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#senate)

## Relationship to Senator

Bicameral legislature and a senator address related but different parts of governance vocabulary.
Bicameral legislature names the two-body structure for proposal review, while a senator names the
delegate role that votes inside the Senate half of that structure. The
[Glossary: Bicameral Legislature](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#bicameral-legislature)
describes the two-tier approval system, and the
[Senate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/governance/senate) documentation describes senators as
Senate delegates who vote on proposals.

For readers, bicameral legislature names the governance structure, while a senator names the voting
role inside the Senate body of that structure.

References:
[Glossary: Bicameral Legislature](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#bicameral-legislature),
[Senate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/governance/senate)

## Relationship to Triumvirate

Bicameral legislature and the Triumvirate address related but different parts of Bittensor
governance vocabulary. Bicameral legislature names the full two-body review structure, while the
Triumvirate names the proposal-origin body inside that structure. The
[Glossary: Bicameral Legislature](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#bicameral-legislature)
describes the Triumvirate-and-Senate system, and the
[Glossary: Triumvirate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#triumvirate) describes
the group responsible for creating proposals.

For readers, bicameral legislature answers how governance review is organized, while the Triumvirate
answers which body creates proposals before Senate review.

References:
[Glossary: Bicameral Legislature](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#bicameral-legislature),
[Glossary: Triumvirate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#triumvirate)

## Relationship to Proposal

Bicameral legislature and a proposal address related but different parts of governance vocabulary.
Bicameral legislature names the two-body structure through which proposals are reviewed, while a
proposal names the governance item itself. The
[Glossary: Bicameral Legislature](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#bicameral-legislature)
describes the Triumvirate-and-Senate approval system, and the
[Glossary: Proposal](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#proposal) describes the
governance item that moves through that review flow.

For readers, bicameral legislature names the review structure, while a proposal names the item that
the Triumvirate creates and the Senate reviews.

References:
[Glossary: Bicameral Legislature](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#bicameral-legislature),
[Glossary: Proposal](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#proposal)
