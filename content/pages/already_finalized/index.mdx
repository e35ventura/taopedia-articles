# Already Finalized

`AlreadyFinalized` is a crowdloan pallet error raised when a call expects the campaign to still be
open but the crowdloan record is already marked finalized. The pallet defines it as "The crowdloan
has already been finalized"
([Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

## What It Means

A successful [crowdloan](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans) finalization sets the
campaign's finalized flag and closes the pre-success lifecycle. Several extrinsics refuse to run
once that flag is set, including `withdraw`, `finalize`, `refund`, `dissolve`,
`update_min_contribution`, `update_end`, `update_cap`, and `set_max_contribution`
([Crowdloans: Crowdloan Lifecycle](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans#crowdloan-lifecycle),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

This is a lifecycle-state rejection, not proof that the cap was never reached, that the contribution
deadline has passed, or that the caller lacks creator permission on a separate check.

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a client retries an operation against a crowdloan that has already completed
finalization. Common cases include submitting `finalize` again after a successful run, calling
`refund` or `dissolve` on a finalized campaign, attempting `withdraw` after finalization, or sending
a creator parameter update after the stored call or transfer has already executed
([Crowdloans: Who can finalize a crowdloan and when?](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans#who-can-finalize-a-crowdloan-and-when),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

When debugging, compare against other rejections on the same extrinsic. On `finalize`,
`CapNotRaised` means funding is still short of the cap and `InvalidOrigin` means the signer is not
the crowdloan creator. On `refund` and `dissolve`, `InvalidOrigin` means the signer is not the
creator, while `NotReadyToDissolve` on `dissolve` means contributor refunds are not yet complete
([Cap Not Raised](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#capnotraised),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

The practical meaning is narrow: treat the campaign as closed to pre-finalization operations and
query its finalized state before retrying lifecycle calls.

## Distinction from Cap Not Raised

`AlreadyFinalized` and `CapNotRaised` both appear on `finalize`, but at different checks.
`AlreadyFinalized` means finalization already completed. `CapNotRaised` means the creator signed
`finalize` while total raised still differs from the cap
([Cap Not Raised](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#capnotraised),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

- **AlreadyFinalized** — the campaign was already finalized.
- **CapNotRaised** — raised total still differs from the cap.

## Distinction from Not Ready to Dissolve

`AlreadyFinalized` and `NotReadyToDissolve` both block cleanup-related calls, but on different
paths. `AlreadyFinalized` rejects any pre-finalization extrinsic once the finalized flag is set.
`NotReadyToDissolve` appears only on `dissolve` when the creator's remaining contribution still does
not equal total raised, meaning other contributor balances have not yet been cleared
([Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

- **AlreadyFinalized** — the campaign is already finalized.
- **NotReadyToDissolve** — contributor refunds are not yet complete for dissolution.

## Distinction from Contribution Period Ended

`AlreadyFinalized` and `ContributionPeriodEnded` both signal that a crowdloan is no longer accepting
the same kind of activity, but on different extrinsics and checks. `ContributionPeriodEnded` blocks
new `contribute` calls once the current block reaches the campaign end. `AlreadyFinalized` blocks
withdrawals, refunds, dissolves, finalize retries, and creator parameter updates after successful
finalization
([Contribution Period Ended](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#contributionperiodended),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

- **AlreadyFinalized** — finalization already completed.
- **ContributionPeriodEnded** — the contribution deadline has passed on `contribute`.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the finalized-state guard across crowdloan
extrinsics. It does not report whether a particular crowdloan is finalized on any network; that is
campaign state readers can query separately
([Crowdloans](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans),
[Bittensor Networks](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/bittensor-networks)).
