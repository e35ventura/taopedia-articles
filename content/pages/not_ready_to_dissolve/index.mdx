# Not Ready to Dissolve

`NotReadyToDissolve` is a crowdloan pallet error raised by the `dissolve` extrinsic. The pallet
defines it as "The crowdloan is not ready to be dissolved, it still has contributions"
([Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

## What It Means

On a non-finalized [crowdloan](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans), the creator can
call `dissolve` to recover the creator deposit and remove campaign storage once contributor balances
are cleared. Before that cleanup runs, `dissolve` compares the creator's remaining contribution
record against total raised and returns `NotReadyToDissolve` when the two amounts differ
([Crowdloans: Crowdloan Lifecycle](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans#crowdloan-lifecycle),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

That comparison means non-creator balances still count toward total raised. The campaign is ready
for dissolution only when every other contributor has been cleared and raised equals the creator's
remaining contribution, typically the creator deposit left after refunds.

This is a refund-completion rejection on the dissolve path, not proof that the campaign was
finalized, that the caller is not the creator, or that the creator has no contribution record at
all.

## Why It Appears

This error appears when the crowdloan creator calls `dissolve` before contributor refunds have fully
cleared non-creator balances. That can happen after a partial `refund` batch, when a client assumes
one refund call cleared everyone, or when `dissolve` is submitted while contributor records still
remain
([Crowdloans: What happens if the cap is not reached?](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans#what-happens-if-the-cap-is-not-reached),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

When debugging, compare against other rejections on the same call. `AlreadyFinalized` means the
campaign already completed finalization. `InvalidOrigin` means the signer is not the crowdloan
creator. `NoContribution` means the creator has no contribution record for this crowdloan, which is
checked before the raised comparison
([Already Finalized](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#alreadyfinalized),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

The practical meaning is narrow: finish clearing contributor balances, calling `refund` again if
needed, then retry `dissolve` once raised equals the creator's remaining contribution.

## Distinction from Already Finalized

`NotReadyToDissolve` and `AlreadyFinalized` both block `dissolve`, but at different checks.
`AlreadyFinalized` means the campaign already completed finalization and cannot be dissolved through
this path. `NotReadyToDissolve` means the campaign is still non-finalized but contributor balances
have not yet been fully cleared
([Already Finalized](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#alreadyfinalized),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

- **NotReadyToDissolve** — contributor refunds are not yet complete for dissolution.
- **AlreadyFinalized** — the campaign was already finalized.

## Distinction from No Contribution

`NotReadyToDissolve` and `NoContribution` both appear on `dissolve`, but at different checks.
`NoContribution` means the creator has no contribution record for this crowdloan.
`NotReadyToDissolve` means the creator does have a contribution record, but that amount still does
not equal total raised
([Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

- **NotReadyToDissolve** — non-creator balances still remain in total raised.
- **NoContribution** — the creator has no contribution record for this crowdloan.

## Distinction from Invalid Origin

`NotReadyToDissolve` and `InvalidOrigin` both block `dissolve`, but at different checks.
`InvalidOrigin` means the signing account is not the crowdloan creator. `NotReadyToDissolve` means
the creator signed the call but contributor balances still prevent cleanup
([Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

- **NotReadyToDissolve** — contributor refunds are not yet complete for dissolution.
- **InvalidOrigin** — the signer is not the crowdloan creator.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the creator-contribution comparison on
`dissolve`. It does not report a particular crowdloan's live raised amount or contributor balances
on any network; those are campaign state readers can query separately
([Crowdloans](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans),
[Bittensor Networks](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/bittensor-networks)).
