# Call Unavailable

`CallUnavailable` is a crowdloan pallet error raised by the `finalize` extrinsic on the
call-dispatch path. The pallet defines it as "Call to dispatch was not found in the preimage
storage"
([Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

## What It Means

Some [crowdloan](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans) campaigns finalize by
dispatching a stored runtime call, such as subnet lease registration, rather than by transferring
funds to a target address. On that call-dispatch path, `finalize` loads the bound call from preimage
storage and returns `CallUnavailable` when the lookup fails
([Crowdloans: Crowdloan Lifecycle](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans#crowdloan-lifecycle),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

This check applies only to call-based finalization. Campaigns configured with a `target_address`
instead of a stored call follow the balance-transfer branch and do not raise this error on
`finalize`.

This is a preimage-lookup rejection on the finalize call path, not proof that the funding cap was
not reached, that the caller is not the crowdloan creator, or that the campaign was already
finalized.

## Why It Appears

This error appears when the crowdloan creator calls `finalize` on a call-based campaign after the
earlier finalize checks pass, but the stored call cannot be retrieved from preimage storage. That
can happen when preimage data was pruned, corrupted, or otherwise removed before finalization
completed
([Crowdloans: Can the purpose of a crowdloan be changed after it starts?](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans#can-the-purpose-of-a-crowdloan-be-changed-after-it-starts),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

When debugging, compare against other rejections on the same call. `CapNotRaised` means total raised
still differs from the cap. `AlreadyFinalized` means finalization already completed. `InvalidOrigin`
means the signer is not the crowdloan creator. `InvalidFinalizationConfig` on `finalize` means the
stored campaign record has an invalid call-and-target combination rather than a missing preimage
([Cap Not Raised](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#capnotraised),
[Already Finalized](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#alreadyfinalized),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the campaign reached finalize with a call-based configuration, but
the stored dispatch call is no longer available in preimage storage.

## Distinction from Invalid Finalization Config

`CallUnavailable` and `InvalidFinalizationConfig` both appear during finalization setup, but at
different checks. `InvalidFinalizationConfig` means the campaign record does not have exactly one of
a stored call or a target address. `CallUnavailable` means the record is call-based, but the bound
call cannot be loaded from preimage storage
([Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

- **CallUnavailable** — the stored call preimage cannot be loaded.
- **InvalidFinalizationConfig** — the campaign has an invalid call-or-target configuration.

## Distinction from Cap Not Raised

`CallUnavailable` and `CapNotRaised` both appear on `finalize`, but at different checks.
`CapNotRaised` means total raised still differs from the cap. `CallUnavailable` means funding met
the cap, but the stored call could not be retrieved for dispatch
([Cap Not Raised](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#capnotraised),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

- **CallUnavailable** — the stored call preimage cannot be loaded.
- **CapNotRaised** — raised total still differs from the cap.

## Distinction from Already Finalized

`CallUnavailable` and `AlreadyFinalized` both appear on `finalize`, but at different checks.
`AlreadyFinalized` means the campaign already completed finalization. `CallUnavailable` means the
campaign is not yet finalized, but the stored call cannot be loaded from preimage storage
([Already Finalized](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#alreadyfinalized),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

- **CallUnavailable** — the stored call preimage cannot be loaded.
- **AlreadyFinalized** — the campaign was already finalized.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the preimage lookup on the call-dispatch
finalize path. It does not report whether a particular crowdloan's stored call still exists on any
network; that is chain state readers can query separately
([Crowdloans](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans),
[Bittensor Networks](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/bittensor-networks)).
