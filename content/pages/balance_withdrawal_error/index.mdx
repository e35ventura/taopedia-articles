# Balance Withdrawal Error

`BalanceWithdrawalError` means the runtime attempted to withdraw TAO from a coldkey account and the
balances pallet rejected the operation. The standard-errors reference describes it as "Could not
withdraw from coldkey account"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#balancewithdrawalerror)).

In `tao.rs`, `recycle_tao` is the explicit raise site. The helper first checks that the requested
amount fits within the coldkey's reducible balance under preservation rules, then calls
`Currency::withdraw` and maps a failed withdrawal to `BalanceWithdrawalError`
([Subtensor source: tao.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/coinbase/tao.rs)).

## What It Means

`recycle_tao` permanently removes TAO from circulation and reduces Subtensor total issuance. Before
calling withdraw, the function ensures the amount does not exceed the coldkey's reducible balance
while preserving the existential deposit. If that pre-check passes but the underlying withdraw still
fails, the extrinsic returns `BalanceWithdrawalError`
([Subtensor source: tao.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/coinbase/tao.rs)).

The error therefore names a runtime withdrawal failure, not a simple "insufficient balance"
comparison on free TAO. Earlier gates on the same path can still reject the call before withdraw is
attempted.

Subnet-registration and add-stake extrinsics document `BalanceWithdrawalError` as a possible coldkey
withdrawal failure on TAO-moving paths that depend on the balances pallet
([Subtensor source: subnet.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/subnet.rs),
[Subtensor source: add_stake.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/add_stake.rs)).

During subnet registration, excess lock TAO can be recycled through `recycle_tao` after TAO is
transferred into the new subnet account
([Subtensor source: subnet.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/subnet.rs)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a TAO recycle or other withdraw-based path reaches `Currency::withdraw` and
the balances pallet returns an error even though the Subtensor pre-check accepted the amount. That
can happen when account state changed between validation and execution, when preservation rules
block the exact withdraw mode requested, or when the underlying currency operation fails for reasons
not captured by the earlier reducible-balance test
([Subtensor source: tao.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/coinbase/tao.rs)).

When debugging, compare against earlier funding gates on the same extrinsic. `InsufficientBalance`
means the requested TAO amount failed a transfer or recycle pre-check before withdraw was attempted.
`NotEnoughBalanceToStake` means add-stake validation found the coldkey could not fund the named
stake amount. `CannotAffordLockCost` means subnet registration failed the earlier lock-cost
affordability check in `do_register_network`
([Subtensor Standard Errors: InsufficientBalance](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#insufficientbalance),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the coldkey withdrawal could not be completed at execution time.

## Distinction from Insufficient Balance

`BalanceWithdrawalError` and `InsufficientBalance` both involve coldkey TAO movement, but at
different failure points. `InsufficientBalance` is raised when a transfer or recycle pre-check
determines the account cannot fund the requested amount while preserving required balance rules.
`BalanceWithdrawalError` is raised later when `Currency::withdraw` itself fails after that pre-check
has already passed
([Subtensor source: tao.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/coinbase/tao.rs),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: InsufficientBalance](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#insufficientbalance)).

- **BalanceWithdrawalError** — the balances-pallet withdraw failed at execution time.
- **InsufficientBalance** — the requested amount failed an earlier affordability pre-check.

## Distinction from Not Enough Balance To Stake

`BalanceWithdrawalError` and `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` both appear on staking-related paths, but
they test different conditions. `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` is the validation rejection when
`can_remove_balance_from_coldkey_account` fails during add-stake input checks.
`BalanceWithdrawalError` is the later runtime withdrawal failure on recycle and related withdraw
paths
([Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake)).

- **NotEnoughBalanceToStake** — add-stake validation found insufficient coldkey funding.
- **BalanceWithdrawalError** — a withdraw call failed after reaching execution.

## Distinction from Cannot Afford Lock Cost

`BalanceWithdrawalError` and `CannotAffordLockCost` both involve TAO leaving a coldkey during subnet
creation, but at different stages and with different checks. `CannotAffordLockCost` is the step-6
affordability gate in `do_register_network` before lock transfer and pruning proceed.
`BalanceWithdrawalError` is the documented withdrawal failure when a recycle or withdraw operation
cannot complete through the balances pallet
([Subtensor source: subnet.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/subnet.rs),
[Subtensor source: tao.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/coinbase/tao.rs)).

- **CannotAffordLockCost** — the coldkey cannot afford the quoted subnet lock cost.
- **BalanceWithdrawalError** — a coldkey TAO withdrawal failed at execution time.

## Distinction from Zero Balance After Withdrawn

`BalanceWithdrawalError` and `ZeroBalanceAfterWithdrawn` both concern coldkey withdrawals, but they
name different rejection reasons. `ZeroBalanceAfterWithdrawn` means the withdrawal would leave the
account with zero balance in a way the chain does not allow. `BalanceWithdrawalError` means the
balances pallet rejected the withdraw operation itself
([Subtensor Standard Errors: ZeroBalanceAfterWithdrawn](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#zerobalanceafterwithdrawn),
[Subtensor source: tao.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/coinbase/tao.rs)).

- **ZeroBalanceAfterWithdrawn** — the withdrawal would zero out the account.
- **BalanceWithdrawalError** — the withdraw call failed at execution time.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the withdraw failure surfaced by `recycle_tao`.
It does not report a wallet's live free balance, fee reserve, or whether a particular withdrawal
amount is safe on a network; those depend on chain state at observation time
([Glossary: Transfer](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#transfer),
[Transaction Fees in Bittensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/fees)).
