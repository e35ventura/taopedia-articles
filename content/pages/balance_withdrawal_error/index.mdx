# Balance Withdrawal Error

`BalanceWithdrawalError` means a coldkey-signed call reached a withdrawal step that could not remove
the requested TAO from the signing account. The standard-errors reference describes it as "Could not
withdraw from coldkey account"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#balancewithdrawalerror)).

## What It Means

Several staking and subnet-creation paths move TAO out of a coldkey before later validation or pool
steps run. `BalanceWithdrawalError` means that withdrawal step failed even though earlier checks on
the same call may already have passed
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#balancewithdrawalerror),
[Managing Your Stakes](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-sdk)).

This is a withdrawal failure on the coldkey account. The error names a failed balance move, not a
subnet pool problem, a hotkey registration problem, or a later stake-size rule.

## Why It Appears

This error appears when the runtime attempts to withdraw TAO from the signing coldkey and the
balances layer rejects the move. That can happen when account preservation rules block the exact
withdraw amount, when balance state changed between client construction and submission, or when the
withdrawal step fails for another balances-layer reason after earlier funding checks passed
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#balancewithdrawalerror),
[Glossary: Coldkey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#coldkey)).

When debugging, compare against earlier funding errors on the same extrinsic family.
`NotEnoughBalanceToStake` means the add-stake path rejected the request because the coldkey could
not fund the named stake amount. `InsufficientBalance` is the broader coldkey funding rejection on
transfer-style balance moves. `CannotAffordLockCost` is the subnet-registration pre-check against
the quoted lock cost
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: InsufficientBalance](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#insufficientbalance),
[Cannot Afford Lock Cost](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#cannotaffordlockcost)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the coldkey withdrawal step failed during the call.

## Distinction from Not Enough Balance To Stake

`BalanceWithdrawalError` and `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` both involve coldkey funding on add-stake
paths, but they reject different stages. `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` is the earlier comparison against
whether the coldkey can fund the requested stake amount. `BalanceWithdrawalError` is the later
rejection when the actual withdrawal from the coldkey account fails
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake)).

- **NotEnoughBalanceToStake** — the coldkey cannot fund the requested stake amount.
- **BalanceWithdrawalError** — the coldkey withdrawal step failed.

## Distinction from Insufficient Balance

`BalanceWithdrawalError` and `InsufficientBalance` both mean a coldkey could not complete a balance
move, but on different rejection layers. `InsufficientBalance` is the general funding rejection when
the requested TAO exceeds what the account can spend for the operation. `BalanceWithdrawalError` is
the documented withdrawal failure after the call reaches the withdraw step
([Subtensor Standard Errors: InsufficientBalance](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#insufficientbalance)).

- **InsufficientBalance** — the coldkey cannot fund the requested TAO amount.
- **BalanceWithdrawalError** — the coldkey withdrawal step failed.

## Distinction from Cannot Afford Lock Cost

`BalanceWithdrawalError` and `CannotAffordLockCost` both appear around subnet registration funding,
but at different stages. `CannotAffordLockCost` is the pre-check that the coldkey cannot afford the
quoted subnet lock cost. `BalanceWithdrawalError` is the documented withdrawal failure when the
runtime cannot remove TAO from the coldkey account during the later transfer step
([Cannot Afford Lock Cost](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#cannotaffordlockcost)).

- **CannotAffordLockCost** — the coldkey cannot afford the quoted lock cost.
- **BalanceWithdrawalError** — the coldkey withdrawal step failed.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the failed coldkey withdrawal it represents. It
does not report a wallet's live free balance or whether a particular amount is withdrawable on a
network; those depend on chain state at observation time
([Glossary: Coldkey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#coldkey),
[Transaction Fees in Bittensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/fees)).
