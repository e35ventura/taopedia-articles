# Announced Coldkey Hash Does Not Match

`AnnouncedColdkeyHashDoesNotMatch` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised by
`swap_coldkey_announced`, the extrinsic that finalizes a previously announced
[coldkey swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/coldkey-swap). The standard-errors reference
describes it as "The announced coldkey hash does not match the new coldkey hash"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#announcedcoldkeyhashdoesnotmatch)).

## What It Means

When a coldkey swap is announced, the chain stores a hash of the intended destination coldkey rather
than the public key itself. `swap_coldkey_announced` takes the pending announcement for the caller,
then compares that stored hash against the hash of the new coldkey supplied in the finalize call.
`AnnouncedColdkeyHashDoesNotMatch` is the rejection when those two hashes differ
([Subtensor source: dispatches.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/macros/dispatches.rs),
[Coldkey Swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/coldkey-swap)).

This is the second of three checks the extrinsic runs in sequence. It only runs after the storage
lookup for a pending announcement succeeds, and before the block-number comparison that confirms the
pending window has elapsed
([Subtensor source: dispatches.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/macros/dispatches.rs)).

## Why It Appears

The most direct cause is calling `swap_coldkey_announced` with a different coldkey than the one
whose hash was submitted through `announce_coldkey_swap`. That can happen when the wrong key file is
used, when the destination key pair was regenerated or replaced after announcement, or when a caller
mistakenly finalizes against a different unused coldkey than the one they committed to at
announcement time
([Subtensor source: dispatches.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/macros/dispatches.rs),
[Coldkey Swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/coldkey-swap)).

Because the announcement step stores only the hash, the finalize step is where the chain verifies
that the revealed coldkey is the same destination that was committed earlier. A mismatch at this
point means the swap cannot proceed even though a valid pending announcement exists for the caller
([Subtensor source: dispatches.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/macros/dispatches.rs)).

## Distinction from Coldkey Swap Announcement Not Found

`AnnouncedColdkeyHashDoesNotMatch` and `ColdkeySwapAnnouncementNotFound` are both raised by
`swap_coldkey_announced`, but they correspond to different steps in the same sequence. The first
step uses `take()` to remove the pending announcement record; if nothing is on record, the call
fails with `ColdkeySwapAnnouncementNotFound` before any hash comparison runs. Only when that lookup
succeeds does the extrinsic compare the supplied new coldkey against the hash stored in the
announcement
([Coldkey Swap Announcement Not Found](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#coldkeyswapannouncementnotfound),
[Subtensor source: dispatches.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/macros/dispatches.rs)).

- **ColdkeySwapAnnouncementNotFound** — no announcement record exists for the caller.
- **AnnouncedColdkeyHashDoesNotMatch** — an announcement exists, but the supplied coldkey doesn't
  match its committed hash.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the hash comparison that raises it. It does not
describe how the announcement hash is created, how long the pending window lasts, or what happens
after a successful match; those are documented separately
([Coldkey Swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/coldkey-swap),
[Subtensor source: dispatches.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/macros/dispatches.rs)).
