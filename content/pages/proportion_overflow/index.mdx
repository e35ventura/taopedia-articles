# Proportion Overflow

`ProportionOverflow` means the stake proportions listed for a parent's child hotkeys on a subnet add
up to more than the chain can represent in one child-set call. The standard-errors reference
describes it as "Proportion overflow when setting children"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#proportionoverflow)).

The check applies to the submitted proportion values themselves, not to whether each child hotkey is
valid or unique ([Child Hotkeys](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys)).

## What It Means

A [child hotkey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys) lets a parent delegate a
share of its stake to separate validating keys on a subnet. Each child in a `set_children` call is
paired with a proportion value that defines how much of the parent's stake routes to that child.
`ProportionOverflow` is the rejection when the sum of those proportions is too large for the chain's
proportion accounting ([Child Hotkeys](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys),
[Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#proportionoverflow)).

In `set_children.rs`, `ensure_total_proportions` adds the submitted proportion values and returns
`ProportionOverflow` when their combined total exceeds `u64::MAX`
([Subtensor source: set_children.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/set_children.rs)).

## Why It Appears

Child-hotkey scheduling validates the full list a parent submits on a subnet. `DuplicateChild`
rejects repeated hotkeys in that list, `TooManyChildren` rejects lists with more than five entries,
and `ProportionOverflow` is the separate rejection when the listed proportion values still sum past
`u64::MAX` ([Child Hotkeys](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: DuplicateChild](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#duplicatechild),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: TooManyChildren](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#toomanychildren),
[Subtensor source: set_children.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/set_children.rs)).

For a reader, the practical meaning is narrow: the listed proportions collectively do not fit the
chain's accounting limit. The problem is not simply that the list has too many entries or that the
same child hotkey appears twice
([Too Many Children](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#toomanychildren),
[Duplicate Child](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#duplicatechild)).

## Distinction from Too Many Children

`ProportionOverflow` and `TooManyChildren` are both raised from child-relation consistency checks,
but they guard different properties of the submitted child list. `ProportionOverflow` sums the stake
proportions across the listed children. `TooManyChildren` counts how many children are listed and
rejects lists with more than five entries
([Too Many Children](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#toomanychildren),
[Subtensor source: set_children.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/set_children.rs)).

- **ProportionOverflow** — the combined proportions exceed the representable total.
- **TooManyChildren** — the child list has more than five entries.

## Distinction from Duplicate Child

`ProportionOverflow` and `DuplicateChild` both appear while validating one submitted child list, but
they reject different defects. `ProportionOverflow` is about the combined size of the proportion
values. `DuplicateChild` is about the same child hotkey being named more than once in the list
([Duplicate Child](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#duplicatechild),
[Subtensor source: set_children.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/set_children.rs)).

- **ProportionOverflow** — the listed proportions sum past the accounting ceiling.
- **DuplicateChild** — the same child hotkey appears more than once in one call.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the combined-proportion check that raises it.
It does not describe childkey take, minimum parent stake thresholds, or the live child list on a
subnet; those are separate reads
([Child Hotkeys](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys),
[Chain Rate Limits: Child hotkey operations rate limit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits#child-hotkey-operations-rate-limit)).
