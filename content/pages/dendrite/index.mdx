# Dendrite

A dendrite is a Bittensor subnet communication component used to transmit information toward axons
during validator and miner interaction. The
[Glossary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#dendrite) defines dendrites around the
transmission of information to axons, while
[Understanding Incentive Mechanisms](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/anatomy-of-incentive-mechanism)
places that communication inside the protocol portion of subnet design.

## Communication Role

The useful starting point is direction. A dendrite belongs to the side of a subnet interaction that
sends information, while an axon belongs to the side that receives it. The
[Glossary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#dendrite) describes dendrites as
transmitting information to axons, and
[Understanding Incentive Mechanisms](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/anatomy-of-incentive-mechanism)
describes subnet protocols as the rules for how validators query miners and how miners respond.

## Relationship to Axons

Dendrites and axons are paired terms. A dendrite is easiest to understand as the outbound side of a
subnet communication path, while an axon is the receiving side. The
[Glossary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#dendrite) names axons as the target of
dendrite transmission, and
[Understanding Incentive Mechanisms](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/anatomy-of-incentive-mechanism)
uses the axon-dendrite model when describing miner-validator protocol design.

## Relationship to Validators and Miners

Dendrites matter because subnet work is evaluated through interaction between validators and miners.
[Understanding Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-subnets) describes
validators as measuring miner work inside specialized subnet markets, while the
[Glossary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#dendrite) places dendrites in the
communication path used by those subnet roles.

## Relationship to Incentive Mechanisms

An incentive mechanism includes more than a communication path. It also includes the task miners
perform and the scoring model validators use to evaluate the work.
[Understanding Incentive Mechanisms](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/anatomy-of-incentive-mechanism)
separates protocol, task, and scoring model as parts of subnet design. Dendrite belongs to the
protocol context rather than to the task or scoring model itself.

## Reader Boundary

Dendrite should be read as subnet communication terminology. At a reference level, the key point is
its relationship to axons inside subnet protocol context. The
[Glossary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#dendrite) provides the term-level
definition, and
[Understanding Incentive Mechanisms](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/anatomy-of-incentive-mechanism)
provides the broader protocol context.
