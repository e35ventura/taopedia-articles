# Zero Max Stake Amount

`ZeroMaxStakeAmount` means a price-protected staking call on netuid 1 or another named subnet could
not execute any stake amount within the submitted limit price. The standard-errors reference
describes it as "Zero max stake amount"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#zeromaxstakeamount)).

The staking implementation reference lists the same label as "No amount executable within price
limit" on limit-variant staking paths such as `add_stake_limit` and `remove_stake_limit`
([Staking Implementation](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/navigating-subtensor/swap-stake)).

## What It Means

Staking moves value through each subnet's TAO and alpha reserves
([Staking and Delegation](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)).

Price protection lets staking and unstaking calls name a limit price and optional partial-fill
behavior on the limit variants documented in the staking implementation guide
([Price Protection](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/price-protection),
[Staking Implementation](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/navigating-subtensor/swap-stake)).

`ZeroMaxStakeAmount` means the limit-price search found no executable stake amount on the netuid
under the submitted protection settings
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#zeromaxstakeamount),
[Staking Implementation](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/navigating-subtensor/swap-stake)).

This is a price-protection sizing rejection, not a coldkey balance failure and not a blanket pool
liquidity failure on an ordinary unstake path.

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a limit-protected add-stake or remove-stake call names a limit price so
tight, or faces pool conditions so adverse, that zero alpha or TAO can trade within the allowed band
on the subnet. That can happen when the limit price is far from the current pool ratio, when strict
protection rejects partial fills, or when a client copies an outdated limit from an earlier block
([Price Protection](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/price-protection),
[Understanding Slippage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/slippage)).

On netuid 1, readers can compare a proposed limit against live subnet pool context through the
subnet documentation and storage query patterns described for hyperparameters and reserves before
resubmitting a protected stake call
([Subnet Hyperparameters: View hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#view-hyperparameters)).

When debugging, rule out balance and stake-size failures first. `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` means the
coldkey cannot fund the requested add-stake amount. `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` means the pair lacks
enough staked alpha for the removal. `AmountTooLow` means the submitted amount is below the minimum
stake increment
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughstaketowithdraw),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: AmountTooLow](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#amounttoolow)).

The practical meaning is narrow: no stake amount can execute within the submitted price limit.

## Distinction from Slippage Too High

`ZeroMaxStakeAmount` and `SlippageTooHigh` both involve price protection on staking paths, but they
reject different outcomes. `ZeroMaxStakeAmount` means no executable amount remains within the limit
price. `SlippageTooHigh` means price protection triggered in strict mode because movement exceeded
tolerance
([Subtensor Standard Errors: SlippageTooHigh](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#slippagetoohigh),
[Staking Implementation](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/navigating-subtensor/swap-stake)).

- **ZeroMaxStakeAmount** — no amount can execute within the limit price.
- **SlippageTooHigh** — strict price protection rejected the trade.

## Distinction from Insufficient Liquidity

`ZeroMaxStakeAmount` and `InsufficientLiquidity` both can appear when a pool-backed conversion
fails, but at different checks. `ZeroMaxStakeAmount` is the limit-price result when zero stake
amount is executable within the submitted protection band. `InsufficientLiquidity` means the pool
swap behind the action could not simulate or complete
([Subtensor Standard Errors: InsufficientLiquidity](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#insufficientliquidity)).

- **ZeroMaxStakeAmount** — no executable amount within the limit price.
- **InsufficientLiquidity** — the pool cannot support the swap simulation or execution.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the zero-executable-amount price-limit gate it
represents. It does not quote a live limit price or pool ratio on a particular netuid; those values
are subnet chain state readers can query for the selected netuid
([Bittensor Networks](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/bittensor-networks)).
