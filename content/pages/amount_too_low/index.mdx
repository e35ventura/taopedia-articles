# Amount Too Low

`AmountTooLow` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised on staking paths. The standard-errors
reference describes it as "Stake amount is too low"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#amounttoolow)).

## What It Means

Staking calls must name amounts large enough to satisfy the chain's minimum stake increment. In
`stake_utils.rs`, `validate_add_stake` and `validate_remove_stake` compare submitted amounts against
`DefaultMinStake` and return `AmountTooLow` when the value is too small for the path
([Managing Your Stakes](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-sdk),
[Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

On add-stake paths, `validate_add_stake` requires the requested TAO to meet a minimum that includes
`DefaultMinStake` plus swap fees, and it checks that the simulated swap's `amount_paid_in` is at
least `DefaultMinStake`
([Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

On remove-stake paths, `validate_remove_stake` rejects a zero unstake amount and, when a partial
unstake would leave non-zero alpha behind, requires the TAO equivalent paid out for the unstaked
portion to meet `DefaultMinStake`. A full unstake bypasses that remainder check
([Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a wallet submits a stake or unstake amount below the documented minimum
transaction size, names zero alpha on an unstake call, or attempts a partial unstake that would
leave a remainder too small to satisfy the floor
([Managing Your Stakes](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-sdk),
[Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

For a reader, the practical meaning is narrow: the amount in the extrinsic is below the minimum
increment for that staking action. The problem is not that the coldkey lacks enough free TAO to fund
a valid-sized stake, that the pair lacks enough staked alpha to cover a removal, or that the swap
pool lacks liquidity
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughstaketowithdraw),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: InsufficientLiquidity](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#insufficientliquidity)).

## Distinction from Not Enough Balance To Stake

`AmountTooLow` and `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` both appear on add-stake paths, but they test different
limits. `AmountTooLow` means the requested amount is smaller than the `DefaultMinStake` floor,
including fee-adjusted minimums from `validate_add_stake`. `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` means the
coldkey's free balance cannot cover a stake amount that already passed the minimum-size checks
([Not Enough Balance To Stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

- **AmountTooLow** — the stake amount is below the minimum allowed increment.
- **NotEnoughBalanceToStake** — free balance cannot fund the requested stake amount.

## Distinction from Not Enough Stake To Withdraw

`AmountTooLow` and `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` both appear around unstaking, but they reject
different problems. `AmountTooLow` is the minimum-size gate, including zero unstake amounts and
partial unstakes that would leave a remainder below `DefaultMinStake`. `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw`
means the requested removal exceeds the staked alpha held by the hotkey-coldkey pair on that subnet
([Not Enough Stake To Withdraw](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughstaketowithdraw),
[Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

- **AmountTooLow** — the unstake amount is zero or below the minimum increment rules.
- **NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw** — the unstake amount exceeds available staked alpha.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the `DefaultMinStake` checks in
`validate_add_stake` and `validate_remove_stake`. It does not describe every other path that can
raise `AmountTooLow`, such as move-stake validation, or the live `DefaultMinStake` value on a
network; those are documented separately
([Managing Your Stakes](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-sdk),
[Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).
