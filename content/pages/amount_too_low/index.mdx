# Amount Too Low

`AmountTooLow` means the submitted stake or unstake amount is below the minimum increment the chain
accepts for that action. The standard-errors reference describes it as "Stake amount is too low"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#amounttoolow)).

Staking calls move TAO and alpha through subnet pool conversions, and the network enforces a minimum
transaction size on add-stake and remove-stake paths
([Staking and Delegation Overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation),
[Managing Your Stakes](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-sdk)).

## What It Means

Add-stake and remove-stake calls must name amounts large enough to satisfy the documented minimum
stake increment. `AmountTooLow` means the value in the extrinsic is too small for the path,
including zero alpha named on an unstake call
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#amounttoolow),
[Managing Your Stakes](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-sdk)).

For debugging, treat this as a minimum-size rejection, not as proof that the coldkey lacks enough
free TAO to fund a valid-sized stake, that the pair lacks enough staked alpha to cover a removal, or
that the caller's slippage cap was exceeded
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughstaketowithdraw),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: SlippageTooHigh](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#slippagetoohigh)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a wallet submits a stake or unstake amount below the documented minimum
transaction size, names zero alpha on an unstake call, or attempts a partial unstake that would
leave a remainder too small to satisfy the floor
([Managing Your Stakes](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-sdk),
[Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#amounttoolow)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the amount in the extrinsic is below the minimum increment for that
staking action.

## Distinction from Not Enough Balance To Stake

`AmountTooLow` and `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` both appear on add-stake paths, but they test different
limits. `AmountTooLow` means the requested amount is smaller than the minimum stake increment.
`NotEnoughBalanceToStake` means the coldkey's free balance cannot cover the requested TAO amount
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: AmountTooLow](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#amounttoolow)).

- **AmountTooLow** — the stake amount is below the minimum allowed increment.
- **NotEnoughBalanceToStake** — free balance cannot fund the requested stake amount.

## Distinction from Not Enough Stake To Withdraw

`AmountTooLow` and `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` both appear around unstaking, but they reject
different problems. `AmountTooLow` is the minimum-size gate, including zero unstake amounts.
`NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` means the requested removal exceeds the staked alpha held by the
hotkey-coldkey pair on that subnet
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughstaketowithdraw),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: AmountTooLow](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#amounttoolow)).

- **AmountTooLow** — the unstake amount is zero or below the minimum increment rules.
- **NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw** — the unstake amount exceeds available staked alpha.

## Distinction from Slippage Too High

`AmountTooLow` and `SlippageTooHigh` both appear during staking validation, but they guard opposite
size limits. `AmountTooLow` rejects amounts below the minimum increment. `SlippageTooHigh` rejects
amounts above the caller's slippage cap when partial execution is disabled
([Subtensor Standard Errors: SlippageTooHigh](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#slippagetoohigh),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: AmountTooLow](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#amounttoolow),
[Understanding Slippage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/slippage)).

- **AmountTooLow** — the stake amount is below the minimum allowed increment.
- **SlippageTooHigh** — the stake amount exceeds the caller's slippage cap.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the minimum-increment condition that raises it.
It does not describe live minimum-stake values on a network, slippage-cap sizing, or every staking
path that can return this error; those are separate topics
([Managing Your Stakes](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-sdk),
[Understanding Slippage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/slippage)).
