# Miner Deregistration

Miner deregistration is the miner-specific case of deregistration in a Bittensor subnet. The
[Glossary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#miner-deregistration) defines it as
the process of removing a poor-performing subnet miner from a UID slot so that the slot can be used
by a newly registered miner.

## Miner-Specific Scope

The broader term [deregistration](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#deregistration)
can apply to subnet miners or subnet validators. Miner deregistration narrows that idea to miners:
the participant being removed is a miner occupying a subnet UID slot.

## Relationship to UID Slots

A UID slot is the subnet position occupied by a miner or validator. Miner deregistration is about
the miner's relationship to that slot, not about changing what a subnet miner means as a role.

## Reader Boundary

This article defines the term at a high level. It does not list current miners, count available
slots, rank subnet participants, or describe registration steps. Readers should use official subnet
references for role-specific details.
