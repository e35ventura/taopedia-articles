# Hotkey Already Registered In Subnet

`HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet` means a registration call tried to assign a UID slot to a hotkey
that already holds one on the targeted subnet. The standard-errors reference describes it as "The
caller is requesting a redundant registration for a neuron"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeyalreadyregisteredinsubnet)).

Subnet registration assigns a neuron slot to a hotkey on a specific `netuid`
([Glossary: Register](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#register)).

## What It Means

Ordinary subnet registration assigns a UID slot to a hotkey on a specific `netuid`.
`HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet` means that hotkey already occupies a slot there, so the extrinsic
rejects the duplicate registration attempt instead of appending or replacing a neuron
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeyalreadyregisteredinsubnet),
[Glossary: Register](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#register)).

This is a per-subnet membership check. The same hotkey may still register on other subnets where it
does not yet hold a UID, but it cannot register twice on the same `netuid` through this path.

For debugging, treat this as a duplicate-registration failure, not as proof that the subnet is full,
that registration is disabled on the subnet, or that the hotkey lacks a UID on a different netuid
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NoNeuronIdAvailable](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#noneuronidavailable),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: SubNetRegistrationDisabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#subnetregistrationdisabled),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeynotregisteredinsubnet)).

## Why It Appears

The most direct cause is calling `register` or `register_limit` again for a hotkey that already
occupies a neuron slot on the chosen subnet. Retry logic, copied extrinsics, or automation that does
not first confirm current registration state can hit this error even when burn balance and coldkey
association would otherwise pass
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeyalreadyregisteredinsubnet),
[Glossary: Register](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#register)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the hotkey already has a slot on that subnet, so the registration
call is redundant rather than competing for a new UID.

## Distinction from Hotkey Not Registered In Subnet

`HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet` and `HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet` are opposite membership outcomes
for the same subnet hotkey lookup. `HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet` blocks registration when the
hotkey already has a UID on that `netuid`. `HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet` blocks subnet-scoped
extrinsics such as `set_weights` when the signing hotkey does not hold a slot on the targeted subnet
([Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeynotregisteredinsubnet)).

- **HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet** — registration attempted for a hotkey that already has a UID.
- **HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet** — another extrinsic requires a UID the hotkey does not have.

## Distinction from No Neuron Id Available

`HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet` and `NoNeuronIdAvailable` both appear during registration
troubleshooting, but they reject different conditions. `HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet` means the
hotkey already holds a UID on the subnet. `NoNeuronIdAvailable` means the subnet has no free neuron
slots for a new registration
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NoNeuronIdAvailable](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#noneuronidavailable)).

- **HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet** — the hotkey already has a UID on the subnet.
- **NoNeuronIdAvailable** — the subnet has no free slots for a new registration.

## Distinction from Subnet Registration Disabled

`HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet` and `SubNetRegistrationDisabled` both block registration attempts,
but for different reasons. `SubNetRegistrationDisabled` means neuron registration is turned off for
the targeted subnet through `NetworkRegistrationAllowed`. `HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet` means
registration is allowed in principle, but the hotkey already occupies a slot there
([Subtensor Standard Errors: SubNetRegistrationDisabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#subnetregistrationdisabled),
[Subnet Hyperparameters: NetworkRegistrationAllowed](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#networkregistrationallowed)).

- **SubNetRegistrationDisabled** — registration is disabled on the subnet.
- **HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet** — the hotkey already has a UID on the subnet.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the duplicate-registration condition that
raises it. It does not describe root registration, subnet capacity pruning, or registration rate
limits in full; those are separate topics
([Glossary: Register](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#register),
[Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters)).
