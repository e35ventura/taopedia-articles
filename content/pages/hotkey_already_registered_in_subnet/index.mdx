# Hotkey Already Registered In Subnet

`HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet` means a registration call tried to assign a UID to a hotkey that
already holds one on that netuid. The standard-errors reference describes it as "The caller is
requesting a redundant registration for a neuron"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeyalreadyregisteredinsubnet)).

Subnet registration is scoped per hotkey and netuid. The same hotkey may still register on other
subnets where it does not yet hold a slot, but it cannot register twice on the same netuid through
this path.

## What It Means

Ordinary subnet registration assigns a UID slot to a hotkey on a specific `netuid`.
`HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet` means that hotkey already occupies a neuron slot there, so the
chain rejects the duplicate registration attempt instead of appending or replacing a neuron
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeyalreadyregisteredinsubnet)).

This check runs before burn payment and coldkey pairing checks. A redundant registration fails even
when the caller has enough balance and a valid coldkey association.

## Why It Appears

The most direct cause is calling `register` or `register_limit` again for a hotkey that already
occupies a neuron slot on the chosen subnet. Retry logic, copied extrinsics, or automation that does
not first confirm current registration state can hit this error even when burn balance and coldkey
association would otherwise pass.

When debugging, rule out capacity and payment failures first. `NoNeuronIdAvailable` means the subnet
has no open UID slots for a new registration, while `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` means the caller lacks
funds for the burn. `HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet` means the hotkey already has a slot; the call
is redundant rather than competing for a new UID
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NoNeuronIdAvailable](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#noneuronidavailable),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake)).

## Distinction from Hotkey Not Registered In Subnet

`HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet` and `HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet` are opposite membership outcomes
for the same subnet hotkey lookup, checked in different extrinsics.
`HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet` blocks a new registration attempt when the hotkey already has a
UID on that `netuid`. `HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet` blocks subnet operations such as `set_weights`
when the signing hotkey does not hold a slot on the targeted subnet
([Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeynotregisteredinsubnet)).

- **HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet** — registration attempted for a hotkey that already has a UID.
- **HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet** — another extrinsic requires a UID the hotkey does not have.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the duplicate-registration check that raises
it. It does not describe root registration, subnet capacity pruning, or registration rate limits;
those are separate topics.
