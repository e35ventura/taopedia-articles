# Hotkey Already Registered In Subnet

`HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised by subnet
registration extrinsics. The standard-errors reference describes it as "The caller is requesting a
redundant registration for a neuron"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#hotkeyalreadyregisteredinsubnet)).

## What It Means

Ordinary subnet registration assigns a UID slot to a hotkey on a specific `netuid`. Before burn
payment and coldkey pairing checks run, `do_register` asks whether that hotkey already appears in
the subnet's `Uids` map. `HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet` means the map already contains an entry
for the hotkey, so the extrinsic rejects the duplicate registration attempt instead of appending or
replacing a neuron
([Subtensor source: registration.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/registration.rs)).

This is a per-subnet slot check. The same hotkey may still register on other subnets where it does
not yet hold a UID, but it cannot register twice on the same `netuid` through this path
([Subtensor source: registration.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/registration.rs)).

## Why It Appears

The most direct cause is calling `register` or `register_limit` again for a hotkey that already
occupies a neuron slot on the chosen subnet. Retry logic, copied extrinsics, or automation that does
not first confirm current registration state can hit this error even when burn balance and coldkey
association would otherwise pass
([Subtensor source: registration.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/registration.rs)).

Because the check runs before balance withdrawal, it is not a payment failure and does not mean the
subnet is full. The hotkey already has a slot; the extrinsic is redundant rather than competing for
a new UID
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NoNeuronIdAvailable](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#noneuronidavailable),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#notenoughbalancetostake)).

## Distinction from Hotkey Not Registered In Subnet

`HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet` and `HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet` are opposite membership outcomes
for the same subnet hotkey lookup. `HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet` is raised inside `do_register`
when registration is attempted while the hotkey already has a UID on that `netuid`.
`HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet` is raised by other extrinsics, such as `set_weights`, when the signing
hotkey does not hold a slot on the targeted subnet
([Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#hotkeynotregisteredinsubnet),
[Subtensor source: registration.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/registration.rs),
[Subtensor source: weights.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/weights.rs)).

- **HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet** — registration attempted for a hotkey that already has a UID.
- **HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet** — another extrinsic requires a UID the hotkey does not have.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the duplicate-registration check that raises
it. It does not describe root registration, subnet capacity pruning, or registration rate limits;
those are separate topics
([Subtensor source: registration.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/registration.rs)).
