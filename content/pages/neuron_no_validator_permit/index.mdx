# Neuron No Validator Permit

`NeuronNoValidatorPermit` means the signing neuron submitted weights that target other neurons
without holding a validator permit on that subnet. The standard-errors reference describes it as
"Attempting to set non-self weights without being a permitted validator"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#neuronnovalidatorpermit)).

Validator permits are subnet roles that allow a neuron to assign weights to peers during an epoch
([Validating in Bittensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators),
[Yuma Consensus Epoch: Validator Permit Management](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/navigating-subtensor/epoch#3-validator-permit-management)).

## What It Means

Weight submission can assign scores to other neurons on a subnet, but non-self assignments require
validator-permit authority. `NeuronNoValidatorPermit` means the call included non-self targets
without that permit, so the extrinsic is rejected
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#neuronnovalidatorpermit),
[Validating in Bittensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators)).

For debugging, treat this as a permit-authority failure on non-self weight assignments, not as proof
that stake weight is below the global threshold, that the weights version key is stale, or that the
submission exceeded the weights rate limit
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughStakeToSetWeights](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughstaketosetweights),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: IncorrectWeightVersionKey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#incorrectweightversionkey),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: SettingWeightsTooFast](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#settingweightstoofast)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a registered hotkey submits weights that target neurons other than itself
without holding a validator permit for that subnet. That can happen when a miner attempts to assign
non-self weights, when a validator permit expired or was not yet granted for the epoch, or when the
submitted vector mixes self and non-self entries without permit coverage for the non-self portion
([Validating in Bittensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators),
[Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#neuronnovalidatorpermit),
[Yuma Consensus Epoch: Validator Permit Management](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/navigating-subtensor/epoch#3-validator-permit-management)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the neuron lacks permit authority for non-self weight assignment on
that subnet.

## Distinction from Not Enough Stake To Set Weights

`NeuronNoValidatorPermit` and `NotEnoughStakeToSetWeights` both appear in weight submission, but
they reject different requirements. `NotEnoughStakeToSetWeights` means stake weight is below the
global threshold. `NeuronNoValidatorPermit` means non-self weights were submitted without a
validator permit
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughStakeToSetWeights](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughstaketosetweights),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NeuronNoValidatorPermit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#neuronnovalidatorpermit),
[Stake Threshold](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters)).

- **NotEnoughStakeToSetWeights** — stake weight is below the global threshold for weight submission.
- **NeuronNoValidatorPermit** — non-self weights were submitted without a validator permit.

## Distinction from Max Weight Exceeded

`NeuronNoValidatorPermit` and `MaxWeightExceeded` both appear during weight troubleshooting, but
they reject different rules. `NeuronNoValidatorPermit` is the permit gate for non-self assignments.
`MaxWeightExceeded` means an upscaled weight value exceeds the subnet max-weight cap
([Subtensor Standard Errors: MaxWeightExceeded](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#maxweightexceeded),
[Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters)).

- **NeuronNoValidatorPermit** — non-self weights were submitted without a validator permit.
- **MaxWeightExceeded** — an upscaled weight value exceeds the subnet max-weight cap.

## Distinction from Hotkey Not Registered In Subnet

`NeuronNoValidatorPermit` and `HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet` both appear during weight
troubleshooting, but they reject different conditions. `HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet` means the
hotkey has no UID on the targeted netuid. `NeuronNoValidatorPermit` means non-self weights were
submitted without a validator permit
([Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeynotregisteredinsubnet)).

- **HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet** — the hotkey has no UID on the subnet the call targets.
- **NeuronNoValidatorPermit** — non-self weights were submitted without a validator permit.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the validator-permit condition that raises it.
It does not describe how validator permits are awarded each epoch, how self-only weight vectors are
handled in full, or live permit rosters on a network; those are documented separately
([Validating in Bittensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators),
[Yuma Consensus Epoch](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/navigating-subtensor/epoch)).
