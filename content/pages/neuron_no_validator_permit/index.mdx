# Neuron No Validator Permit

`NeuronNoValidatorPermit` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised by the `set_weights`
extrinsic. The standard-errors reference describes it as "Attempting to set non-self weights without
being a permitted validator"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#neuronnovalidatorpermit)).

## What It Means

After earlier registration, stake, and rate-limit checks pass, `do_set_weights` asks whether the
caller may submit the weight vector it sent. `check_validator_permit` returns true for a pure
self-weight submission, for the subnet owner's UID, or when the caller's UID currently holds a
validator permit on that subnet. `NeuronNoValidatorPermit` means none of those exceptions applied
and the neuron tried to score other UIDs without permit rights
([Subtensor source: weights.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/weights.rs),
[Validator Permit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-permit)).

This is a rights check on who may influence consensus through non-self weights, not a test of
whether the vector's UIDs or values are well formed. Those shape checks run later in the same
extrinsic
([Subtensor source: weights.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/weights.rs)).

## Why It Appears

The most direct cause is a registered neuron submitting weights for other UIDs after losing
validator permit, or before ever earning it, while the vector is not a pure self-weight entry.
Miners and other non-validator UIDs can still hold subnet slots and stake, but non-self weight
submission requires the permit flag that the epoch logic awards to top-stake neurons up to the
subnet cap ([Validator Permit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-permit),
[Subtensor source: weights.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/weights.rs)).

Because permit state can change between epochs, a neuron that could set validator weights earlier
may hit this error later even when stake and registration are unchanged. The problem is missing
permit rights for the attempted vector, not insufficient balance or an unregistered hotkey
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughStakeToSetWeights](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughstaketosetweights),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeynotregisteredinsubnet)).

## Distinction from Not Enough Stake To Set Weights

`NeuronNoValidatorPermit` and `NotEnoughStakeToSetWeights` both appear on the `set_weights` path,
but they gate different requirements. `NotEnoughStakeToSetWeights` is checked earlier and compares
the caller's stake against the subnet's minimum stake to set weights. `NeuronNoValidatorPermit`
applies only after that stake gate passes and only when the submitted vector is not treated as
self-weight and the caller lacks validator permit
([Subtensor source: weights.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/weights.rs)).

- **NotEnoughStakeToSetWeights** — the caller's stake is below the subnet minimum for weight calls.
- **NeuronNoValidatorPermit** — stake is sufficient, but the vector scores other UIDs without
  permit.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the validator-permit gate that raises it. It
does not describe how permits are awarded each epoch, how to regain permit after losing it, or how
self-weight submissions are detected; those are separate topics
([Validator Permit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-permit),
[Subtensor source: weights.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/weights.rs)).
