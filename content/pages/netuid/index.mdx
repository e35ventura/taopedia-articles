# Netuid

A netuid is the identifier assigned to a subnet within the Bittensor network. The official glossary
defines it as a unique identifier for a subnet, while the subnet documentation describes subnets as
incentive-based markets made up of miners and validators around a specific digital commodity.

References: [Netuid](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#netuid),
[Understanding Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-subnets)

## Why Netuids Matter

Netuids give subnet-level context to Bittensor information. When a reader sees a subnet article,
chain record, or documentation reference tied to a number, the netuid is the part that says which
subnet the information belongs to. It is a label for locating the subnet context, not a description
of the work quality or maturity of that subnet.

References: [Netuid](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#netuid),
[Understanding Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-subnets)

## Relationship to Subnets

Bittensor subnets are separate incentive markets. Each subnet can define its own work, miner role,
and validator evaluation pattern. The netuid is the compact identifier used to refer to one of those
subnet contexts without repeating the full subnet name or description.

Reference: [Understanding Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-subnets)

## Interpretation Boundaries

A netuid should not be read as a ranking, endorsement, live status report, or participation guide.
It identifies a subnet, but it does not by itself explain that subnet's rules, current identity
fields, participants, or evaluation behavior. Those details need their own current source.

Reference: [Netuid](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#netuid)
