# Tx Delegate Take Rate Limit

Tx delegate take rate limit is a network-global chain setting, stored on chain as
`TxDelegateTakeRateLimit`, that sets how frequently a delegate's take can be increased. The
documentation lists it as a rate limit on delegate-take increases, and the chain rate-limit
reference documents a value of 216,000 blocks, roughly 30 days, between accepted updates
([Chain Rate Limits: Delegate take rate limit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits#delegate-take-rate-limit),
[TxDelegateTakeRateLimit Storage](https://subtensor.com/reference/subtensormodule/storage/TxDelegateTakeRateLimit)).

## What It Controls

The setting paces how often a delegate may raise the take they keep from delegated-stake emissions;
it does not set the take percentage itself. A validator configures take with `btcli sudo set-take`
within the documented range, but after an accepted increase this cooldown must elapse before another
increase is accepted
([Staking and Delegation Overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation),
[Bittensor CLI: set-take](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/btcli#btcli-sudo-set-take)).

The pacing protects nominators from sudden, repeated commission hikes. By forcing a wait between
take increases, it gives delegated stakers a predictable window rather than allowing a delegate to
ratchet their take up rapidly across consecutive blocks
([Chain Rate Limits](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits),
[Glossary: Validator Take %](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-take-)).

## Reading a Delegate's Take on netuid 1

A delegate that accepts nominations on a subnet such as netuid 1 keeps its configured take from
delegated-stake dividends there, while the cooldown on raising that take is the global
`TxDelegateTakeRateLimit` rather than a per-subnet value. The take is set per delegate hotkey, so
readers can check a delegate's current take through the documented set-take command and verify the
216,000-block limit in the chain rate-limit reference and on-chain storage
([Bittensor CLI: set-take](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/btcli#btcli-sudo-set-take),
[Chain Rate Limits: Delegate take rate limit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits#delegate-take-rate-limit),
[TxDelegateTakeRateLimit Storage](https://subtensor.com/reference/subtensormodule/storage/TxDelegateTakeRateLimit)).
Netuid 1 is used only as an example label for where delegated-stake dividends accrue; the rate limit
itself is network-global
([Bittensor Networks](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/bittensor-networks)).

## Scope and Permission

Tx delegate take rate limit is a global value rather than a per-subnet hyperparameter, so the same
cooldown governs delegate-take increases network-wide rather than being tuned per netuid. The
documentation groups it with variables configured under root permission
([Chain Rate Limits](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits),
[TxDelegateTakeRateLimit Storage](https://subtensor.com/reference/subtensormodule/storage/TxDelegateTakeRateLimit)).

Because it is measured in blocks, the real-time wait depends on block production, and the live value
is chain state that can differ from the documented default
([Bittensor Networks](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/bittensor-networks)).

## Distinction from Validator Take

Validator take is the share a delegate keeps from delegated-stake emissions before the remainder
flows to stakers. Tx delegate take rate limit does not set that share; it only paces how often the
share can be increased, so the take is the commission while this limit is the cooldown on raising it
([Glossary: Validator Take %](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-take-),
[Chain Rate Limits: Delegate take rate limit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits#delegate-take-rate-limit)).

- **Validator take** — the commission a delegate keeps from delegated-stake dividends.
- **Tx delegate take rate limit** — the block-counted cooldown before that commission can be raised
  again.

## Distinction from Subnet Owner Take

Subnet owner take is a documented owner cut from a subnet's emission bucket. Tx delegate take rate
limit concerns the timing of delegate-take increases, a separate commission and a separate control
from the owner cut
([Glossary: Validator Take %](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-take-),
[Emission: Distribution](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions#distribution)).

- **Subnet owner take** — the owner's share of a subnet's emission bucket.
- **Tx delegate take rate limit** — the cooldown on delegate-take increases.

## Distinction from Serving Rate Limit

Serving rate limit is a per-subnet hyperparameter pacing how often a neuron may update its on-chain
axon endpoint. Tx delegate take rate limit is a network-global cooldown on delegate-take increases,
so they pace different actions at different scopes
([Chain Rate Limits](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits),
[Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters)).

- **Serving rate limit** — per-subnet pacing of axon endpoint updates.
- **Tx delegate take rate limit** — network-global pacing of delegate-take increases.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the concept at a high level. It does not report any delegate's current take, when
a specific delegate could next raise it, or the live limit in force. Those are live chain state on
the delegate hotkey and should be checked before planning a take change. The value of 216,000 blocks
is the documented figure, and the setting is global and root-controlled.
