# Tx Delegate Take Rate Limit

Tx delegate take rate limit is a global chain parameter, named in the documentation as
`TxDelegateTakeRateLimit`, that sets how often a delegate may change its take. The rate-limits
reference lists it at roughly 216,000 blocks, about 30 days, and raises the
`DelegateTxRateLimitExceeded` error when a change is attempted too soon
([Rate Limits](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits)).

## What It Controls

A delegate's [take](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#delegation) is the percentage
a validator keeps from delegated-stake emissions before the rest flows to its stakers. This rate
limit is the cooldown between adjustments to that percentage, so it governs how frequently a
delegate can retune its fee rather than the size of the fee itself
([Rate Limits](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits),
[Validator Take](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-take)).

## When It Matters

A common misconception is that a delegate can adjust its take freely to chase stake, lowering it to
attract delegators and raising it once stake arrives. The rate limit blocks that: after one change,
the take is locked for roughly 30 days before it can move again
([Rate Limits](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits)).

Concretely, a validator that drops its take to draw in delegators cannot quietly raise it back the
next week. The roughly 216,000-block window must elapse first, so the lowered take stays in place
for about a month. That waiting cost is the point: it makes take a credible, durable commitment to
stakers rather than a value that can be flipped around their expectations
([Rate Limits](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits),
[Validator Take](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-take)).

## Documented Nature

The [Rate Limits](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits) reference lists
`TxDelegateTakeRateLimit` as a chain state variable measured in blocks, with the cooldown at about
216,000 blocks. Because it is live chain state rather than a per-subnet owner setting, the active
value is read from the chain, and the initial value is exposed among the network constants
([Rate Limits](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits),
[Constants](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/constants)).

## Distinction from Child Key Take Rate Limit

Both are roughly 30-day take cooldowns, but they apply to different takes. Tx delegate take rate
limit gates changes to a delegate's take on delegated stake, while the child key take rate limit
gates changes to a child hotkey's take
([Rate Limits](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits)).

- **Tx delegate take rate limit** — cooldown between delegate take changes.
- **Child key take rate limit** — cooldown between child hotkey take changes.

## Distinction from Tx Rate Limit

Tx rate limit is the short network-wide general transaction cooldown that currently applies to
hotkey swaps. Tx delegate take rate limit is a much longer cooldown specific to changing delegate
take ([Rate Limits](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits)).

- **Tx rate limit** — short general transaction cooldown.
- **Tx delegate take rate limit** — long cooldown for delegate take changes.
