# Stake Unavailable

`StakeUnavailable` means an alpha-reducing call named more alpha than the coldkey can currently
remove on the subnet after conviction locks are excluded. The standard-errors reference describes it
as "Stake is unavailable"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#stakeunavailable)).

Conviction locks constrain how far a coldkey's subnet alpha can fall while a lock is active
([Conviction and Locked Stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).

## What It Means

On alpha-reducing paths that decrease a coldkey's subnet stake while conviction locks are active,
part of the alpha balance may remain locked. That includes remove-stake and move-stake calls, alpha
recycle paths such as `recycle_alpha`, and limit-order swap validation when execution reduces
unlocked stake
([Staking and Delegation Overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: CannotBurnOrRecycleOnRootSubnet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#cannotburnorrecycleonrootsubnet),
[Subnet Liquidity Pools](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-subnets#liquidity-pools),
[Conviction and Locked Stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).

`StakeUnavailable` means the call tried to move or remove more alpha than remains unlocked after
those locks are applied, so the extrinsic is rejected
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#stakeunavailable),
[Conviction and Locked Stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).

For debugging, treat this as a lock-aware availability failure, not as proof that the hotkey-coldkey
pair lacks enough total staked alpha on the subnet, that the request is below the minimum staking
increment, or that the subnet staking cooldown has not elapsed
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#notenoughstaketowithdraw),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: AmountTooLow](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#amounttoolow),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: StakingOperationRateLimitExceeded](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#stakingoperationratelimitexceeded)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a coldkey submits an unstake, move-stake, recycle-alpha, or limit-order swap
amount that is larger than the unlocked alpha available on the subnet. That happens when part of the
coldkey's alpha on the subnet is locked and the call names more than the unlocked remainder
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#stakeunavailable),
[Conviction and Locked Stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the requested alpha exceeds the unlocked stake available on the
subnet.

## Distinction from Not Enough Stake To Withdraw

`StakeUnavailable` and `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` both block stake removal, but they compare against
different balances. `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` means the hotkey-coldkey pair lacks enough total
staked alpha on the subnet for the requested amount. `StakeUnavailable` means enough total alpha may
exist, but the locked portion leaves insufficient unlocked alpha for the request
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#notenoughstaketowithdraw),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: StakeUnavailable](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#stakeunavailable),
[Conviction and Locked Stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).

- **StakeUnavailable** — the requested amount exceeds unlocked alpha on the subnet.
- **NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw** — total staked alpha on the pair is too low.

## Distinction from Insufficient Stake For Lock

`StakeUnavailable` and `InsufficientStakeForLock` both involve conviction locks, but on opposite
directions. `InsufficientStakeForLock` blocks creating or increasing a lock when total alpha on the
subnet is too low. `StakeUnavailable` blocks alpha-reducing paths when the request exceeds unlocked
alpha after existing locks are excluded
([Subtensor Standard Errors: InsufficientStakeForLock](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#insufficientstakeforlock),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: StakeUnavailable](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#stakeunavailable),
[Conviction and Locked Stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).

- **InsufficientStakeForLock** — not enough total alpha to create or increase a lock.
- **StakeUnavailable** — not enough unlocked alpha for the removal request.

## Distinction from Staking Operation Rate Limit Exceeded

`StakeUnavailable` and `StakingOperationRateLimitExceeded` can both appear on staking extrinsics,
but they enforce different rules. `StakingOperationRateLimitExceeded` is the per-subnet pacing
rejection when staking operations are submitted too frequently. `StakeUnavailable` is the lock-aware
availability rejection on the requested alpha amount
([Subtensor Standard Errors: StakingOperationRateLimitExceeded](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#stakingoperationratelimitexceeded),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: StakeUnavailable](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#stakeunavailable)).

- **StakeUnavailable** — locked stake leaves too little unlocked alpha for the request.
- **StakingOperationRateLimitExceeded** — the subnet staking cooldown has not elapsed.

## Distinction from Amount Too Low

`StakeUnavailable` and `AmountTooLow` both stop alpha-reducing calls, but for different reasons.
`AmountTooLow` means the submitted amount falls below the chain's minimum staking increment or is
zero on an unstake path. `StakeUnavailable` means the amount exceeds the unlocked alpha currently
available on the subnet
([Subtensor Standard Errors: AmountTooLow](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#amounttoolow),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: StakeUnavailable](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#stakeunavailable)).

- **StakeUnavailable** — the request exceeds unlocked alpha on the subnet.
- **AmountTooLow** — the request is below the minimum allowed amount.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the lock-aware availability condition that
raises it on alpha-reducing paths. It does not report a wallet's live locked or unlocked alpha on
any subnet, or describe conviction lock creation in full; those depend on chain state and are
documented separately
([Conviction and Locked Stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking),
[Staking and Delegation Overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)).
