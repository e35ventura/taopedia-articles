# Stake Unavailable

`StakeUnavailable` means a stake-removal call named more alpha than the coldkey can currently
unstake on the subnet after locked stake is excluded. In `lock.rs`, the runtime defines
`available_to_unstake` as total coldkey alpha on the subnet minus the current locked amount, and
`ensure_available_to_unstake` returns this error when the requested amount exceeds that value
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

## What It Means

Conviction locks constrain how far a coldkey's subnet alpha can fall while a lock is active
([Conviction and locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).

On unstake-related paths, Subtensor separates three quantities through `stake_availability`: total
alpha on the subnet, locked alpha, and the remaining available alpha. `StakeUnavailable` means the
call tried to move or remove more alpha than that available figure allows
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

The check runs on remove-stake validation, move-stake validation, alpha recycle, and order-swap
paths that call `ensure_available_to_unstake` before decreasing stake
([Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs),
[Subtensor source: recycle_alpha.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/recycle_alpha.rs)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a coldkey submits an unstake, move-stake, recycle, or order-swap amount that
is larger than `available_to_unstake` for that subnet. That happens when part of the coldkey's alpha
on the subnet is locked and the call names more than the unlocked remainder
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs),
[Conviction and locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).

When debugging, rule out earlier stake-size checks on the same extrinsic. `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw`
means the hotkey-coldkey pair does not hold enough total staked alpha for the requested amount.
`StakeUnavailable` is the later lock-aware check when total stake may be sufficient but the unlocked
portion is not
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughstaketowithdraw),
[Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the requested alpha exceeds the unlocked stake available on the
subnet.

## Distinction from Not Enough Stake To Withdraw

`StakeUnavailable` and `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` both block stake removal, but they compare against
different balances. `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` means the hotkey-coldkey pair lacks enough total
staked alpha on the subnet. `StakeUnavailable` means enough total alpha may exist, but the locked
portion leaves insufficient unlocked alpha for the request
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughstaketowithdraw),
[Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

- **StakeUnavailable** — the requested amount exceeds unlocked alpha on the subnet.
- **NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw** — total staked alpha on the pair is too low.

## Distinction from Insufficient Stake For Lock

`StakeUnavailable` and `InsufficientStakeForLock` both involve locked stake, but on opposite
directions. `InsufficientStakeForLock` is raised when creating or topping up a lock without enough
total alpha on the subnet to cover the lock amount. `StakeUnavailable` is raised when removing or
moving alpha would exceed what remains unlocked
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

- **InsufficientStakeForLock** — not enough total alpha to create or increase a lock.
- **StakeUnavailable** — not enough unlocked alpha for the removal request.

## Distinction from Staking Operation Rate Limit Exceeded

`StakeUnavailable` and `StakingOperationRateLimitExceeded` can both appear on staking extrinsics,
but they enforce different rules. `StakingOperationRateLimitExceeded` is the per-subnet pacing
rejection when staking operations are submitted too frequently. `StakeUnavailable` is the lock-aware
availability rejection on the requested alpha amount
([Staking Operation Rate Limit Exceeded](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#stakingoperationratelimitexceeded),
[Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

- **StakeUnavailable** — locked stake leaves too little unlocked alpha for the request.
- **StakingOperationRateLimitExceeded** — the subnet staking cooldown has not elapsed.

## Distinction from Amount Too Low

`StakeUnavailable` and `AmountTooLow` both stop stake-removal calls, but for different reasons.
`AmountTooLow` means the submitted amount falls below the chain's minimum staking or swap increment
for the path. `StakeUnavailable` means the amount exceeds the unlocked alpha currently available on
the subnet
([Subtensor Standard Errors: AmountTooLow](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#amounttoolow),
[Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

- **StakeUnavailable** — the request exceeds unlocked alpha on the subnet.
- **AmountTooLow** — the request is below the minimum allowed amount.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the lock-aware availability check in
`ensure_available_to_unstake`. It does not report a wallet's live locked or unlocked alpha on any
subnet; those values depend on chain state at observation time
([Conviction and locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).
