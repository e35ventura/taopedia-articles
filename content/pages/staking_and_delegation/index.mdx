# Staking and Delegation (TAO)

TAO holders can stake any amount of liquidity to validators. Staking aligns a staker with a
validator’s performance and typically entitles the staker to a share of validator dividends.

References:
[Managing Stake with BTCLI](https://docs.bittensor.com/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-btcli),
[Docs Home](https://docs.bittensor.com/)

## Key Concepts

- **Stake to validators:** Stakers choose validators to support by delegating TAO to them.
- **Minimum transaction amount:** 500,000 RAO (0.0005 TAO) for stake/unstake/move/transfer (per
  docs).
- **Wallets:** You need a [[wallets|wallet]] to manage stake (CLI or Wallet SDK).
- **Rewards:** Validator dividends are shared with their stakers, per network rules and validator
  setup.

Reference:
[Managing Stake with BTCLI](https://docs.bittensor.com/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-btcli)

## Conviction and Locked Stake

Beyond ordinary stake and delegation, Bittensor supports locking subnet alpha stake to build a
public on-chain commitment score called conviction. Locking binds a portion of alpha-denominated
stake inside a subnet so it cannot drop below the locked amount without passing through the lock
behavior described in the official docs. This is a commitment signal, not a separate token or
validator-delegation choice.

See [[conviction_and_locked_stake|Conviction and Locked Stake]] for the concept boundary, lock
modes, and how conviction differs from general staking status.

Reference:
[Conviction and locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)

## Typical Workflow (Conceptual)

- Create or import a wallet (hotkey/coldkey; see [[wallets|Wallets]]).
- Select a validator to stake to, based on performance, transparency, and risk tolerance.
- Use the CLI to stake TAO to the validator; confirm transaction and monitor rewards over time.
- Rebalance by moving or unstaking as conditions change.

## Considerations

- **Risk:** Validator performance and mechanism outcomes vary; rewards are not guaranteed.
- **Costs:** Transaction fees and minimums apply; plan for operational cadence rather than frequent
  micro-movements.
- **Security:** Protect wallet keys and verify official CLI/docs to avoid phishing or malicious
  lookalikes.
