# Staking and Delegation (TAO)

TAO holders can stake liquidity to validators, subject to transaction minimums and the minimum
required stake for nominators. Staking aligns a staker with a validator's performance and typically
entitles the staker to a share of validator dividends.

References:
[Staking/delegation overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation),
[Managing Stake with BTCLI](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-btcli)

## Key Concepts

- **Stake to validators:** Stakers choose validators to support by delegating TAO to them.
- **Minimum transaction amount:** 500,000 RAO (0.0005 TAO) applies to stake, unstake, move-stake,
  and transfer-stake transactions. This is a transaction amount threshold, not the same thing as the
  required stake minimum.
- **Minimum required stake:** The official staking overview states that nominators have a 0.1 TAO
  minimum required stake.
- **Subnet-local stake:** Staking is local to a subnet; staking to the same validator hotkey on
  different subnets is treated independently.
- **Wallets:** You need a wallet to manage stake (CLI or Wallet SDK).
- **Rewards:** Validator dividends are shared with their stakers, per network rules and validator
  setup.

Reference:
[Staking/delegation overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation),
[Managing Stake with BTCLI](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-btcli)

## Typical Workflow (Conceptual)

- Create or import a wallet (hotkey/coldkey; see keys article).
- Select a validator to stake to, based on performance, transparency, and risk tolerance.
- Use the CLI to stake TAO to the validator; confirm transaction and monitor rewards over time.
- Rebalance by moving or unstaking as conditions change.

## Considerations

- **Risk:** Validator performance and mechanism outcomes vary; rewards are not guaranteed.
- **Costs:** Transaction fees, transaction minimums, the nominator stake minimum, slippage, and
  price-protection settings can all affect staking operations; plan for operational cadence rather
  than frequent micro-movements.
- **Security:** Protect wallet keys and verify official CLI/docs to avoid phishing or malicious
  lookalikes.
