# Staking and Delegation (TAO)

TAO holders can stake any amount of liquidity to validators. Staking aligns a staker with a
validator’s performance and typically entitles the staker to a share of validator dividends.

References:
[Managing Stake with BTCLI](https://docs.bittensor.com/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-btcli),
[Docs Home](https://docs.bittensor.com/)

## Key Concepts

- **Stake to validators:** Stakers choose validators to support by delegating TAO to them.
- **Minimum transaction amount:** 500,000 RAO (0.0005 TAO) for stake/unstake/move/transfer (per
  docs).
- **Wallets:** You need a wallet to manage stake (CLI or Wallet SDK).
- **Rewards:** Validator dividends are shared with their stakers, per network rules and validator
  setup.

Reference:
[Managing Stake with BTCLI](https://docs.bittensor.com/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-btcli)

## Typical Workflow (Conceptual)

- Create or import a wallet (hotkey/coldkey; see keys article).
- Select a validator to stake to, based on performance, transparency, and risk tolerance.
- Use the CLI to stake TAO to the validator; confirm transaction and monitor rewards over time.
- Rebalance by moving or unstaking as conditions change.

## Considerations

- **Risk:** Validator performance and mechanism outcomes vary; rewards are not guaranteed.
- **Costs:** Transaction fees and minimums apply; plan for operational cadence rather than frequent
  micro-movements.
- **Security:** Protect wallet keys and verify official CLI/docs to avoid phishing or malicious
  lookalikes.

## Relationship to Nominate

Staking and delegation and nomination are related but different parts of Bittensor vocabulary.
Staking and delegation name the act of committing TAO to back a validator, while nomination names
the validator-side status that makes a validator eligible to receive that delegated stake. The
[Staking/delegation overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)
describes how holders delegate stake to validators, and the
[Glossary: Nominate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominate) describes
nomination as a validator declaring itself open to delegation.

For readers, staking and delegation name the staker-side action, while nomination names the
validator-side precondition that action depends on. One is the delegator's commitment; the other is
the validator's eligibility to receive it.

References:
[Staking/delegation overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation),
[Glossary: Nominate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominate)
