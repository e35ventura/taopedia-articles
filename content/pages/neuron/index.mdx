# Neuron

A neuron is the general term for a participant inside a Bittensor subnet. The official
[Glossary: Neuron](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#neuron) entry describes a
neuron as a subnet participant that can be either a subnet miner or a subnet validator.

## Role Scope

Neuron is an umbrella term, not a third role separate from miners and validators. In reader-facing
prose, it is useful when a statement applies to either kind of subnet participant. When the role
matters, the more specific terms miner and validator should be used.

The official [Understanding Neurons](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/neurons) page uses the
same framing: a neuron is the generalized name for a subnet participant that can be a miner or a
validator.

## UID Context

The glossary ties each neuron to a unique UID within its subnet. This means the term is scoped to a
particular subnet context. A neuron should not be read as a universal participant label across every
subnet, because subnet membership and UID assignment are subnet-specific.

Reference: [Glossary: Neuron](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#neuron)

## Reader Boundary

This article defines the term at a concept level. It does not list current participants, count live
subnet members, rank participants, or describe operational procedures. Readers looking for the miner
or validator side of the role should use the official role-specific documentation.
