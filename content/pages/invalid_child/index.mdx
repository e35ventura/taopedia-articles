# Invalid Child

`InvalidChild` means a parent hotkey tried to schedule itself as one of its own children on a
subnet. The standard-errors reference describes it as "Invalid child for a hotkey"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#invalidchild)).

The check applies to the parent-child relationship in the submitted list, not to whether a listed
child hotkey is registered or already assigned elsewhere
([Child Hotkeys](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys)).

## What It Means

A [child hotkey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys) lets a parent delegate a
share of its stake to a separate validating key on one subnet. `set_children` submits the full child
list a parent wants on that subnet. `InvalidChild` is the rejection when that list includes the
parent hotkey itself, creating a self-loop the chain will not accept
([Child Hotkeys](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys),
[Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#invalidchild)).

Child-hotkey scheduling routes stake from parent to other validating keys. A parent cannot also
appear as its own child entry in the same submitted list.

## Why It Appears

This error appears when the submitted child list names the parent hotkey as one of its own children.
Automation that reuses the parent key as a child entry, or a merged list that accidentally includes
the signing hotkey, can hit this error even when count and proportions would otherwise look valid.

When debugging, rule out `InvalidChild` before assuming a duplicate-entry, count, or proportion
failure. `DuplicateChild` means some child hotkey was listed twice. `TooManyChildren` means the list
has more than five entries. `ProportionOverflow` means the combined stake proportions do not fit
([Subtensor Standard Errors: DuplicateChild](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#duplicatechild),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: TooManyChildren](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#toomanychildren),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: ProportionOverflow](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#proportionoverflow)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the parent cannot route stake to itself through the child-hotkey
mechanism on that subnet.

## Distinction from Duplicate Child

`InvalidChild` and `DuplicateChild` both involve hotkey identity in a submitted child list, but they
reject different defects. `InvalidChild` means the parent hotkey was included as its own child.
`DuplicateChild` means some other child hotkey was named more than once in the same call
([Subtensor Standard Errors: DuplicateChild](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#duplicatechild)).

- **InvalidChild** — the parent hotkey appears in its own child list.
- **DuplicateChild** — the same child hotkey is listed more than once.

## Distinction from Too Many Children

`InvalidChild` and `TooManyChildren` are both raised while validating a submitted child list, but
they guard different properties. `InvalidChild` rejects a parent self-loop. `TooManyChildren`
rejects lists with more than five child entries
([Subtensor Standard Errors: TooManyChildren](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#toomanychildren)).

- **InvalidChild** — the parent is listed as its own child.
- **TooManyChildren** — the child list has more than five entries.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the self-loop condition that raises it. It does
not describe childkey take, minimum parent stake requirements, or the live child list on a subnet;
those are separate topics
([Child Hotkeys](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys),
[Invalid Childkey Take](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#invalidchildkeytake)).
