Leadpoet is Bittensor Subnet 71, a decentralized AI subnet for sales lead generation. Its on-chain
description is "Intent-driven AI for modern sales teams," and the codebase for the subnet is the
`leadpoet/leadpoet` repository. The subnet's job is to surface high-quality sales leads — companies
that fit a buyer's ideal customer profile and show genuine signs of being in the market to buy.

## What Leadpoet Rewards

A buyer describes the kind of customer they are looking for — industry, size, stage, location, and
the buying signals that matter to them. Leadpoet rewards miners for finding real companies from the
open web that match that profile and for backing each one with evidence that it is genuinely
in-market, rather than returning generic lists. Miners compete in two ways: an open competition to
build the best lead-finding model, and paid work fulfilling specific client requests for enriched
leads. In both, the same standard applies — better-matched, better-evidenced leads earn more.

## Miner and Validator Roles

Miners build the lead-generation AI. Given a buyer's profile, a miner finds the companies — and, for
client work, the enriched contact and intent details — that best fit it.

Validators decide how good those leads are. Running a common scoring pipeline, they judge each
submission on how well it fits the requested profile, how accurate its data is, and how strong its
intent evidence is, then set weights on the network so the highest-quality leads earn the most,
which is how reward flows through [[Yuma Consensus]].

## On-Chain Identity

Live SN71 subnet data is available on [TaoStats](https://taostats.io/subnets/71), which identifies
the subnet as Leadpoet. Readers can reproduce the subnet identity fields directly with the Bittensor
CLI command documented in the
[btcli reference](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/btcli/#btcli-subnets-get-identity):
`btcli subnets get-identity --netuid 71 --network finney`. That Finney identity is the source of
truth for the subnet name and the GitHub repository associated with the subnet.
