# Root Subnet

The Root Subnet, also called Subnet Zero, is a special Bittensor subnet. The
[Glossary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#root-subnetsubnet-zero) describes it
as a subnet where miners cannot register and no validation work is performed, while validators can
register and TAO holders can stake to those validators.

## Subnet-Agnostic Staking

Root Subnet staking is subnet-agnostic. Instead of tying stake directly to one ordinary subnet's
local context, the glossary describes Root as a way for TAO holders to stake to validators across
the wider Bittensor setting.

## Validator Weight Context

The [Glossary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#root-subnetsubnet-zero) explains
why subnet-agnostic Root staking matters for validator weight: a validator's weight in a subnet
includes both their share of that subnet's alpha and their share of staked TAO in Subnet Zero.

## Relationship to Root Claims

The Root Subnet and root claims address related but different parts of Bittensor staking context.
The [Understanding Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-subnets) overview
describes the Root Subnet as a special subnet where validators register and TAO holders can stake in
a subnet-agnostic way, while the
[Root Claim Overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/root-claims) describes
how alpha dividends from that stake are handled.

For readers, the Root Subnet is the staking environment, while root claims name the
dividend-handling mechanism for stake placed there.

References: [Understanding Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-subnets),
[Root Claim Overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/root-claims)

## Relationship to Related Terms

Root Subnet is the base term. Root claims describe how dividends are handled for stake placed
through Root, while root proportion describes how much of a subnet's dividend context is attributed
to Root-routed TAO stake. Those related terms should not be treated as the same concept as Root
Subnet itself.

## Relationship to Multiple Mechanisms

The Root Subnet is a special subnet where miners cannot register and no validation work is
performed. The
[Glossary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#multiple-incentive-mechanisms) notes
that validators must evaluate miners separately for each mechanism when a subnet runs more than one
incentive mechanism.

For readers, Root Subnet still names subnet-agnostic validator staking. That special role is
separate from the per-mechanism miner evaluation context used in ordinary subnets.

References:
[Multiple Incentive Mechanisms Within Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-multiple-mech-subnets),
[Glossary: Multiple Incentive Mechanisms](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#multiple-incentive-mechanisms)

## Reader Boundary

This article defines the term at a high level. It does not list current Root validators, describe
staking steps, compare staking choices, or explain root-claim settings. Readers should use official
staking references for those details.

## Relationship to Subtensor

Root Subnet and Subtensor are related but different parts of Bittensor chain vocabulary. Subtensor
names Bittensor's layer 1 blockchain and system of record, while Root Subnet names a special subnet
on that chain layer where validators can register and TAO holders can stake in a subnet-agnostic
way. The [Glossary: Subtensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#subtensor)
describes the blockchain layer, and the
[Glossary: Root Subnet/Subnet Zero](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#root-subnetsubnet-zero)
describes the special subnet role.

For readers, Subtensor names the chain layer, while Root Subnet names a special subnet context
recorded on that layer.

References: [Glossary: Subtensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#subtensor),
[Glossary: Root Subnet/Subnet Zero](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#root-subnetsubnet-zero)

## Relationship to Extrinsics

Root Subnet and extrinsics are related but different parts of Bittensor chain action vocabulary.
Extrinsics name signed transactions and calls submitted to Subtensor, while Root Subnet names the
special subnet context where validator registration and subnet-agnostic staking actions can be
processed on chain. The
[Glossary: Extrinsics](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#extrinsics) describes
signed transactions submitted to the chain, and
[Understanding Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-subnets) describes
Root Subnet as a special subnet where validators register and TAO holders can stake.

For readers, extrinsics name submitted chain actions, while Root Subnet names the special subnet
environment those staking-related actions can target.

References: [Glossary: Extrinsics](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#extrinsics),
[Understanding Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-subnets)

## Relationship to Netuid

Root Subnet and a netuid are related but different parts of Bittensor vocabulary. A netuid
identifies a subnet on the network, while Root Subnet names the special subnet also called Subnet
Zero on that network. The
[Glossary: Netuid](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#netuid) places netuid at the
subnet level, and the
[Glossary: Root Subnet/Subnet Zero](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#root-subnetsubnet-zero)
describes a subnet where miners cannot register and validators can register for subnet-agnostic
staking.

For readers, a netuid selects which subnet is in scope, while Root Subnet names the special subnet
role associated with Subnet Zero.

References: [Glossary: Netuid](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#netuid),
[Glossary: Root Subnet/Subnet Zero](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#root-subnetsubnet-zero)

## Relationship to Mempool

Root Subnet and the mempool are related but different stages of Bittensor chain activity. The
mempool names pending transactions before block inclusion, while Root Subnet names the special
subnet context where accepted staking-related actions are recorded after processing. The
[Glossary: Mempool](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#mempool) describes the
pending-transaction context, and
[Understanding Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-subnets) describes
Root Subnet as a special staking environment on the network.

For readers, the mempool belongs to the pre-inclusion waiting stage, while Root Subnet names the
special subnet destination for subnet-agnostic staking once those actions succeed on chain.

References: [Glossary: Mempool](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#mempool),
[Understanding Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-subnets)
