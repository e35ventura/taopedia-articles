# Immune Owner Uids Limit

Immune owner uids limit is a per-subnet hyperparameter, listed in the documentation as
`ImmuneOwnerUidsLimit`, that caps how many neurons on a subnet may be marked as owner-immune. The
reference describes it as determining the maximum number of neurons that can be marked owner-immune
on a subnet, with a default of one
([Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters)).

## What It Controls

The hyperparameter sets a count ceiling, not which specific UIDs receive immunity or how long
ordinary
[immunity period](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#immunityperiod)
protection lasts after registration. It limits how many neurons the subnet owner can designate as
owner-immune at once
([Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters),
[Glossary: Immunity Period](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#immunity-period)).

Owner-immune status is separate from the post-registration immunity window that shields new entrants
from deregistration. The limit governs how many standing neurons may carry the owner-immune label,
which affects how many slots the owner can shield from ordinary competitive displacement rules on
that netuid
([Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters)).

The cap is owner-tunable per subnet, so netuid 1 can use the default while another netuid allows a
higher owner-immune count, and the live value is chain state rather than a network constant
([Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters),
[Bittensor Networks](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/bittensor-networks)).

## Documented Type and Default

The [Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters)
reference lists `ImmuneOwnerUidsLimit` as a `u16` with a default of one and marks the permission
required to change it as subnet owner through the `sudo_set_owner_immune_neuron_limit` extrinsic
([Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters)).

Because each subnet carries its own hyperparameters, the owner-immune cap on one netuid can differ
from the documented default and from the cap on another subnet
([Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters)).

## Reading the Live Value for netuid 1

Immune owner uids limit is per-subnet chain state, so any live claim is tied to a specific netuid.
For the documented example netuid 1 on Finney mainnet, the limit appears in that subnet's
hyperparameter table, which the reference describes under viewing a subnet's hyperparameters
([Subnet Hyperparameters: View hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#view-hyperparameters)).
The infobox uses netuid 1 only as an example label; other subnets expose their own values
([Bittensor Networks](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/bittensor-networks)).

## Distinction from Immunity Period

Immunity period is the block-counted window after registration when a new neuron is protected from
deregistration. Immune owner uids limit is the separate cap on how many neurons may be marked
owner-immune regardless of registration timing
([Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters),
[Glossary: Immunity Period](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#immunity-period)).

- **Immunity period** — post-registration protection window for new entrants.
- **Immune owner uids limit** — maximum count of owner-immune neurons on a subnet.

## Distinction from Min Non Immune Uids

Min non immune uids sets the minimum count of UID slots that must remain outside immune categories
on a subnet. Immune owner uids limit caps how many neurons the owner may mark owner-immune. The two
hyperparameters bound opposite sides of the same composition question: one enforces a floor on
ordinary competitive slots, the other a ceiling on owner-shielded slots
([Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters)).

- **Min non immune uids** — minimum non-immune UID slots required on the subnet.
- **Immune owner uids limit** — maximum owner-immune neurons the owner may designate.

## Distinction from Pruning Score

Pruning score ranks which UID opens when a full subnet accepts a new registration. Owner-immune
neurons are excluded from that displacement ranking, and the subnet owner's hotkey carries permanent
immunity from deregistration separate from post-registration immunity period
([Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters),
[Glossary: Pruning Score](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#pruning-score),
[Mining: Immunity period](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/miners#immunity-period)).

On-chain pruning prefers the lowest-ranked non-immune neuron while enough non-immune slots remain
above the subnet's minimum non-immune floor. Owner-immune designations sit outside that ordinary
comparison path rather than competing as another pruning-score candidate
([`get_neuron_to_prune`](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/registration.rs),
[Glossary: Immunity Period](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#immunity-period)).

- **Pruning score** — ranking input when one incumbent slot must open on a full subnet.
- **Immune owner uids limit** — ceiling on how many owner-immune neurons may exist on the subnet.

## Reader Boundary

Immune owner uids limit should not be read as immunity period, a live count of owner-immune UIDs, or
displacement advice for any particular netuid. It names only the hyperparameter ceiling on
owner-immune designations on the selected subnet
([Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters)).
