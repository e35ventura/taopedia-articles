# Alpha Staker

An alpha staker is a staker who holds subnet alpha through staking in a specific Bittensor subnet
([Staking into Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation#staking-into-subnets),
[Emission: Distribution](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions#distribution)).

The term names the subnet-local staking side of Bittensor's TAO-and-alpha staking model. It belongs
with subnet alpha, not with Root Subnet TAO staking.

## Distinction from Effective Stake

An alpha staker and effective stake describe subnet staking as the holder and the validator total it
feeds. An alpha staker is a participant who holds a subnet's alpha by staking to a validator, while
effective stake is that validator's combined self-and-delegated total
([Staking into Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation#staking-into-subnets),
[Glossary: Effective Stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#effective-stake),
[Staking and delegation overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)).

- **Alpha staker** — holder role staking subnet alpha to a validator.
- **Effective stake** — combined self-and-delegated total behind that validator.

## Distinction from Delegate Stake

An alpha staker holds subnet alpha by delegating support to a validator. Delegate stake names only
the TAO the validator stakes personally on that hotkey
([Glossary: Delegate Stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#delegate-stake),
[Staking into Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation#staking-into-subnets)).

- **Alpha staker** — holder-side role after staking into a subnet.
- **Delegate stake** — validator-supplied TAO only.

## Distinction from Root Staker

Alpha staker and root staker describe different staking paths. Alpha staker names subnet alpha held
through subnet staking, while root staker names TAO staked through the root subnet path
([Glossary: Root Subnet/Subnet Zero](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#root-subnetsubnet-zero),
[Staking into Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation#staking-into-subnets)).

- **Alpha staker** — holder of subnet alpha through subnet staking.
- **Root staker** — holder of TAO staked through the root subnet path.

## Distinction from Dividends

Alpha staker names the role holding subnet alpha through staking to a validator. Dividends name
validator-side emissions Yuma Consensus produces before distribution to supporting stake
([Staking into Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation#staking-into-subnets),
[Yuma Consensus: Validator Emissions](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus#validator-emissions)).

- **Alpha staker** — participant holding subnet alpha by staking to a validator.
- **Dividends** — validator-side emissions shared through distribution.

Holder-role vocabulary and validator-outcome vocabulary answer different distribution questions
([Emission: Distribution](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions#distribution)).

## Distinction from Root Proportion

Alpha staker and root proportion describe different parts of a subnet's dividend context. Alpha
staker names the role on the subnet-alpha side, while root proportion measures how much of a
subnet's dividend context is attributed to TAO staked through the root path
([Glossary: Root Proportion](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#root-proportion),
[Staking into Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation#staking-into-subnets)).

- **Alpha staker** — who holds subnet alpha through staking on a mining subnet.
- **Root proportion** — how much of a subnet's dividend context is attributed to root-routed TAO.

## Distinction from Yuma Consensus

Alpha staker names the role for participants staking subnet alpha to a validator. Yuma Consensus
settles validator weight submissions into emission shares each tempo
([Glossary: Protocol Alpha](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#protocol-alpha),
[Yuma Consensus](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus)).

- **Alpha staker** — participant staking subnet alpha in tokenomics flows.
- **Yuma Consensus** — settlement turning validator weights into emission shares.

Staking-role vocabulary and settlement vocabulary answer different subnet reward layers
([Staking and Delegation](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)).

## Distinction from Unstaking

An alpha staker holds a subnet's alpha by staking to a validator; unstaking is the reverse action
that reduces or exits the position, withdrawing support and converting the held subnet alpha back to
TAO through the subnet's automated market maker
([Glossary: Unstaking](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#unstaking)).

- **Alpha staker** — the holder of an open subnet-alpha position.
- **Unstaking** — the action that reduces or exits it, returning TAO.

## Distinction from Slippage

An alpha staker holds a subnet-alpha position, while slippage is the trade-induced difference
between the expected and realized amount or price when value moves through the subnet's automated
market maker — a cost that can apply both when entering and when exiting that position
([Glossary: Slippage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#slippage)).

- **Alpha staker** — a participant who holds subnet alpha.
- **Slippage** — the expected-versus-realized gap incurred on the trades that adjust the position.

## Distinction from Stake

Alpha staker names the role holding subnet alpha through staking on a mining subnet. Stake names the
validator-associated amount after support exists in a subnet context
([Glossary: Stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#stake),
[Emission: Distribution](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions#distribution)).

- **Alpha staker** — role holding subnet alpha through staking.
- **Stake** — validator-associated amount behind a position.

Role vocabulary names who holds subnet alpha; stake vocabulary names committed support amount
([Staking and delegation overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)).

## Distinction from Multiple-Mechanism

Alpha staker names stake held inside one subnet context. Multiple incentive mechanisms add separate
evaluation tracks that should be read by mechanism rather than as one blended label
([Multiple Incentive Mechanisms Within Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-multiple-mech-subnets),
[Emission: Distribution](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions#distribution)).

- **Alpha staker** — subnet alpha held through staking on the subnet in scope.
- **Multiple mechanisms** — separate evaluation tracks within one netuid.

Role vocabulary names the staking position; mechanism-count vocabulary names evaluation scope
([Glossary: Multiple Incentive Mechanisms](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#multiple-incentive-mechanisms)).

## Reader Boundary

Alpha staker identifies a staker holding subnet alpha through staking in a specific subnet
([Staking into Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation#staking-into-subnets),
[Emission: Distribution](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions#distribution)).

When the focus is TAO staked through Subnet Zero, root staker is the more precise term. When the
focus is subnet alpha held through staking, alpha staker is the more precise term.
