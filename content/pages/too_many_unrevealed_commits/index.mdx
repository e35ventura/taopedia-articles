# Too Many Unrevealed Commits

`TooManyUnrevealedCommits` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised by commit-reveal weight
commit extrinsics. The standard-errors reference describes it as "Maximum commit limit reached"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#toomanyunrevealedcommits)).

## What It Means

When a commit-reveal weight commit is accepted, it is appended to a per-hotkey per-subnet queue of
pending reveals. The chain keeps at most ten non-expired commits in that queue at once. Before
adding a new commit, the extrinsic prunes any commits that have expired, then checks whether the
remaining queue is already at the limit; `TooManyUnrevealedCommits` is raised when it is, before the
new commit is added
([Subtensor source: weights.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/weights.rs),
[Commit-Reveal](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/commit-reveal)).

The limit applies per hotkey per subnet. A validator with ten pending unrevealed commits on one
subnet is not affected on any other subnet it participates in
([Subtensor source: weights.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/weights.rs)).

## Why It Appears

Because expired commits are pruned before the count is checked, a hotkey can reduce its pending
queue by letting commits age out; only the non-expired queue length is what the check enforces. A
validator that commits repeatedly for many epochs without revealing those commitments will
accumulate pending commits until this limit fires
([Subtensor source: weights.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/weights.rs)).

## Distinction from Committing Weights Too Fast

`TooManyUnrevealedCommits` and `CommittingWeightsTooFast` are both checked during the commit step of
commit-reveal weight submission, but they measure different things. `CommittingWeightsTooFast` is a
per-neuron rate limit on how quickly commits can be submitted in terms of blocks.
`TooManyUnrevealedCommits` counts how many non-expired commits are still pending in the queue,
regardless of timing
([Subtensor Standard Errors: CommittingWeightsTooFast](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#committingweightstoofast),
[Subtensor source: weights.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/weights.rs)).

- **CommittingWeightsTooFast** — commits are arriving faster than the per-neuron rate allows.
- **TooManyUnrevealedCommits** — too many commits are already pending reveal in the queue.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the queue-length check that raises it. It does
not describe how to reveal pending commits or how expired commits are pruned; those are documented
separately ([Commit-Reveal](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/commit-reveal)).
