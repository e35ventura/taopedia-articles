# Triumvirate

The Triumvirate is the Bittensor governance body associated with creating proposals. The
[Glossary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#triumvirate) defines it as a group
responsible for creating proposals, and the
[Governance Overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/governance) places that role before Senate
approval or rejection.

## Composition

The [Glossary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#triumvirate) identifies the
Triumvirate as a group of three Opentensor Foundation employees. The
[Senate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/governance/senate), by contrast, is a group of
[delegates](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#delegate) — validators that TAO
holders stake to — who control a significant portion of total network stake.

## Governance Role

In the governance flow, the Triumvirate originates proposals and the Senate reviews them. This split
keeps proposal creation separate from proposal approval. The
[Senate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/governance/senate) documentation provides the review-body
context for proposals after they are created.

## Reader Boundary

This term names a governance role, not a live membership list, proposal status page, or statement
that any proposal has been approved. Readers should use the official governance references for
proposal records and Senate review context.

## Relationship to Bicameral Legislature

The Triumvirate and bicameral legislature address related but different parts of Bittensor
governance vocabulary. Bicameral legislature names the two-body review structure comprising the
Triumvirate and the Senate, while the Triumvirate names the proposal-origin body inside that
structure. The
[Glossary: Bicameral Legislature](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#bicameral-legislature)
describes the two-tier approval system, and the
[Glossary: Triumvirate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#triumvirate) describes
the group responsible for creating proposals.

For readers, bicameral legislature answers how governance review is structured, while the
Triumvirate answers which body originates proposals inside that structure.

References:
[Glossary: Bicameral Legislature](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#bicameral-legislature),
[Glossary: Triumvirate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#triumvirate)

## Relationship to Senate

The Triumvirate and the Senate address related but different parts of Bittensor governance flow. The
Triumvirate creates proposals, while the Senate reviews and approves or rejects them. The
[Glossary: Triumvirate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#triumvirate) describes
proposal creation, and the
[Glossary: Senate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#senate) describes the review
body formed from elected delegates.

For readers, the Triumvirate names proposal origin, while the Senate names the approval body that
reviews those proposals before execution.

References: [Glossary: Triumvirate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#triumvirate),
[Glossary: Senate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#senate)

## Relationship to Senator

The Triumvirate and a senator address related but different governance roles. The Triumvirate
originates proposals, while a senator is the delegate role that votes on those proposals inside the
Senate. The [Glossary: Triumvirate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#triumvirate)
describes proposal creation, and the
[Governance Overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/governance) places senators on the Senate
review side of the governance flow.

For readers, the Triumvirate names the proposal-origin body, while a senator names the voting
delegate who acts on proposals after they are created.

References: [Glossary: Triumvirate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#triumvirate),
[Governance Overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/governance)

## Relationship to Proposal

The Triumvirate and a proposal address related but different parts of Bittensor governance
vocabulary. A proposal names the governance item containing privileged call data, while the
Triumvirate is the body that creates those proposals before Senate review. The
[Glossary: Proposal](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#proposal) describes the
governance item, and the [Governance Overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/governance) describes
Triumvirate proposal creation before Senate approval.

For readers, a proposal names the item under review, while the Triumvirate names the body that
originates that item in the governance flow.

References: [Glossary: Proposal](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#proposal),
[Governance Overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/governance)
