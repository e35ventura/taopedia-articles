# New Hotkey Is Same With Old

`NewHotKeyIsSameWithOld` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised by the hotkey swap
extrinsic. The standard-errors reference describes it as "The new hotkey is the same as old one"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#newhotkeyissamewithold)).

## What It Means

A hotkey swap replaces a coldkey's hotkey with a different hotkey, moving stake and registrations
over to the new key. `do_swap_hotkey` compares the submitted new hotkey against the old hotkey being
replaced, and raises `NewHotKeyIsSameWithOld` when the two are identical, since a swap to the same
key would not change anything
([Subtensor source: swap_hotkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_hotkey.rs)).

The comparison is a single equality check on the two submitted account values. It does not depend on
ownership, registration, or any other property of either key, only on whether they are the same
account
([Subtensor source: swap_hotkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_hotkey.rs)).

## Why It Appears

`do_swap_hotkey` runs this check after confirming the calling coldkey owns the old hotkey, and after
confirming the coldkey also owns the new hotkey when that new hotkey already exists on chain. Both
of those earlier checks can pass even when the two hotkeys are identical, so this separate equality
check is what specifically catches a same-key swap attempt
([Subtensor source: swap_hotkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_hotkey.rs)).

## Distinction from Non Associated Cold Key

`NewHotKeyIsSameWithOld` and `NonAssociatedColdKey` are both checked early in `do_swap_hotkey`, but
they guard different properties of the call. The ownership checks confirm the calling coldkey
actually controls the old hotkey, and the new hotkey if it already exists, and raise
`NonAssociatedColdKey` when that ownership is missing. `NewHotKeyIsSameWithOld` runs after those
ownership checks pass and catches the separate case where the submitted hotkeys are identical
([Non Associated Cold Key](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#nonassociatedcoldkey),
[Subtensor source: swap_hotkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_hotkey.rs)).

- **NonAssociatedColdKey** — the caller does not own the old or new hotkey.
- **NewHotKeyIsSameWithOld** — the new and old hotkeys are the same account.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the equality check that raises it. It does not
describe the rest of the hotkey swap validation sequence, such as the transaction rate limit or
per-subnet registration checks that run afterward; those are separate checks with their own error
labels
([Subtensor source: swap_hotkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_hotkey.rs)).
