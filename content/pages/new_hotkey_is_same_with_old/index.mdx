# New Hotkey Is Same With Old

`NewHotKeyIsSameWithOld` means a hotkey swap named the same account as both the source and
destination hotkey. The standard-errors reference describes it as "The new hotkey is the same as old
one"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#newhotkeyissamewithold)).

A [hotkey swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/coldkey-hotkey-security) moves subnet
participation from one hotkey to another under the same coldkey. The call must name two different
hotkey accounts for that move to mean anything
([Glossary: Hotkey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#hotkey)).

## What It Means

Hotkey swaps transfer registrations, stake routing, and related state from an old hotkey to a new
one. `NewHotKeyIsSameWithOld` is the early rejection when those two submitted addresses are
identical, so there is no replacement key to receive the swap
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#newhotkeyissamewithold),
[Hotkey Swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/coldkey-hotkey-security)).

This is an identity check on the submitted pair. The error does not, by itself, say anything about
coldkey ownership, swap fees, subnet registration, or root-side claim state on the hotkeys.

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a wallet or script submits a hotkey swap with matching old and new hotkey
fields. That can happen when a form reuses one address for both inputs, when automation copies the
current hotkey into the destination field by mistake, or when a retry resubmits the same pair
without changing the destination key
([Hotkey Swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/coldkey-hotkey-security)).

When debugging, rule out this identity failure before reading later swap rejections.
`NonAssociatedColdKey` means the signing coldkey does not own the old hotkey.
`HotKeySetTxRateLimitExceeded` means the coldkey retried the swap before the short transaction
cooldown cleared. `HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet` means the new hotkey already holds a UID on a
subnet included in the swap
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NonAssociatedColdKey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#nonassociatedcoldkey),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeySetTxRateLimitExceeded](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeysettxratelimitexceeded),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeyalreadyregisteredinsubnet)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the swap must name two different hotkey accounts.

## Distinction from New Hotkey Not Clean For Root Swap

`NewHotKeyIsSameWithOld` and `NewHotKeyNotCleanForRootSwap` both appear on hotkey swap calls, but
they reject different problems. `NewHotKeyIsSameWithOld` means the submitted old and new hotkeys are
the same account. `NewHotKeyNotCleanForRootSwap` means the replacement hotkey still carries root
claimable balance or root stake on a root-touching swap
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NewHotKeyNotCleanForRootSwap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#newhotkeynotcleanforrootswap)).

- **NewHotKeyIsSameWithOld** — the old and new hotkeys are the same account.
- **NewHotKeyNotCleanForRootSwap** — the new hotkey is not clear for a root-touching swap.

## Distinction from Hotkey Already Registered In Subnet

`NewHotKeyIsSameWithOld` and `HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet` both involve the replacement hotkey
on a swap path, but at different stages. `NewHotKeyIsSameWithOld` is the identity check that fails
when no replacement account was provided. `HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet` means the new hotkey
already has a UID on a subnet the swap would move into
([Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeyalreadyregisteredinsubnet)).

- **NewHotKeyIsSameWithOld** — the old and new hotkeys are identical.
- **HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet** — the new hotkey already has a UID on the subnet.

## Distinction from Non Associated Cold Key

`NewHotKeyIsSameWithOld` and `NonAssociatedColdKey` are both early hotkey swap rejections, but they
test different inputs. `NonAssociatedColdKey` means the signing coldkey does not own the old hotkey
named in the swap. `NewHotKeyIsSameWithOld` means the old and new hotkey addresses match
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NonAssociatedColdKey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#nonassociatedcoldkey)).

- **NewHotKeyIsSameWithOld** — the submitted hotkeys are the same account.
- **NonAssociatedColdKey** — the coldkey does not own the old hotkey.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the same-address check that raises it. It does
not describe swap fees, per-subnet cooldowns, or the full hotkey rotation workflow; those are
documented separately ([Hotkey Swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/coldkey-hotkey-security),
[Chain Rate Limits: Hotkey swap rate limit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits#hotkey-swap-rate-limit)).
