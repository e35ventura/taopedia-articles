# Contribution Too Low

`ContributionTooLow` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised by the `contribute` extrinsic
in the crowdloan pallet. The Subtensor API error reference describes it as "The contribution is too
low"
([Subtensor API: Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#contributiontoolow)).

## What It Means

Each [crowdloan](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans) sets a minimum contribution at
creation. The `contribute` extrinsic compares the submitted amount against that minimum and raises
`ContributionTooLow` when the submitted amount is below it
([Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

The minimum is per crowdloan, not a network-wide constant, so the threshold a contribution must
clear depends on the specific crowdloan being contributed to
([Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

## Why It Appears

This check runs after confirming the contribution deadline has not passed and the funding cap has
not been reached, but before the contributor count limit is checked. A contribution that clears the
deadline and cap checks but is below the crowdloan's own minimum amount fails here
([Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

## Distinction from Cap Raised

`ContributionTooLow` and `CapRaised` both fire during the `contribute` call, but they check
different conditions. `CapRaised` fires because the crowdloan has already collected its full funding
target. `ContributionTooLow` fires because the submitted amount is individually too small,
regardless of how much total funding has been raised
([Subtensor Standard Errors: CapRaised](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#capraised),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

- **CapRaised** — the crowdloan's total funding cap has been reached.
- **ContributionTooLow** — the individual contribution amount is below the minimum.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the minimum-amount check that raises it. It
does not describe how crowdloan minimums are set or the rest of the contribution flow; those are
documented separately ([Crowdloans](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans)).
