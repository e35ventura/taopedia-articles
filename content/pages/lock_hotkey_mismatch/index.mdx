# Lock Hotkey Mismatch

`LockHotkeyMismatch` means a conviction-lock call used a hotkey that does not match the hotkey
already tied to the coldkey's lock on that subnet. The runtime compares the submitted hotkey against
the one stored with the existing lock and returns this error when they differ
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs),
[Conviction and locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).

## What It Means

Conviction locks bind part of a coldkey's subnet alpha to a specific hotkey and a commitment signal
([Conviction and locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).

On the lock-stake path, a coldkey may create a new lock or add alpha to an existing one. When a lock
already exists on the subnet, the call must target the same hotkey that lock is bound to.
`LockHotkeyMismatch` means a different hotkey was submitted
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

The same hotkey-binding rule applies when locked alpha moves between coldkeys on the same subnet. If
the destination coldkey already has a lock, the transfer can carry locked mass only when that lock
points at the same hotkey as the source
([Conviction and locked stake: Transferring locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking#transferring-locked-stake),
[Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a lock-stake call tops up or continues a lock on the wrong hotkey, or when a
stake transfer would move locked alpha to a destination coldkey whose existing lock is bound to
another hotkey
([Conviction and locked stake: Transferring locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking#transferring-locked-stake),
[Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

When debugging, compare against other lock-related rejections on the same call.
`InsufficientStakeForLock` means total staked alpha on the subnet is below the lock amount.
`AccountRejectsLockedAlpha` means the destination coldkey has not opted in to receive incoming
locked alpha. `StakeUnavailable` means the source coldkey cannot free enough unlocked alpha for the
request
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the hotkey named in the call does not match the lock's bound
hotkey.

## Distinction from Insufficient Stake For Lock

`LockHotkeyMismatch` and `InsufficientStakeForLock` are both raised on the lock-stake path, but they
test different conditions. `InsufficientStakeForLock` means the coldkey does not hold enough total
alpha on the subnet to cover the lock request. `LockHotkeyMismatch` means the hotkey binding is
wrong even if balance might otherwise be sufficient
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

- **LockHotkeyMismatch** — the submitted hotkey does not match the existing lock.
- **InsufficientStakeForLock** — total subnet alpha is below the lock request.

## Distinction from Account Rejects Locked Alpha

`LockHotkeyMismatch` and `AccountRejectsLockedAlpha` both block same-subnet transfers that carry
lock state, but at different checks. `AccountRejectsLockedAlpha` means the destination coldkey
rejects incoming locked alpha. `LockHotkeyMismatch` means the destination already has a lock tied to
a different hotkey than the source
([Conviction and locked stake: Transferring locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking#transferring-locked-stake),
[Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

- **AccountRejectsLockedAlpha** — the destination rejects incoming locked alpha.
- **LockHotkeyMismatch** — the destination lock points at a different hotkey.

## Distinction from Hotkey Account Not Exists

`LockHotkeyMismatch` and `HotKeyAccountNotExists` both involve hotkey validity on lock paths, but at
different stages. `HotKeyAccountNotExists` means the named hotkey account is absent from chain
state. `LockHotkeyMismatch` means the hotkey exists but is not the one already bound to the
coldkey's lock on the subnet
([Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyAccountNotExists](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#hotkeyaccountnotexists),
[Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

- **HotKeyAccountNotExists** — the hotkey account does not exist.
- **LockHotkeyMismatch** — the hotkey does not match the existing lock binding.

## Distinction from Stake Unavailable

`LockHotkeyMismatch` and `StakeUnavailable` both appear around locked stake moves, but they name
different problems. `StakeUnavailable` means the source coldkey cannot supply enough unlocked alpha
for the request. `LockHotkeyMismatch` means locked mass cannot follow the transfer because the
destination lock is bound to another hotkey
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

- **StakeUnavailable** — the source lacks enough unlocked alpha for the request.
- **LockHotkeyMismatch** — the destination lock is tied to a different hotkey.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the hotkey-binding check on conviction-lock
paths. It does not report which hotkey a coldkey's live lock is bound to on any subnet; that depends
on chain state at observation time
([Conviction and locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).
