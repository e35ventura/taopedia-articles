Nodexo is Bittensor Subnet 106. Its on-chain identity lists the subnet name as Nodexo, describes the
subnet as "tokenized ai compute," and points to [nodexo.ai](https://nodexo.ai) as the project
website. The subnet is operated by NeuralInternet, who also operate [Subnet 27](/subnet_27), a
separate Bittensor compute subnet also named Nodexo.

The on-chain GitHub field references `neuralinternet/SN27`, which is not publicly accessible at the
time of writing. This article therefore relies on on-chain identity fields and publicly available
NeuralInternet materials.

## What Nodexo Provides

The on-chain description frames SN106 as tokenized AI compute. NeuralInternet's broader work, as
reflected in public repositories, centers on open monitoring and tooling for Bittensor compute
subnets. The `SN27-opencompute` repository describes open metrics, dashboards, and tools for
monitoring and analyzing compute subnet activity on Bittensor.

Within the Bittensor framework, a compute subnet of this kind turns contributed hardware into a
rewarded network service. Miners supply compute capacity, validators assess whether that capacity is
genuine and useful, and [[Yuma Consensus]] converts those assessments into on-chain weights and
emissions.

## Participating as a Miner

Because the referenced GitHub repository is not publicly accessible, specific miner setup
instructions cannot be verified from primary sources. Prospective participants should consult
[nodexo.ai](https://nodexo.ai) or the NeuralInternet Discord for current guidance.

In general Bittensor compute subnets, miners register a hotkey on the subnet, expose compute
resources to the network, and respond to validator challenges designed to confirm that declared
capacity is real and performant. Reward flows to miners whose resources are confirmed as available
and valuable by the validator set.

## Participating as a Validator

Without access to the subnet codebase, the exact scoring methodology cannot be independently
verified. In the standard Bittensor compute model, validators issue challenges to miners, measure
response quality and latency, and assign weights that determine how emissions are distributed.
Prospective validators should check [nodexo.ai](https://nodexo.ai) for current requirements.
