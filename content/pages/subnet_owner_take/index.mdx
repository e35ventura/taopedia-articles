# Subnet Owner Take

Subnet owner take, which the documentation also calls the owner cut, is the share of a subnet's
alpha emissions that goes to the subnet's owner. Documentation states that the ratio of all subnet
alpha emissions given to the subnet owner as stake is 18 percent, applied globally across subnets
([Emission](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions)).

## What It Is

Each subnet continuously emits alpha to its participants. The owner take is the slice of that
emission reserved for whoever owns the subnet, set aside before the remainder flows to validators,
miners, and stakers through consensus. It is received as stake rather than as a liquid payout, so it
accrues to the owner's position on the subnet
([Emission](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions)).

## The Documented Share

The documentation gives the owner take as 18 percent and describes it as a global, fixed value
rather than a per-subnet setting an owner chooses. Because it is expressed as a share of emissions,
the actual amount an owner receives still depends on how much that subnet emits, which varies with
subnet performance over time ([Emission](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions)).

## Distinction from Validator Take

Owner take is the subnet creator's documented emission share, while validator take is a separate
configurable cut a validator or delegate keeps from rewards of those who stake to it
([Emission](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions),
[Glossary: Validator Take %](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-take-)).

Owner take funds the subnet creator from the documented emission split. Validator take is a
configurable commission a validator keeps from delegated-stake rewards.

- **Subnet owner take** — documented owner share of subnet alpha emissions.
- **Validator take** — configurable commission on delegated-stake rewards.

## Distinction from Tempo

Subnet owner take is the documented owner share allocated when alpha is distributed at tempo end.
Tempo is the subnet's recurring reward interval that triggers that distribution
([Emission](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions),
[Glossary: Tempo](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#tempo),
[Emission: Distribution](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions#distribution)).

Alpha may accumulate over the tempo. The owner cut is allocated when distribution runs according to
the documented split proportions.

- **Subnet owner take** — documented owner share of distributed alpha.
- **Tempo** — the recurring interval when alpha distribution runs.

## Distinction from Emission Injection

Owner take names how much of distributed alpha the subnet owner receives. Emission injection is the
earlier step that adds alpha to each subnet's pools every block
([Emission](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions),
[Glossary: Emission](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#emission)).

Injection adds alpha over time. Owner take describes the owner's share when the documented 18-41-41
split applies at tempo distribution.

- **Subnet owner take** — owner share when alpha is distributed at tempo end.
- **Emission injection** — per-block alpha added to subnet pools before distribution.

## Distinction from Crowdloans

Crowdloans name shared subnet ownership, while subnet owner take names the documented owner share of
subnet alpha at tempo distribution on netuid 1 and other subnets
([Emission](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions),
[Crowdloans](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans)).

When ownership is shared through crowdloans, the same owner take percentage is divided among
contributors rather than paid to one owner.

- **Crowdloans** — shared subnet ownership with proportional owner-cut splits.
- **Subnet owner take** — documented owner share of subnet alpha at tempo distribution.

## Subnet Context

Owner take applies per subnet, for example netuid 1, since each subnet emits its own alpha and has
its own owner. The share itself is the documented protocol value, while the resulting owner
emissions on any subnet are live chain state
([Emission](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions)).

## Chain Reads for netuid 1

The documented 18-41-41 split, including the 18 percent owner take, is illustrated on netuid 1
throughout this article. Readers can verify live subnet state for that example with
`btcli subnets metagraph --netuid 1 --network finney` or
[TaoStats netuid 1](https://taostats.io/subnets/1)
([Subnet Metagraph](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/metagraph),
[Emission: Distribution](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions#distribution)).

That read path keeps live owner-take examples tied to a parseable netuid. Statements about tempo-end
owner stake on netuid 1 should be checked against the metagraph and emission documentation for
netuid 1 rather than inferred from the documented percentage alone.

## Development Stage Context

The
[Introduction to Bittensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/introduction#subnet-development)
describes subnet development as moving from localnet to testnet and then mainnet. For subnet owner
take on a subnet such as netuid 1, that sequence changes how readers should interpret documented
18-41-41 distribution examples and tempo-end owner stake credits.

In localnet, owner-take examples can be exercised in an isolated environment. Local tempo
distribution and owner-stake outcomes reflect local chain configuration rather than production
subnet emissions.

On testnet, owner-take behavior can be observed in a shared, non-production network. Testnet alpha
distribution and owner stake context are separate from mainnet subnet state
([Emission](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions)).

On mainnet, subnet owner take is the live documented owner share of subnet alpha distributed at
tempo end on the production Bittensor network
([Emission: Distribution](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions#distribution)).

The [Bittensor Networks](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/bittensor-networks) reference
separates mainnet, testnet, and localnet. An owner-take example from one environment should not be
read as representing production owner emissions on another network.

## Distinction from Rank

Subnet owner take is the documented owner share carved from the tempo emission split. Rank is how
Yuma Consensus divides the miner bucket among miners after validator weights are aggregated
([Emission: Distribution](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions#distribution),
[Glossary: Rank](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#rank),
[Yuma Consensus](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus)).

Owner take goes to the subnet owner before miner ranking runs. Rank subdivides the miner share, not
the owner bucket.

- **Subnet owner take** — documented owner share allocated at tempo distribution.
- **Rank** — consensus standing that divides the miner emission bucket.

## Distinction from Validator Dividend

Subnet owner take names the documented owner allocation before validator and miner buckets are
subdivided on netuid 1, while validator dividend names the validator-side emission result from
consensus evaluation ([Emission](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions),
[Emission: Distribution](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions#distribution)).

Owner take is a documented protocol bucket allocated at distribution. Validator dividend is the
validator-side emission earned through consensus evaluation.

- **Subnet owner take** — documented owner share allocated at tempo distribution.
- **Validator dividend** — validator-side emission earned through consensus evaluation.

## Distinction from Child Hotkeys

On a subnet such as netuid 1, a child hotkey is an optional validation role: a delegate re-delegates
a portion of its stake to that hotkey, which then earns a childkey take on the re-delegated stake.
Subnet owner take is instead the documented protocol share of that subnet's alpha emission allocated
to its owner, given as 18 percent within the documented 18-41-41 split on a subnet such as netuid 1
([Child Hotkeys](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys),
[Emission: Distribution](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions#distribution)).

The two are credited through different paths. A childkey take arises when stake is re-delegated to a
hotkey for validation, while the owner take is part of that documented tempo-end emission split
([Delegation](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)).

- **Child hotkey** — an optional validating key earning a childkey take on re-delegated stake.
- **Subnet owner take** — the documented protocol share of a subnet's emission paid to its owner.

## Distinction from Yuma Consensus

Subnet owner take names the documented owner share carved from a subnet's emission split before
validator and miner buckets are subdivided. Yuma Consensus is the separate mechanism that converts
validator weight submissions into the miner and validator portions of that split each tempo
([Yuma Consensus](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus),
[Emission](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions)).

- **Subnet owner take** — documented owner allocation from the tempo emission split.
- **Yuma Consensus** — mechanism that turns validator weights into miner and validator shares.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the concept at a high level. It does not report the owner take actually accruing
on any particular subnet or name a specific owner. Those are live chain state and should be checked
for the relevant netuid. The 18 percent figure is the documented value and is the protocol's stated
owner share at the time of writing ([Emission](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions)).
