# Non Associated Cold Key

`NonAssociatedColdKey` means the signing coldkey is not the owner of the hotkey required by the
executed path. The standard-errors reference describes it as "Request made by a coldkey that is not
associated with the hotkey account"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#nonassociatedcoldkey)).

The runtime raises this error on several call families that require coldkey ownership of a hotkey,
including registration ownership checks, hotkey-swap ownership checks, and child-key scheduling
ownership checks
([Subtensor source: registration.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/registration.rs),
[Subtensor source: swap_hotkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_hotkey.rs),
[Subtensor source: set_children.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/set_children.rs)).

## What It Means

This error is about ownership, not about funds or timing. In Bittensor, hotkeys are associated with
owning coldkeys, and runtime paths can require that ownership before proceeding. Where the code
calls `coldkey_owns_hotkey`, the path rejects with `NonAssociatedColdKey` when that relationship is
false ([Hotkey and Coldkey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/getting-started/wallets),
[Subtensor source: registration.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/registration.rs),
[Subtensor source: swap_hotkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_hotkey.rs)).

So when this error appears, the immediate meaning is narrow: for that specific path, the signer does
not own the required hotkey. It does not, by itself, say anything about how much balance or stake
the account holds
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#nonassociatedcoldkey),
[Glossary: Coldkey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#coldkey)).

## Why It Appears

The wallet model in Bittensor pairs a coldkey with the hotkeys it owns, and multiple runtime paths
enforce that pairing. In `do_register`, the check runs before capacity logic. In `do_swap_hotkey`,
it applies both to owning the old hotkey and, when the new hotkey already exists, to owning that new
hotkey. In `do_schedule_children`, it ensures the caller owns the parent hotkey before child
schedule updates
([Subtensor source: registration.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/registration.rs),
[Subtensor source: swap_hotkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_hotkey.rs),
[Subtensor source: set_children.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/set_children.rs)).

In practice this surfaces when a call uses the wrong coldkey-hotkey pairing, or when swap and child
operations reference hotkeys not owned by the signer. Check ownership pairing before balance or
rate-limit hypotheses because the runtime rejects ownership mismatch first on those guarded paths
([Hotkey and Coldkey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/getting-started/wallets),
[Subtensor source: registration.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/registration.rs),
[Subtensor source: swap_hotkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_hotkey.rs)).

## Distinction from Hot Key Account Not Exists

`NonAssociatedColdKey` and `HotKeyAccountNotExists` are different checks. `HotKeyAccountNotExists`
means the hotkey account does not exist. `NonAssociatedColdKey` means the hotkey exists but the
signer does not own it for the guarded path
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeyaccountnotexists),
[Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#nonassociatedcoldkey),
[Subtensor source: swap_hotkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_hotkey.rs)).

- **NonAssociatedColdKey** — the hotkey exists, but this coldkey is not its owner.
- **HotKeyAccountNotExists** — the hotkey is not present on chain at all.

## Distinction from Not Enough Balance To Stake

`NonAssociatedColdKey` is an ownership check, while `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` is a funds check. The
standard-errors reference describes `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` as not enough balance in the coldkey
account when requesting more stake than exists there, so it concerns the amount, not the ownership
relationship
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#nonassociatedcoldkey)).

- **NonAssociatedColdKey** — wrong coldkey for the hotkey.
- **NotEnoughBalanceToStake** — right coldkey, but insufficient coldkey balance.

## Distinction from Hot Key Already Registered In Subnet

`NonAssociatedColdKey` and `HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet` both appear on registration and swap
paths, but they represent different states. `NonAssociatedColdKey` means signer ownership fails.
`HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet` means the targeted hotkey is already present on that subnet
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#nonassociatedcoldkey),
[Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeyalreadyregisteredinsubnet),
[Subtensor source: registration.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/registration.rs),
[Subtensor source: swap_hotkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_hotkey.rs)).

- **NonAssociatedColdKey** — signer does not own the targeted hotkey.
- **HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet** — hotkey is already present on that subnet.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and its ownership-guard semantics across relevant
paths. It does not tell a reader which coldkey owns a given hotkey on a live network; that depends
on current chain state for those accounts
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#nonassociatedcoldkey),
[Hotkey and Coldkey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/getting-started/wallets)).
