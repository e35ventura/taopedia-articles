# Not Enough Balance To Pay Swap Cold Key

`NotEnoughBalanceToPaySwapColdKey` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised when a coldkey
swap cannot collect its required swap cost from the account
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetopayswapcoldkey)).

## What It Means

Coldkey swap logic charges a swap cost before the rest of the coldkey-transfer work can proceed. The
runtime helper `charge_swap_cost` calls `recycle_tao` and maps a failed charge to
`NotEnoughBalanceToPaySwapColdKey`, so the swap stops at the cost-payment step instead of continuing
into the later state transfers
([Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the account cannot pay the required swap charge while preserving
the minimum balance rules used by the runtime
([Subtensor test: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/tests/swap_coldkey.rs)).

## Why It Appears

Current test coverage shows this error in both coldkey-swap entry points that collect the swap cost.
`announce_coldkey_swap` returns `NotEnoughBalanceToPaySwapColdKey` when the account has no balance
or only enough balance to fall below the existential-deposit requirement after the charge, and
`swap_coldkey` fails for the same reason during the charged swap path
([Subtensor test: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/tests/swap_coldkey.rs),
[Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs)).

For a reader, the useful takeaway is that this error is about paying the coldkey-swap fee, not about
whether the destination coldkey is valid
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetopayswapcoldkey)).

## Distinction from Cold Key Already Associated

`NotEnoughBalanceToPaySwapColdKey` and `ColdKeyAlreadyAssociated` can both stop a coldkey swap, but
they fail at different checks. `NotEnoughBalanceToPaySwapColdKey` means the account cannot fund the
swap charge. `ColdKeyAlreadyAssociated` means the chosen destination coldkey already has staking
hotkeys associated with it
([Subtensor Standard Errors: ColdKeyAlreadyAssociated](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#coldkeyalreadyassociated),
[Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs)).

- `NotEnoughBalanceToPaySwapColdKey` means the swap cost cannot be paid safely.
- `ColdKeyAlreadyAssociated` means the destination coldkey is already associated with staking
  hotkeys.

## Distinction from New Cold Key Is Hotkey

`NotEnoughBalanceToPaySwapColdKey` and `NewColdKeyIsHotkey` also block different parts of the same
coldkey-swap flow. `NotEnoughBalanceToPaySwapColdKey` is a balance check on paying the swap charge.
`NewColdKeyIsHotkey` is a destination-role check that rejects a target account already acting as a
hotkey
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NewColdKeyIsHotkey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#newcoldkeyishotkey),
[Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs)).

- `NotEnoughBalanceToPaySwapColdKey` means the cost payment fails.
- `NewColdKeyIsHotkey` means the destination account is already a hotkey.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the swap-cost check behind it. It does not
document the full coldkey-swap procedure or every transfer that happens after the cost is paid;
those are separate parts of the coldkey-swap flow
([Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs)).
