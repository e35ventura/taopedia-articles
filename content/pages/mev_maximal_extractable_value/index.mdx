# MEV (Maximal Extractable Value)

MEV, or maximal extractable value, is profit that can be extracted by reordering, inserting, or
censoring transactions within a block. The official
[Glossary: MEV](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#mev-maximal-extractable-value)
uses this label for blockchain networks generally and connects the idea to visible pending
transactions before block inclusion.

## Mempool Visibility Context

MEV risk begins while transactions are still pending. The
[Glossary: Mempool](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#mempool) describes the
mempool as the temporary holding area where unconfirmed transactions sit before inclusion in a
block. When pending transactions are visible, other actors may try to gain an ordering advantage
around them.

For readers, MEV is therefore a transaction-ordering risk term, not a subnet miner score, a
validator permit label, or a wallet file location.

References: [Mempool](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#mempool),
[Mempool Visibility](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#mempool-visibility)

## Staking and Unstaking Context

Bittensor documentation also places MEV vocabulary near staking activity. The MEV glossary entry
notes relevance to staking and unstaking because pending transaction details can affect token
movement and price execution before inclusion. That makes MEV part of the safety vocabulary around
financial operations, not part of incentive-mechanism design by itself.

This article does not describe how to submit transactions, change mempool ordering, or exploit
pending activity. Those topics belong outside Taopedia concept articles.

References:
[Staking and delegation overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation),
[Glossary: MEV](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#mev-maximal-extractable-value)

## Contrast with MEV Shield

MEV names the extractable-value risk, while MEV Shield names a Bittensor mitigation feature. The
[MEV Shield](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/mev-shield/) documentation describes encrypted
mempool protection for sensitive transactions whose details should not be visible while pending.

For readers, the useful distinction is risk versus mitigation: MEV is the concept term for ordering
advantage around pending transactions, and MEV Shield is one official Bittensor feature aimed at
reducing that visibility risk.

## Contrast with Mempool Labels

Mempool and mempool visibility describe where and how pending transactions become visible. MEV
describes what ordering advantage may be extracted from that visibility. Keeping those labels
separate prevents a mempool definition from being read as a profit estimate or a product
recommendation.

## Relationship to Multiple Mechanisms

MEV belongs to transaction safety vocabulary before block inclusion. The
[Glossary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#multiple-incentive-mechanisms) notes
that subnets can run more than one incentive mechanism.

For readers, MEV still names a pre-inclusion transaction-ordering risk. Subnet mechanism count
belongs with subnet incentive design, not with the MEV term itself.

References:
[Multiple Incentive Mechanisms Within Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-multiple-mech-subnets),
[Glossary: Multiple Incentive Mechanisms](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#multiple-incentive-mechanisms)

## Reader Boundary

MEV should not be read as a live profit report, an instruction to monitor mempools, or a substitute
for official wallet safety guidance. It is a concept term for extractable value tied to transaction
ordering before block inclusion.
