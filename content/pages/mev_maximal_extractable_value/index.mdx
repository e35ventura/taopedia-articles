# MEV (Maximal Extractable Value)

MEV, short for Maximal Extractable Value, is the value that can be extracted by controlling or
observing the order of transactions before they are included in a block
([Glossary: MEV](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#mev-maximal-extractable-value)).

In Bittensor, MEV risk arises when pending transactions in the mempool expose enough information for
another party to profit by acting ahead of or around them. This is most relevant for subnet pool
operations where transaction contents can affect pool prices.

## Mempool Visibility

MEV opportunities begin with mempool visibility. When a transaction is pending but not yet included
in a block, its contents may be readable. A party observing those contents can act on that
information before the original transaction settles
([Glossary: MEV](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#mev-maximal-extractable-value)).

This is the root of MEV risk in Bittensor. The value is not created by the attacking party — it is
transferred from the original transaction's sender to whoever acts on the ordering opportunity.

## MEV Shield Relationship

MEV Shield is Bittensor's encrypted mempool-protection mechanism. It addresses MEV risk by hiding
sensitive transaction details before block inclusion, reducing the information available to parties
looking for ordering opportunities
([Glossary: MEV](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#mev-maximal-extractable-value)).

MEV names the problem; MEV Shield names one mechanism designed to reduce that problem. The two terms
are related but distinct: understanding MEV explains why MEV Shield exists.

## Pool Context

MEV risk is most discussed in the context of subnet pool operations such as staking and unstaking,
where transaction ordering can affect pool prices. A transaction that moves a pool price creates an
opportunity for someone observing the pending transaction to act first
([Understanding Slippage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/slippage)).

Price protection is a related tool that allows callers to set bounds on acceptable execution rates
for pool operations ([Price Protection](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/price-protection)).

## Reader Boundary

MEV is concept vocabulary for value extraction through transaction ordering. It should not be read
as a consensus attack, a validator scoring issue, or a description of normal pool slippage
([Glossary: MEV](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#mev-maximal-extractable-value)).

The MEV concept applies most precisely to contexts where transaction order is visible before
finalization. It is less relevant for operations that do not involve pool pricing or mempool
exposure.

## Relationship to Mempool

MEV and the mempool are related but different parts of Bittensor transaction vocabulary. The mempool
names the temporary holding area for pending transactions before block inclusion, while MEV names
value extractable by observing or manipulating transaction ordering before inclusion. The
[Glossary: Mempool](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#mempool) describes pending
transactions before confirmation, and the
[Glossary: MEV](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#mev-maximal-extractable-value)
defines maximal extractable value from ordering opportunities.

For readers, the mempool names the pre-inclusion waiting stage, while MEV names profit sought from
ordering advantage in that stage.

References: [Glossary: Mempool](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#mempool),
[Glossary: MEV](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#mev-maximal-extractable-value)

## Relationship to MEV Shield

MEV and MEV Shield are related but different parts of Bittensor transaction-protection vocabulary.
MEV names value extractable through transaction ordering manipulation, while MEV Shield names
Bittensor's encrypted mempool-protection mechanism that hides sensitive transaction details before
block inclusion. The
[Glossary: MEV](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#mev-maximal-extractable-value)
defines the ordering-extraction problem, and
[MEV Shield](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/mev-shield/) describes encryption of sensitive
transaction contents during the pre-inclusion wait.

For readers, MEV names the ordering-extraction risk, while MEV Shield names one official response to
sensitive-detail exposure before inclusion.

References:
[Glossary: MEV](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#mev-maximal-extractable-value),
[MEV Shield](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/mev-shield/)

## Relationship to Extrinsics

MEV and extrinsics are related but different parts of Bittensor chain action vocabulary. Extrinsics
name signed transactions and calls submitted to Subtensor, while MEV names value that can be
extracted when those pending submissions are visible and reorderable before inclusion. The
[Glossary: Extrinsics](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#extrinsics) describes
signed chain submissions, and the
[Glossary: MEV](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#mev-maximal-extractable-value)
defines maximal extractable value from ordering opportunities.

For readers, extrinsics name submitted chain actions, while MEV names profit sought from ordering
advantage over visible pending actions.

References: [Glossary: Extrinsics](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#extrinsics),
[Glossary: MEV](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#mev-maximal-extractable-value)

## Relationship to Subtensor

MEV and Subtensor are related but different parts of Bittensor chain vocabulary. Subtensor names
Bittensor's layer 1 blockchain and system of record, while MEV names value extractable from
transaction ordering on that chain layer before pending actions are included in blocks. The
[Glossary: Subtensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#subtensor) describes the
blockchain layer, and the
[Glossary: MEV](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#mev-maximal-extractable-value)
defines maximal extractable value in mempool and pool-operation context.

For readers, Subtensor names the chain layer, while MEV names an ordering-extraction concern on
pending activity within that layer.

References: [Glossary: Subtensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#subtensor),
[Glossary: MEV](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#mev-maximal-extractable-value)
