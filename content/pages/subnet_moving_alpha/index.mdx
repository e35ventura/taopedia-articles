# Subnet Moving Alpha

Subnet moving alpha is a single global coefficient, stored on chain as `SubnetMovingAlpha`, that
caps how quickly a subnet's moving price can track its spot price. It is not a per-subnet value: one
`SubnetMovingAlpha` applies across the whole network
([Subtensor source: pallets/subtensor/src/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/lib.rs)).

## What It Controls

Each subnet keeps a moving price, an exponential moving average (EMA) of its alpha price that the
runtime uses to weight how block emissions are split across subnets. `SubnetMovingAlpha` is the
smoothing coefficient in that EMA: a larger value lets a subnet's moving price follow its spot price
more quickly, while a smaller value makes the moving price slower and steadier, so emission shares
react less to short-term price swings
([Subtensor source: staking/stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

## How It Enters the EMA

When the runtime updates a subnet's moving price, it does not apply `SubnetMovingAlpha` directly.
The effective smoothing weight is `SubnetMovingAlpha` scaled by the subnet's age, computed as
`blocks_since_start_call / (blocks_since_start_call + halving_time)`, where `halving_time` is the
subnet's `EMAPriceHalvingBlocks`. So the weight starts near zero when a subnet begins and rises
toward `SubnetMovingAlpha` as the subnet ages
([Subtensor source: staking/stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

Because the ceiling is global while the age ramp is per-subnet, `SubnetMovingAlpha` sets the same
upper bound on tracking speed for every subnet, and each subnet approaches it on its own schedule
([Subtensor source: staking/stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

## Default and Setter

The runtime default is `0.000003`, chosen so that a subnet's moving price reaches about half of a
price change in roughly thirty days and about ninety percent in roughly three and a half months. The
value is a single network-wide setting changed only by the root origin through the
`sudo_set_subnet_moving_alpha` extrinsic
([Subtensor source: pallets/subtensor/src/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/lib.rs),
[Subtensor source: admin-utils/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/admin-utils/src/lib.rs)).

## Distinction from EMA Price Halving Period

`SubnetMovingAlpha` and the EMA price halving period (`EMAPriceHalvingBlocks`) both shape the moving
price EMA, but at different scopes. `SubnetMovingAlpha` is the single global ceiling on the
smoothing weight. `EMAPriceHalvingBlocks` is a per-subnet setting that controls how quickly a
subnet's smoothing weight ramps up toward that ceiling
([Subtensor source: staking/stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

- **Subnet moving alpha** — the global ceiling on how fast any moving price can track spot price.
- **EMA price halving period** — the per-subnet ramp speed toward that ceiling.
