# No Existing Lock

`NoExistingLock` means a move-lock call arrived when the signing coldkey had no conviction lock on
the submitted subnet to reassign. The runtime looks up the coldkey's lock state on that netuid and
returns this error when none is present
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs),
[Conviction and locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).

## What It Means

Conviction locks bind part of a coldkey's subnet alpha to a hotkey and a commitment signal
([Conviction and locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).

A move-lock call reassigns an existing lock on one subnet from its current hotkey to a destination
hotkey. The locked alpha mass on that netuid is conserved across the move. `NoExistingLock` means
the coldkey had no lock on the submitted subnet for the runtime to move
([Conviction and locked stake: Moving a lock](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking#moving-a-lock),
[Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

This is a missing-lock rejection on the move-lock path, not proof that the coldkey lacks staked
alpha on the subnet or that the destination hotkey is invalid.

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a coldkey signs a move-lock call for a netuid where it has not created a
conviction lock. That can happen when automation targets the wrong subnet, when a lock was already
moved or fully unlocked, or when the client still assumes a lock exists after chain state changed
([Conviction and locked stake: Moving a lock](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking#moving-a-lock),
[Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

When debugging, compare against other lock-path rejections on related calls. `LockHotkeyMismatch`
means a lock-stake top-up or locked transfer used the wrong hotkey for an existing lock.
`HotKeyAccountNotExists` means the destination hotkey account is absent. `InsufficientStakeForLock`
means a lock-stake request named more alpha than the coldkey holds on the subnet
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

The practical meaning is narrow: no conviction lock exists on the subnet for this coldkey to move.

## Distinction from Lock Hotkey Mismatch

`NoExistingLock` and `LockHotkeyMismatch` both involve conviction locks, but at different stages.
`NoExistingLock` means there is no lock on the subnet to move at all. `LockHotkeyMismatch` means a
lock exists but the submitted hotkey does not match the one it is bound to
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

- **NoExistingLock** — no lock exists on the subnet for this coldkey.
- **LockHotkeyMismatch** — the submitted hotkey does not match the existing lock.

## Distinction from Insufficient Stake For Lock

`NoExistingLock` and `InsufficientStakeForLock` both appear on conviction-lock paths, but on
different operations. `InsufficientStakeForLock` is raised when a lock-stake call tries to create or
increase a lock without enough total alpha on the subnet. `NoExistingLock` is raised when a
move-lock call finds no existing lock to reassign
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

- **NoExistingLock** — no lock exists to move on the subnet.
- **InsufficientStakeForLock** — total subnet alpha is below the lock-stake request.

## Distinction from Active Lock Exists

`NoExistingLock` and `ActiveLockExists` are opposite lock-presence failures on different paths.
`NoExistingLock` means a move-lock call found no lock to move. `ActiveLockExists` means a coldkey
swap or similar path found an active lock that must be cleared first
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

- **NoExistingLock** — no lock exists on the subnet for the move-lock call.
- **ActiveLockExists** — an active lock blocks the requested operation.

## Distinction from Hotkey Account Not Exists

`NoExistingLock` and `HotKeyAccountNotExists` can both appear on move-lock calls, but they test
different preconditions. `HotKeyAccountNotExists` means the destination hotkey account is absent
from chain state. `NoExistingLock` means the source coldkey has no lock on the subnet to move even
if the destination hotkey is valid
([Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyAccountNotExists](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeyaccountnotexists),
[Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

- **NoExistingLock** — no source lock exists on the subnet.
- **HotKeyAccountNotExists** — the destination hotkey account does not exist.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the missing-lock check on the move-lock path.
It does not report whether a particular coldkey currently holds a conviction lock on any live
subnet; that depends on chain state at observation time
([Conviction and locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).
