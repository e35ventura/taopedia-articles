Subnet 13, Data Universe, is a decentralized data-scraping and storage network owned and operated by
[Macrocosmos](https://www.macrocosmos.ai/). Its purpose is to continuously collect social media
content from X (formerly Twitter), Reddit, and YouTube transcripts, and make that data available to
downstream consumers — other Bittensor subnets and external businesses — through a queryable,
validator-maintained index.

Macrocosmos describes Data Universe as the world's largest open-source social media dataset.[^1] The
network is designed to support up to 50 petabytes of data distributed across miners while requiring
only around 10 GB of local storage on each validator.[^1] The operator's primary consumer product,
[Gravity](https://app.macrocosmos.ai/gravity), lets anyone submit targeted scraping jobs and receive
sentiment analysis results without writing code.

Macrocosmos was co-founded by Will Squires (CEO) and Steffen Cruz (CTO). Cruz previously served as
CTO of the OpenTensor Foundation and is the original architect of [[subnet_1_apex]]'s codebase.[^1]

## How the Mechanism Works

Every piece of collected content is a **DataEntity** — a single scraped item with a URI validators
can use to independently retrieve and verify it. DataEntities are grouped into **DataEntityBuckets**
identified by three attributes: the data source (X, Reddit, or YouTube), the time bucket when the
content was created, and a content label such as a keyword or ticker symbol. The full set of a
miner's buckets is its **MinerIndex**.

Validators periodically fetch each miner's MinerIndex and maintain a local database giving them a
network-wide view of what data exists and where. They also run random spot-checks, pulling sample
DataEntities from miners and verifying them against the live source. These checks produce a
per-miner **credibility score** that scales the miner's overall reward — failing checks degrades
credibility and makes any inventory misrepresentation strictly worse than honest reporting.[^1]

Miner scoring rests on three factors:

**Data freshness.** Content older than 30 days receives no score. More recent data is weighted
proportionally higher within that window.[^1]

**Data desirability.** The subnet maintains a
[Dynamic Desirability List](https://docs.macrocosmos.ai/) reflecting what Gravity users are actively
requesting. Data matching high-demand labels scores at full value; unspecified labels receive
roughly 30% of that value.[^1]

**Duplication factor.** When many miners hold identical data, each copy is worth less. Per-copy
value decreases proportional to the number of miners holding the same bucket, pushing participants
toward diverse, under-replicated content.[^1]

Miners also upload their stores to S3-compatible public storage, anonymized to comply with each
platform's terms of service, enabling direct dataset access independent of validator queries.[^1]
Weight vectors from validator scoring flow into [[Yuma Consensus]], which converts them into
per-block TAO emissions distributed via [[Dynamic TAO]].

## Participating as a Miner

Miners run scraping processes targeting X, Reddit, and YouTube, organizing collected content into
DataEntityBuckets and reporting their MinerIndex to validators on request. Because duplicate data
earns diminishing rewards, miners are encouraged to consult the
[Data Universe dashboard](https://sn13-dashboard.api.macrocosmos.ai/) before configuring targets —
the dashboard shows which bucket types are already saturated across the network, helping identify
underserved niches that maximize score.[^1]

Miners are subject to Macrocosmos's data compliance policy, which aligns with UK GDPR obligations,
prohibits collection of illegal or copyrighted content, and requires prompt reporting of any
personal data breaches.[^1] Detailed setup guidance, including S3 storage configuration, is
available in the
[miner documentation](https://github.com/macrocosm-os/data-universe/blob/main/docs/miner.md).

## Participating as a Validator

Validators maintain the full MinerIndex database and run the credibility-check spot-sampling
described above. Because validators track only metadata and spot-sampled records — rather than the
full dataset — their storage footprint remains around 10 GB even as the network scales to petabyte
range.[^1] Validator activity is logged anonymously to
[Weights & Biases](https://wandb.ai/macrocosmos/data-universe-validators) for network-wide
transparency. Detailed setup instructions are in the
[validator documentation](https://github.com/macrocosm-os/data-universe/blob/main/docs/validator.md).
