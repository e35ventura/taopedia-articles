# Tempo Out Of Bounds

`TempoOutOfBounds` means a subnet-owner tempo update on netuid 1 or another named subnet submitted a
block count outside the documented allowed band. The standard-errors reference describes it as "The
supplied tempo is outside the allowed range"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#tempooutofbounds)).

Tempo is the block-based interval that groups subnet reward and weight processing
([Glossary: Tempo](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#tempo),
[Subnet Hyperparameters: tempo](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#tempo)).

## What It Means

The subnet hyperparameter reference documents `tempo` as a `u16` measured in blocks, with a default
of 360. The documented owner-path setter is bounded from 360 to 50,400 blocks, corresponding to the
chain's MinTempo and MaxTempo limits for that path
([Subnet Hyperparameters: tempo](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#tempo),
[Subtensor Constants: initialTempo](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/constants#initialtempo-u16)).

`TempoOutOfBounds` means that owner-path update rejected the submitted tempo because it fell below
360 blocks or above 50,400 blocks
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#tempooutofbounds)).

This is a value-range gate on the owner tempo setter. The failure concerns the submitted block
count, not whether the caller lacks permission or whether the admin freeze window is active.

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a subnet owner submits a tempo below 360 blocks or above 50,400 blocks. That
can happen when a client uses the wrong units, copies an outdated extrinsic, or confuses the owner
tempo setter with the separate root setter that is not bounded by this error
([Subnet Hyperparameters: tempo](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#tempo)).

When debugging on netuid 1, readers can read the live tempo with
`btcli subnet hyperparameters --netuid 1 --network finney` before resubmitting an update
([Subnet Hyperparameters: View hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#view-hyperparameters)).

When debugging, rule out authorization and timing failures first. `BadOrigin` and `NotSubnetOwner`
mean the signer lacks subnet-owner permission. `AdminActionProhibitedDuringWeightsWindow` means the
call arrived during the epoch-end admin freeze. `TxRateLimitExceeded` means the tempo-update
cooldown has not elapsed
([Subtensor Standard Errors: BadOrigin](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#badorigin),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NotSubnetOwner](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notsubnetowner),
[Admin Action Prohibited During Weights Window](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#adminactionprohibitedduringweightswindow),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: TxRateLimitExceeded](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#txratelimitexceeded)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the submitted tempo is outside the allowed owner-path range.

## Distinction from Admin Action Prohibited During Weights Window

`TempoOutOfBounds` and `AdminActionProhibitedDuringWeightsWindow` both appear on subnet
hyperparameter updates, but they reject different problems. `TempoOutOfBounds` means the submitted
tempo is outside the documented 360 to 50,400 block band. `AdminActionProhibitedDuringWeightsWindow`
means the signer was otherwise permitted, but the admin freeze window was active
([Admin Action Prohibited During Weights Window](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#adminactionprohibitedduringweightswindow)).

- **TempoOutOfBounds** — the submitted tempo is outside the allowed range.
- **AdminActionProhibitedDuringWeightsWindow** — the call arrived during the admin freeze window.

## Distinction from Tx Rate Limit Exceeded

`TempoOutOfBounds` and `TxRateLimitExceeded` both appear on owner tempo updates, but at different
stages. `TempoOutOfBounds` is the value-range rejection when the tempo falls outside 360 to 50,400
blocks. `TxRateLimitExceeded` is the later pacing rejection when the update arrives before the
tempo-update cooldown has elapsed
([Subtensor Standard Errors: TxRateLimitExceeded](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#txratelimitexceeded)).

- **TempoOutOfBounds** — the submitted tempo is outside the allowed range.
- **TxRateLimitExceeded** — the tempo update arrived too soon after the last one.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the 360 to 50,400 block gate it represents. It
does not report the live tempo on a particular netuid; that value is subnet chain state readers can
query for the selected netuid
([Subnet Hyperparameters: View hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#view-hyperparameters),
[Bittensor Networks](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/bittensor-networks)).
