# Tempo Out Of Bounds

`TempoOutOfBounds` means a subnet-owner `set_tempo` call submitted a tempo value outside the allowed
block range. The standard-errors reference describes it as "The supplied tempo is outside the
allowed range"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#tempooutofbounds)).

Tempo is the block-based interval that groups subnet reward and weight processing
([Glossary: Tempo](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#tempo),
[Subnet Hyperparameters: tempo](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#tempo)).

## What It Means

The `set_tempo` extrinsic lets a subnet owner change the tempo hyperparameter on an existing subnet.
`TempoOutOfBounds` means the submitted tempo was below the documented minimum or above the
documented maximum for that owner path
([Subnet Hyperparameters: tempo](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#tempo),
[Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#tempooutofbounds)).

The documented owner-path band is 360 to 50,400 blocks, corresponding to the chain's MinTempo and
MaxTempo bounds for `set_tempo`
([Subnet Hyperparameters: tempo](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#tempo),
[Subtensor Constants: initialTempo](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/constants#initialtempo)).

This is a value-range gate on the subnet-owner tempo setter. The failure concerns the submitted
block count, not whether the caller lacks permission or whether the admin freeze window is active.

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a subnet owner submits `set_tempo` with a tempo below 360 blocks or above
50,400 blocks. That can happen when a client uses the wrong units, copies an outdated extrinsic, or
confuses the owner `set_tempo` path with the separate root `sudo_set_tempo` setter, which is not
bounded by this error
([Subnet Hyperparameters: tempo](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#tempo)).

When debugging, rule out earlier gates on the same extrinsic first. `BadOrigin` and `NotSubnetOwner`
mean the signer is not the subnet owner. `AdminActionProhibitedDuringWeightsWindow` means the call
arrived during the epoch-end admin freeze. `TxRateLimitExceeded` means the tempo-update rate limit
has not elapsed
([Subtensor Standard Errors: BadOrigin](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#badorigin),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NotSubnetOwner](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notsubnetowner),
[Admin Action Prohibited During Weights Window](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#adminactionprohibitedduringweightswindow),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: TxRateLimitExceeded](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#txratelimitexceeded)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the submitted tempo is outside the allowed owner-path range.

## Distinction from Admin Action Prohibited During Weights Window

`TempoOutOfBounds` and `AdminActionProhibitedDuringWeightsWindow` both appear on subnet
hyperparameter updates, but they reject different problems. `TempoOutOfBounds` means the submitted
tempo value is outside the documented MinTempo to MaxTempo band.
`AdminActionProhibitedDuringWeightsWindow` means the signer was otherwise permitted, but the admin
freeze window around epoch boundaries was active
([Admin Action Prohibited During Weights Window](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#adminactionprohibitedduringweightswindow)).

- **TempoOutOfBounds** — the submitted tempo is outside the allowed range.
- **AdminActionProhibitedDuringWeightsWindow** — the call arrived during the admin freeze window.

## Distinction from Tx Rate Limit Exceeded

`TempoOutOfBounds` and `TxRateLimitExceeded` both appear on `set_tempo`, but at different stages.
`TempoOutOfBounds` is the value-range rejection when the tempo falls outside MinTempo to MaxTempo.
`TxRateLimitExceeded` is the later pacing rejection when the subnet owner resubmits before the
tempo-update cooldown has elapsed
([Subtensor Standard Errors: TxRateLimitExceeded](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#txratelimitexceeded)).

- **TempoOutOfBounds** — the submitted tempo is outside the allowed range.
- **TxRateLimitExceeded** — the tempo update arrived too soon after the last one.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the MinTempo to MaxTempo gate on `set_tempo`.
It does not report the live tempo on a particular netuid; that value is subnet chain state readers
can query through subnet hyperparameter tools
([Subnet Hyperparameters: View hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#view-hyperparameters),
[Glossary: Tempo](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#tempo)).
