# Uid Not Found

`UidNotFound` is a documented Subtensor custom error raised when a call names a hotkey that does not
hold a UID on the submitted subnet. The custom-errors reference describes it as "The specified
netuid does not exist or the provided hotkey does not own this subnet"
([Subtensor Custom Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/custom)).

## What It Means

The `associate_evm_key` extrinsic links an EVM address to a Bittensor hotkey on a named subnet
([Token Bridging](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/evm-tutorials/bridge-vtao),
[Subtensor Extrinsics](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/navigating-subtensor/subtensor-extrinsics)).

`UidNotFound` means the signing hotkey failed the subnet membership lookup for that netuid before
the association path could continue. The chain treats the hotkey as not registered on the subnet, so
the call is rejected as a custom transaction-validity error rather than proceeding to later
association checks ([Subtensor Custom Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/custom),
[Hotkey Not Registered In Subnet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeynotregisteredinsubnet)).

This is a subnet UID membership rejection on the EVM association path. It does not, by itself, mean
the association rate limit was exceeded, that signature recovery failed, or that the hotkey account
is absent from chain state.

## Why It Appears

`UidNotFound` appears when `associate_evm_key` is validated and the runtime cannot resolve a UID for
the signing hotkey on the submitted netuid. That can happen when the hotkey never registered on that
subnet, when automation targets the wrong netuid, or when a hotkey lost its slot through pruning but
clients still use an outdated mapping
([Subtensor Custom Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/custom),
[Subtensor source: guards/check_evm_key_association.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/guards/check_evm_key_association.rs)).

The pinned EVM-key association guard performs this membership lookup before rate-limit and dispatch
checks. When the lookup fails, the pallet raises `HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet`, which the runtime
maps to the `UidNotFound` custom transaction error during validation
([Subtensor source: guards/check_evm_key_association.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/guards/check_evm_key_association.rs),
[Subtensor source: extensions/subtensor.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/extensions/subtensor.rs)).

## Distinction from Hotkey Not Registered In Subnet

`UidNotFound` and `HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet` describe the same membership failure, but under
different error labels. `UidNotFound` is the custom transaction-validity code returned when
`associate_evm_key` is rejected during validation. `HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet` is the standard
pallet error label used on other subnet-scoped extrinsics such as weight submission
([Subtensor Custom Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/custom),
[Hotkey Not Registered In Subnet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeynotregisteredinsubnet)).

- **UidNotFound** — custom validity rejection on the EVM association path.
- **HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet** — standard pallet error on other subnet-scoped calls.

## Distinction from EVM Key Associate Rate Limit Exceeded

`UidNotFound` and `EvmKeyAssociateRateLimitExceeded` both block `associate_evm_key`, but at
different stages. `UidNotFound` is the membership check that runs when the hotkey has no UID on the
named subnet. `EvmKeyAssociateRateLimitExceeded` is the later pacing rejection when association
attempts arrive too frequently after membership has already passed
([EVM Key Associate Rate Limit Exceeded](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#evmkeyassociateratelimitexceeded),
[Subtensor Custom Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/custom)).

- **UidNotFound** — the hotkey is not registered on the subnet.
- **EvmKeyAssociateRateLimitExceeded** — the hotkey is registered, but the call arrived too soon.

## Distinction from Hotkey Account Not Exists

`UidNotFound` and `HotKeyAccountNotExists` both involve hotkey validity, but at different scopes.
`UidNotFound` means the hotkey account is present but has no UID on the named subnet.
`HotKeyAccountNotExists` means the hotkey account itself is absent from chain state
([Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyAccountNotExists](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeyaccountnotexists),
[Subtensor Custom Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/custom)).

- **UidNotFound** — the hotkey exists but is not registered on the subnet.
- **HotKeyAccountNotExists** — the hotkey account does not exist.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the custom error label and the subnet UID membership guard it
represents on the `associate_evm_key` validation path. It does not describe how to register a
neuron, how EVM signatures are verified, or the live association rate limit for a subnet
([Token Bridging](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/evm-tutorials/bridge-vtao),
[Subtensor Extrinsics](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/navigating-subtensor/subtensor-extrinsics)).
