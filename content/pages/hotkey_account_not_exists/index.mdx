# Hotkey Account Not Exists

`HotKeyAccountNotExists` means a staking-related call named a hotkey that is not present on chain.
The standard-errors reference describes it as "Hotkey account does not exist"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#hotkeyaccountnotexists)).

In Subtensor, hotkey existence is tied to the chain's recorded coldkey-to-hotkey ownership
association rather than to a wallet file on disk
([Hotkey and Coldkey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/getting-started/wallets)).

## What It Means

This error is a global account-existence check. The chain does not recognize the targeted hotkey as
an existing owned account, so the call stops with `HotKeyAccountNotExists`
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#hotkeyaccountnotexists),
[Hotkey and Coldkey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/getting-started/wallets)).

For debugging, treat this as a missing hotkey account, not as proof that the hotkey lacks a UID on a
subnet or that the signing coldkey lacks free TAO. Subnet membership and coldkey funding are
separate questions
([Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#hotkeynotregisteredinsubnet),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#notenoughbalancetostake)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a call references a hotkey that has not yet been associated on chain, names
the wrong hotkey account, or uses a stale target that no longer matches the intended hotkey.

When debugging, compare existence failures at different scopes. `NonAssociatedColdKey` means the
hotkey exists, but the signing coldkey is not its recorded owner. `HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet`
means the hotkey exists on chain but does not hold a UID on the selected netuid
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NonAssociatedColdKey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#nonassociatedcoldkey),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#hotkeynotregisteredinsubnet)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the chain does not know that hotkey as an existing owned account.

## Distinction from Non Associated Cold Key

`HotKeyAccountNotExists` and `NonAssociatedColdKey` are different account-state failures.
`HotKeyAccountNotExists` means the targeted hotkey is not present on chain at all.
`NonAssociatedColdKey` means the hotkey exists, but the signing coldkey is not its recorded owner
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NonAssociatedColdKey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#nonassociatedcoldkey),
[Hotkey and Coldkey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/getting-started/wallets)).

- **HotKeyAccountNotExists** — the hotkey is not present on chain.
- **NonAssociatedColdKey** — the hotkey exists, but this coldkey does not own it.

## Distinction from Hotkey Not Registered In Subnet

`HotKeyAccountNotExists` and `HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet` answer different existence questions.
`HotKeyAccountNotExists` is the global account-existence check for the hotkey itself.
`HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet` is the subnet-membership check for a hotkey that may already exist on
chain but does not have a UID on the selected netuid
([Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#hotkeynotregisteredinsubnet)).

- **HotKeyAccountNotExists** — no chain-level hotkey account is recorded.
- **HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet** — the hotkey exists, but not on that subnet.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the global hotkey-account existence condition.
It does not tell a reader whether a hotkey has a validator permit, enough stake, or a UID on a
specific subnet; those are separate topics
([Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#hotkeynotregisteredinsubnet),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NonAssociatedColdKey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#nonassociatedcoldkey),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#notenoughbalancetostake)).
