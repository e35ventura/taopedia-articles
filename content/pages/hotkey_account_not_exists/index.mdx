# Hotkey Account Not Exists

`HotKeyAccountNotExists` means a staking-related call named a hotkey that is not present on chain.
The standard-errors reference describes it as "Hotkey account does not exist"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeyaccountnotexists)).

In Subtensor, hotkey existence is tied to the chain's recorded coldkey-to-hotkey ownership
association rather than to a wallet file on disk
([Hotkey and Coldkey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/getting-started/wallets),
[Subtensor source: helpers.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/helpers.rs)).

## What It Means

This error is an account-existence check. Before staking helpers continue with balance, stake, or
swap validation, the runtime verifies that the targeted hotkey already exists in chain state. If no
owner association is recorded for that hotkey, the call stops with `HotKeyAccountNotExists`
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeyaccountnotexists),
[Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs),
[Subtensor source: helpers.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/helpers.rs)).

For a reader, the practical meaning is narrow: the chain does not know that hotkey as an existing
owned account. The label does not, by itself, say anything about subnet membership, stake size, or
coldkey funding.

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a call references a hotkey that has not yet been associated on chain, names
the wrong hotkey account, or uses a stale target that no longer matches the intended hotkey. On
staking paths, the chain checks account existence before later balance and swap rules, so a missing
hotkey account is rejected before a reader reaches stake-size or liquidity questions
([Hotkey and Coldkey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/getting-started/wallets),
[Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs),
[Subtensor source: remove_stake.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/remove_stake.rs)).

That makes `HotKeyAccountNotExists` different from a wallet-balance problem. The hotkey itself is
missing from the chain's ownership records, so funding or unstake details are not the first thing
the label is telling the reader.

## Distinction from Non Associated Cold Key

`HotKeyAccountNotExists` and `NonAssociatedColdKey` are different account-state failures.
`HotKeyAccountNotExists` means the targeted hotkey is not present on chain at all.
`NonAssociatedColdKey` means the hotkey exists, but the signing coldkey is not its recorded owner
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NonAssociatedColdKey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#nonassociatedcoldkey),
[Hotkey and Coldkey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/getting-started/wallets)).

- **HotKeyAccountNotExists** — the hotkey is not present on chain.
- **NonAssociatedColdKey** — the hotkey exists, but this coldkey does not own it.

## Distinction from Hotkey Not Registered In Subnet

`HotKeyAccountNotExists` and `HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet` answer different existence questions.
`HotKeyAccountNotExists` is the global account-existence check for the hotkey itself.
`HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet` is the subnet-membership check for a hotkey that may already exist on
chain but does not have a UID on the selected netuid
([Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeynotregisteredinsubnet),
[Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeyaccountnotexists)).

- **HotKeyAccountNotExists** — no chain-level hotkey account is recorded.
- **HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet** — the hotkey exists, but not on that subnet.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the global hotkey-account existence check. It
does not tell a reader whether a hotkey has a validator permit, enough stake, or a UID on a specific
subnet; those are separate conditions checked elsewhere
([Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeynotregisteredinsubnet),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NonAssociatedColdKey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#nonassociatedcoldkey),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake)).
