# Insufficient Balance

`InsufficientBalance` means a coldkey-signed balance operation named more TAO than the account could
spend for that action. The standard-errors reference describes it as "The caller does not have
enough balance for the operation"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#insufficientbalance)).

## What It Means

Many wallet operations move free TAO from a coldkey account. `InsufficientBalance` means the runtime
rejected the call because the requested amount could not be withdrawn from that coldkey balance
under the preservation rules for the path
([Glossary: Transfer](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#transfer),
[Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#insufficientbalance)).

This is a funding gate on the coldkey account. The failure concerns how much free TAO the signer can
move, not whether a hotkey is registered on a subnet or whether a subnet pool can complete a swap.

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a transfer or other coldkey balance move names more TAO than the account can
spend. That can happen when fees or earlier transactions reduced free balance after the client built
the call, when the amount ignores the transaction fee reserve, or when the extrinsic simply requests
more TAO than the coldkey holds
([Glossary: Transfer](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#transfer),
[Transaction Fees in Bittensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/fees)).

When debugging, compare against staking-specific balance errors on other extrinsics.
`NotEnoughBalanceToStake` means an add-stake call could not fund the requested stake amount.
`AmountTooLow` means a staking amount fell below the chain's minimum increment rather than exceeding
available balance
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: AmountTooLow](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#amounttoolow)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the coldkey cannot fund the requested TAO amount for the operation.

## Distinction from Not Enough Balance To Stake

`InsufficientBalance` and `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` both involve coldkey funding failures, but on
different extrinsic families. `InsufficientBalance` is the general balance rejection on TAO transfer
and related coldkey withdrawal paths. `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` is the add-stake rejection when free
balance cannot cover the stake amount named in that extrinsic
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#notenoughbalancetostake)).

- **InsufficientBalance** — the coldkey cannot fund the requested TAO for the balance operation.
- **NotEnoughBalanceToStake** — the coldkey cannot fund the requested add-stake amount.

## Distinction from Zero Balance After Withdrawn

`InsufficientBalance` and `ZeroBalanceAfterWithdrawn` both involve coldkey withdrawals, but they
reject different problems. `InsufficientBalance` means the account cannot fund the requested amount
under the path's spend rules. `ZeroBalanceAfterWithdrawn` means the withdrawal would leave the
account with zero balance in a way the chain does not allow
([Subtensor Standard Errors: ZeroBalanceAfterWithdrawn](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#zerobalanceafterwithdrawn)).

- **InsufficientBalance** — the coldkey cannot fund the requested amount.
- **ZeroBalanceAfterWithdrawn** — the withdrawal would zero out the account.

## Distinction from Insufficient Liquidity

`InsufficientBalance` and `InsufficientLiquidity` both block value-moving calls, but at different
layers. `InsufficientBalance` is a coldkey free-balance rejection. `InsufficientLiquidity` is a
subnet pool rejection when a staking swap cannot be simulated or completed
([Insufficient Liquidity](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#insufficientliquidity)).

- **InsufficientBalance** — the coldkey lacks enough free TAO for the operation.
- **InsufficientLiquidity** — the subnet pool cannot support the staking conversion.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the coldkey funding gate it represents. It does
not report a wallet's live free balance, fee quote, or whether a particular transfer amount is
spendable on a network; those depend on chain state at observation time
([Glossary: Transfer](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#transfer),
[Transaction Fees in Bittensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/fees)).
