# Subnet 19: Blockmachine

Blockmachine is Bittensor Subnet 19 (SN19), operated by [taostat](https://blockmachine.io). The
subnet decentralizes blockchain node infrastructure by coordinating a competitive network of
independent node operators who serve data requests through a protocol-owned gateway. Rewards flow to
miners who provide accurate, fast, and reliable service; validators independently verify correctness
and penalize miners who return bad data.

References: [taostat/blockmachine (GitHub)](https://github.com/taostat/blockmachine/),
[blockmachine.io](https://blockmachine.io)

## How the Mechanism Works

Blockmachine routes user requests for blockchain data through a gateway to registered miners. Before
results reach the customer, the gateway verifies that the miner's response is correct. Only valid,
successful responses count toward a miner's earnings — failed or incorrect responses generate
nothing. This ties miner income directly to delivered, verified work.

After each epoch, validators audit miner performance by independently verifying sampled responses
and measuring latency and reliability. Miners whose data does not match what the chain actually
contains are permanently banned. Validator quality scores feed into weight vectors submitted to
[[Yuma Consensus]], distributing emissions via [[Dynamic TAO]].

The subnet prices its service in USD-equivalent terms rather than in token units, decoupling
infrastructure cost from token price volatility and making the marketplace predictable for
customers.

References: [taostat/blockmachine (GitHub)](https://github.com/taostat/blockmachine/)

## Participating as a Miner

Miners on Blockmachine operate blockchain archive and full nodes that serve data requests routed
from the gateway. Their economic role is to deliver accurate, low-latency responses consistently.
The marketplace is competitive: miners who provide faster, more reliable service earn more
emissions, and those who return incorrect data are permanently removed. There is no reward for
failed responses — only verified, correct answers count.

References: [taostat/blockmachine (GitHub)](https://github.com/taostat/blockmachine/)

## Participating as a Validator

Validators on Blockmachine independently verify the accuracy and performance of miners each epoch.
They spot-check miner responses against known correct chain data, measure response speed and
reliability, and compute per-miner quality scores. Validators submit weight vectors derived from
these scores to [[Yuma Consensus]] and flag any miners confirmed to have returned incorrect data for
permanent removal from the network.

References: [taostat/blockmachine (GitHub)](https://github.com/taostat/blockmachine/)
