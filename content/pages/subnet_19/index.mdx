# Subnet 19: Blockmachine

Blockmachine is Bittensor Subnet 19 (SN19), operated by [taostat](https://github.com/taostat). The
subnet runs a decentralized marketplace for blockchain data services: customers access blockchain
data through a competitive field of miners, validators independently score each miner on the
accuracy and reliability of what they serve, and the highest-scoring miners earn the largest share
of subnet emissions. The design applies Bittensor's competitive incentives to the problem of
sourcing trustworthy blockchain data.

References: [taostat/blockmachine (GitHub)](https://github.com/taostat/blockmachine),
[blockmachine.io](https://blockmachine.io)

## How the Mechanism Works

Validators score miners continuously, each epoch assessing how accurately and reliably each miner
served blockchain data during that period. Accuracy is verified against independently-maintained
reference data, so scores reflect genuine service quality rather than self-reported results. Miners
are also evaluated on response speed and uptime: a miner who answers correctly but inconsistently
or slowly earns a lower score than one who is both accurate and dependable.

Scores across the validator set feed into weight vectors submitted to [[Yuma Consensus]], which
distributes emissions through [[Dynamic TAO]]. Miners who consistently rank at the top of the
accuracy and availability leaderboard capture the largest share of emissions.

References: [taostat/blockmachine (GitHub)](https://github.com/taostat/blockmachine)

## Participating as a Miner

Miners on Blockmachine serve blockchain data requests to customers and earn Bittensor emissions
proportional to the quality of their service. Their economic role is to maintain consistently
accurate, fast, and available responses across the blockchain networks the subnet supports. Miners
with high accuracy and uptime earn more emissions; those whose responses are incorrect or slow fall
in the scoring rankings and earn proportionally less.

The subnet supports multiple blockchain networks including Bittensor's own chain, Ethereum, and
BSC, allowing miners to earn emissions by serving data across several chains simultaneously.

References: [taostat/blockmachine (GitHub)](https://github.com/taostat/blockmachine)

## Participating as a Validator

Validators on Blockmachine run the independent scoring layer that determines which miners earn
emissions. Each epoch, validators assess the accuracy, speed, and availability of every registered
miner by comparing what miners served against correct reference data the validator maintains
independently. The result is a per-miner quality score that validators submit as a weight vector
to [[Yuma Consensus]].

References: [taostat/blockmachine (GitHub)](https://github.com/taostat/blockmachine)
