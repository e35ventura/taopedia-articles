# Subnet Owner Cut

Subnet owner cut is a network parameter, listed in the documentation as `SubnetOwnerCut`, that sets
the ratio of a subnet's alpha emissions given to the subnet owner as stake. The reference describes
it as global and fixed at 18%, set at root permission
([Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters),
[Emission](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions)).

## What It Controls

The owner cut is the slice of each subnet's alpha emission reserved for the subnet owner before the
rest is distributed. The documentation breaks the per-tempo alpha distribution down as 18% to the
subnet owner, with the remainder going to validators as dividends and miners as incentives
([Emission](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions)).

The cut is deducted before the validator and miner distributions, not after. The coinbase computes
the owner cut first and subtracts it from the alpha to be distributed, so the remaining roughly 82%
is what flows to validators and miners through Yuma Consensus
([Coinbase Implementation](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/navigating-subtensor/emissions-coinbase),
[Yuma Consensus](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus)).

Owner emissions do not pay out every block. They accumulate in a pending owner cut store across the
tempo and are released when the epoch completes, alongside the pending validator and miner emissions
([Coinbase Implementation](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/navigating-subtensor/emissions-coinbase),
[Emission](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions)).

## How the Owner Receives It

The cut is paid as staked alpha on the subnet rather than as liquid TAO. Receiving the share as
alpha ties the owner's payout to the subnet's own token, so its value tracks how the subnet performs
rather than being a fixed TAO amount ([Emission](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions),
[Glossary: Alpha Token](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#alpha-token)).

## Reading the Accrual for netuid 1

Although the cut ratio is global, the accrued owner emission is held per subnet. The pending owner
cut store is keyed by netuid, so the amount waiting to be paid out accumulates separately for each
subnet and is observed against a specific netuid rather than network-wide
([Coinbase Implementation](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/navigating-subtensor/emissions-coinbase)).
For the documented example netuid 1 on Finney mainnet, the owner's accrued and released cut is
observed through that subnet's emission and metagraph state, which the references describe under
viewing a subnet's hyperparameters and emission flow
([Subnet Hyperparameters: View hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#view-hyperparameters),
[Emission](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions)). The infobox uses netuid 1 only as an
example label; the 18% ratio applies the same way to every subnet, while the accrued amount differs
per netuid ([Bittensor Networks](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/bittensor-networks)).

## Relationship to the Owner Cut Toggles

Subnet owner cut sets the size of the cut; two separate per-subnet flags govern its handling. Owner
cut enabled determines whether the owner receives the cut at all, and owner cut auto lock enabled
determines whether the received cut is locked as stake or taken as free stake
([Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters)).

- **Subnet owner cut** — the fixed ~18% size of the owner's emission share.
- **Owner cut enabled** — whether that share is distributed at all.
- **Owner cut auto lock enabled** — whether the received share is auto-locked as stake.

## Documented Value and Setter

The [Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters)
reference describes `SubnetOwnerCut` as global and fixed at 18%, changed through the
`sudo_set_subnet_owner_cut` extrinsic at root permission. Because it is global rather than
per-subnet, the same cut applies across subnets rather than being tuned by individual owners
([Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters)).

## Distinction from Owner Cut Enabled

Subnet owner cut is the magnitude of the owner's emission share. Owner cut enabled is the on/off
switch deciding whether that share is paid out, so one sets how much and the other sets whether
([Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters)).

- **Subnet owner cut** — how large the owner's share is.
- **Owner cut enabled** — whether the owner's share is distributed.

## Distinction from TAO Weight

Both are global parameters near 18%, but they govern different things. TAO weight scales how much a
validator's TAO stake counts toward stake weight; subnet owner cut is the share of alpha emissions
routed to the subnet owner ([Emission](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions)).

- **Subnet owner cut** — owner's share of alpha emissions.
- **TAO weight** — scaling of TAO stake in validator stake weight.
