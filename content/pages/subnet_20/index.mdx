# Subnet 20: GroundLayer

GroundLayer is Bittensor Subnet 20 (SN20), operated by
[RogueTensor](https://github.com/RogueTensor). The team describes the project as "the capital layer
for Bittensor" ([groundlayer.xyz](https://www.groundlayer.xyz/))—an institutional-grade OTC
marketplace where subnet owners can raise capital through structured alpha token deals without
selling into the open market. Fund managers and investors participate through on-chain deal
positions, and Bittensor emissions reward the fund managers who lock the most capital into active
deals.

## How the Mechanism Works

GroundLayer organises participants into three roles: subnet owners (sellers), fund managers
(miners), and investors.

Subnet owners list OTC allocations on-chain with defined terms—discount rates relative to spot,
lockup schedules, and per-deal supply caps. This structure allows a subnet to attract capital while
preventing the dilutive spot selling that a simple token sale would trigger. Because the terms are
enforced on-chain, buyers receive verifiable commitments rather than informal agreements.

Fund managers operate as SN20 miners. They source investor capital and commit it into on-chain smart
contract positions against the deals listed by subnet owners. Validator nodes measure each fund
manager's total assets under management (AUM) locked across active deals, and this AUM figure drives
the weight assignment that determines emissions. A longevity component in the scoring rewards fund
managers who sustain stable, long-duration positions rather than cycling capital in and out of
short-lived deals.

Validators collect on-chain deal state, compute per-miner AUM scores, apply moving averages to
smooth out single-epoch volatility, and submit the resulting weights to the Bittensor chain.
[[Yuma Consensus]] converts those weights into continuous emissions for the top-performing fund
managers. By tying rewards directly to capital locked under deal terms, the mechanism aligns miner
incentives with the long-term health of the [[Dynamic TAO]] ecosystem: fund managers only earn if
real capital is committed to real deals.

Investors access the protocol through the fund managers they choose to back. Rather than negotiating
directly with subnet owners, investors commit capital to established fund managers who have
demonstrated deal execution track records. The on-chain framework ensures investors receive the deal
terms their chosen manager agreed to, enforced without intermediaries.

## Participating as a Miner

Miners on GroundLayer function as fund managers. The role centres on attracting investor capital and
deploying it into OTC allocations listed by subnet owners. A miner registers a hotkey on SN20, runs
the required validator-facing software, and executes deal commitments on-chain according to the
terms each subnet owner has set.

Emissions are proportional to AUM locked, so miners who establish larger positions and honour longer
lockup schedules earn greater rewards. Miners who build a reputation for reliable deal execution
attract more investor capital, compounding their competitive advantage. Protocol documentation is
maintained in the team's GitHub repository at
[github.com/RogueTensor/comingsoon](https://github.com/RogueTensor/comingsoon) as the full
marketplace approaches launch.

## Participating as a Validator

Validators on SN20 form the verification layer. Each scoring epoch, a validator reads on-chain deal
state for every active fund manager, computes the AUM committed to live positions, and weights
scores using the performance and longevity formula. The resulting per-miner weights are submitted to
the Bittensor chain, where [[Yuma Consensus]] distributes emissions accordingly.

Validators must maintain a registered hotkey on SN20 with sufficient stake to remain in the active
validator set. Consistent uptime is important: missed scoring epochs translate directly into less
accurate weight submissions and reduced influence over emissions.
