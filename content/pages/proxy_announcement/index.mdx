# Proxy Announcement

A proxy announcement is the public on-chain record associated with a delayed proxy call. The
[Proxies documentation](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/proxies) places the announcement as
part of the framework that supports delayed-proxy relationships, alongside the delay window and the
real account's documented standing.

The same source names the announcement as the conceptual boundary between a delegate's pending
action and a real account's window, and identifies the real account as the party whose standing
applies to the announced action.

## Why Proxy Announcements Exist

A participant who keeps their main coldkey in cold storage can grant a delayed proxy to a delegate.
The [Proxies documentation](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/proxies) names the announcement as
the public record that corresponds to that relationship pattern, and notes that without the
delay-and-announce pattern, proxies do not provide the security benefit they are designed for.

## What a Proxy Announcement Names

A proxy announcement names two roles in a single per-call record:

- **Delegate** — the proxy wallet associated with the announcement.
- **Real account** — the safe wallet that the announcement is visible to, and the party whose
  standing applies to the announced action before the delay period ends.

Together, these two roles are what the source describes as the announcement mechanism for delayed
proxy calls.

## Relationship to Proxies

A proxy announcement and a proxy are related but different parts of the Bittensor wallet security
vocabulary. A proxy is the standing permission that grants a delegate the ability to perform a
scoped set of actions on behalf of a real account. A proxy announcement is the per-call record that
corresponds to a delayed exercise of that permission.

For readers, a proxy names the standing permission a delegate holds, while a proxy announcement
names the per-call record associated with a delayed exercise of that permission.

## Reader Boundary

This article defines a proxy announcement as a concept. It is limited to conceptual framing and does
not document the operational details of working with announcements in any specific interface, and
does not quote current per-proxy or per-account limits. Readers should use the official proxies
documentation and the live chain state for operational detail.
