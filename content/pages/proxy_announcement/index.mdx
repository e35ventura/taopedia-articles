# Proxy Announcement

A proxy announcement is the public on-chain declaration a delegate creates before executing a
delayed proxy call. The [Proxies documentation](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/proxies)
describes the pattern: when a delegate holds a delayed proxy, the delegate cannot execute a call
immediately and must first announce the intended action and wait for the delay period to pass.

The same source names the announcement as the boundary that gives the real account a veto window
over a delegate's actions, and identifies the real account as the party that can reject the
announcement and effectively block the call.

## Why Proxy Announcements Exist

A participant who holds tokens in a real account on cold storage can use a delayed proxy to let a
delegate perform day-to-day operations. The
[Proxies documentation](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/proxies) places the announcement as the
boundary that lets the real account observe an announced action and reject it before the delegate is
allowed to execute, giving the cold-storage holder a window to detect and block unauthorized use of
the delegate's authority.

## What a Proxy Announcement Names

A proxy announcement names two roles in a single pre-execution notice:

- **Delegate** — the proxy wallet that creates the announcement and waits through the delay period
  before executing the announced action.
- **Real account** — the safe wallet that the announcement is visible to and that can reject the
  announced action before the delegate is allowed to execute it.

These two roles, taken together, are what the source describes as the announcement mechanism for
delayed proxy calls. The announcement is the public record that connects them in time: a delegate
declares intent, and a real account has the documented ability to reject that intent before it can
become a chain action.

## Relationship to Proxies

A proxy announcement and a proxy are related but different parts of the Bittensor wallet security
vocabulary. A proxy is a permission grant — a delegate account authorized to perform a scoped set of
actions on behalf of a real account. A proxy announcement is the per-call declaration a delegate
creates before the proxy's authority is exercised through a delayed call.

The [Proxies documentation](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/proxies) names the announcement as
the mechanism that applies to delayed proxies, and notes that without using the delay-and-announce
constraints, proxies do not provide the security benefit they are designed for.

For readers, a proxy names the standing permission a delegate holds, while a proxy announcement
names the per-call pre-execution record that the delegate creates when exercising that permission
through a delayed call.

## Reader Boundary

This article defines a proxy announcement as a concept. It does not describe how to create, monitor,
or reject an announcement in any specific interface, and does not quote current per-proxy or
per-account limits. Readers should use the official proxies documentation and the live chain state
for operational detail.
