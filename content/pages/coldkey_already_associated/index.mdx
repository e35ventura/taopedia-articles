# Cold Key Already Associated

`ColdKeyAlreadyAssociated` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised by
[coldkey swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/coldkey-swap). The standard-errors reference
describes it as "The coldkey has already been swapped"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#coldkeyalreadyassociated)).

## What It Means

`do_swap_coldkey` migrates a wallet's on-chain identity from an old coldkey to a new one. Before
moving balances, stake, subnet ownership, or hotkey associations, it checks whether the proposed
destination already has staking hotkeys linked to it. `ColdKeyAlreadyAssociated` is the rejection
when that lookup finds existing associations, meaning the destination address is already in use as a
coldkey rather than as a fresh unused account
([Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs)).

This is the first of two early existence checks `do_swap_coldkey` runs on the destination address.
The later check covers a different case: whether the destination already exists as a hotkey account
somewhere on chain. A caller only reaches that second check if the destination passes this
association lookup first
([Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs)).

## Why It Appears

The most direct cause is attempting to swap onto an address that already received a prior coldkey
migration and therefore already carries hotkey associations on record. The coldkey swap
documentation states that the destination coldkey must be unused on chain; an address that already
has linked hotkeys fails that requirement even if the caller is trying to reuse it as a new swap
destination ([Coldkey Swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/coldkey-swap),
[Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs)).

Because the check runs at the start of `do_swap_coldkey`, none of the migration steps proceed when
it fails: balances, stake, ownership records, and hotkey links are not partially moved to an address
the chain treats as already associated
([Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs)).

## Distinction from New Coldkey Is Hotkey

`ColdKeyAlreadyAssociated` and `NewColdKeyIsHotkey` are both early existence checks on the
destination address in `do_swap_coldkey`, but they inspect different roles. The associated check
looks for staking hotkeys already linked to the destination as a coldkey, which indicates prior swap
reuse. `NewColdKeyIsHotkey` is the separate later check for whether the destination already exists
as a hotkey account anywhere on chain
([New Coldkey Is Hotkey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#newcoldkeyishotkey),
[Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs)).

- **ColdKeyAlreadyAssociated** — the destination already has coldkey-side hotkey associations.
- **NewColdKeyIsHotkey** — the destination already exists as a hotkey account.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the association lookup that raises it. It does
not describe the announcement, pending window, dispute path, or finalization step of a coldkey swap;
those are documented separately ([Coldkey Swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/coldkey-swap),
[Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs)).
