# Overflow

`Overflow` is documented in both the crowdloan pallet and the Subtensor pallet as "An overflow
occurred"
([Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs),
[Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#overflow)).

## What It Means

The runtime uses `Overflow` when a `checked_add` call would exceed the representable range for a
counter or balance field.

In the crowdloan pallet, `Overflow` can appear on `create` when incrementing `NextCrowdloanId`, on
`contribute` when updating `raised`, per-contributor totals, or `contributors_count`, and on
`refund` when incrementing the refund batch counter
([Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

In the Subtensor pallet, `Overflow` appears when `get_next_lease_id` cannot increment
`NextSubnetLeaseId` during leased-network registration
([Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

This is an arithmetic guard on ID counters or balance totals, not proof that a crowdloan id is
invalid, that a lease beneficiary is wrong, or that a contribution is below the campaign minimum.

## Why It Appears

On crowdloan `create`, `Overflow` appears when `NextCrowdloanId` cannot advance by one. On
`contribute`, it appears on whichever `checked_add` step fails while updating raised totals,
per-contributor totals, or contributor counts after earlier gates on the same call have passed. On
`refund`, it appears when the refund-loop counter cannot increment
([Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

On `register_leased_network`, `Overflow` appears when `NextSubnetLeaseId` is already at the maximum
value that can be incremented safely
([Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

When debugging `contribute`, compare against other rejections on the same call. `CapRaised` means
total raised is already at or above the cap. `InsufficientBalance` means the contributor cannot fund
the accepted amount. `Underflow` on the same extrinsic means a `checked_sub` step would underflow
instead ([Cap Raised](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#capraised),
[Insufficient Balance](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#insufficientbalance),
[Underflow](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#underflow),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

When debugging `register_leased_network`, `InvalidLeaseBeneficiary` means the caller is not the
crowdloan creator. `LeaseCannotEndInThePast` means a supplied end block is not in the future
([Invalid Lease Beneficiary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#invalidleasebeneficiary),
[Lease Cannot End In The Past](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#leasecannotendinthepast),
[Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

## Distinction from Underflow on contribute

`Overflow` and `Underflow` both appear on `contribute`, but on different arithmetic directions.
`Overflow` is returned by failing `checked_add` steps when updating raised totals, per-contributor
totals, or contributor counts. `Underflow` is returned by failing `checked_sub` steps when computing
headroom before accepting a contribution
([Underflow](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#underflow),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

- **Overflow** — a `checked_add` step would overflow.
- **Underflow** — a `checked_sub` step would underflow.

## Distinction from Cap Raised on contribute

`Overflow` and `CapRaised` both involve funding totals on `contribute`, but at different checks.
`CapRaised` is returned when `crowdloan.raised` is already at or above `crowdloan.cap` before the
accepted contribution is added. `Overflow` is returned only if a `checked_add` on raised totals or
related counters would overflow
([Cap Raised](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#capraised),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

- **CapRaised** — total raised already meets or exceeds the cap.
- **Overflow** — a `checked_add` step would overflow.

## Distinction from Invalid Lease Beneficiary on register_leased_network

`Overflow` and `InvalidLeaseBeneficiary` are both rejections on the leased-network registration
path, but at different checks. `InvalidLeaseBeneficiary` confirms the caller is the crowdloan
creator. `Overflow` is reached later when the lease ID counter itself cannot advance
([Invalid Lease Beneficiary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#invalidleasebeneficiary),
[Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

- **InvalidLeaseBeneficiary** — the caller is not the crowdloan creator.
- **Overflow** — the next subnet lease ID cannot be allocated.

## Distinction from Lease Cannot End In The Past on register_leased_network

`Overflow` and `LeaseCannotEndInThePast` both appear while registering a leased network, but on
different inputs. `LeaseCannotEndInThePast` compares a supplied optional end block against the
current block. `Overflow` means the lease ID counter cannot increment
([Lease Cannot End In The Past](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#leasecannotendinthepast),
[Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

- **LeaseCannotEndInThePast** — the supplied end block is not in the future.
- **Overflow** — the lease ID counter cannot increment.

## Distinction from Proportion Overflow

`Overflow` and `ProportionOverflow` both use overflow wording, but on separate extrinsics.
`ProportionOverflow` blocks child-hotkey scheduling when combined stake proportions exceed what the
chain can represent. `Overflow` blocks crowdloan counter updates or leased-network registration when
a `checked_add` on the relevant counter would overflow
([Proportion Overflow](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#proportionoverflow),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs),
[Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

- **ProportionOverflow** — child stake proportions do not fit.
- **Overflow** — a `checked_add` step on crowdloan or lease counters would overflow.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the `checked_add` guards that raise it on
crowdloan and lease paths. It does not report live raised totals, crowdloan ids, or lease ids on any
network; those are runtime state readers can query separately
([Crowdloans](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans),
[Bittensor Networks](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/bittensor-networks)).
