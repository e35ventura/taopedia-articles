# Transfer Disallowed

TransferDisallowed means a stake-transfer call tried to move delegated alpha between coldkeys on a
subnet where the transfer toggle was off. The standard-errors reference describes it as "Transfers
are disallowed on this subnet"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#transferdisallowed),
[Subtensor API Errors: TransferDisallowed](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#transferdisallowed)).

## What It Means

Transferring stake changes which coldkey owns a staked position without unstaking and restaking
([Transferring Stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)).

Each subnet exposes a transfer toggle through the ToggleTransfer hyperparameter. When that setting
is false on a netuid, coldkey-to-coldkey stake transfers are blocked there
([Subnet Hyperparameters: ToggleTransfer](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#toggletransfer)).

TransferDisallowed is the policy rejection when a transfer call names such a subnet. Within-subnet
transfers depend on the origin netuid toggle. Cross-subnet transfers require the toggle to allow
transfers on both the origin and destination netuids
([Subnet Hyperparameters: ToggleTransfer](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#toggletransfer)).

This is a subnet policy gate, not proof that the signing coldkey lacks permission for the hotkey or
that the requested alpha amount exceeds the staked balance.

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a stake-transfer call is submitted to a subnet where the transfer toggle is
off. That can happen when transfers are disabled on the named subnet, when automation targets the
wrong netuid, or when a cross-subnet transfer includes a destination subnet that has transfers
turned off
([Subnet Hyperparameters: ToggleTransfer](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#toggletransfer),
[Transferring Stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)).

Readers can confirm the live transfer toggle for a subnet through the hyperparameter view before
retrying a stake transfer
([Subnet Hyperparameters: View hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#view-hyperparameters)).

When debugging, compare against other stake-move rejections. [[subtoken_disabled|Subtoken Disabled]]
means subtoken trading is turned off on the subnet.
[[account_rejects_locked_alpha|Account Rejects Locked Alpha]] means the destination coldkey has not
opted in to receive incoming locked alpha. [[non_associated_cold_key|Non Associated Cold Key]] means
the signing coldkey does not own the hotkey named in the call
([Subtensor Standard Errors: SubtokenDisabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#subtokendisabled),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NonAssociatedColdKey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#nonassociatedcoldkey)).

The practical meaning is narrow: stake transfers are disabled on at least one subnet named in the
call.

## Distinction from Subtoken Disabled

TransferDisallowed and [[subtoken_disabled|Subtoken Disabled]] both block stake-management calls,
but they test different subnet switches. Subtoken Disabled means subtoken trading is unavailable on
the subnet because SubtokenEnabled is false. Transfer Disallowed means the transfer toggle is off
and coldkey-to-coldkey stake transfers are blocked even when other stake actions may still be open
([Subtensor Standard Errors: SubtokenDisabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#subtokendisabled)).

## Distinction from Account Rejects Locked Alpha

TransferDisallowed and [[account_rejects_locked_alpha|Account Rejects Locked Alpha]] both stop stake
moves, but at different layers. Transfer Disallowed is the subnet-wide transfer toggle. Account
Rejects Locked Alpha means the destination coldkey rejects incoming locked alpha on an otherwise
permitted transfer path
([Subtensor Standard Errors: AccountRejectsLockedAlpha](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#accountrejectslockedalpha)).

## Distinction from Non Associated Cold Key

TransferDisallowed and [[non_associated_cold_key|Non Associated Cold Key]] both block stake-transfer
calls, but for different reasons. Non Associated Cold Key means the signing coldkey is not paired
with the hotkey named in the call. Transfer Disallowed means the subnet policy forbids transfers
even when coldkey-hotkey pairing would otherwise pass
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NonAssociatedColdKey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#nonassociatedcoldkey)).

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the transfer-toggle check that raises it. It
does not report the live toggle value for any specific subnet; that depends on chain reads at the
time of use
([Subnet Hyperparameters: View hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#view-hyperparameters),
[Transferring Stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)).
