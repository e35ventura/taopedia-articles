# Transfer Disallowed

`TransferDisallowed` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised when coldkey-to-coldkey stake
transfer is turned off on a subnet. The standard-errors reference describes it as "Transfers are
disallowed on this subnet"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#transferdisallowed)).

## What It Means

The `transfer_stake` extrinsic moves a staked position from one coldkey to another while keeping the
same hotkey. Before balance, liquidity, and lock checks run, `validate_stake_transition` reads the
per-subnet `TransferToggle` storage map and returns `TransferDisallowed` when that switch is false
for the origin netuid. If the call crosses subnets, the destination netuid must also have
`TransferToggle` enabled, or the same error is returned
([Subtensor source: move_stake.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/move_stake.rs),
[Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

Subnet owners control the switch through the documented `ToggleTransfer` hyperparameter, exposed in
`btcli` as `transfers_enabled`
([[Toggle Transfer]](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a coldkey signs a `transfer_stake` call on a subnet whose transfer toggle is
off, or when a cross-subnet transfer names a destination subnet with transfers disabled. That can
happen after a subnet owner disables coldkey-to-coldkey stake transfer, when automation targets the
wrong netuid, or when a cross-subnet transfer crosses into a subnet that has closed transfers
([[Toggle Transfer]](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters),
[Managing Your Stakes](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-sdk)).

When debugging, rule out earlier gates on the same extrinsic first. `SameNetuid` means the call
would not change hotkey, coldkey, or subnet. `SubnetNotExists` means one of the named subnets is not
present. `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` means the origin position cannot cover the requested alpha
amount ([Same Netuid](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#samenetuid),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: SubnetNotExists](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#subnetnotexists),
[Not Enough Stake To Withdraw](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughstaketowithdraw)).

The practical meaning is narrow: coldkey-to-coldkey stake transfer is disabled on at least one
subnet named in the call.

## Distinction from Move Stake and Swap Stake

`TransferDisallowed` applies only to `transfer_stake`, which passes `check_transfer_toggle: true`
into the shared validation helper. `move_stake` and `swap_stake` pass `false`, so they do not read
`TransferToggle` and cannot raise this error even when transfers are disabled on the subnet
([Subtensor source: move_stake.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/move_stake.rs)).

- **TransferDisallowed** — `transfer_stake` hit a subnet with `TransferToggle` off.
- **move_stake / swap_stake** — change hotkey or subnet assignment without checking the transfer
  toggle.

## Distinction from Subtoken Disabled

`TransferDisallowed` and `SubtokenDisabled` both gate stake movement, but they test different
switches. `TransferDisallowed` means the subnet's `TransferToggle` has blocked coldkey-to-coldkey
stake transfer on `transfer_stake` paths. `SubtokenDisabled` means the subnet's `SubtokenEnabled`
setting has turned off subtoken trading on pool-backed staking paths
([Subtoken Disabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#subtokendisabled),
[Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

- **TransferDisallowed** — coldkey-to-coldkey stake transfer is disabled on the subnet.
- **SubtokenDisabled** — subtoken trading is disabled on the subnet.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the `TransferToggle` gate on `transfer_stake`.
It does not describe how subnet owners flip the toggle, how to query the live setting for a specific
netuid, or every extrinsic that moves stake without checking this switch; those are documented
separately ([[Toggle Transfer]](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters),
[Subtensor Storage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/storage)).
