# Transfer Disallowed

`TransferDisallowed` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised when stake transfers are turned
off on a subnet. The standard-errors reference describes it as "Transfers are disallowed on this
subnet"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#transferdisallowed)).

## What It Means

Each subnet can allow or block stake transfers through the `ToggleTransfer` hyperparameter. When
that setting is false on a subnet involved in a move-stake or transfer-stake call, the extrinsic is
rejected with `TransferDisallowed` instead of proceeding to amount and balance checks
([Subnet Hyperparameters: ToggleTransfer](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters),
[Transferring Stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation),
[Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

The documented hyperparameter defaults to enabled and can be changed through subnet configuration.
When the check runs, every subnet in the call path must have transfers enabled or the extrinsic
returns this error
([Subnet Hyperparameters: ToggleTransfer](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters),
[Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a move-stake or transfer-stake call reaches the transfer toggle while
`ToggleTransfer` is false for a subnet in that path. For a reader, the practical meaning is narrow:
stake transfers are disallowed on that subnet. The problem is not that subtoken trading is disabled
altogether, that the removal amount exceeds available staked alpha, or that the subnet does not
exist ([Subtoken Disabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#subtokendisabled),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughstaketowithdraw),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: SubnetNotExists](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#subnetnotexists)).

## Distinction from Subtoken Disabled

`TransferDisallowed` and `SubtokenDisabled` both gate stake movement on a subnet, but they test
different switches. `TransferDisallowed` means the subnet's `ToggleTransfer` setting has blocked
stake transfers. `SubtokenDisabled` means the subnet's `SubtokenEnabled` setting has turned off
subtoken trading altogether
([Subtoken Disabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#subtokendisabled),
[Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters)).

- **TransferDisallowed** — stake transfers are disallowed on the subnet.
- **SubtokenDisabled** — subtoken trading is disabled on the subnet.

## Distinction from Not Enough Stake To Withdraw

`TransferDisallowed` and `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` both appear around stake rearrangement, but they
reject different problems. `TransferDisallowed` is the transfer-toggle rejection when transfers are
turned off. `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` means the requested removal exceeds the staked alpha recorded
for the position on that subnet
([Not Enough Stake To Withdraw](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughstaketowithdraw),
[Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

- **TransferDisallowed** — stake transfers are disallowed on the subnet.
- **NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw** — unstake amount exceeds available staked alpha.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the `ToggleTransfer` gate that raises it. It
does not describe how to query the live setting for a specific netuid or every extrinsic that
touches `ToggleTransfer`; those are documented separately
([Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters),
[Subtensor Storage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/storage)).
