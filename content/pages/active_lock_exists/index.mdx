# Active Lock Exists

`ActiveLockExists` means a coldkey swap tried to move conviction locks onto a destination coldkey
that already holds active locked mass on at least one subnet. Before locks transfer, the runtime
scans the destination's conviction locks and returns this error when any rolled lock still has
positive locked mass
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs),
[Conviction and locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).

## What It Means

Conviction locks bind part of a coldkey's subnet alpha to a hotkey and a commitment signal
([Conviction and locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).

A coldkey swap migrates stake, hotkey associations, and conviction locks from one coldkey to
another. The destination must be clear of active locks so the source locks can transfer without
conflicting with locks the destination already owns
([Coldkey swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/coldkey-swap),
[Conviction and locked stake: Coldkey swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).

`ActiveLockExists` means the destination coldkey still has at least one conviction lock whose locked
mass is greater than zero after the chain rolls that lock forward to the current block. The swap
stops before any lock transfer begins
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs),
[Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs)).

This is a destination-lock conflict on the coldkey swap path, not proof that the source coldkey
lacks locks to move or that the announced swap hash is wrong.

## Why It Appears

This error appears when the new coldkey in a coldkey swap already participates in conviction locking
on one or more subnets. Common causes include reusing a coldkey that still holds a decaying or
perpetual lock, attempting a swap before an old lock has fully decayed to zero mass, or pointing the
swap at a wallet that was previously used for conviction staking
([Conviction and locked stake: Coldkey swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking),
[Coldkey swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/coldkey-swap)).

When debugging, compare against other coldkey-swap rejections on the same call.
`ColdKeyAlreadyAssociated` means the destination already lists staking hotkeys. `NewColdKeyIsHotkey`
means the destination account is registered as a hotkey. `AccountRejectsLockedAlpha` means the
destination has not opted in to receive incoming locked alpha from the source
([Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the destination coldkey must have no active conviction locks before
the swap can copy the source locks onto it.

## Distinction from No Existing Lock

`ActiveLockExists` and `NoExistingLock` are opposite lock-presence failures on different paths.
`ActiveLockExists` means a coldkey swap found active locks on the destination that block the
transfer. `NoExistingLock` means a move-lock call found no lock on the submitted subnet for the
signing coldkey to reassign
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

- **ActiveLockExists** — the destination coldkey already has active locked mass.
- **NoExistingLock** — no lock exists on the subnet for the move-lock call.

## Distinction from Account Rejects Locked Alpha

`ActiveLockExists` and `AccountRejectsLockedAlpha` both involve coldkey swaps and conviction locks,
but they test different destination requirements. `AccountRejectsLockedAlpha` means the destination
coldkey rejects incoming locked alpha by default and has not opted in to receive the source locks.
`ActiveLockExists` means the destination already owns active conviction locks that must be cleared
first
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

- **ActiveLockExists** — destination already holds active conviction locks.
- **AccountRejectsLockedAlpha** — destination has not opted in to receive incoming locked alpha.

## Distinction from Cold Key Already Associated

`ActiveLockExists` and `ColdKeyAlreadyAssociated` both block coldkey swaps, but at different stages.
`ColdKeyAlreadyAssociated` means the destination coldkey already has staking hotkeys linked to it.
`ActiveLockExists` means the destination has active conviction locked mass even if those hotkey
associations are otherwise acceptable
([Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs)).

- **ActiveLockExists** — active conviction locks remain on the destination.
- **ColdKeyAlreadyAssociated** — the destination already has linked staking hotkeys.

## Distinction from Insufficient Stake For Lock

`ActiveLockExists` and `InsufficientStakeForLock` both involve conviction locks, but on different
operations. `InsufficientStakeForLock` is raised when a lock-stake call names more alpha than the
coldkey holds on a subnet. `ActiveLockExists` is raised when a coldkey swap cannot proceed because
the destination coldkey already has active locked mass
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

- **ActiveLockExists** — destination locks block the coldkey swap.
- **InsufficientStakeForLock** — total subnet alpha is below the lock-stake request.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the active-lock check on the coldkey swap path.
It does not report whether a particular coldkey currently holds active conviction locks on any live
subnet; that depends on chain state at observation time
([Conviction and locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).
