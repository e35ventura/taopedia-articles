# Delegate Take Too Low

`DelegateTakeTooLow` means a delegate-take update failed a direction or minimum check on whichever
extrinsic was called. The standard-errors reference describes it as "Delegate take is too low"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#delegatetoolow)).

Validator take is the commission percentage a delegate keeps from delegated returns before the
remainder is shared with delegators. The chain exposes separate extrinsics to raise and lower that
commission
([Staking and Delegation Overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)).

## What It Means

`DelegateTakeTooLow` means the submitted take value failed the direction or minimum check on the
called path, so the update is rejected before storage changes
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#delegatetoolow)).

On the increase path, the new take must be strictly greater than the hotkey's current take when a
current value already exists. On the decrease path, the new take must be strictly lower than the
current value and must stay at or above the chain's minimum allowed take.

## Why It Appears on Increase

`DelegateTakeTooLow` on the increase path means the caller submitted a take that is equal to or
below the hotkey's stored take, so the call is not a valid increase.

When debugging increases, rule out the ceiling and cooldown first. `DelegateTakeTooHigh` means the
submitted value exceeds the chain maximum. `DelegateTxRateLimitExceeded` means a prior take increase
has not cleared the delegate-take cooldown yet
([Subtensor Standard Errors: DelegateTakeTooHigh](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#delegatetakehigh),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: DelegateTxRateLimitExceeded](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#delegatetxratelimitexceeded),
[Chain Rate Limits: Delegate take rate limit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits#delegate-take-rate-limit)).

The practical meaning on this path is narrow: the requested value does not actually raise take.

## Why It Appears on Decrease

`DelegateTakeTooLow` on the decrease path covers two separate rejections: the submitted value is not
strictly below the current take, or it would fall below the chain's minimum allowed take.

Decreases take effect immediately, unlike take increases that are paced by a cooldown. On this path,
`DelegateTakeTooLow` is a value-and-direction rejection rather than a timing failure
([Chain Rate Limits: Delegate take rate limit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits#delegate-take-rate-limit)).

## Distinction from Delegate Take Too High

`DelegateTakeTooLow` and `DelegateTakeTooHigh` are paired bound checks on delegate-take changes, but
they apply on different rejection paths. `DelegateTakeTooLow` rejects invalid increases, invalid
decreases, or values below the minimum on the decrease path. `DelegateTakeTooHigh` rejects only
increase calls whose submitted value is above the chain maximum
([Subtensor Standard Errors: DelegateTakeTooHigh](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#delegatetakehigh)).

- **DelegateTakeTooLow** — the submitted take is not a valid increase or decrease, or is below the
  minimum.
- **DelegateTakeTooHigh** — the submitted take exceeds the configured maximum on increase.

## Distinction from Delegate Tx Rate Limit Exceeded

`DelegateTakeTooLow` and `DelegateTxRateLimitExceeded` can both appear while a delegate is trying to
change take, but they reject different problems. `DelegateTakeTooLow` is a value check on the
submitted commission. `DelegateTxRateLimitExceeded` is the cooldown rejection that applies only when
a take increase is retried too soon
([Delegate Tx Rate Limit Exceeded](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#delegatetxratelimitexceeded),
[Chain Rate Limits: Delegate take rate limit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits#delegate-take-rate-limit)).

- **DelegateTakeTooLow** — the submitted take fails direction or minimum checks.
- **DelegateTxRateLimitExceeded** — the increase was attempted before the cooldown expired.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the direction or minimum checks that raise it
on the increase and decrease paths. It does not explain delegation rewards, how delegators choose
validators, or the full cooldown rule for repeated take increases; those are documented separately
([Staking and Delegation Overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation),
[Chain Rate Limits: Delegate take rate limit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits#delegate-take-rate-limit)).
