# Delegate Take Too Low

`DelegateTakeTooLow` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised by delegate-take update calls.
The standard-errors reference describes it as "Delegate take is too low"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#delegatetoolow)).

## What It Means

Validator take is the commission percentage a delegate keeps from delegated returns before the
remainder is shared with delegators. The chain exposes separate extrinsics to raise and lower that
commission. `DelegateTakeTooLow` means the submitted take value failed the direction or minimum
check on whichever extrinsic was called, so the update is rejected before storage changes
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#delegatetoolow),
[Staking and Delegation Overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation),
[Subtensor source: increase_take.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/increase_take.rs),
[Subtensor source: decrease_take.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/decrease_take.rs)).

On the increase path, `do_increase_take` requires the new take to be strictly greater than the
hotkey's current take when a current value already exists. On the decrease path, `do_decrease_take`
requires the new take to be strictly lower than the current value, and also requires the result to
stay at or above `MinDelegateTake`
([Subtensor source: increase_take.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/increase_take.rs),
[Subtensor source: decrease_take.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/decrease_take.rs)).

## Why It Appears on Increase

The delegation reference describes `increase_take` as the extrinsic that raises delegate commission.
`DelegateTakeTooLow` on that path means the caller submitted a take that is equal to or below the
hotkey's stored take, so the call is not a valid increase
([Delegation Mechanism](https://subtensor.com/reference/mechanisms/delegation),
[Subtensor source: increase_take.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/increase_take.rs)).

For a reader, the practical meaning on this path is narrow: the requested value does not actually
raise take. The problem is not that the value exceeds the configured maximum, that the change was
attempted too soon, or that delegation itself is unavailable
([Delegate Take Too High](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#delegatetakehigh),
[Delegate Tx Rate Limit Exceeded](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#delegatetxratelimitexceeded)).

## Why It Appears on Decrease

The delegation reference describes `decrease_take` as the extrinsic that lowers delegate commission.
`DelegateTakeTooLow` on that path covers two separate rejections: the submitted value is not
strictly below the current take, or it would fall below the chain's minimum allowed take
([Delegation Mechanism](https://subtensor.com/reference/mechanisms/delegation),
[Subtensor source: decrease_take.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/decrease_take.rs)).

Because decrease is documented as effective immediately, unlike take increases that are paced by a
cooldown, this error is a value-and-direction rejection rather than a timing failure
([Chain Rate Limits: Delegate take rate limit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits#delegate-take-rate-limit)).

## Distinction from Delegate Take Too High

`DelegateTakeTooLow` and `DelegateTakeTooHigh` are paired bound checks on delegate-take changes, but
they apply on different extrinsics. `DelegateTakeTooLow` rejects invalid increases, invalid
decreases, or values below the minimum on the decrease path. `DelegateTakeTooHigh` rejects only
increase calls whose submitted value is above `MaxDelegateTake`
([Delegate Take Too High](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#delegatetakehigh),
[Subtensor source: increase_take.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/increase_take.rs),
[Subtensor source: decrease_take.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/decrease_take.rs)).

- **DelegateTakeTooLow** — the submitted take is not a valid increase or decrease, or is below the
  minimum.
- **DelegateTakeTooHigh** — the submitted take exceeds the configured maximum on increase.

## Distinction from Delegate Tx Rate Limit Exceeded

`DelegateTakeTooLow` and `DelegateTxRateLimitExceeded` can both appear while a delegate is trying to
change take, but they reject different problems. `DelegateTakeTooLow` is a value check on the
submitted commission. `DelegateTxRateLimitExceeded` is the separate cooldown rejection that applies
only when a take increase is retried too soon
([Delegate Tx Rate Limit Exceeded](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#delegatetxratelimitexceeded),
[Subtensor source: increase_take.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/increase_take.rs)).

- **DelegateTakeTooLow** — the submitted take fails direction or minimum checks.
- **DelegateTxRateLimitExceeded** — the increase was attempted before the cooldown expired.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the direction or minimum checks that raise it
on the increase and decrease paths. It does not explain delegation rewards, how delegators choose
validators, or the full cooldown rule for repeated take increases; those are documented separately
([Staking and Delegation Overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation),
[Chain Rate Limits: Delegate take rate limit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits#delegate-take-rate-limit)).
