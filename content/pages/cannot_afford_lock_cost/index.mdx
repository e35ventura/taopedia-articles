# Cannot Afford Lock Cost

`CannotAffordLockCost` means the signing coldkey cannot cover the TAO lock cost required to register
a new subnet. In `do_register_network`, the runtime reads the current lock cost through
`get_network_lock_cost()` and returns this error when `can_remove_balance_from_coldkey_account`
fails for that amount
([Subtensor source: subnet.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/subnet.rs),
[Create a Subnet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/create-a-subnet)).

The check runs after mechanism, registration-window, rate-limit, and subnet-capacity gates and
before any subnet is pruned, initialized, or charged
([Subtensor source: subnet.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/subnet.rs)).

## What It Means

Subnet registration is a coldkey-signed add-network transaction that locks TAO from the caller's
coldkey account. The create-a-subnet guide describes that cost as dynamic and tied to recent
subnet-creation demand, with a documented floor through network min lock cost
([Create a Subnet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/create-a-subnet),
[Network Min Lock Cost](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#networkminlockcost)).

In `do_register_network`, step 6 computes `lock_amount` with `get_network_lock_cost()` and rejects
the call when the coldkey cannot remove that much free balance
([Subtensor source: subnet.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/subnet.rs),
[Subtensor source: root.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/coinbase/root.rs)).

`get_network_lock_cost()` combines the last recorded lock, the network min lock floor, elapsed
blocks since the last lock, and the lock reduction interval. The helper returns the TAO amount the
registration path expects at submission time
([Subtensor source: root.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/coinbase/root.rs)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a coldkey signs a subnet-registration call but its spendable TAO balance is
below the current lock cost. That can happen when the quoted lock cost rose after the client built
the transaction, when fees or prior transfers reduced free balance, or when the wallet simply did
not reserve enough TAO for the dynamic lock amount
([Create a Subnet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/create-a-subnet)).

When debugging, rule out earlier gates on the same extrinsic. `MechanismDoesNotExist` means the
submitted mechanism index is not supported on the registration path. `SubNetRegistrationDisabled`
means subnet registration has not reached its start block. `NetworkTxRateLimitExceeded` means the
network-wide subnet-creation cooldown has not elapsed. `SubnetLimitReached` means the chain is at
its subnet cap with no eligible subnet to prune
([Subtensor Standard Errors: MechanismDoesNotExist](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#mechanismdoesnotexist),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: SubNetRegistrationDisabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#subnetregistrationdisabled),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NetworkTxRateLimitExceeded](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#networktxratelimitexceeded)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the coldkey does not have enough free TAO for the current subnet
lock cost.

## Distinction from Network Tx Rate Limit Exceeded

`CannotAffordLockCost` and `NetworkTxRateLimitExceeded` both appear on subnet-creation paths, but
they test different conditions. `NetworkTxRateLimitExceeded` is the timing gate on how often a new
subnet can be created. `CannotAffordLockCost` is the balance gate on whether the coldkey can fund
the current lock cost
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NetworkTxRateLimitExceeded](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#networktxratelimitexceeded),
[Subtensor source: subnet.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/subnet.rs)).

- **CannotAffordLockCost** — the coldkey lacks enough free TAO for the lock cost.
- **NetworkTxRateLimitExceeded** — the subnet-creation cooldown has not elapsed.

## Distinction from Subnet Limit Reached

`CannotAffordLockCost` and `SubnetLimitReached` are both raised in `do_register_network`, but at
different stages. `SubnetLimitReached` is the capacity rejection when the chain is at its subnet cap
and no eligible subnet can be pruned. `CannotAffordLockCost` is the later affordability check after
capacity logic has already passed or identified a prune target
([Subtensor source: subnet.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/subnet.rs)).

- **SubnetLimitReached** — no subnet slot is available without a prune candidate.
- **CannotAffordLockCost** — the coldkey cannot fund the lock cost.

## Distinction from Not Enough Balance To Stake

`CannotAffordLockCost` and `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` both signal a coldkey funding failure, but on
different extrinsics. `CannotAffordLockCost` applies to subnet registration through
`do_register_network`. `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` applies to neuron registration and add-stake paths
that compare the requested TAO amount against coldkey free balance on existing subnets
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Subtensor source: registration.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/registration.rs)).

- **CannotAffordLockCost** — the coldkey cannot pay the subnet-registration lock cost.
- **NotEnoughBalanceToStake** — the coldkey cannot fund the TAO amount named in a stake call.

## Distinction from Balance Withdrawal Error

`CannotAffordLockCost` and `BalanceWithdrawalError` both involve moving TAO from a coldkey, but at
different points in the registration flow. `CannotAffordLockCost` is the pre-transfer affordability
check before subnet initialization. `BalanceWithdrawalError` is documented as a withdrawal failure
from the coldkey account and can surface when the later lock transfer cannot complete even though
the earlier affordability test passed
([Subtensor Standard Errors: BalanceWithdrawalError](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#balancewithdrawalerror),
[Subtensor source: subnet.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/subnet.rs)).

- **CannotAffordLockCost** — the coldkey cannot afford the quoted lock cost at the pre-check stage.
- **BalanceWithdrawalError** — the runtime could not withdraw TAO from the coldkey account.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the lock-cost affordability check in
`do_register_network`. It does not quote a live lock cost for any network, describe subnet pruning
rules in full, or explain how to fund a coldkey; those are documented separately
([Create a Subnet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/create-a-subnet),
[Network Min Lock Cost](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#networkminlockcost)).
