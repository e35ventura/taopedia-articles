# New Coldkey Is Hotkey

`NewColdKeyIsHotkey` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised by
[coldkey swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/coldkey-swap). The standard-errors reference
describes it as "New coldkey is hotkey"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#newcoldkeyishotkey)).

## What It Means

Coldkeys and hotkeys share the same account address space, so the chain has to confirm a swap
destination is not already serving a different role. `do_swap_coldkey` checks whether the proposed
new coldkey address already exists as a hotkey account anywhere on chain, and rejects the swap with
`NewColdKeyIsHotkey` if it does
([Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs)).

The check exists to keep one account from carrying both roles at once. Letting a swap proceed onto
an address that is already someone's hotkey would blur the coldkey/hotkey distinction that the rest
of the chain's ownership and signing logic depends on
([Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs)).

## Why It Appears

This is one of two existence checks `do_swap_coldkey` runs on the destination address before
anything else proceeds. The other, separate check confirms the destination is not already associated
with an existing coldkey swap; `NewColdKeyIsHotkey` is specifically the hotkey-side version of that
same kind of guard
([Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs)).

## Distinction from Cold Key Already Associated

`NewColdKeyIsHotkey` and `ColdKeyAlreadyAssociated` are both early existence checks on the
destination address in `do_swap_coldkey`, but they check it against different roles. The associated
check covers the case where the destination has already been used as a coldkey in a prior swap.
`NewColdKeyIsHotkey` covers the separate case where the destination already exists as a hotkey
account
([Cold Key Already Associated](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#coldkeyalreadyassociated),
[Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs)).

- **ColdKeyAlreadyAssociated** — the destination has already been swapped to as a coldkey.
- **NewColdKeyIsHotkey** — the destination already exists as a hotkey account.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the existence check that raises it. It does not
describe the rest of the coldkey swap process, such as the announcement, pending window, or dispute
path; those are documented separately
([Coldkey Swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/coldkey-swap),
[Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs)).
