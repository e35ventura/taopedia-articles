Endure Network is Bittensor Subnet 30. Its on-chain identity describes it as the risk intelligence
network and lists [endure.network](https://endure.network) as the project site.

## How the Mechanism Works

Endure turns financial risk analysis into a competitive subnet task. Miners submit risk parameters
for DeFi lending markets — things like recommended loan-to-value ratios, interest rate curves, and
liquidation thresholds. After each epoch, validators measure how well those submissions matched what
actually happened in the market, scoring each miner on both safety and capital efficiency.

Emissions follow performance. Miners whose models hold up against real outcomes earn more; miners
whose models diverge lose share immediately. The operator's rationale, explained on the project
site, is that many independent analysts with different methodologies make the network harder to game
and more resilient when any single approach fails.

The consensus output from the subnet feeds into products. The first product built on Endure is
Forge, a decentralized lending market that uses the subnet's consensus risk parameters as its live
inputs. According to the project site, 60% of Forge revenue is used to buy back the subnet's alpha
token, creating a direct link between product usage and network incentives.

## Participating as a Miner

Miners compete as risk analysts. The task is to submit parameter sets — LTV recommendations,
interest rate curves, liquidation thresholds — that validators will later score against observed
market behavior. Submissions that are both safe and capital-efficient earn higher scores than those
that are too conservative or too aggressive. Current miner documentation can be found at
[endure.network](https://endure.network).

## Participating as a Validator

Validators score miner submissions each epoch by comparing them to what the market actually did. The
scoring axis covers whether a miner's model correctly predicted market behavior and whether its
recommended parameters would have been safe for deployed capital. Validators then submit weights
through [[Yuma Consensus]], directing emissions toward the miners whose risk models most
consistently matched observed outcomes.

## On-Chain Identity

Live SN30 data is available on [TaoStats](https://taostats.io/subnets/30). The Finney identity
registers the subnet name as Endure Network, the description as the risk intelligence network, and
the subnet URL as endure.network.
