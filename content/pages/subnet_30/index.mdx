# Subnet 30: Endure Network

Endure Network is Bittensor Subnet 30, operated by the Endure team. Its incentive mechanism
organizes a competitive market for financial risk intelligence: miners produce risk parameters such
as loan-to-value recommendations, interest rate curves, and liquidation thresholds, while validators
continuously score those submissions against real market outcomes. The subnet's first downstream
product is Forge, a Bittensor-native money market that consumes the network's consensus risk output
directly. The project describes itself as building "the risk intelligence network" and is accessible
at [endure.network](https://endure.network).

## How the Mechanism Works

Endure's incentive mechanism centers on outcome-scored risk analysis. Each epoch, miners submit risk
parameters covering the safety and efficiency of capital deployment in financial markets. Those
submissions are scored against what actually happened in those markets, measuring whether a miner's
recommended parameters would have been both safe and capital-efficient.

Validators apply [[Yuma Consensus]] to aggregate miner weight vectors and determine per-epoch
emissions. Performance is continuous: miners that predict market behavior accurately earn more
emissions, and miners whose models diverge from outcomes lose share immediately. The design
intentionally prevents any single team from dominating through tenure or reputation alone.

The website describes two competing scoring pressures — security and capital efficiency. Miners are
not rewarded only for conservative parameters; they must find the equilibrium between maximizing
available capital and controlling protocol risk. Submissions that are too conservative under-utilize
capital, while submissions that are too aggressive increase risk. Validators measure both
dimensions.

## Participating as a Miner

Miners on Endure compete as independent risk analysts. The network accepts diverse methodologies,
and the scoring design rewards accuracy over any particular approach. Miners register on Subnet 30,
submit risk parameter recommendations each epoch, and receive emissions in proportion to how well
those recommendations align with observed market outcomes.

Because the scoring layer evaluates parameters against real capital behavior rather than synthetic
benchmarks, miners benefit from domain expertise in financial risk modeling, lending markets, and
on-chain capital dynamics. The website notes that diverse methodologies are a feature: the network
is designed to adapt when any single model breaks.

## Participating as a Validator

Validators on Subnet 30 run the scoring logic that compares miner submissions to real market
outcomes. Each epoch, validators evaluate whether a miner's recommended LTV ratios, interest rate
curves, and liquidation thresholds were accurate predictors of what markets required. Validators
hold a [[Validator Permit]], run the Endure validator code, and submit weight vectors that determine
miner emissions via [[Yuma Consensus]].

Validators benefit from the same outcome data that powers scoring: real market behavior is the
ground truth, not a manually curated benchmark. This aligns validator incentives with producing
accurate assessments rather than favoring any particular miner methodology.

## On-Chain Identity

identity reports the subnet name as Endure Network, the description as "The risk intelligence

public GitHub repository field in the registered identity record.
