Endure Network is Bittensor Subnet 30, a decentralized risk intelligence network for DeFi. Its
registered on-chain description is "The risk intelligence network" and its project site is
[endure.network](https://endure.network). The subnet turns open competition among independent risk
analysts into a continuous, market-tested consensus on lending parameters. The first product built
on Endure's output is Forge, a Bittensor-native lending market that uses the network's risk
parameters to govern lending conditions.

## How the Mechanism Works

Miners on Endure act as independent risk analysts. Each epoch, they submit recommended values for
DeFi lending parameters such as loan-to-value ratios, interest rate curves, and liquidation
thresholds. Because miners apply different analytical approaches, the network maintains
methodological diversity; when one model breaks down under unusual market conditions, others
continue to produce accurate outputs.

Validators score each miner's recommendations against what actually happened on-chain and in
markets. A submission is judged on two dimensions: whether it would have protected lenders from
losses (safety), and whether it would have attracted the most capital at the right risk level
(capital efficiency). Scoring is continuous throughout the epoch — validators compare predictions to
real outcomes rather than running a periodic batch review — and the results feed into the
[[Yuma Consensus]] weight matrix that determines miner emissions. Miners whose parameters prove
accurate over time earn the largest share of emissions.

## Participating as a Miner

Miners participate by submitting risk parameter recommendations each epoch. Success depends on the
quality of the underlying analytical methodology. The network scores submissions against real market
outcomes, so analysts who track live conditions and iterate on their models are rewarded over time.
The [Endure Network website](https://endure.network) is the authoritative source for current
participation details.

## Participating as a Validator

Validators check whether each miner's submitted parameters would have been safe and efficient under
actual market conditions during the epoch. They require access to reliable on-chain and market data
sources to make those comparisons accurately. Their scoring feeds directly into the
[[Yuma Consensus]] weight matrix. The [Endure Network website](https://endure.network) is the
authoritative source for current validator requirements.
