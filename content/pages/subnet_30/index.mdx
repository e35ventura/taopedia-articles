Endure Network is Bittensor Subnet 30, a decentralized risk intelligence platform. Its on-chain
description is "The risk intelligence network," and its public website is
[endure.network](https://endure.network). The subnet organizes independent analysts into a
competitive, continuously scored network that produces financial risk parameters for DeFi protocols
and other financial products.

Reference: [Endure Network website](https://endure.network)

## How the Mechanism Works

The core idea behind Endure is that financial risk intelligence — LTV recommendations, interest rate
curves, liquidation thresholds — should be produced competitively and scored against real outcomes
rather than managed by closed internal teams or static models.

Miners submit risk parameters that cover areas such as lending collateral ratios, interest rate
recommendations, and liquidation thresholds. Diverse methodologies are encouraged: the network is
designed so that no single model dominates, and when one approach breaks down under market
conditions, others compensate.

Validators score each submission against what actually happened in the markets over the epoch. The
central scoring question is whether a miner's parameters were accurate and safe: did the model
predict market behavior correctly, and were the recommended parameters efficient under live
conditions? Scoring is continuous rather than periodic, and emissions follow performance
immediately. Top performers earn a larger share; poor performers lose share without delay. The
network does not reward tenure or reputation — only accuracy.

Reference: [Endure Network — how it works](https://endure.network)

## Participating as a Miner

Miners on Endure are independent risk analysts or automated models that produce financial risk
parameters each epoch. To participate, operators register on SN30 and submit parameter sets covering
the active risk challenges. The mechanism rewards diverse methodology: a miner whose model agrees
with consensus but fails under stress conditions loses emissions faster than one with a distinct,
correct approach.

Prospective miners should consult [endure.network](https://endure.network) for current participation
requirements, as the network's active challenges and submission specifications are maintained there.

## Participating as a Validator

Validators on Endure measure miner submissions against real market outcomes at the end of each
epoch. Scoring captures both predictive accuracy (did the miner's model anticipate market behavior?)
and parameter quality (were the recommended thresholds safe and efficient given what actually
happened?). Validator weights feed into [[Yuma Consensus]] to distribute emissions.

Reference: [Endure Network website](https://endure.network)

## Products Powered by Endure

The first product built on the Endure consensus risk intelligence layer is Forge, a lending market
that consumes the network's output directly. Forge provides Endure with live demand, real outcome
data, and a proving ground for the subnet's parameters. The roadmap describes future expansion into
broader protocol integrations, institutional risk services, and additional financial markets as the
miner population and model capabilities scale.

Reference: [Endure Network — Forge](https://endure.network)
