Endure Network is Bittensor Subnet 30. Its on-chain identity describes the subnet as "the risk
intelligence network," lists [endure.network](https://endure.network) as the subnet URL, and
registers no GitHub repository on-chain. The subnet has 256 active neurons and operates under
[[Yuma Consensus]].

## How the Mechanism Works

Endure turns competitive financial analysis into on-chain risk intelligence. Miners submit
parameters — loan-to-value ratios, interest rate curves, and liquidation thresholds — for financial
products that need continuous risk management. Validators score those submissions against what
actually happens in the market each epoch: did the recommended parameters keep capital safe? Did
they achieve the efficiency targets they projected?

The scoring is continuous rather than periodic. Because real capital is at stake in downstream
products, miners face immediate consequences for poor models. The
[Endure website](https://endure.network) describes the design philosophy as accuracy over tenure:
top performers earn more, poor performers lose share immediately, and no reputation moat insulates
incumbents from better models.

Endure's first product is Forge, a Bittensor-native money market powered by the subnet's consensus
risk output. Forge allows users to borrow TAO against alpha token collateral or lend TAO for yield.
The parameters that govern Forge — collateral ratios, interest curves, liquidation triggers — are
set by the Endure miner competition rather than by a team or governance vote. The website states
that 60% of Forge protocol revenue is used to buy back the subnet's alpha token.

## Participating as a Miner

Miners on Endure produce risk parameter submissions. Operators should consult the
[Endure documentation](https://docs.endure.network/) for the current submission format, active
challenge specifications, and registration requirements. The website describes miner
responsibilities as submitting LTV recommendations, interest rate curves, and liquidation thresholds
that are measurable against real market behavior.

Because submissions are scored against live outcomes, miners need both analytical competence and
reliable infrastructure for timely delivery each epoch. The documentation at
[docs.endure.network](https://docs.endure.network/) is the authoritative source for current
participant guidance.

## Participating as a Validator

Validators coordinate scoring for the network. Each epoch, validators measure submitted parameters
against what actually happened in the market. The website describes validators as scoring against
real outcomes — whether predictions were accurate and whether recommended parameters were safe and
capital-efficient.

Validators set weights based on those outcome scores, which then feed into [[Yuma Consensus]] to
distribute emissions. Operators should verify current validator requirements and scoring criteria in
the [Endure documentation](https://docs.endure.network/).

## On-Chain Identity

Live SN30 data is available on [TaoStats](https://taostats.io/subnets/30). The current on-chain
identity reports the subnet name as Endure Network, the registered description as "The risk
intelligence network," the subnet URL as endure.network, and no registered GitHub repository. The
subnet has 256 neurons on Finney.
