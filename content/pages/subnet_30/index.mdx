Endure Network is Bittensor Subnet 30. Its on-chain identity describes the subnet as a risk
intelligence network and lists [endure.network](https://endure.network) as the subnet URL. The live
identity does not publish a GitHub repository.

## How the Mechanism Works

Endure turns financial risk analysis into a competitive on-chain task. Miners act as independent
analysts who submit risk parameters — including loan-to-value recommendations, interest rate curves,
and liquidation thresholds — for DeFi lending markets. Each submission represents a distinct
methodology, and the diversity of approaches is intentional: the network is designed so that no
single model dominates, and the consensus output adapts when any individual methodology degrades.

Every epoch, a validator layer scores miner submissions against what actually happened in the
underlying markets. Scoring is continuous rather than periodic: validators measure whether a miner's
parameters were safe, efficient, and predictively accurate relative to realized price movements and
liquidation events. Emissions follow that scoring directly — top performers earn a larger share, and
underperformers lose share immediately. The design removes reputation moats and tenure advantages;
only sustained accuracy produces consistent rewards.

## Participating as a Miner

Miners on Endure compete by producing risk intelligence — specifically, quantitative parameters for
financial markets such as collateral ratios, interest rate functions, and liquidation criteria. The
subnet's scoring mechanism compares each submission to realized market outcomes, so miners must
develop or apply models that accurately forecast risk rather than optimize for surface-level
metrics. Because the network favors diverse methodologies, there is no single required approach to
the modeling task. Operators should treat the Endure documentation at
[endure.network](https://endure.network) as the primary source for current miner setup guidance.

## Participating as a Validator

Validators on Endure score miner submissions against realized market data each epoch. Because the
scoring baseline is real outcomes rather than a fixed reference model, validators must have access
to the same market data used to evaluate risk parameters. Validators who correctly measure
performance accuracy determine weight assignments, which flow into [[Yuma Consensus]] and govern
miner emissions. Operators should consult the Endure documentation for current validator
requirements.

## Products Built on Endure

The network's first downstream application is Forge, a lending market that consumes Endure's
consensus risk parameters directly. Forge provides the subnet with live market outcome data that
feeds back into the validator scoring loop, creating a closed feedback system between the risk
intelligence layer and a real financial product. The subnet's roadmap describes further distribution
through APIs and protocol integrations, as well as expansion into institutional risk use cases.
