# Subnet 30: Endure Network

Endure Network is Bittensor Subnet 30, a decentralized risk intelligence network. Its registered
on-chain description is "The risk intelligence network," and its project site is
[endure.network](https://endure.network). The subnet produces risk parameters — including
loan-to-value recommendations, interest rate curves, and liquidation thresholds — through open
competition among miners, with validators scoring submissions against real market outcomes rather
than against models or reputation.

The first product built on Endure's consensus output is Forge, a Bittensor-native money market that
uses the network's risk intelligence to set lending parameters.

References: [endure.network](https://endure.network)

## How the Mechanism Works

Miners on Endure submit risk parameters for DeFi assets. Each miner independently proposes values
such as LTV ratios, interest rate curves, and liquidation thresholds. Because different analysts
apply different methodologies, the network maintains a diverse set of models; when any single
approach breaks down, others continue to score well.

Validators score submissions against what actually happened on-chain and in markets. Each epoch, the
network measures whether a miner's proposed parameters were both safe and efficient — whether they
would have protected lenders from losses while also not over-restricting capital. Continuous scoring
replaces periodic reviews, and emissions flow to miners whose predictions prove accurate over time.

Top performers earn the largest share of emissions. Miners whose models consistently predict market
behavior correctly accumulate stake and grow their share. Poor performance immediately reduces a
miner's emissions, with no tenure protection.

References: [How Endure works (endure.network)](https://endure.network)

## Participating as a Miner

Miners on Endure are risk analysts competing to deliver the most accurate DeFi risk parameters.
Participation requires an understanding of DeFi market dynamics, collateral risk, and lending
economics. Miners register on Subnet 30 and submit their recommended risk parameters each epoch. The
network scores those recommendations against real outcomes, so performance depends on the quality of
the underlying analytical methodology rather than on compute resources alone.

References: [endure.network](https://endure.network)

## Participating as a Validator

Validators measure miner submissions against what actually occurred in markets. Each epoch,
validators compare submitted risk parameters against real market data to determine which miner
recommendations would have been safe and efficient. Scoring is continuous — validators run
throughout the epoch rather than on a fixed schedule — and the results feed directly into the
[[Yuma Consensus]] weight matrix that determines miner emissions.

References: [endure.network](https://endure.network)
