# Existential Deposit

An existential deposit is the minimum TAO balance required for an account to exist on the Bittensor
blockchain. The official
[Glossary: Existential Deposit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#existential-deposit)
describes accounts below this threshold as reaped, or removed, to limit dust accounts and conserve
network resources.

## Account Lifecycle

The term comes from a broader account-state pattern in Substrate-based chains. Polkadot's official
[existential deposit article](https://support.polkadot.network/support/solutions/articles/65000168651-what-is-the-existential-deposit-)
describes it as the minimum amount that keeps an account active and explains that the rule prevents
very small balances from bloating chain state.

On Bittensor, the
[glossary entry](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#existential-deposit) applies the
idea to TAO balances on chain. When an account balance falls below the existential deposit, the
account can be removed from active chain state rather than preserved as a live account with a dust
balance.

## Runtime Context

The [Bittensor glossary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#existential-deposit)
identifies the existential deposit as a runtime constant in the Balances pallet configuration. That
makes it a chain parameter, not a general transaction fee or a staking minimum.

Because the current amount is a chain value, the important reader-facing idea is the threshold
itself, not a hard-coded number. Readers who need the exact current threshold should use the current
official docs or supported tooling.

## Reader Boundary

The existential deposit is not a fee paid to perform an action, a delegation amount, or a validator
requirement. It is the account-existence threshold that determines whether an on-chain account can
remain active with its balance.
