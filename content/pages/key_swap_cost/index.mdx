# Key Swap Cost

Key swap cost is a fixed, network-wide fee, defined by the runtime constant `KeySwapCost`, that a
coldkey pays when it swaps a hotkey or a coldkey. It is one global amount for the whole network,
exposed as a `u64` in rao, TAO's base unit. The constants reference lists `keySwapCost` with a value
of `100000000` rao, which is `0.1` TAO
([Subtensor Constants](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/constants),
[Subtensor source: utils/misc.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/utils/misc.rs)).

## When It Is Charged

On a hotkey swap, the runtime reads the swap cost, requires the coldkey to hold at least that much
free balance, and rejects the call with `NotEnoughBalanceToPaySwapHotKey` when it does not. If the
balance is sufficient, the cost is removed from the coldkey's account and recycled
([Subtensor source: swap/swap_hotkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_hotkey.rs)).

On a coldkey swap, the same cost is charged once, at the first announcement of the swap. A later
re-announcement of the same swap does not charge it again
([Subtensor source: macros/dispatches.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/macros/dispatches.rs)).

## What It Is and Is Not

Key swap cost is a flat rao amount, not a percentage of stake or balance, and it is the same for
every account because it comes from a single network-wide constant rather than a per-subnet or
per-account setting
([Subtensor source: utils/misc.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/utils/misc.rs)).

Because the charged rao is recycled rather than transferred to another account, paying the cost
reduces the caller's balance without crediting a counterparty
([Subtensor source: swap/swap_hotkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_hotkey.rs)).

## Distinction from Swap Rate Limits

Key swap cost and the swap rate limits guard different things on a swap. Key swap cost is a one-time
rao fee for performing a swap. The rate limits instead cap how often swaps may occur, and a swap
attempted too soon is rejected on timing rather than on cost
([Subtensor source: swap/swap_hotkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_hotkey.rs)).

- **Key swap cost** — the flat rao fee a swap charges.
- **Swap rate limits** — how frequently a swap may be performed.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the `KeySwapCost` constant, its rao unit, and when it is charged. It does not
document the full hotkey or coldkey swap procedures; the live constant value can be read from chain
constants ([Subtensor Constants](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/constants)).
