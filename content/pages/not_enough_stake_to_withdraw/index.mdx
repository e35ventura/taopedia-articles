# Not Enough Stake To Withdraw

`NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` means the requested alpha removal exceeds the staked balance held by the
hotkey-coldkey pair on the targeted subnet. The standard-errors reference describes it as "Not
enough stake to withdraw"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughstaketowithdraw)).

Stake is tracked per hotkey, coldkey, and subnet. Remove-stake and move-stake calls name an alpha
amount to decrease for that pair on a `netuid`
([Staking and Delegation Overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)).

## What It Means

`NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` means the pair does not hold enough staked alpha on the subnet to cover
the amount named in the extrinsic, so the call is rejected
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughstaketowithdraw),
[Staking and Delegation Overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)).

For debugging, treat this as a staked-alpha shortfall for the specific hotkey-coldkey pair on the
subnet, not as proof that the coldkey lacks free TAO for a new stake, that the hotkey account does
not exist, or that the subnet itself is missing
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyAccountNotExists](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeyaccountnotexists),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: SubnetNotExists](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#subnetnotexists)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when the unstake or move-stake amount in the extrinsic is larger than the staked
alpha recorded for that hotkey-coldkey pair on the subnet. That can happen after partial prior
unstakes, when the client cached an outdated stake figure, or when the call targets the wrong subnet
for the pair's stake
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughstaketowithdraw),
[Staking and Delegation Overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the removal request exceeds what that pair has staked there.

## Distinction from Not Enough Balance To Stake

`NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` and `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` both block staking extrinsics, but they
compare different balances on opposite paths. `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` is raised on add-stake calls
when the signing coldkey does not have enough free balance to fund the requested TAO amount.
`NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` is raised on remove-stake and move-stake paths when the requested alpha
removal exceeds the pair's existing staked alpha on the subnet
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughstaketowithdraw)).

- **NotEnoughBalanceToStake** — the coldkey cannot pay the TAO needed to add stake.
- **NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw** — the pair does not have enough staked alpha to remove.

## Distinction from Not Enough Stake To Set Weights

`NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` and `NotEnoughStakeToSetWeights` both involve stake balances, but on
different extrinsics. `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` blocks unstaking when the requested removal exceeds
the staked alpha held by a hotkey-coldkey pair on a subnet. `NotEnoughStakeToSetWeights` blocks
weight submission when total stake weight is below the global threshold
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughStakeToSetWeights](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughstaketosetweights),
[Stake Threshold](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters)).

- **NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw** — unstake amount exceeds available staked alpha.
- **NotEnoughStakeToSetWeights** — stake weight is too low to submit weights.

## Distinction from Stake Unavailable

`NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` and `StakeUnavailable` both block stake removal, but they compare against
different balances. `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` means the hotkey-coldkey pair lacks enough total
staked alpha on the subnet for the requested amount. `StakeUnavailable` means the requested amount
exceeds unlocked alpha after conviction locks are applied
([Subtensor Standard Errors: StakeUnavailable](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#stakeunavailable),
[Conviction and Locked Stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).

- **NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw** — total staked alpha on the pair is too low.
- **StakeUnavailable** — the requested amount exceeds unlocked alpha on the subnet.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the staked-alpha comparison that raises it. It
does not describe conviction lock rules, swap slippage limits, or minimum unstake thresholds in
full; those are separate topics

[Staking and Delegation Overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)).
