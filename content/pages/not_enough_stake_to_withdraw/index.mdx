# Not Enough Stake To Withdraw

`NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised on stake-removal paths.
The standard-errors reference describes it as "Not enough stake to withdraw"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughstaketowithdraw)).

## What It Means

Stake is tracked per hotkey, coldkey, and subnet. Before an unstake or move-stake call proceeds, the
runtime compares the requested alpha amount against the staked balance for that specific pair on the
targeted `netuid`. `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` means the caller asked to remove more alpha than that
pair currently holds on the subnet, so the extrinsic stops before swap and counter updates run
([Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs),
[Subtensor source: remove_stake.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/remove_stake.rs)).

In `validate_remove_stake`, this comparison happens through `calculate_reduced_stake_on_subnet`,
which returns the error when the current stake is less than the requested decrement
([Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

## Why It Appears

This error fires when the unstake amount in the extrinsic is larger than the staked alpha recorded
for that hotkey-coldkey pair on the subnet, such as after partial prior unstakes, when the client
cached an outdated stake figure, or when the call targets the wrong subnet for the pair's stake
([Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

For a reader, the practical meaning is narrow: the removal request exceeds what that pair has staked
there. The problem is not that the coldkey lacks free TAO for a new stake, that the hotkey account
does not exist, or that the subnet itself is missing
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyAccountNotExists](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeyaccountnotexists),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: SubnetNotExists](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#subnetnotexists)).

## Distinction from Not Enough Balance To Stake

`NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` and `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` both block staking extrinsics, but they
compare different balances on different paths. `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` is raised on add-stake
calls when the signing coldkey does not have enough free balance to fund the requested TAO amount.
`NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` is raised on remove-stake and move-stake paths when the requested alpha
removal exceeds the pair's existing staked alpha on the subnet
([Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

- **NotEnoughBalanceToStake** — the coldkey cannot pay the TAO needed to add stake.
- **NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw** — the pair does not have enough staked alpha to remove.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning we mean by “stake to withdraw” and the staked-alpha comparison that
raises the error. It does not describe lock rules that can reduce available unstake separately, swap
slippage limits, or minimum unstake thresholds; those are checked elsewhere in the same validation
path
([Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).
