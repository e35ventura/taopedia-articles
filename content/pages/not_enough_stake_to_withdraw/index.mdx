# Not Enough Stake To Withdraw

`NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` means a remove-stake or move-stake call requested more alpha than the
hotkey-coldkey pair currently holds on that netuid. The standard-errors reference describes it as
"Not enough stake to withdraw"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughstaketowithdraw)).

Stake is tracked per hotkey, coldkey, and subnet. An unstake compares the requested alpha amount
against the balance recorded for that specific pair on the targeted netuid, not against the
coldkey's free TAO balance.

## What It Means

Before an unstake or move-stake call proceeds, the chain compares the requested alpha amount against
the staked balance for that hotkey-coldkey pair on the targeted `netuid`. `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw`
means the caller asked to remove more alpha than that pair currently holds on the subnet, so the
extrinsic stops before swap and counter updates run
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughstaketowithdraw)).

This is a staked-alpha comparison on the removal path. It does not test whether the coldkey has free
TAO available to add new stake.

## Why It Appears

This error fires when the unstake amount in the extrinsic is larger than the staked alpha recorded
for that hotkey-coldkey pair on the subnet, such as after partial prior unstakes, when the client
cached an outdated stake figure, or when the call targets the wrong subnet for the pair's stake.

When debugging, compare add-stake and remove-stake failures before assuming an account or subnet
problem. `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` means the coldkey lacks free TAO on the add-stake path.
`HotKeyAccountNotExists` and `SubnetNotExists` are separate rejections on other checks
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyAccountNotExists](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeyaccountnotexists),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: SubnetNotExists](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#subnetnotexists)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the removal request exceeds what that pair has staked there.

## Distinction from Not Enough Balance To Stake

`NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` and `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` both block staking extrinsics, but they
compare different balances on different paths. `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` is raised on add-stake
calls when the signing coldkey does not have enough free balance to fund the requested TAO amount.
`NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` is raised on remove-stake and move-stake paths when the requested alpha
removal exceeds the pair's existing staked alpha on the subnet
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake)).

- **NotEnoughBalanceToStake** — not enough free TAO to add stake.
- **NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw** — not enough staked alpha to remove.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the staked-alpha comparison that raises it. It
does not describe lock rules that can reduce available unstake separately, swap slippage limits, or
minimum unstake thresholds; those are checked elsewhere in the same validation path.
