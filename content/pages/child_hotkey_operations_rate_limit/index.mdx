# Child Hotkey Operations Rate Limit

Child hotkey operations rate limit controls how often a parent hotkey can set or revoke
[child hotkeys](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys) on a given subnet. The
rate-limits reference puts it at 150 blocks, roughly 30 minutes, and notes that it applies per
subnet ([Rate Limits](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits)).

## What It Controls

[Child hotkeys](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys) let a parent hotkey
delegate a proportion of its stake weight to other hotkeys, which then participate in consensus as
if they held that stake. This rate limit is the cooldown between changes to that arrangement on a
subnet, so it governs how frequently the parent's child configuration can change rather than how the
stake is split among children
([Child Hotkeys](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys)).

## When It Matters

A common misconception is that revoking children is a separate, unrestricted action from setting
them. In practice, a revoke is treated as another child-configuration change: it draws from the same
roughly 150-block budget as a set, so a parent cannot set children and then immediately undo or
replace that choice on the same subnet
([Rate Limits](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits)).

Consider a parent hotkey that has just assigned children on netuid 1 and then notices a wrong
proportion. It cannot correct the mistake right away: any further change to its children on netuid
1, whether adjusting the list or clearing it, must wait until the roughly 150-block (~30-minute)
window elapses. Because the limit is scoped per subnet, the same parent is free to manage its
children on a different subnet during that wait; only netuid 1 is on cooldown
([Child Hotkeys](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys),
[Rate Limits](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits)).

## Why the Limit Exists

Counting sets and revokes against one cooldown stops a parent from rapidly churning child
assignments to shift where its stake weight lands from one epoch to the next. Keeping the limit per
subnet makes the restriction targeted, so a recent change on one subnet does not freeze the parent's
child management everywhere
([Rate Limits](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits)).

## Distinction from Child Key Take Rate Limit

The two limits gate different child-hotkey actions. This rate limit covers setting or revoking the
children themselves, while the child key take rate limit covers changing a child's take percentage,
on a far longer cycle ([Child Hotkeys](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys)).

- **Child hotkey operations rate limit** — about 150 blocks between set or revoke operations.
- **Child key take rate limit** — about 30 days between take changes.
