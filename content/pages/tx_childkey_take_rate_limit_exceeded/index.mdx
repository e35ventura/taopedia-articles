# Tx Childkey Take Rate Limit Exceeded

`TxChildkeyTakeRateLimitExceeded` is a documented Subtensor standard error that signals a
childkey-take increase was attempted before the childkey-take cooldown had elapsed. The
standard-errors reference describes it as "Childkey take rate limit exceeded," and the rate-limits
reference ties it to the child key take rate limit
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#txchildkeytakeratelimitexceeded),
[Chain Rate Limits: Child key take rate limit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits#child-key-take-rate-limit)).

## What It Means

[Childkey take](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys) is the share of validation
dividends a child hotkey keeps when validating on behalf of a parent on one subnet. This error is
about pacing increases to that rate, not about whether child-hotkey relationships exist or whether
dividends are being earned. The rate-limits reference documents a 216,000-block cooldown between
successful childkey-take increases, approximately 30 days
([Chain Rate Limits: Child key take rate limit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits#child-key-take-rate-limit),
[Child Hotkeys](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys)).

When this error appears, the narrow meaning is that a childkey-take increase was retried too soon.
It is a timing rejection on the increase path, not a statement that the submitted take is outside
the allowed range
([Subtensor Standard Errors: InvalidChildkeyTake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#invalidchildkeytake),
[Subtensor source: set_children.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/set_children.rs)).

## Why It Appears

In `set_children.rs`, `do_set_childkey_take` applies the cooldown only when the submitted take is
higher than the stored take for that hotkey on the selected subnet. If the increase path fails the
subnet-specific rate-limit check, it returns `TxChildkeyTakeRateLimitExceeded` before updating
storage
([Subtensor source: set_children.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/set_children.rs)).

Child-hotkey documentation states that lowering take can proceed without waiting out that window.
The cooldown applies only to increases, and it is separate from the pacing rule on assigning or
revoking child hotkeys through `set_children`
([Child Hotkeys](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys),
[Chain Rate Limits: Child hotkey operations rate limit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits#child-hotkey-operations-rate-limit)).

For a reader, the practical meaning is narrow: the caller tried to raise childkey take again before
the increase cooldown cleared. The problem is not an out-of-range take value
([Invalid Childkey Take](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#invalidchildkeytake)).

## Distinction from Invalid Childkey Take

`TxChildkeyTakeRateLimitExceeded` and `InvalidChildkeyTake` both appear on childkey-take updates,
but they reject different stages. `InvalidChildkeyTake` is the range check on the submitted value.
`TxChildkeyTakeRateLimitExceeded` is the later cooldown rejection when the new take is higher than
the stored take and the rate limit has not elapsed
([Invalid Childkey Take](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#invalidchildkeytake),
[Subtensor source: set_children.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/set_children.rs)).

- **InvalidChildkeyTake** — the submitted take is outside the allowed range.
- **TxChildkeyTakeRateLimitExceeded** — a take increase was attempted before the cooldown expired.

## Distinction from Delegate Tx Rate Limit Exceeded

`TxChildkeyTakeRateLimitExceeded` and `DelegateTxRateLimitExceeded` are both take-increase cooldown
errors, but they belong to different extrinsics. The childkey-take error paces increases to the
parent-child dividend split on one subnet. The delegate-take error paces increases to validator
commission on delegated returns
([Delegate Tx Rate Limit Exceeded](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#delegatetxratelimitexceeded),
[Chain Rate Limits: Delegate take rate limit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits#delegate-take-rate-limit),
[Chain Rate Limits: Child key take rate limit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits#child-key-take-rate-limit)).

- **TxChildkeyTakeRateLimitExceeded** — childkey-take increase cooldown breach.
- **DelegateTxRateLimitExceeded** — delegate-take increase cooldown breach.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the childkey-take increase cooldown that raises
it. It does not tell a reader exactly when a specific hotkey will next be able to raise take on a
live network; that depends on chain state and when the last successful increase occurred. The
documented reference point is the child key take rate-limit rule itself
([Chain Rate Limits: Child key take rate limit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits#child-key-take-rate-limit)).
