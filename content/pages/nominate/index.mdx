# Nominate

To nominate is to stake TAO with a validator or delegate in Bittensor. The
[Glossary: Nominate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominate) describes
nomination as the staking process by which TAO holders participate through validators.

## Distinction from Nominator

A nominator is the participant role that delegates TAO to a validator. Nominate is the staking
action that attaches that support to a chosen validator hotkey
([Glossary: Nominator](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominator),
[Glossary: Nominate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominate)).

Role vocabulary names who supplies support; nominate vocabulary names the attachment action.

## Distinction from Delegation

Delegation names the support relationship attaching nominated TAO to a validator hotkey. Nominate
names the specific action that creates or increases that support
([Glossary: Delegation](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#delegation),
[Glossary: Nominate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominate)).

- **Delegation** — support relationship behind a validator hotkey.
- **Nominate** — action that stakes TAO with a chosen validator.

Relationship vocabulary names ongoing support; nominate vocabulary names the attachment action
([Glossary: Nominator](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominator)).

## Distinction from Staking

Staking is the general process of attaching TAO support to a validator hotkey. Nominate is the
glossary action label for a nominator performing that attachment to a delegate
([Glossary: Staking](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#staking),
[Glossary: Nominate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominate)).

General process vocabulary and nominator-action vocabulary answer different reader questions.

The
[staking and delegation documentation](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)
uses delegation to describe staking TAO with validators. In Taopedia prose, nominate should be read
as delegation vocabulary rather than as a subnet role, account count, or chain-state claim.

## Distinction from Delegate

A delegate is a validator that can receive nominated stake. Nominate is the action a nominator takes
to attach TAO to that validator hotkey
([Glossary: Delegate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#delegate),
[Glossary: Nominate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominate)).

Receiving-role vocabulary and support-action vocabulary sit on opposite sides of the same delegation
relationship.

## Distinction from Effective Stake

Nominate is the action of staking TAO with a chosen validator. Effective stake is the combined total
recorded behind that validator hotkey after nomination is included
([Glossary: Nominate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominate),
[Glossary: Effective Stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#effective-stake)).

Effective stake updates after nomination is recorded on chain. It reflects delegate-owned stake plus
nominated support behind the same hotkey, so a nominate action changes the measured total even
before any subsequent emission or reward outcome is credited
([Staking and delegation overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation),
[Glossary: Nominator](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominator)).

- **Nominate** — action that attaches TAO support to a validator hotkey.
- **Effective stake** — combined total recorded behind that hotkey after nomination.

## Distinction from Unstaking

Unstaking withdraws delegated stake back toward the coldkey. Nominate attaches TAO support to a
validator hotkey
([Glossary: Unstaking](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#unstaking),
[Glossary: Nominate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominate)).

Nomination and unstaking move support in opposite directions on the same delegation path. A
nominator uses nominate to increase or begin delegated support on a validator hotkey, while
unstaking removes previously delegated alpha or TAO from that relationship and returns it toward the
owner's coldkey
([Staking and delegation overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation),
[Staking and Delegation: Unstaking back to TAO](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation#unstaking-back-to-tao)).

Readers should not read nomination as proof that stake can immediately exit again. Unstaking follows
subnet and wallet rules for the delegated position, and returned funds arrive on the nominator's
coldkey rather than on the validator hotkey that received the nomination
([Glossary: Unstaking](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#unstaking)).

- **Nominate** — attach TAO support to a validator hotkey.
- **Unstaking** — withdraw delegated stake back toward the owner's coldkey.

## Distinction from Yuma Consensus

Nominate names the staking action that attaches TAO support to a validator hotkey. Yuma Consensus
settles validator weight submissions into emission shares each tempo
([Glossary: Nominate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominate),
[Yuma Consensus](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus)).

- **Nominate** — attach TAO support to a validator hotkey.
- **Yuma Consensus** — on-chain mechanism that uses validator weights to determine emission shares
  ([Yuma Consensus: Validator emissions](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus#validator-emissions)).

Action vocabulary names where stake is placed; Yuma vocabulary names reward settlement
([Staking and delegation overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)).

## Distinction from Validator Hotkey

Nominate names the delegation action of staking TAO on a validator hotkey. Validator hotkey names
the operational key that receives that delegated support
([Glossary: Nominate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominate),
[Glossary: Hotkey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#hotkey)).

- **Nominate** — delegation action attaching TAO to a validator.
- **Validator hotkey** — operational key receiving delegated support.

Action vocabulary names the staking step; hotkey vocabulary names the destination key
([Glossary: Delegate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#delegate)).

## Distinction from Nomination Applies Within a Subnet

A netuid selects which subnet context is in scope. Nominate names the delegation action supporting a
validator inside that context
([Glossary: Netuid](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#netuid),
[Glossary: Nominate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominate)).

- **Netuid** — selector for which subnet market is in scope.
- **Nominate** — delegation action attaching TAO to a validator hotkey.

## Distinction from Delegation Rewards

Nominating and delegation rewards relate as a staking action and its subsequent payout. To nominate
is to assign TAO to a validator hotkey, while delegation rewards are the share of validator-side
emissions a nominator receives from that delegated stake after the validator's take is applied
([Glossary: Nominate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominate),
[Staking and delegation overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation),
[Emission: Distribution](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions#distribution)).

Delegation rewards therefore follow nomination rather than replacing it. The staking documentation
describes nominators as participants who stake TAO with validators and receive a share of the
validator's rewards, net of take, after Yuma credits validator-side emissions on the selected netuid
([Staking and delegation overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation),
[Yuma Consensus: Validator emissions](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus#validator-emissions)).

Nomination alone does not guarantee a payout amount. Rewards still depend on validator performance,
subnet emission context, take settings, and the distribution pass on the relevant netuid
([Glossary: Validator Take %](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-take-),
[Emission](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions)).

- **Nominate** — the act of assigning stake to a validator's hotkey.
- **Delegation rewards** — the emissions earned from that stake, net of validator take.

## Reader Boundary

Nominate should not be read as a subnet role, a completed governance appointment, or proof that
emissions were received. In staking vocabulary it names the delegation action of attaching TAO to a
validator hotkey ([Glossary: Nominate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominate),
[Staking and delegation documentation](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)).
