# Nominator

A nominator is a TAO holder who stakes with a validator in Bittensor delegation vocabulary
([Glossary: Nominator](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominator),
[Staking and delegation overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)).

## What It Represents

Nominator names the supplying side of the staking relationship. A
[delegate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#delegate) receives that nominated
support and performs validation work
([Staking and delegation overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)).

Readers should separate four terms that sound similar:

- **Nominator** — the TAO-holder role that supplies support behind a validator hotkey.
- **Delegate** — the receiving validator role on the other side of that relationship.
- **Delegation** — the ongoing support relationship created by that stake assignment.
- **Nominate** — the on-chain action that attaches support to the chosen validator.

Nominate is the action. Nominator is the role label for the holder who performs it. Delegation names
the relationship after support is attached
([Glossary: Nominate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominate),
[Glossary: Delegation](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#delegation)).

[Delegate stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#delegate-stake) is TAO the
validator stakes from its own balance. Nominator vocabulary identifies support from other holders.
[Effective stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#effective-stake) combines both
when readers need the full backing total behind a hotkey
([Staking and delegation overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)).

Staking documentation distinguishes delegating to root validators on Subnet Zero from delegating to
mining-subnet validators. Nominator names the TAO-holder role in either path
([Staking into Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation#staking-into-subnets),
[Glossary: Root Subnet/Subnet Zero](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#root-subnetsubnet-zero)).

Nominating does not transfer ownership of the TAO. The nominator's coldkey keeps custody of the
staked balance and can unstake it later, so delegation assigns support to a validator's hotkey
without handing the holder's TAO to the validator
([Staking and delegation overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation),
[Glossary: Nominator](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominator)).

The delegation overview also documents two participation boundaries for nominators. Staking is
always local to a subnet, so nominated support is attached to a delegate hotkey on a chosen netuid
rather than as one global stake balance. Support delegated to the same validator on netuid 1 is
independent from support delegated to that validator's hotkey on another subnet
([Staking and Delegation Overview: Staking into Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation#staking-into-subnets),
[Glossary: Netuid](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#netuid)).

The same guide lists a minimum required stake of 0.1 TAO for nominators. Readers comparing delegate
hotkeys on a subnet such as netuid 1 should treat that floor as part of eligibility before calling
`btcli stake add --netuid 1` toward a chosen validator
([Staking and Delegation Overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation),
[BTCLI: stake add](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/btcli#btcli-stake-add)).

Nominator returns are also net of validator take at tempo distribution. The same overview documents
a default validator take of 18% on delegated-stake emissions, configurable by the delegate, so by
default a nominator should expect the delegate to retain that share before the proportional split
reaches stakers unless that validator has set a different take
([Staking and Delegation Overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation),
[Glossary: Validator Take %](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-take-),
[Emission: Distribution](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions#distribution)).

Nominator examples from one network environment should not be read as representing production
staking relationships on another
([Bittensor Networks](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/bittensor-networks)).

## Distinction from Delegate

- **Nominator** — supplying role that attaches TAO support to a chosen validator.
- **Delegate** — receiving validator role that performs validation and earns validator take.

## Distinction from Delegation

- **Delegation** — the support relationship created by staking TAO behind a validator hotkey.
- **Nominator** — the TAO-holder role that supplies that support.

## Distinction from Nominate

- **Nominate** — the on-chain action that attaches support to a validator hotkey.
- **Nominator** — the holder role associated with that action.

## Distinction from Delegate Stake

- **Nominator** — TAO-holder role supplying delegated support from other holders.
- **Delegate stake** — TAO the validator stakes from its own balance on the same hotkey.

## Distinction from Delegation Rewards

- **Nominator** — supplying role that attaches nominated TAO to a hotkey.
- **Delegation rewards** — return outcomes for nominated support after validator take and
  settlement.

## Distinction from Senate

- **Nominator** — supplying role that delegates TAO behind a validator.
- **Senate** — governance body of top delegate keys reviewing proposals.

## Reader Boundary

Nominator should not be collapsed into delegate. The nominator supplies support, and the delegate
receives support as the validator role
([Glossary: Nominator](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominator),
[Glossary: Delegate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#delegate)).

The role label does not by itself identify a subnet, validator, live balance, minimum eligibility,
or reward outcome.
