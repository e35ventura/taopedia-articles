# Nominator

A nominator is a Bittensor staking role. The
[Glossary: Nominator](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominator) entry defines it
as an account that stakes TAO on a validator's hotkey. In plain terms, a nominator backs a validator
with TAO and can participate in subnet-related emissions without performing the validator's
evaluation work.

## Staking Role

Nominators sit on the staker side of Bittensor's staking relationship. The official
[Staking/Delegation Overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)
describes staking into subnets and validators, while the glossary ties the nominator role to TAO
staked on a validator hotkey. The important reader-facing point is the role split: the nominator
supplies stake, while the validator performs validation.

References: [Glossary: Nominator](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominator),
[Staking/Delegation Overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)

## Relationship to Delegates

A delegate is the validator side of the relationship. The
[Glossary: Delegate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#delegate) entry describes a
delegate as a subnet validator that receives staked TAO from delegators and performs validation
tasks. A nominator is not the same role as the delegate; the nominator chooses where to stake TAO,
while the delegate is the validator receiving that stake.

References: [Glossary: Nominator](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominator),
[Glossary: Delegate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#delegate)

## Dividends and Control

The glossary says nominators participate in subnet consensus and earn dividends while keeping
control of their tokens. This should be read as a role-level description, not as a guarantee of a
particular return. Dividend outcomes depend on validator and subnet context, and current values need
current network data.

Reference: [Glossary: Nominator](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominator)

## Reader Boundary

Nominator is a participant role, not a validator ranking, a live staking recommendation, or a
transaction guide. The nearby term
[Glossary: Nominate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominate) names the process
of staking TAO on a validator hotkey; this article is about the account role that does so.

References: [Glossary: Nominator](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominator),
[Glossary: Nominate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominate)
