# No Neuron Id Available

`NoNeuronIdAvailable` means a registration call reached the subnet capacity stage and found no UID
that could be assigned. The standard-errors reference describes it as "No neuron ID is available in
the subnet"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#noneuronidavailable)).

The check runs after registration-policy, hotkey-membership, burn-price, and balance gates have
already passed
([Subtensor source: registration.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/registration.rs)).

## What It Means

Neuron registration assigns each hotkey a UID slot on a subnet up to the subnet's `MaxAllowedUids`
limit. In `do_register`, the runtime rejects the call with `NoNeuronIdAvailable` when
`get_max_allowed_uids` is zero, or when the subnet is already at capacity and `get_neuron_to_prune`
returns no replacement candidate
([Subtensor source: registration.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/registration.rs),
[Subnet Hyperparameters: MaxAllowedUids](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#maxalloweduids)).

Inside `register_neuron`, the same error is returned when the subnet is full and pruning logic
cannot select a UID to replace, so no slot can be handed to the registering hotkey
([Subtensor source: registration.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/registration.rs)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a coldkey signs a registration call on a subnet that is already full and has
no eligible prune target, or when the configured UID cap for that subnet is zero. That can happen
during high competition for slots, when every existing UID is protected from pruning, or when
automation retries registration against a saturated subnet
([Subtensor source: registration.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/registration.rs)).

When debugging, rule out earlier gates on the same extrinsic first. `SubNetRegistrationDisabled`
means registration is turned off on the subnet. `HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet` means the hotkey
already holds a UID there. `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` means the coldkey cannot fund the current burn
price
([Subtensor Standard Errors: SubNetRegistrationDisabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#subnetregistrationdisabled),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#hotkeyalreadyregisteredinsubnet),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#notenoughbalancetostake)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the subnet has no UID slot available for a new registration.

## Distinction from Subnet Registration Disabled

`NoNeuronIdAvailable` and `SubNetRegistrationDisabled` both block registration, but at different
stages. `SubNetRegistrationDisabled` is the policy gate when `NetworkRegistrationAllowed` is false.
`NoNeuronIdAvailable` is the capacity rejection after registration is allowed and the caller has
passed earlier funding and membership checks
([Subtensor Standard Errors: SubNetRegistrationDisabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#subnetregistrationdisabled),
[Subtensor source: registration.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/registration.rs)).

- **SubNetRegistrationDisabled** — registration is turned off on the subnet.
- **NoNeuronIdAvailable** — registration is allowed but no UID slot is available.

## Distinction from Hotkey Already Registered In Subnet

`NoNeuronIdAvailable` and `HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet` are both registration failures, but they
describe opposite slot states. `HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet` means the signing hotkey already
occupies a UID on that subnet. `NoNeuronIdAvailable` means the subnet cannot assign a new UID to any
hotkey because capacity is exhausted
([Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#hotkeyalreadyregisteredinsubnet),
[Subtensor source: registration.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/registration.rs)).

- **HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet** — the hotkey already has a UID on the subnet.
- **NoNeuronIdAvailable** — the subnet has no free or replaceable UID slot.

## Distinction from Not Enough Balance To Stake

`NoNeuronIdAvailable` and `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` can both appear on `do_register`, but they
reject different problems. `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` is the funding check against the current
registration burn price. `NoNeuronIdAvailable` is the later capacity check after burn pricing and
coldkey-hotkey pairing have already passed
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Subtensor source: registration.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/registration.rs)).

- **NotEnoughBalanceToStake** — the coldkey cannot fund the registration burn.
- **NoNeuronIdAvailable** — the subnet has no assignable UID slot.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the capacity checks that raise it in
`do_register` and `register_neuron`. It does not describe pruning-score rules, immunity windows, or
how to read the live UID count for a subnet; those are documented separately
([UID Trimming](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#uid-slot),
[Subnet Hyperparameters: MaxAllowedUids](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#maxalloweduids)).
