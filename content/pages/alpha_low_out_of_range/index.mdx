# Alpha Low Out Of Range

`AlphaLowOutOfRange` means a `sudo_set_alpha_values` call submitted an alpha low bound that fails
the chain's range check. The standard-errors reference describes it as "Alpha low value is out of
allowed range"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#alphalowoutofrange)).

The check applies only after liquid alpha is enabled on the target subnet, authorization has passed,
and the submitted `alpha_high` has cleared the alpha high floor
([Subnet Hyperparameters: LiquidAlphaEnabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#liquidalphaenabled)).

## What It Means

The `sudo_set_alpha_values` extrinsic writes the `(alpha_low, alpha_high)` pair that bounds liquid
alpha bond smoothing. In `do_set_alpha_values`, the runtime computes a minimum floor from
`u16::MAX / 40` and returns `AlphaLowOutOfRange` when the submitted `alpha_low` is strictly below
that floor or strictly above the submitted `alpha_high` value
([Subtensor source: run_epoch.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/epoch/run_epoch.rs),
[Subnet Hyperparameters: alpha_low](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#alphalow)).

Alpha low is the lower bound of the liquid alpha range that sets how slowly validator-miner bonds
smooth when alignment is weak
([Consensus-based Weights](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/consensus-based-weights)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a caller submits `sudo_set_alpha_values` with an `alpha_low` that is too
small, inverted relative to `alpha_high`, or otherwise outside the allowed pair. Mis-scaled units,
copied extrinsics with swapped bounds, or automation that does not validate the pair before
submission can hit this error even when liquid alpha is already enabled on the subnet
([Subtensor source: run_epoch.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/epoch/run_epoch.rs)).

When debugging, rule out earlier gates on the same extrinsic first. `LiquidAlphaDisabled` means
liquid alpha is not enabled on the subnet. `AlphaHighTooLow` means the submitted `alpha_high` value
is below the minimum floor before the alpha low check runs
([Subtensor Standard Errors: LiquidAlphaDisabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#liquidalphadisabled),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: AlphaHighTooLow](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#alphahightoolow)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the submitted alpha low u16 is outside the allowed range relative
to the floor and the submitted alpha high.

## Distinction from Alpha High Too Low

`AlphaLowOutOfRange` and `AlphaHighTooLow` are both range checks in `do_set_alpha_values`, but they
guard different submitted values and run in sequence. `AlphaHighTooLow` compares only `alpha_high`
against the minimum floor. `AlphaLowOutOfRange` compares `alpha_low` against the same floor and also
requires `alpha_low` to stay at or below the submitted `alpha_high`
([Subtensor source: run_epoch.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/epoch/run_epoch.rs)).

- **AlphaHighTooLow** — the submitted alpha high is below the minimum floor.
- **AlphaLowOutOfRange** — alpha low is below the floor or above alpha high.

## Distinction from Liquid Alpha Disabled

`AlphaLowOutOfRange` and `LiquidAlphaDisabled` can both appear on `sudo_set_alpha_values`, but at
different stages. `LiquidAlphaDisabled` is the feature toggle rejection when `LiquidAlphaEnabled` is
false. `AlphaLowOutOfRange` is the later pair-range rejection after the toggle and alpha high floor
checks have already passed
([Subtensor Standard Errors: LiquidAlphaDisabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#liquidalphadisabled),
[Subtensor source: run_epoch.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/epoch/run_epoch.rs)).

- **LiquidAlphaDisabled** — liquid alpha is not enabled on the subnet.
- **AlphaLowOutOfRange** — alpha low is below the floor or above alpha high.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the alpha low range check in
`do_set_alpha_values`. It does not describe how liquid alpha varies bonds during an epoch, how to
read live alpha bounds for a subnet, or how bonds moving average behaves when liquid alpha is off;
those are documented separately
([Subnet Hyperparameters: alpha_low](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#alphalow),
[Yuma Consensus: Bonding mechanics](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus#bonding)).
