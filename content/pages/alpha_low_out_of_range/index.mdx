# Alpha Low Out Of Range

`AlphaLowOutOfRange` means a `sudo_set_alpha_values` call submitted an alpha low bound outside the
allowed range. The standard-errors reference describes it as "Alpha low value is out of allowed
range"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#alphalowoutofrange)).

The check applies only after liquid alpha is enabled on the target subnet and the submitted alpha
high value has already passed its own floor check
([Subnet Hyperparameters: LiquidAlphaEnabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#liquidalphaenabled),
[Alpha High Too Low](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#alphahightoolow)).

## What It Means

`sudo_set_alpha_values` writes the alpha low and alpha high pair that bounds liquid alpha bond
smoothing. `AlphaLowOutOfRange` means the submitted alpha low was either below the chain's minimum
allowed floor or above the alpha high value named in the same call, so the chain rejected the update
and left the stored bounds unchanged
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#alphalowoutofrange),
[Subnet Hyperparameters: alpha_low](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#alphalow)).

Alpha low is the lower bound of the liquid alpha range that sets how slowly validator-miner bonds
can smooth when alignment is weak
([Consensus-based Weights](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/consensus-based-weights)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a caller submits `sudo_set_alpha_values` with an alpha low that is too small
or that sits above the alpha high submitted in the same extrinsic. Swapped bounds, mis-scaled
values, or automation that does not validate the pair before broadcast can hit this error even when
liquid alpha is already enabled on the subnet
([Subnet Hyperparameters: alpha_low](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#alphalow)).

When debugging, rule out earlier gates on the same extrinsic first. `LiquidAlphaDisabled` means
liquid alpha is not enabled on the subnet. `AlphaHighTooLow` means the submitted alpha high is below
the minimum floor, which is checked before the alpha low range test runs
([Subtensor Standard Errors: LiquidAlphaDisabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#liquidalphadisabled),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: AlphaHighTooLow](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#alphahightoolow)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the submitted alpha low does not form a valid lower bound relative
to the chain floor and the alpha high in the same call. The problem is not that liquid alpha is
still off or that alpha high alone is below the minimum floor.

## Distinction from Alpha High Too Low

`AlphaLowOutOfRange` and `AlphaHighTooLow` are both bound checks on `sudo_set_alpha_values`, but
they guard different submitted values and run in sequence. `AlphaHighTooLow` rejects an alpha high
that is below the minimum floor. `AlphaLowOutOfRange` rejects an alpha low that is below that same
floor or above the submitted alpha high, and it is only reached after the alpha high floor check has
already passed
([Subtensor Standard Errors: AlphaHighTooLow](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#alphahightoolow)).

- **AlphaHighTooLow** — the submitted alpha high is below the minimum floor.
- **AlphaLowOutOfRange** — alpha low is below the floor or above alpha high.

## Distinction from Liquid Alpha Disabled

`AlphaLowOutOfRange` and `LiquidAlphaDisabled` can both appear on alpha-bounds updates, but at
different stages. `LiquidAlphaDisabled` is the feature-toggle rejection when liquid alpha is still
off. `AlphaLowOutOfRange` is a later range rejection after the toggle and alpha high floor checks
have already passed
([Subtensor Standard Errors: LiquidAlphaDisabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#liquidalphadisabled)).

- **LiquidAlphaDisabled** — liquid alpha is not enabled on the subnet.
- **AlphaLowOutOfRange** — alpha low is outside the allowed range.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the alpha low range check on
`sudo_set_alpha_values`. It does not describe how liquid alpha varies bonds during an epoch, how to
read live alpha bounds for a subnet, or how bonds moving average behaves when liquid alpha is off;
those are documented separately
([Subnet Hyperparameters: alpha_low](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#alphalow),
[Consensus-based Weights](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/consensus-based-weights)).
