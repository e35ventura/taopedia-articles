# Cap Too Low

`CapTooLow` is a crowdloan pallet error raised when a submitted funding cap sits below a required
lower bound. The pallet defines it as "The crowdloan cap is too low"
([Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

## What It Means

Each [crowdloan](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans) names a funding cap — the total
amount the campaign can raise. The runtime applies `CapTooLow` on two extrinsics with different
comparisons.

On `create`, the cap must be strictly greater than the creator's initial deposit. On `update_cap`,
the new cap must be at least as large as total raised so far
([Crowdloans: Crowdloan Lifecycle](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans#crowdloan-lifecycle),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

This is a cap-floor rejection on campaign setup or cap updates, not proof that total raised already
equals the cap on `finalize`, that the creator deposit is below the runtime minimum, or that the
campaign was already finalized.

## Why It Appears

On `create`, this error appears when the submitted cap is less than or equal to the creator deposit.
On `update_cap`, it appears when the crowdloan creator submits a new cap below current total raised
after the campaign loads and creator-origin checks pass
([Crowdloans: Can the campaign parameters be updated mid‑flight?](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans#can-the-campaign-parameters-be-updated-midflight),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

When debugging, compare against other rejections on the same call. On `create`, `DepositTooLow`
means the initial deposit is below the runtime minimum deposit. On `update_cap`, `AlreadyFinalized`
means the campaign already completed finalization and `InvalidOrigin` means the signer is not the
crowdloan creator. `CapNotRaised` is the separate rejection on `finalize` when total raised still
differs from the cap
([Deposit Too Low](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#deposittoolow),
[Cap Not Raised](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#capnotraised),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

The practical meaning is narrow: raise the cap above the relevant lower bound before retrying
`create` or `update_cap`.

## Distinction from Cap Not Raised

`CapTooLow` and `CapNotRaised` both involve the funding cap, but on different extrinsics and checks.
`CapTooLow` rejects a cap parameter that is too small relative to the creator deposit on `create` or
to total raised on `update_cap`. `CapNotRaised` rejects `finalize` when total raised still differs
from the cap ([Cap Not Raised](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#capnotraised),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

- **CapTooLow** — the submitted cap is below a required lower bound.
- **CapNotRaised** — raised total still differs from the cap on finalize.

## Distinction from Deposit Too Low

`CapTooLow` and `DepositTooLow` both appear on `create`, but at different checks. `DepositTooLow`
means the initial deposit is below the runtime minimum deposit. `CapTooLow` means the funding cap is
not strictly greater than the deposit
([Deposit Too Low](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#deposittoolow),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

- **CapTooLow** — the cap is not greater than the creator deposit.
- **DepositTooLow** — the creator deposit is below the runtime minimum deposit.

## Distinction from Cap Raised

`CapTooLow` and `CapRaised` both involve the funding cap, but on different extrinsics. `CapTooLow`
rejects cap settings that are too low on `create` or `update_cap`. `CapRaised` rejects `contribute`
when total raised has already reached the stored cap
([Cap Raised](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#capraised),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

- **CapTooLow** — the submitted cap is below a required lower bound.
- **CapRaised** — the crowdloan's total funding cap is already full on contribute.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the cap lower-bound checks on `create` and
`update_cap`. It does not report a particular crowdloan's live cap or raised amount on any network;
those are campaign state readers can query separately
([Crowdloans](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans),
[Bittensor Networks](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/bittensor-networks)).
