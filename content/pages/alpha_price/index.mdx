# Alpha Price

Alpha price is the price of a subnet's alpha token denominated in TAO. The official
[Understanding Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-subnets) documentation
sets the ideal price of a subnet's alpha token as the subnet's TAO reserve divided by its alpha
reserve, and the [Emission](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions) documentation states
the same relationship: a subnet's token price is its TAO in reserve divided by its alpha in reserve.

## Pool Price Context

Each Bittensor subnet runs as an automated market maker with two reserves: a TAO reserve and a
subnet-specific alpha reserve.
[Understanding Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-subnets) describes the
subnet pool this way and derives the alpha token's price from those two reserves. Alpha price is
therefore a pool-derived quote, not a balance you hold or a number set by an external exchange.

Because the price is read off the pool, it is expressed as TAO per alpha: the amount of TAO in
reserve for each unit of alpha in reserve. The same documentation gives a worked example where a
pool with more alpha than TAO in reserve yields an alpha price well below one TAO.

## Relationship To Reserve Ratio

Alpha price is closely tied to the [[reserve_ratio|Reserve Ratio]] but is not the same article-level
idea. The reserve ratio describes the relationship between the [[tao_reserve|TAO Reserve]] and the
[[alpha_reserve|Alpha Reserve]] that must be read together. Alpha price is the directional result of
that relationship: TAO reserve over alpha reserve, read as a per-token price.
[Understanding Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-subnets) makes this
explicit, stating that a subnet pool's reserve ratio determines the price of its alpha token.

Keeping the two separate helps when reading claims. A statement about the reserve ratio is about the
pool relationship; a statement about alpha price is about the TAO-denominated quote that
relationship produces.

## Ideal Price Versus Executed Amount

The reserve-based figure is an ideal price for the current pool state, not a guaranteed execution
rate. Staking moves TAO into the pool and unstaking moves alpha out, and both actions change the
reserves, so the amount actually received can differ from the ideal price. That difference is
[[slippage|Slippage]], which
[Understanding Slippage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/slippage) explains as the gap between
an expected amount and the amount received when converting through subnet pool reserves.

For a reader, the boundary is simple: alpha price names the current pool quote, while slippage
describes why a real staking or unstaking outcome can land away from that quote.

## Emission Injection And Price Stability

Subnet emissions interact with the same reserves without distorting the alpha price by themselves.
The [Emission](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions#alpha-reserve-injection)
documentation explains that when TAO is injected into a subnet's TAO reserve, alpha is injected in
proportion to that TAO so the pool can grow without the injection itself changing the alpha price.
The proportional alpha amount is the injected TAO divided by the current price.

This is why reserve growth from emissions is described as price-neutral at the moment of injection:
both reserves move together, so their ratio, and therefore the alpha price, is preserved by the
injection step.

## Per-Subnet Prices

Alpha price is subnet-specific. Each subnet has its own alpha token and its own reserves under
[[dynamic_tao|Dynamic TAO]], so each subnet has its own alpha price rather than a single shared
value. [[alpha_tokens|Alpha Tokens]] are the per-subnet asset being priced, and two subnets with
different reserve balances will quote different alpha prices at the same time.

## Reader Boundary

Alpha price is a current-state value read from subnet pool reserves. It is not a live exchange
guarantee, a subnet ranking, a yield figure, or investment advice. Exact prices and reserve balances
are chain state that should be checked in current official tools or chain data, and any specific
figure needs the source behind it.
