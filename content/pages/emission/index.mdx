# Emission

Emission describes newly distributed token rewards. In Bittensor, emission is the tokenomics term
readers encounter when discussing how rewards are allocated to network participants through the
protocol's incentive system.

References: [Glossary: Emission](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#emission),
[Emission](https://docs.bittensor.com/emissions)

## Reward Context

Emission is different from a transfer between two ordinary wallets. A transfer moves existing tokens
from one holder to another, while emission describes protocol distribution of newly allocated
rewards. This distinction helps separate token movement from reward creation.

References: [Glossary: Emission](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#emission),
[Emission](https://docs.bittensor.com/emissions)

## Relationship to Incentives

Emission is closely tied to incentives because rewards must be allocated according to protocol
rules. In subnet context, validator evaluations and consensus outcomes help determine how rewards
are distributed among participating roles.

References: [Glossary: Emission](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#emission),
[Yuma Consensus](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/yuma-consensus/)

## Reader Boundary
