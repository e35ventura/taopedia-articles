# Emission

Emission is Bittensor's term for protocol reward distribution. The official glossary defines
emission as newly distributed token rewards, and Bittensor emissions documentation describes
distribution involving newly created TAO and subnet-specific alpha tokens.

## TAO and Alpha Context

Bittensor emissions are not only a single TAO payout. Current emissions documentation describes TAO
reserve injection and alpha distribution in the Dynamic TAO model. Alpha distribution can involve
miners, validators, stakers, and subnet creators depending on the emission stage and subnet context.

## Miner Incentives and Validator Dividends

Emission is not a simple wallet transfer. In subnet context, miner incentives and validator
dividends are reward outcomes. Validator evaluations, submitted weights, and consensus outcomes help
determine how rewards are split among participating roles.

## Difference from Transfers

A transfer moves existing tokens from one wallet to another. Emission describes protocol-level
reward distribution. Keeping those ideas separate helps readers understand why a reward event, a
staking event, and a user-initiated transfer are different token movements.

## Source Notes

This article defines the term at a high level. Exact reward amounts, timing, and live subnet shares
depend on current chain state and should be checked from current Bittensor sources.
