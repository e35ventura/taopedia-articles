# Subnet 8: Vanta

Vanta is Bittensor Subnet 8 (SN8), operated by [Taoshi](https://www.vantanetwork.io/). The subnet
collects trading signals — long, short, and flat positions — from miners running quantitative or
deep-learning trading systems, then uses a validator-driven scoring mechanism to reward those whose
signals produce the best risk-adjusted returns. Vanta was previously called the Proprietary Trading
Network before rebranding in 2024.

References: [taoshidev/vanta-network (GitHub)](https://github.com/taoshidev/vanta-network),
[vantanetwork.io](https://www.vantanetwork.io/)

## How the Mechanism Works

Miners submit directional signals — long, short, or flat — for supported trade pairs across three
asset classes: foreign exchange, cryptocurrency, and equities. Each signal represents the miner's
predicted direction for a given asset over a future window, not a recommendation to an end user but
a stake in a performance competition.

Validators receive these signals and track the simulated portfolio outcomes under a shared ruleset.
The ruleset accounts for carry fees (an annual holding cost applied to open positions), spread fees
for crypto pairs, and slippage proportional to order leverage. These simulated costs ensure that
only strategies that would be viable on a real exchange score well.

Scoring is debt-based: each miner accumulates a performance score tracked weekly, adjusted for
penalties. Weights are set based on the prior week's results, targeting payout completion by
midnight each Sunday. Miners must sustain strong PnL while staying within a 10 percent maximum
drawdown limit — breaching this threshold results in elimination regardless of cumulative
performance.

The validator-aggregated weights feed into [[Yuma Consensus]], which translates them into per-block
TAO emissions distributed via [[Dynamic TAO]].

References: [taoshidev/vanta-network (GitHub)](https://github.com/taoshidev/vanta-network),
[docs.taoshi.io](https://docs.taoshi.io/)

## Participating as a Miner

Miners on Vanta register on netuid 8 and submit directional trade signals during market hours for
any of the supported forex, crypto, or equity pairs. The network is openly competitive — any
quantitative, algorithmic, or deep-learning trading system can participate, provided it generates
original signals.

Plagiarism detection is built into the protocol. Validators analyze the uniqueness of each submitted
order, and miners found to be copying another miner's positions are eliminated. Eliminated hotkeys
are permanently blacklisted and cannot be re-registered — any future participation requires a
completely new hotkey. This policy prevents circumventing elimination penalties by simply
re-entering under the same identity.

Miners who score below the 15th highest ranking miner in an asset class enter a probationary period,
during which they have 30 days to outscore the 15th-ranked active miner or face elimination.
Performance, originality, and risk management are all required for long-term participation.

References: [taoshidev/vanta-network (GitHub)](https://github.com/taoshidev/vanta-network),

## Participating as a Validator

Validators on Vanta run the software that tracks miner signal submissions, applies the shared
ruleset to simulate portfolio performance, and sets weights based on the resulting scores. They do
not execute trades; instead they serve as the network's auditors, ensuring that every miner's
reported signals are correctly evaluated under consistent rules.

Validators must remain online continuously during market hours and keep their local signal databases
synchronized. Validators that maintain accurate, consistent evaluation earn emissions through
[[Yuma Consensus]]. Taoshi publishes a live dashboard at
[dashboard.taoshi.io](https://dashboard.taoshi.io/) where validator and miner performance metrics
are publicly visible.
