# Subnet 8: Vanta

Vanta is Bittensor Subnet 8 (SN8), developed and operated by [Taoshi](https://taoshi.io/). The
subnet functions as a decentralized competition for quantitative and machine-learning trading
strategies: miners submit directional signals across forex, cryptocurrency, and equity markets, and
the network scores them on actual trading performance rather than self-reported results. The best
performers earn TAO emissions; underperformers and cheaters are eliminated. Taoshi maintains a
public [trading dashboard](https://dashboard.taoshi.io/) where miner rankings and portfolio results
are visible in real time.

References: [taoshidev/vanta-network (GitHub)](https://github.com/taoshidev/vanta-network),
[vantanetwork.io](https://www.vantanetwork.io/)

## How the Mechanism Works

Vanta coordinates two parties: miners who submit trade signals and validators who score those
signals against live market data.

Each miner maintains a live simulated portfolio tracked by validators. Miners submit directional
signals — long, short, or flat — for supported asset pairs across forex, cryptocurrency, and equity
markets. Validators record each position and continuously mark it to market using live price feeds.
Returns, costs, and risk accumulate in real time until the miner closes the position or is
eliminated.

Weights are recalculated weekly based on each miner's rolling portfolio performance, so rankings
shift as strategies improve or deteriorate. Emissions follow those weights and settle weekly.

Two integrity mechanisms prevent the network from being gamed. First, signals are screened for
uniqueness across all active participants: a miner whose positions consistently mirror another
miner's is identified as plagiarizing and eliminated. Second, risk is capped: a miner whose
portfolio falls too far from its peak is removed regardless of absolute return. Together these
constraints mean only strategies with genuine, independent risk-adjusted alpha survive long-term.

A probationary tier applies to miners who fall outside the top performers in a given asset class.
Such miners have a fixed window to recover their standing before facing elimination.

Validator consensus flows into [[Yuma Consensus]], which converts weight-setting outcomes into
per-block TAO emissions distributed via [[Dynamic TAO]].

References: [taoshidev/vanta-network (GitHub)](https://github.com/taoshidev/vanta-network)

## Participating as a Miner

Miners on Vanta are traders or quantitative model developers whose strategies must deliver
independent, risk-adjusted performance across one or more supported asset classes. The subnet
supports forex, cryptocurrency, and equity pairs, and miners may concentrate in any combination.

The competition is designed to reward genuine alpha. Positions are evaluated against realistic
simulated costs — including financing charges on open positions and transaction-cost friction on
crypto pairs — so a strategy must outperform those costs to generate a positive score. Plagiarism
detection means copied signals earn nothing, reinforcing the premium on original strategy
development.

Taoshi provides community resources and strategy guidance to help miners develop effective systems,
and maintains a Discord for coordination and feedback.

Reference: [taoshidev/vanta-network (GitHub)](https://github.com/taoshidev/vanta-network)

## Participating as a Validator

Validators on Vanta operate the scoring infrastructure that receives miner signals, maintains
running portfolio state for every registered miner, and derives the performance-based weights
submitted to the network each week. Their core function is translating raw trading signals into
verified, comparable performance records across the full miner set.

To do this accurately, validators consume live price feeds for the asset classes they monitor —
marking positions to market in real time and tracking accumulated costs on each portfolio.
Validators that produce honest weights consistent with true miner performance earn emissions through
[[Yuma Consensus]].
