# Subnet 8: Vanta

Vanta is Bittensor Subnet 8 (SN8), developed and operated by [Taoshi](https://www.taoshi.io/). The
subnet functions as a decentralized tournament for quantitative and machine-learning trading
strategies: miners submit directional signals across forex, cryptocurrency, and equity markets, and
the network scores them on actual trading performance rather than self-reported results. The best
performers earn TAO emissions; underperformers and cheaters are eliminated. Taoshi maintains a
public [dashboard](https://dashboard.taoshi.io/) where miner rankings and portfolio results are
visible in real time, and offers an API for downstream consumers of the aggregated signal data.

References: [taoshidev/vanta-network (GitHub)](https://github.com/taoshidev/vanta-network),
[vantanetwork.io](https://www.vantanetwork.io/)

## How the Mechanism Works

Miners submit position signals — long, short, or flat — for supported asset pairs during market
hours. These are not merely predictions: each signal is treated as a live position in a simulated
portfolio that accumulates real returns and real costs over time, measured under conditions designed
to mirror what a live trading operation would actually face.

The scoring system is continuous and competitive. Weights are recalculated weekly from each miner's
rolling portfolio performance, so rankings shift as strategies drift or deteriorate. Emissions
follow those weights.

Two integrity mechanisms prevent the network from being gamed. First, signals are screened for
uniqueness: a miner who consistently copies another miner's positions is identified and eliminated.
Second, risk is capped — a miner whose portfolio falls too far from its peak is removed regardless
of how their absolute return looks. This means only strategies that generate genuine alpha with
controlled drawdown survive long-term.

A probationary tier sits below the main competition. Miners who fall outside the top performers in
an asset class enter a grace window to recover their standing or face removal. After elimination —
for any reason — the miner's registered hotkey is permanently barred from the network.

Validator consensus, aggregated through [[Yuma Consensus]], converts scoring outcomes into per-block
emissions distributed via [[Dynamic TAO]].

References: [taoshidev/vanta-network (GitHub)](https://github.com/taoshidev/vanta-network)

## Participating as a Miner

Vanta miners are traders or model developers who believe their strategy can outperform sustained
competition. The subnet supports multiple asset classes — forex, crypto, and equities — and miners
may compete across all of them or concentrate where their edge is strongest.

The bar is high. Because performance is scored against realistic simulated costs and drawdown limits
eliminate strategies that take excessive risk to chase returns, only approaches with genuine
risk-adjusted alpha survive. Taoshi runs a community Discord and shares strategy resources —
including partnerships with data providers Glassnode and LunarCrush — to help miners develop
effective systems.

Each registration uses a fresh, unused hotkey. Hotkeys are permanently blacklisted upon any
departure from the network, so miners who re-enter after elimination must register anew.

References: [taoshidev/vanta-network (GitHub)](https://github.com/taoshidev/vanta-network),
[vantanetwork.io](https://www.vantanetwork.io/)

## Participating as a Validator

Validators on Vanta operate the scoring infrastructure that receives miner signals, maintains
running portfolio state for each registered miner, and derives the performance-based weights
submitted to the network each week. Their core function is translating raw trading signals into
verified, comparable performance records across a heterogeneous miner set.

Effective validators require live market pricing data to continuously mark positions to market and
accurately assess accumulated costs. Validators that produce honest weights consistent with true
miner performance earn emissions through [[Yuma Consensus]].
