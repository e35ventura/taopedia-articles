Subnet 8, **Vanta**, is a decentralized trading-signal competition operated by
[Taoshi](https://taoshi.io) on the Bittensor network. Miners submit directional trade signals —
long, short, or flat — across multiple asset classes, and validators score those signals by tracking
simulated portfolio returns. The network is designed to surface the world's highest-quality
quantitative and machine-learning-based trading strategies, with weekly TAO emissions paid to the
top-performing miners. Vanta was formerly known as the Proprietary Trading Network (PTN) before
rebranding.

## How the Mechanism Works

Miners in Vanta select a single asset class — **crypto, forex, commodities, or equities** — and
submit directional orders against supported trade pairs within that class. An order specifies a
trade pair, direction (LONG or SHORT), and leverage. Leverage acts as the fraction of the miner's
simulated portfolio allocated to the position; a value of 1.0x means the entire portfolio is
deployed on that pair.

Validators receive every order, validate its legality (market hours, rate limits, leverage caps),
and simulate each miner's running portfolio using mark-to-market pricing. Weights are set based on
the previous week's performance, measured as profit-and-loss scaled by risk penalties. Weekly
emissions targeting midnight Sunday finalize payouts
([source](https://github.com/taoshidev/vanta-network/blob/main/docs/miner.md)).

Several costs are simulated to mirror real-world trading conditions:

- **Carry fee** — positions held overnight accrue a per-year fee (10.95% crypto / 3% forex / 5.25%
  equities at 1x leverage).
- **Spread** — crypto pairs incur a 0.1% × leverage transaction cost per order.
- **Slippage** — modeled based on order leverage and asset liquidity.

Miner account size is tied to **Theta** collateral: each Theta token deposited unlocks \$500 of
trading capacity, with a minimum deposit of 300 Theta and a cap of 1,000 Theta. Account size in turn
determines a miner's leverage tier, which governs maximum positional and portfolio leverage
([Theta token details](https://www.taoshi.io/theta)).

## Participating as a Miner

Miners register a fresh hotkey on the Bittensor network (registration fee < 1 TAO on mainnet) and
install the [miner software](https://github.com/taoshidev/vanta-network/blob/main/docs/miner.md).
Each miner must select one asset class before placing orders; cross-class trading is not permitted.

New miners enter a **challenge period** of up to 90 days during which they receive small emissions
to avoid deregistration. To pass the challenge period a miner must:

- Complete at least 61 full days of trading.
- Never exceed a 5% intraday drawdown or 5% end-of-day drawdown.
- Achieve cumulative returns above 10% (crypto/equities) or 8% (forex).
- Rank at or above the 25th miner in their asset class ([source](https://docs.taoshi.io/tips/p21/)).

Miners who pass the challenge period enter the main competition, where the drawdown limit relaxes
slightly to 8% EOD. Dropping below the top 25 in the chosen asset class triggers a 60-day
**probation period**; failure to recover results in elimination. Elimination also occurs for
plagiarism (copying another miner's trades) or exceeding the max drawdown threshold.

**Critical:** every eliminated or voluntarily deregistered hotkey is permanently blacklisted.
Re-registering a used hotkey causes all orders to be silently rejected; a completely new hotkey is
required for each new participation attempt
([source](https://github.com/taoshidev/vanta-network/blob/main/docs/miner.md)).

A public leaderboard and performance metrics are available at the
[Taoshi Dashboard](https://dashboard.taoshi.io/).

## Participating as a Validator

Validators receive trade signals from miners and are responsible for simulating portfolio
performance, applying carry/spread/slippage costs, detecting plagiarism, enforcing drawdown rules,
and setting on-chain weights. Full validator setup instructions are in the
[validator installation guide](https://github.com/taoshidev/vanta-network/blob/main/docs/validator.md).
