# Too Many Uids Per Mechanism

`TooManyUIDsPerMechanism` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised by
`sudo_set_max_allowed_uids`. The Subtensor API error reference describes it as "The maximum allowed
UIDs times mechanism count should not exceed 256"
([Subtensor API: Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors)).

## What It Means

`sudo_set_max_allowed_uids` sets how many UIDs a subnet may have registered at once. Before
accepting a new value, the extrinsic checks whether that proposed value, multiplied by the subnet's
current registered mechanism count, would exceed the network-wide `InitialMaxAllowedUids` constant
of 256. `TooManyUIDsPerMechanism` is raised when that product would exceed the ceiling
([Subtensor source: mechanism.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/mechanism.rs),
[Subtensor source: admin-utils/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/admin-utils/src/lib.rs)).

The ceiling is a fixed network-wide constant, not per-subnet chain state. A subnet with multiple
registered mechanisms is subject to a proportionally lower per-mechanism UID limit, since the total
across all mechanisms combined must stay within 256
([Subtensor source: mechanism.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/mechanism.rs)).

## Why It Appears

A subnet that already has multiple mechanisms registered can reach this limit even with a modest
per-mechanism UID count, because the check multiplies the proposed value by the current mechanism
count rather than comparing it directly to 256. A proposed `max_allowed_uids` that would be
individually below 256 can still fail once scaled by the number of registered mechanisms
([Subtensor source: mechanism.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/mechanism.rs)).

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the product check that raises it. It does not
describe how mechanisms are registered, how max allowed UIDs interacts with other subnet
hyperparameters, or the full validation sequence of `sudo_set_max_allowed_uids`; those are
documented separately
([Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters)).
