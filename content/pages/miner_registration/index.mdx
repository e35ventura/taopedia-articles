# Miner Registration

Miner registration is how a participant enters a Bittensor subnet as a miner. The
[Mining: Miner registration](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/miners#miner-registration)
documentation describes registration as the step that gives a miner a UID on a subnet so the miner
can take part in that subnet's work and reward flow.

## What It Represents

Miner registration is the paid entry process that assigns a miner
[UID slot](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#uid-slot) on a specific subnet. It
names how a hotkey joins a subnet's participant set; it does not describe mining setup, scoring
formulas, ranking tables, or a recommendation to join any particular subnet.

Readers should separate four ideas that miner registration vocabulary often mixes:

- **Entry versus ongoing performance** — registration is a one-time entry event that grants a UID
  slot and begins subnet participation. Validator evaluation that drives rank and emission share
  continues for as long as the miner holds the slot
  ([Mining: Miner registration](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/miners#miner-registration),
  [Understanding Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-subnets)).
- **Registration versus outcome proof** — a successful registration shows subnet participation and
  UID context. It does not by itself prove ongoing miner quality, emissions, or deregistration
  outcome; incentive-mechanism documentation covers later evaluation
  ([Understanding Incentive Mechanisms](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/anatomy-of-incentive-mechanism)).
- **Netuid before burn** — registration always happens on a subnet identified by
  [netuid](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#netuid). The burn purchases entry into
  one subnet under that subnet's rules, not a transferable option across subnets. Subnet selection
  and registration cost belong together in live tooling rather than in a static article quote.
- **Hotkey identity versus coldkey authority** — the
  [Glossary: UID Slot](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#uid-slot) assigns the slot
  to a hotkey when it registers as a miner or validator. Coldkey authority still governs wallet
  ownership and stake movement; registration attaches subnet participation to the operational hotkey
  named in registration vocabulary.

When miner slots on a subnet are full, new registration can still occur because the network may
replace a low-performing miner to make room
([Miner Deregistration](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/miners#miner-deregistration)). Readers
should not treat registration as only an empty-slot action. On a full subnet, successful
registration implies both payment of the entry burn and displacement of an existing participant
under the subnet's performance rules. Which miner is removed belongs to live subnet state at
submission time, not to the registration transaction alone.

Subnet development moves from localnet to testnet to mainnet
([Introduction to Bittensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/introduction#subnet-development),
[Bittensor Networks](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/bittensor-networks)). Each environment
has separate subnet UID registries, registration fees, and immunity rules. A registration example
from one network should not be read as representing UID state or costs in another.

After entry, immunity period and deregistration describe later subnet lifecycle rules around the
slot. Registration starts participation; those terms describe what can happen to the slot afterward
([Glossary: Immunity Period](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#immunity-period)).

One hotkey can register on more than one subnet, but not twice on the same one. The mining
documentation states that a hotkey can hold multiple UIDs across separate subnets, while within a
single subnet each UID must have a unique hotkey, so the same hotkey cannot occupy two slots on one
subnet ([Mining](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/miners)).

## Distinction from Recycling

- **Miner registration** — entry process that grants a miner UID slot.
- **Recycling** — TAO-burn mechanism that pays for that slot
  ([Glossary: Recycling](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#recycling-and-burning)).

## Distinction from Register

- **Register** — general on-chain action that purchases a UID slot on a subnet.
- **Miner registration** — miner-specific entry path into subnet participation and evaluation
  ([Glossary: Register](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#register)).

## Distinction from Subnet Deregistration

- **Miner registration** — participant-level entry that assigns a miner UID slot on a subnet.
- **Subnet deregistration** — network-level removal of an entire subnet and all its UID slots
  ([subnet deregistration](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-deregistration)).

## Distinction from Immunity Period

- **Miner registration** — initial entry step into a subnet UID slot.
- **Immunity period** — post-registration grace window before ordinary low-performance removal rules
  apply
  ([Glossary: Immunity Period](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#immunity-period)).

## Distinction from Registration Cost

- **Miner registration** — paid entry step that connects a miner hotkey to a subnet UID slot.
- **Registration cost** — dynamic registration burn in TAO that changes over time; mining
  documentation names the step, not a fixed price quote
  ([Glossary: Register](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#register)).

## Reader Boundary

Miner registration should not be read as a recommendation to join any particular subnet, as a
command tutorial, or as a guarantee of emissions. It is the concept term for how a miner enters a
subnet UID slot and begins participating in subnet mining vocabulary.
