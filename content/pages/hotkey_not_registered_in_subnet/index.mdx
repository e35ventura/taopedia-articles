# Hotkey Not Registered In Subnet

`HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised when a call needs a
hotkey to be registered on a specific subnet, but the runtime cannot find a UID entry for that
hotkey on the targeted `netuid`
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeynotregisteredinsubnet)).

## What It Means

Subnet participation in Bittensor is tracked per `netuid`. A hotkey that is valid as an account is
not automatically registered on every subnet. When a subnet-scoped path tries to resolve that hotkey
into a UID on the selected network and the lookup fails, the runtime returns
`HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet`
([Understanding Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-subnets),
[Understanding Neurons](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/neurons),
[Subtensor source: uids.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/uids.rs)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the target subnet exists, but this hotkey does not currently hold
the subnet slot that the call requires. The label does not, by itself, mean the account format is
invalid or that the chain cannot find the hotkey as an account
([Wallets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/wallets)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears on subnet-scoped paths that require an already-registered hotkey before they can
continue. One direct example is `set_coldkey_auto_stake_hotkey`, which checks the subnet first and
then rejects the call if the chosen hotkey is absent from that subnet's `Uids` map. Another is the
EVM-key association guard, which fails when it cannot resolve the signing hotkey to a UID on the
specified subnet
([Subtensor source: dispatches.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/macros/dispatches.rs),
[Subtensor source: check_evm_key_association.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/guards/check_evm_key_association.rs)).

For a reader, the useful takeaway is that the operation required existing subnet membership. The
runtime stopped at the membership check before it could proceed to the later logic specific to that
call.

## Distinction from Hotkey Account Not Exists

`HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet` and `HotKeyAccountNotExists` both block work that mentions a hotkey,
but they fail at different layers. `HotKeyAccountNotExists` means the chain cannot treat the hotkey
as an existing hotkey account at all. `HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet` means the hotkey exists, but it
does not have a registered UID on the targeted subnet
([Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyAccountNotExists](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeyaccountnotexists),
[Subtensor source: uids.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/uids.rs)).

- **HotKeyAccountNotExists** — the hotkey account itself does not exist for the required path.
- **HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet** — the hotkey exists, but not as a registered neuron on that
  subnet.

## Distinction from Subnet Not Exists

`SubnetNotExists` and `HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet` are also different checks. `SubnetNotExists`
means the requested `netuid` is not a valid subnet for the call. `HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet` means
the subnet passed that check, but the hotkey still has no UID there
([Subtensor Standard Errors: SubnetNotExists](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#subnetnotexists),
[Subtensor source: dispatches.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/macros/dispatches.rs)).

- **SubnetNotExists** — the selected subnet is invalid for the call.
- **HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet** — the subnet exists, but the hotkey is not registered on it.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the subnet-membership lookup that raises it. It
does not report whether any specific live hotkey is currently registered on a subnet; that is a
chain-state question readers must query at the time they inspect the network
([Understanding Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-subnets)).
