# Blockchain Validator

A blockchain validator is a participant in the Subtensor blockchain consensus mechanism that
produces and validates blocks. The official
[Glossary: Blockchain Validator](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#blockchain-validator)
describes this role at the blockchain level rather than inside individual subnet markets.

## Network-Layer Context

Blockchain validators belong to base-chain consensus vocabulary. The glossary contrasts them with
subnet validators by scope: blockchain validators participate in network-wide validation, while
subnet validators evaluate miner work inside one subnet. For readers, blockchain validator names a
network-layer role, not a subnet evaluation label.

This keeps two validator terms distinct: **blockchain validator** names base-chain participation,
and **subnet validator** names subnet-local evaluation of miner tasks.

References:
[Glossary: Blockchain Validator](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#blockchain-validator),
[Blockchain Validator vs Subnet Validator](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#blockchain-validator-vs-subnet-validator)

## Block and Transaction Context

Blockchain validation is tied to block production and transaction validation on Subtensor. The
[Glossary: Block](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#block) defines a block as a
unit of blockchain data processed on a regular cadence, and blockchain validators operate in that
base-chain context rather than in a subnet incentive-mechanism context.

For readers, this role should not be read as a synonym for every use of the word validator in
Bittensor prose. Subnet documentation often discusses validators in a subnet-local sense.

## Contrast with Subnet Validator

Subnet validators evaluate miners inside a subnet market. The
[Glossary: Subnet Validator](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#subnet-validator)
describes validators that operate within a specific subnet's consensus mechanism, while blockchain
validators function at the core consensus layer that affects the entire network.

Taopedia also maintains a comparison article for readers who want the two terms side by side. This
article defines blockchain validator on its own so the role label can be read without collapsing it
into subnet-validator vocabulary.

## Relationship to Multiple Mechanisms

Blockchain validators operate at the network layer, while multiple incentive mechanisms belong to
subnet design. The
[Glossary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#multiple-incentive-mechanisms) notes
that subnets may run more than one incentive mechanism with separate evaluation context.

For readers, blockchain validator still names base-chain validation. Multiple-mechanism context
belongs with subnet incentive design, not with this network-layer role term.

References:
[Multiple Incentive Mechanisms Within Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-multiple-mech-subnets),
[Glossary: Multiple Incentive Mechanisms](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#multiple-incentive-mechanisms)

## Reader Boundary

Blockchain validator should not be read as a subnet miner label, a staking recommendation, or a live
node-status report. It is a concept term for network-layer validation on Subtensor. Exact operator
requirements and runtime details belong in official Subtensor documentation.
