# Blockchain Validator

A blockchain validator is the chain-level validation role in Bittensor. The
[Glossary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#blockchain-validator) defines it as a
node participating in Subtensor consensus to produce and validate blocks, while
[Introduction to Bittensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/introduction) describes the
Bittensor blockchain as the system of record for balances and transactions across network
participants.

## Chain-Level Role

A blockchain validator belongs to the Subtensor blockchain layer. In the glossary's distinction, the
role is tied to network-wide consensus: validating transactions, producing blocks, and helping the
network agree on chain state. That makes it different from a subnet role that evaluates the quality
of a specific digital commodity.

The practical reading is architectural. Blockchain validator names the role associated with block
and transaction consensus, not every place where the word validator appears in Bittensor material.
The surrounding layer is part of the meaning.

## Relationship to Subnet Validators

A subnet validator evaluates miner work inside one subnet. The
[Glossary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#subnet-validator) defines a subnet
validator as the role that evaluates miner performance and sets weights based on miner output, while
[Understanding Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-subnets) describes
subnets as marketplaces where miners produce work and validators measure that work against
subnet-specific standards.

The distinction matters because the two roles answer different questions. A blockchain validator is
about network-wide block and transaction consensus. A subnet validator is about local evaluation
inside a subnet, where miner scores and weight signals help the subnet interpret useful work.

## Network Scope

[Introduction to Bittensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/introduction) separates Bittensor
into subnets, the blockchain, and supporting tools. Within that split, blockchain validator belongs
with the chain component: the shared record and consensus layer. Subnet validators belong with the
subnet component, where participants evaluate work and incentives.

This scope keeps reader expectations clear. Blockchain validator does not describe miner quality,
subnet scoring, or a subnet validator's weight vector. Those are subnet-level concepts. The
chain-level term tells readers where block validation sits in Bittensor's architecture.

## Reader Boundary

For Taopedia, the stable meaning is the layer and scope of the role. The official glossary
definition supports chain consensus, block production and validation, and a distinction from
subnet-level evaluation. It does not establish claims about a particular validator's identity,
current status, rewards, or subnet performance.

That boundary prevents one validator concept from absorbing the other. Use blockchain validator for
the Subtensor consensus role, and use [[subnet_validator|Subnet Validator]] when the article is
about evaluating miner work inside a subnet.
