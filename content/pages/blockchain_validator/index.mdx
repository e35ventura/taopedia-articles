# Blockchain Validator

A blockchain validator is a chain-level validator in Bittensor. The official glossary defines the
role as a node in the Subtensor blockchain that produces and validates blocks. It also states that
blockchain validators operate at the blockchain level, not inside individual subnets.

Reference:
[Blockchain Validator](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#blockchain-validator)

## Network-Level Role

The important Taopedia-level distinction is scope. A blockchain validator belongs to the
network-wide chain layer. Its role is about producing and validating blocks for the broader chain,
rather than the task-specific evaluation process inside a subnet.

Reference:
[Blockchain Validator](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#blockchain-validator)

## Difference From Subnet Validators

The glossary separates blockchain validators from subnet validators. A subnet validator evaluates
miner work within a specific subnet, while a blockchain validator produces and validates blocks for
the broader network. Keeping the terms separate prevents every use of "validator" from being read as
a subnet evaluation role.

Reference:
[Blockchain Validator](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#blockchain-validator)

## Interpretation Boundaries

Blockchain validator is a role label, not a current validator list, a participation guide, or a
subnet quality signal. The article should be read as a terminology page for distinguishing the
chain-level validator role from the subnet validator role.

Reference:
[Blockchain Validator](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#blockchain-validator)
