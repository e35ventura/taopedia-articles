# Blockchain Validator

A blockchain validator is a node that participates in the
[Subtensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#subtensor) blockchain's consensus
mechanism to produce and validate blocks. The
[Glossary: Blockchain Validator](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#blockchain-validator)
entry states that blockchain validators operate at the blockchain level, not within individual
subnets.

## What It Does

A blockchain validator works at the base-chain layer: it validates transactions, helps produce
blocks, and takes part in the network-wide consensus that keeps the Subtensor ledger consistent. Its
job is the integrity of the chain itself, not the evaluation of any subnet's miners
([Subtensor Nodes](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-nodes)).

This places it alongside the node infrastructure — the chain-level machinery that orders
transactions and finalizes blocks for every subnet at once.

## Distinction from Subnet Validator

The glossary draws this contrast directly. A blockchain validator secures network-wide block
consensus, while a subnet validator runs inside a single subnet, evaluating miner work and
submitting [weights](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#weight-vector) that feed
[Yuma Consensus](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus)
([Glossary: Subnet Validator](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#subnet-validator)).

- **Blockchain validator** — produces and validates blocks for the whole network.
- **Subnet validator** — scores miners and sets weights within one subnet.

## Why the Two Layers Are Separate

Bittensor separates chain consensus from subnet incentive evaluation. Block production must be
correct for every subnet regardless of what any subnet rewards, so it lives at the blockchain layer;
deciding which miners earn emissions is subnet-specific and lives in each subnet's incentive
mechanism ([Understanding Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-subnets)).

For readers, this means "validator" can mean two different things in Bittensor. A claim about block
production refers to a blockchain validator, while a claim about scoring miners or setting weights
refers to a subnet validator
([Yuma Consensus](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus)).
