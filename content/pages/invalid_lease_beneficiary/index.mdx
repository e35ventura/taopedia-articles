# Invalid Lease Beneficiary

`InvalidLeaseBeneficiary` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised by
`do_register_leased_network`, the extrinsic that finalizes a
[crowdloan](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans) into a registered
[subnet lease](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans). The standard-errors reference
describes it as "Invalid lease beneficiary to register the leased network"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#invalidleasebeneficiary)).

## What It Means

Subnet lease registration ties a finalized crowdloan to a beneficiary who will operate the leased
subnet. `do_register_leased_network` loads the crowdloan currently being finalized and requires the
signed caller to be that crowdloan's creator. `InvalidLeaseBeneficiary` is the rejection when the
caller's account does not match the creator recorded for that crowdloan
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#invalidleasebeneficiary),
[Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

This is the first validation step in the registration path. Later checks, such as whether an
optional lease end block is still in the future, only run after the caller has already passed this
creator match
([Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

## Why It Appears

The error appears when a wallet other than the crowdloan creator submits the leased-network
registration call. That includes a contributor, a proxy, or any account that did not create the
crowdloan being finalized, even if that account otherwise participates in the crowdloan
([Subnet Lease](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans),
[Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

Because the check runs before subnet registration, lease storage, or beneficiary proxy setup begin,
none of those steps proceed when it fails
([Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

For a reader, the practical meaning is narrow: only the crowdloan creator may finalize that
crowdloan into a leased subnet. The problem is not a stale end block, a missing lease record, or a
post-registration lookup failure
([Lease Cannot End In The Past](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#leasecannotendinthepast),
[Lease Does Not Exist](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#leasedoesnotexist)).

## Distinction from Lease Cannot End In The Past

`InvalidLeaseBeneficiary` and `LeaseCannotEndInThePast` are both early checks in
`do_register_leased_network`, but they validate different inputs. `InvalidLeaseBeneficiary` confirms
the caller is the crowdloan creator. `LeaseCannotEndInThePast` is the separate check on an optional
end block, reached only after the creator match succeeds
([Lease Cannot End In The Past](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#leasecannotendinthepast),
[Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

- **InvalidLeaseBeneficiary** — the caller is not the crowdloan creator.
- **LeaseCannotEndInThePast** — the supplied end block has already passed.

## Distinction from Expected Beneficiary Origin

`InvalidLeaseBeneficiary` and `ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin` both involve beneficiary identity, but on
different extrinsics. `InvalidLeaseBeneficiary` blocks leased-network registration when the caller
is not the crowdloan creator. `ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin` blocks lease termination when the caller
is not the stored lease beneficiary
([Subtensor Standard Errors: ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#expectedbeneficiaryorigin),
[Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

- **InvalidLeaseBeneficiary** — wrong account is trying to register the leased network.
- **ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin** — wrong account is trying to terminate an existing lease.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the crowdloan-creator check that raises it. It
does not describe crowdloan contribution rules, dividend distribution, or lease termination after
creation; those are documented separately
([Subnet Lease](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans),
[Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).
