Tag101 is a Bittensor subnet for decentralized semantic tagging of social media posts, registered on
Finney under netuid 101.

## How the Mechanism Works

Miners transform X (formerly Twitter) posts into small sets of structured tags that identify key
entities, topics, events, or contextual meanings within each post. Validators retrieve posts from a
centralized database of publicly disclosed whitelisted accounts, then distribute each post to miners
as an independent tagging task.

Each submitted tag is scored across three dimensions: consensus with other miners' submissions,
validity relative to the original post, and diversity within the miner's own tag set. The final
miner score combines these three components, rewarding tags that align with the network's semantic
consensus while remaining relevant and non-redundant.
