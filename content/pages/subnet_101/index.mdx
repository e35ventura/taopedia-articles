Tag101 is Bittensor Subnet 101, a decentralized tagging network for social media posts.

## How the Mechanism Works

The [Tag101 GitHub repository](https://github.com/tag101-ai/tag101) documents a subnet where
validators distribute posts from X (formerly Twitter) to miners, who return a small set of semantic
tags identifying the key entities, topics, events, and meanings within each post. Tasks are drawn
from a curated database of X accounts; the initial scope focuses on AI-related content, with the
project noting that additional topic domains may be added in future seasons.

Each submitted tag is scored on three criteria: alignment with the broader network's interpretation
(consensus score), relevance to the source post (validity score), and distinctness from the miner's
other submitted tags (diversity score). A miner's overall task score is the average of its
individual tag scores. Validators aggregate task-level scores over time and submit weights that feed
into the [[Yuma Consensus]] calculation.
