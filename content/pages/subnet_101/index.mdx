Tag101 is Bittensor Subnet 101 (SN101), a subnet for decentralized social post tagging.

## How the Mechanism Works

Miners receive Twitter/X posts drawn from a publicly disclosed whitelist of accounts and return a
small set of tags identifying the key entities, topics, events, or contextual meanings in each post.
Validators score each miner submission on three dimensions: how well the tags align with the
semantic interpretation formed across the network (consensus), whether each tag is relevant to the
post and meets format requirements (validity), and whether the miner's own tag set avoids redundancy
(diversity). A combined score rewards miners whose tags are semantically accurate, grounded in the
post content, and non-repetitive.

The initial whitelist focuses on AI-related accounts, providing a consistent topic domain during
early operation. The whitelist may be updated as the subnet introduces new topic seasons.

Source: [tag101-ai/tag101](https://github.com/tag101-ai/tag101) README.
