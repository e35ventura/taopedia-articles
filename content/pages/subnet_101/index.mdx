Tag101 is Bittensor Subnet 101.

## What Tag101 Provides

Tag101 is described on-chain as a subnet for decentralized social post tagging. Participants
transform posts from X (formerly Twitter) into structured tag sets that capture the key entities,
topics, and meanings within each post. These tags turn unstructured social content into structured
semantic signals useful for indexing, topic tracking, and downstream AI workflows. The network
evaluates tagging quality through competition among independent participants and distributes rewards
to those whose tags most consistently meet the network's quality standard.

## Miner and Validator Roles

The two roles follow the standard Bittensor split between producing output and evaluating it. Miners
receive posts as tasks and return structured tag sets. Validators assess tag quality and assign
weights that feed into [[Yuma Consensus]], which converts those evaluations into the emissions that
reward miners.

## On-Chain Identity

Live SN101 subnet data is available on [TaoStats](https://taostats.io/subnets/101). The Finney
identity for netuid 101 registers the name Tag101, the description "Tag101 is a Bittensor subnet for
decentralized social post tagging", and the GitHub repository
[tag101-ai/tag101](https://github.com/tag101-ai/tag101).
