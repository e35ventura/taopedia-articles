Tag101 is Bittensor Subnet 101. Its on-chain identity describes the subnet as "a Bittensor subnet
for decentralized social post tagging" and lists
[tag101-ai/tag101](https://github.com/tag101-ai/tag101) as its GitHub repository.

## How the Mechanism Works

The Tag101 README describes a subnet where miners receive posts from X (formerly Twitter) and return
a small set of semantic tags identifying the key entities, topics, events, and contextual meanings
in each post. Tasks are drawn from a curated database of X accounts; the initial focus is on
AI-related content, with the project noting that additional topic domains may be added in future
seasons.

Each submitted tag is scored on three criteria: its alignment with the interpretation formed by the
broader network, its relevance to the source post, and its distinctness from the miner's other
submitted tags. A miner's task score is the average of its individual tag scores. Validators
aggregate these task-level scores over time and convert them into miner weights for the
[[Yuma Consensus]] calculation.
