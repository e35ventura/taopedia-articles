Tag101 is Bittensor Subnet 101 (SN101). The subnet runs a social post tagging task: miners label
Twitter / X posts with semantic tags and validators score submissions on accuracy and label quality.

## How the Mechanism Works

According to the [tag101-ai/tag101](https://github.com/tag101-ai/tag101) README, miners receive
Twitter / X posts drawn from a disclosed whitelist of accounts and return a small set of semantic
tags. Tasks initially focus on AI-related accounts.

Tag submissions are scored on three dimensions: a consensus score (60 percent weight) measuring how
closely a miner's tags align with the semantic cluster formed by all submissions, a validity score
measuring content relevance and format correctness, and a diversity score rewarding non-repetitive
labels within a miner's own set. The validity and diversity scores together carry 40 percent weight.
