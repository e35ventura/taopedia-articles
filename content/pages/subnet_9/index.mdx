Subnet 9 runs IOTA — **I**ncentivized **O**rchestrated **T**raining **A**rchitecture — a framework
for pretraining large language models across a network of heterogeneous, permissionless,
token-incentivized machines. Developed by [Macrocosm OS](https://www.macrocosmos.ai/), IOTA is
designed to distribute the computational burden of LLM training across many independent participants
rather than centralizing it on a single datacenter. The full technical design is described in the
team's research primer ([macrocosmos.ai](https://www.macrocosmos.ai/research/iota_primer.pdf)) and
an ArXiv preprint ([arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.17766)).

## How the Mechanism Works

IOTA uses a **pipeline-parallel** training approach, meaning the layers of a neural network are
split across different miners rather than each miner holding the entire model. An orchestrator node
sits at the center of the network: it assigns model layers to miners, streams intermediate
activations between them, and manages the overall training flow.

During a training run, each miner is responsible for a slice of the model. Activations (the
intermediate outputs that pass from one layer to the next) travel from miner to miner following the
depth of the network. Miners periodically merge their locally updated weights using a variant of the
Butterfly All-Reduce algorithm, a communication pattern that lets distributed participants converge
on shared weight values without a single bottleneck.

Validators spot-check the work miners submit. A miner that processes activations correctly and
uploads coherent weight updates earns a positive score; one that submits garbage or stalls the
pipeline does not. Rewards flow through the standard [[Yuma Consensus]] mechanism on
[[Dynamic TAO]], meaning weight assignments by validators determine each miner's emission share from
the subnet's alpha token pool.

The active run targets a 1.5-billion-parameter Llama-inspired architecture split into three pipeline
stages: a head layer, a body layer, and a tail layer, each handled by a different set of miners.
Future runs are planned to scale toward 15B, 50B, and 100B parameter models as the subnet matures
([macrocosm-os/iota](https://github.com/macrocosm-os/iota)).

## Participating as a Miner

Miners join IOTA by registering a hotkey on Subnet 9 and running the miner process. The setup script
provided by Macrocosm OS guides participants through installing dependencies and configuring a
connection to the orchestrator. Because the pipeline splits the model into layers, a miner does not
need enough VRAM to hold the entire model — only its assigned slice — though the team recommends a
CUDA-capable GPU with at least 16 GB of VRAM to handle the active run comfortably, along with Ubuntu
22.04.

Once running, miners wait for the orchestrator to assign them a pipeline stage and begin receiving
activation tensors. The miner applies its assigned layers' forward and backward passes, uploads
updated weights to the shared storage bucket, and awaits the next batch. Earnings depend on how many
activations a miner processes relative to the rest of the network, giving an incentive to maintain
reliable uptime and sufficient compute. The official miner setup guide is maintained at
[docs.macrocosmos.ai](https://docs.macrocosmos.ai/subnets/subnet-9-pre-training/subnet-9-iota-mining-setup-guide).

## Participating as a Validator

Validators on IOTA do not run a full training replica. Instead, they perform spot-checks on miner
outputs, verifying that submitted activations and weight updates are consistent with expected
behavior. A validator samples a subset of the training work completed by miners and scores each one
based on correctness.

Because the scoring is based on verifiable mathematical checks of neural network computations rather
than subjective quality judgments, validators can operate with lower hardware requirements than
miners while still providing meaningful oversight of the training process. The official validator
documentation is at
[docs.macrocosmos.ai](https://docs.macrocosmos.ai/subnets/subnet-9-pre-training/subnet-9-validating).
