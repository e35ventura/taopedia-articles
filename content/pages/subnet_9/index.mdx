Subnet 9 hosts **iota** — the Incentivized Orchestrated Training Architecture — a framework
developed by [Macrocosmos](https://www.macrocosmos.ai/) for pretraining large language models across
a permissionless network of heterogeneous GPU machines. Rather than concentrating compute in a
single data center, iota distributes the model itself across independent miners, each responsible
for a slice of the network. The project is described in the
[iota technical primer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.17766) published by Macrocosmos.

## How the Mechanism Works

iota implements pipeline-parallel training by splitting a language model into distinct layer groups
— a head, a body, and a tail — and assigning each group to a different set of miners. An
orchestrator streams activations (the intermediate numerical outputs produced at each layer) from
one group to the next, so the full forward and backward training pass completes across multiple
machines in sequence rather than on a single device.

Miners compete to process activation batches as they flow through the pipeline. Periodically, each
miner uploads its locally computed weight updates and merges them with the rest of the network using
a variant of Butterfly All-Reduce — a communication pattern that synchronizes parameters across
participants without requiring all-to-all messaging ([source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.17766)).

Validators perform spot-checks on a subset of miners to verify that activations were computed
correctly. Honest miners who pass verification receive higher scores; those whose outputs fail the
check are penalized. Final emissions are distributed via [[Yuma Consensus]] based on these
validator-assigned scores. Live training metrics and miner status are visible on the
[iota dashboard](https://iota.macrocosmos.ai/dashboard/mainnet).

## Participating as a Miner

Miners on SN9 hold one or more model layers and process the activation batches routed to them by the
orchestrator. Because the pipeline distributes layers across participants, each miner handles only a
segment of the full model rather than the whole network. Rewards scale with the volume of
activations processed correctly and verified by validators.

Participants interested in mining should review the
[Macrocosmos documentation](https://docs.macrocosmos.ai/) and the
[GitHub repository](https://github.com/macrocosm-os/iota) for current prerequisites and registration
details.

## Participating as a Validator

Validators on SN9 sample a portion of miner work and independently verify that activations were
computed as expected. Miners whose outputs match pass verification; those that do not have their
scores reduced. This sampling approach keeps validation computationally tractable while maintaining
a meaningful check against idle or fraudulent participants.

Validators stake TAO on the subnet and receive emissions proportional to their influence under
[[Yuma Consensus]]. Setup and configuration details are documented in the
[Macrocosmos documentation](https://docs.macrocosmos.ai/).
