# Cannot Unstake Lock

`CannotUnstakeLock` means a stake-removal call on netuid 1 or another named subnet tried to withdraw
alpha that is still bound by an active conviction lock. The standard-errors reference describes it
as "Cannot unstake lock amount"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#cannotunstakelock)).

## What It Means

Conviction locks bind part of a coldkey's subnet alpha to a hotkey and a commitment signal. Locked
mass can decay over time in decaying mode, freeing alpha that may later become removable
([Conviction and locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).

`CannotUnstakeLock` is a lock-boundary rejection on stake removal. It means the call tried to
unstake alpha that the conviction lock still treats as locked rather than as freely removable stake
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#cannotunstakelock)).

This is a conviction-lock rejection, not a coldkey TAO balance failure and not a generic pool
liquidity failure on an add-stake or remove-stake swap.

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a coldkey submits a stake-removal amount that includes alpha still covered
by an active conviction lock on the netuid. That can happen when a client treats total staked alpha
as fully removable, when automation retries an unstake before locked mass has decayed, or when a
call names the locked portion directly instead of the unlocked remainder
([Conviction and locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).

On netuid 1, readers can compare a proposed unstake against live conviction-lock state through the
storage query patterns described in the Subtensor documentation before resubmitting the removal
([Subtensor Storage Query Examples](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-nodes/subtensor-storage-query-examples)).

When debugging, rule out other stake-removal failures first. `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` means the
hotkey-coldkey pair lacks enough total staked alpha for the request. `StakeUnavailable` means the
request exceeds unlocked alpha after existing locks are excluded, without naming the locked mass
itself as the withdrawal target
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#notenoughstaketowithdraw),
[Subtensor API Errors: StakeUnavailable](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#stakeunavailable)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the call tried to remove alpha that is still locked on the subnet.

## Distinction from Stake Unavailable

`CannotUnstakeLock` and `StakeUnavailable` both involve conviction locks on stake removal, but they
reject different problems. `CannotUnstakeLock` means the call targets locked alpha that cannot be
withdrawn while the lock is active. `StakeUnavailable` means the request exceeds the unlocked alpha
available after locked mass is excluded from the removable balance
([Conviction and locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).

- **CannotUnstakeLock** — the call tries to unstake locked alpha on the subnet.
- **StakeUnavailable** — the request exceeds unlocked alpha on the subnet.

## Distinction from Not Enough Stake To Withdraw

`CannotUnstakeLock` and `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` both appear on stake-removal paths, but they
compare against different limits. `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` means total staked alpha on the
hotkey-coldkey pair is below the requested amount. `CannotUnstakeLock` means the call reaches the
conviction-lock boundary because locked alpha cannot be removed yet
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#notenoughstaketowithdraw)).

- **CannotUnstakeLock** — locked alpha on the subnet cannot be unstaked yet.
- **NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw** — total staked alpha is too low for the request.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the locked-alpha withdrawal gate it represents.
It does not report a wallet's live locked or unlocked alpha on any subnet; those values depend on
chain state at observation time
([Conviction and locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking),
[Bittensor Networks](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/bittensor-networks)).
