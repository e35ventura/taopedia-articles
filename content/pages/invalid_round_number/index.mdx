# Invalid Round Number

`InvalidRoundNumber` is a drand pallet error raised when a submitted beacon pulse carries a round
number that does not strictly exceed the last stored round. The pallet defines it as "the round
number did not increment"
([Subtensor source: drand/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/drand/src/lib.rs)).

## What It Means

The drand pallet stores randomness pulses from the drand beacon and requires them to arrive in
strictly increasing round order. The `write_pulse` extrinsic, submitted by the offchain worker,
processes each pulse in the payload in sequence. After the cryptographic signature of a pulse is
verified, the pallet checks that the pulse's round number is strictly greater than
`LastStoredRound`. If the round number is equal to or less than the last stored round,
`InvalidRoundNumber` fires and the call is rejected
([Subtensor source: drand/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/drand/src/lib.rs)).

This ensures the on-chain round sequence is monotonically increasing and that no pulse can be
replayed or inserted out of order
([Subtensor source: drand/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/drand/src/lib.rs)).

## Distinction from Pulse Verification Error

`InvalidRoundNumber` and `PulseVerificationError` both block `write_pulse` for a given pulse, but at
different validation steps. `PulseVerificationError` fires when the cryptographic verification of
the pulse itself fails — the pulse's BLS signature does not verify against the beacon's public key.
`InvalidRoundNumber` fires after the signature is verified successfully, when the pulse's round
position in the sequence is not valid
([Subtensor source: drand/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/drand/src/lib.rs)).

- **PulseVerificationError** — the pulse's cryptographic signature is invalid.
- **InvalidRoundNumber** — the pulse's round number does not advance the stored sequence.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the round monotonicity check that raises it. It
does not describe how drand beacon pulses are fetched, how the BLS signature scheme works, or how
randomness from stored pulses is consumed by other pallets
