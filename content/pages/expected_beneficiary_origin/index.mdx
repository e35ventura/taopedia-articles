# Expected Beneficiary Origin

`ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised in `do_terminate_lease`,
the helper called by the `terminate_lease` extrinsic when a
[subnet lease](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans)'s beneficiary ends the lease
after its optional end block. The standard-errors reference describes it as "Expected beneficiary
origin"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#expectedbeneficiaryorigin)).

## What It Means

`do_terminate_lease` loads the lease record for the supplied lease ID and compares the transaction
signer against the beneficiary stored on that lease. `ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin` is raised when
those accounts do not match
([Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

This check runs only after the lease lookup succeeds. Later steps in the same function, such as the
end-block timing check and the beneficiary-hotkey ownership check, are reached only once a stored
lease has been found and the signer has been confirmed as that lease's beneficiary
([Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

## Why It Appears

A wallet other than the lease beneficiary can submit `terminate_lease` with a valid lease ID and
still fail here if the signing account is not the beneficiary recorded on that lease. That includes
a contributor coldkey, a proxy without beneficiary scope, or any account that targets the wrong
lease record in automation ([Subnet Lease](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans),
[Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

Crowdloan documentation states that the beneficiary may terminate a lease only after an optional end
block has passed. `ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin` is separate from that timing rule: it rejects the
signer's identity before the end-block comparison runs
([Crowdloans](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans),
[Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

## Distinction from Lease Does Not Exist

`ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin` and `LeaseDoesNotExist` are both early checks in `do_terminate_lease`,
but they apply at different stages. `LeaseDoesNotExist` is the storage lookup that fails when no
lease record matches the supplied lease ID. `ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin` is the next check,
confirming the transaction signer is the beneficiary recorded on a lease that was already found
([Subtensor Standard Errors: LeaseDoesNotExist](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#leasedoesnotexist),
[Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

- **LeaseDoesNotExist** — no lease record exists for the supplied lease ID.
- **ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin** — a lease was found, but the signer is not its beneficiary.

## Distinction from Invalid Lease Beneficiary

`ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin` and `InvalidLeaseBeneficiary` both involve beneficiary identity, but on
different extrinsics. `InvalidLeaseBeneficiary` blocks leased-network registration in
`do_register_leased_network` when the caller is not the crowdloan creator.
`ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin` blocks lease termination in `do_terminate_lease` when the caller is not
the stored lease beneficiary
([Subtensor Standard Errors: InvalidLeaseBeneficiary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#invalidleasebeneficiary),
[Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

- **InvalidLeaseBeneficiary** — the caller is not the crowdloan creator on registration.
- **ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin** — the signer is not the lease beneficiary on termination.

## Distinction from Beneficiary Does Not Own Hotkey

`ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin` and `BeneficiaryDoesNotOwnHotkey` both gate `do_terminate_lease` by
checking account associations, but at different stages. `ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin` confirms the
transaction signer is the beneficiary recorded on the lease. `BeneficiaryDoesNotOwnHotkey` is a
later check confirming the hotkey argument supplied for the ownership transfer is owned by that same
beneficiary coldkey
([Beneficiary Does Not Own Hotkey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#beneficiarydoesnotownhotkey),
[Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

- **ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin** — the transaction signer is not the lease's beneficiary.
- **BeneficiaryDoesNotOwnHotkey** — the beneficiary's supplied hotkey is not owned by them.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the beneficiary-identity check that raises it.
It does not describe crowdloan contribution rules or the full lease termination sequence beyond this
signer match; those are documented separately
([Subnet Lease](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans)).
