# Expected Beneficiary Origin

`ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised by `do_terminate_lease`,
the extrinsic a [subnet lease](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans)'s beneficiary
calls to claim ownership once the lease has run its course. The standard-errors reference describes
it as "Expected beneficiary origin"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#expectedbeneficiaryorigin)).

## What It Means

After resolving the lease record for the supplied lease ID, `do_terminate_lease` compares the
calling account against the `beneficiary` field stored on that lease. `ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin` is
raised when the caller is not that recorded beneficiary
([Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

This check only runs once the lease has already been found; it depends on the earlier lookup
succeeding, since there is no beneficiary to compare against until a real lease record is resolved
([Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

## Why It Appears

Termination is restricted to the specific account recorded as the lease's beneficiary at
registration. A crowdloan contributor, the original crowdloan creator, or any other account that is
not that recorded beneficiary will see this error even if they hold a valid lease ID and the lease
has otherwise ended
([Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

## Distinction from Lease Does Not Exist

`ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin` and `LeaseDoesNotExist` are both early checks in `do_terminate_lease`,
but they apply at different stages. `LeaseDoesNotExist` is the first check, on whether the lease
record exists at all. `ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin` is the next, separate check, on whether the caller
matches the beneficiary recorded on a lease that has already been found
([Subtensor Standard Errors: LeaseDoesNotExist](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#leasedoesnotexist),
[Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

- **LeaseDoesNotExist** — no lease record exists for the supplied lease ID.
- **ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin** — a lease was found, but the caller isn't its beneficiary.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the identity comparison that raises it. It does
not describe how a lease's beneficiary is set at registration or the rest of the termination flow;
those are documented separately
([Subnet Lease](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans)).
