# Expected Beneficiary Origin

`ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin` is a documented Subtensor standard error. The standard-errors reference
describes it as "Expected beneficiary origin"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#expectedbeneficiaryorigin)).

## What It Means

A leased subnet names a beneficiary who is allowed to take ownership of the subnet after the lease
ends. Terminating a lease moves ownership to that beneficiary
([Subnet Crowdloans](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans)).

`ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin` means the runtime looked up a lease by its ID, found it, and then
discovered that the signed caller is not the account recorded as that lease's beneficiary. The
termination is refused and the lease state does not change
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#expectedbeneficiaryorigin),
[Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

## Why It Appears

The check is the second precondition inside `do_terminate_lease`, called by the `terminate_lease`
extrinsic. The function has already confirmed that the supplied lease ID resolves to a stored lease;
the next step is to verify that the caller is the beneficiary recorded on that lease. The runtime
rejects the call when the caller's account does not match the lease's stored beneficiary field,
before any later step runs
([Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

For a reader, the practical meaning is narrow: the caller knew a valid lease ID, but the lease
itself names a different beneficiary. The runtime refuses the transfer rather than write a subnet
ownership relationship the recorded beneficiary did not initiate
([Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

## Distinction from Lease Does Not Exist

`ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin` and `LeaseDoesNotExist` are both early checks inside
`do_terminate_lease`, but they reject different defects. `LeaseDoesNotExist` is the first check, on
whether the supplied lease ID resolves to any stored lease at all. `ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin` is
the next, separate check, reached only once the lease lookup has already succeeded; it rejects when
the caller is not the beneficiary recorded on that lease
([Subtensor Standard Errors: LeaseDoesNotExist](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#leasedoesnotexist),
[Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

- **LeaseDoesNotExist** — no lease record exists for the supplied lease ID.
- **ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin** — a lease was found, but the caller is not its beneficiary.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the `ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin` error label and the caller-
versus-beneficiary check inside `do_terminate_lease`. It does not describe lease creation, the
crowdloan that backs a lease, the conditions that make a lease eligible for termination, or the
broader subnet-ownership transfer flow; those are separate conditions with their own error labels
and their own articles ([Subnet Crowdloans](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans),
[Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).
