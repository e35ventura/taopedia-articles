# Validator-Miner Bonds

Validator-miner bonds are smoothed relationships between validators and miners inside Bittensor's
Yuma Consensus process. They connect validator evaluation history to validator-side emission
outcomes, rather than naming a social endorsement or a general quality score
([Glossary: Validator-Miner Bonds](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-miner-bonds),
[Yuma Consensus](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus)).

The term separates validator weights from the smoothed bond state used later in consensus. Validator
weights are evaluation inputs; validator-miner bonds are relationship values that help shape
dividends
([Glossary: Weight Vector](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#weight-vector),
[Glossary: Dividends](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#emission)).

## What It Represents

Validator-miner bonds carry a smoothed validator-miner relationship across Yuma passes. Weights are
fresh evaluation signals each epoch; bonds accumulate how that evaluation history connects a
validator to each miner over time
([Glossary: Validator-Miner Bonds](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-miner-bonds),
[Yuma Consensus](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus)).

On netuid 1, bond examples illustrate one subnet's metagraph relationship fields. They should not be
read as representing production validator-miner relationships on another network
([Subnet Metagraph](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/metagraph),
[Bittensor Networks](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/bittensor-networks)).

## Instant Bond From Stake-Weighted Weights

Each epoch, Yuma forms an instantaneous bond `ΔB_ij` between validator `i` and miner `j` from
stake-weighted bond-weights. Documentation gives:

`ΔB_ij = (S_i · W̃_ij) / Σ_k S_k · W̃_kj`

where `S_i` is validator `i`'s stake and `W̃_ij` is that validator's bond-weight toward miner `j`
after out-of-consensus adjustment toward the consensus benchmark
([Yuma Consensus: Bonding mechanics](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus#bonding-mechanics),
[Glossary: Stake Weight](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#stake-weight)).

Instant bonds therefore normalize each validator's influence across miners on the subnet. Bond
vocabulary names the smoothed history built from these per-epoch contributions, not the raw weight
vector a validator submitted
([Glossary: Weight Vector](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#weight-vector)).

## EMA Smoothing Across Epochs

Yuma does not use instantaneous bonds directly in dividend allocation. Each pair's bond state
updates with an exponential moving average:

`B_ij^(t) = α · ΔB_ij^(t) + (1 − α) · B_ij^(t−1)`

where `α` is the smoothing factor from the subnet's bond-smoothing path and `t` is the current epoch
([Yuma Consensus: Bonding mechanics](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus#bonding-mechanics),
[Exponential Moving Averages](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/ema)).

[Consensus-based weights](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/consensus-based-weights) can make
`α` vary by validator-miner pair from consensus alignment.
[Bonds moving average](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters) and
liquid-alpha settings configure the baseline smoothing envelope on a subnet
([Glossary: Validator-Miner Bonds](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-miner-bonds)).

## Bonds Feed Validator Dividends

Smoothed bonds pair with miner
[incentives](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#incentives) to produce validator
[dividends](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#dividends):

`V_i = Σ_j B_ij · M_j`

where `M_j` is miner `j`'s incentive outcome from the same Yuma pass
([Yuma Consensus: Validator emissions](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus#validator-emissions),
[Glossary: Dividends](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#emission)).

Bond vocabulary names the relationship state on the validator path; dividend vocabulary names the
credited validator-side outcome that results from multiplying those bonds against miner incentives
([Subnet Metagraph](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/metagraph)).

## Distinction from Weight Setting

Weight setting is the validator action that assigns miner scores each round. Validator-miner bonds
are the smoothed relationships built from those repeated submissions
([Glossary: Weight Vector](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#weight-vector),
[Glossary: Validator-Miner Bonds](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-miner-bonds)).

- **Weight setting** — validator submission of miner scores each round.
- **Validator-miner bonds** — smoothed relationships accumulated from those submissions.

## Distinction from Bonds Moving Average

Validator-miner bonds name the smoothed relationship values Yuma Consensus maintains between
validators and miners. Bonds moving average names the subnet hyperparameter that configures how
those bond values are averaged over time
([Glossary: Validator-Miner Bonds](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-miner-bonds),
[Exponential Moving Averages](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/ema),
[Yuma Consensus](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus)).

- **Validator-miner bonds** — accumulated validator-miner relationship state.
- **Bonds moving average** — hyperparameter that sets smoothing across rounds.

## Distinction from Dividends

- **Validator-miner bonds** — smoothed validator-miner relationship state.
- **Dividends** — validator-side payout that follows bonded evaluation.

## Distinction from Incentives

Incentives name miner-side emission outcomes from aggregate miner ranking. Validator-miner bonds
connect validator evaluation history to validator-side dividend calculation inside the same Yuma
pass ([Glossary: Incentives](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#incentives),
[Glossary: Validator-Miner Bonds](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-miner-bonds)).

- **Incentives** — miner-side emission outcomes from ranking.
- **Validator-miner bonds** — smoothed relationships on the validator dividend path.

## Distinction from Liquid Alpha

Liquid alpha can vary the EMA smoothing factor for validator-miner bonds according to consensus
alignment. Validator-miner bonds remain the smoothed relationship; liquid alpha modifies how quickly
that relationship updates
([Consensus-based Weights](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/consensus-based-weights),
[Exponential Moving Averages](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/ema)).

- **Validator-miner bonds** — smoothed validator-miner relationship state.
- **Liquid alpha** — modifier on bond-smoothing responsiveness.

## Distinction from Multiple Incentive Mechanisms

On multi-mechanism subnets, bond context can be mechanism-specific because separate mechanisms can
maintain separate bond pools for Yuma Consensus calculations
([Multiple Incentive Mechanisms Within Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-multiple-mech-subnets),
[Glossary: Multiple Incentive Mechanisms](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#multiple-incentive-mechanisms)).

- **Validator-miner bonds** — smoothed relationship within one mechanism track.
- **Multiple incentive mechanisms** — split evaluation with separate bond pools.

## Distinction from Yuma Consensus

- **Validator-miner bonds** — smoothed validator-miner relationships updated across Yuma passes.
- **Yuma Consensus** — on-chain settlement that turns validator weights into emission shares.

## Reader Boundary

Validator-miner bonds are consensus-reference vocabulary. They are not a staking guide, validator
strategy, miner ranking table, or payout forecast
([Glossary: Validator-Miner Bonds](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-miner-bonds),
[Yuma Consensus](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus)).
