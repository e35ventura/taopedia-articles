# Cold Key Already Associated

`ColdKeyAlreadyAssociated` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised during a coldkey swap
when the chosen destination coldkey already has staking hotkeys associated with it
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#coldkeyalreadyassociated)).

## What It Means

A coldkey swap moves balances, stake relationships, and related ownership data from one coldkey to
another. Before that transfer work begins, `do_swap_coldkey` checks whether the destination coldkey
already has staking hotkeys associated with it. If it does, the runtime stops with
`ColdKeyAlreadyAssociated` and does not continue the swap
([Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the destination coldkey is already in use as the associated coldkey
for staking hotkeys, so it is not accepted as a clean swap target.

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a coldkey swap is announced and then finalized toward a destination coldkey
that already has staking hotkeys linked to it. The current test coverage shows exactly that case:
after a swap announcement delay passes, the swap fails with `ColdKeyAlreadyAssociated` once the
destination coldkey has an existing hotkey association
([Subtensor test: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/tests/swap_coldkey.rs),
[Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs)).

For a reader, the useful takeaway is that the destination account is rejected before later swap
steps can move stake, ownership, or balance state.

## Distinction from New Cold Key Is Hotkey

`ColdKeyAlreadyAssociated` and `NewColdKeyIsHotkey` both guard the destination of a coldkey swap,
but they reject different destination states. `ColdKeyAlreadyAssociated` is returned when the
destination coldkey already has staking hotkeys associated with it. `NewColdKeyIsHotkey` is returned
when the destination account is itself already a hotkey account
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NewColdKeyIsHotkey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#newcoldkeyishotkey),
[Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs)).

- `ColdKeyAlreadyAssociated` means the destination coldkey already has staking hotkeys.
- `NewColdKeyIsHotkey` means the destination account is itself already a hotkey.

## Distinction from Not Enough Balance To Pay Swap Coldkey

`ColdKeyAlreadyAssociated` and `NotEnoughBalanceToPaySwapColdKey` can both stop a coldkey swap, but
they fail for different reasons. `ColdKeyAlreadyAssociated` rejects the destination account state.
`NotEnoughBalanceToPaySwapColdKey` rejects the swap cost payment
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToPaySwapColdKey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#notenoughbalancetopayswapcoldkey),
[Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs)).

- `ColdKeyAlreadyAssociated` means the destination coldkey is already associated.
- `NotEnoughBalanceToPaySwapColdKey` means the account cannot fund the swap cost.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the destination-association check behind it. It
does not describe the full coldkey swap procedure or every state transfer that happens after the
guard passes; those are separate parts of the coldkey swap flow
([Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs)).
