# Moving Stake

Moving stake is the operation of moving a staked position between hotkeys and/or between subnets in
a single atomic transaction, without first withdrawing to the coldkey. The
[Staking and delegation overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)
lists it alongside transferring stake and direct unstaking as one of the participant-facing ways to
rearrange an existing staked position.

## What It Represents

Moving stake names a delegation-change operation that re-points an existing staked position without
returning it to free balance first. The operation keeps delegated exposure active while changing the
hotkey, the subnet, or both
([Glossary: Stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#stake),
[Staking and delegation overview: Moving stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation#moving-stake)).

Readers should separate four stake-management operations that sound similar:

- **Staking** — adds new TAO to a validator hotkey on a subnet.
- **Unstaking** — withdraws delegated stake back toward the coldkey.
- **Transferring stake** — changes which coldkey owns a staked position.
- **Moving stake** — changes which hotkey and/or subnet holds the position while delegation stays
  active.

Moving stake relocates delegated stake; it does not send free, unstaked TAO between coldkeys. Free
balance moves use [transfer](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#transfer) after
unstaking or from existing coldkey balance, not a move-stake extrinsic alone
([Glossary: Unstaking](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#unstaking)).

[Toggle transfer](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters) is a per-subnet
permission that allows or blocks coldkey-to-coldkey reassignment of stake. Moving stake answers
which hotkey or subnet holds a position, not whether coldkey ownership may change on that subnet.

[Coldkey swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/coldkey-swap) migrates the full wallet identity
to a new coldkey. Moving stake keeps the controlling coldkey unchanged and only re-points
delegation.

[Autostaking](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/miners/autostaking) routes miner emission proceeds
into delegated stake over time. Moving stake repositions an already-accumulated position without
withdrawing from it first.

[Conviction and locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)
locks subnet alpha for a time-based commitment score. A conviction lock can constrain how much of a
position moving stake could take out of that subnet; relocation and commitment locking answer
different questions.

Moving stake examples from one network environment should not be read as representing production
stake-management behavior on another
([Bittensor Networks](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/bittensor-networks)).

## Distinction from Staking

- **Staking** — adds new TAO to a validator hotkey on a subnet.
- **Moving stake** — rearranges an existing delegated position without withdrawing first.

## Distinction from Unstaking

- **Unstaking** — withdraws delegated stake back to the coldkey.
- **Moving stake** — changes hotkey or subnet while delegation stays active.

## Distinction from Transferring Stake

- **Transferring stake** — changes which coldkey owns a staked position.
- **Moving stake** — changes which hotkey and/or subnet holds that position.

## Distinction from Toggle Transfer

- **Toggle transfer** — per-subnet permission for coldkey-to-coldkey stake reassignment.
- **Moving stake** — relocation between hotkeys or subnets under unchanged coldkey ownership.

## Distinction from Hotkey

- **Hotkey** — operational key that holds delegated stake for a validator role.
- **Moving stake** — operation that can reassign stake from one hotkey to another.

## Distinction from Subnet

- **Subnet** — incentive market whose pool holds the delegated position.
- **Moving stake** — operation that can reassign stake from one subnet to another.

## Distinction from Autostaking

- **Autostaking** — automatic routing of emission proceeds into delegated stake over time.
- **Moving stake** — atomic repositioning of an existing delegated position.

## Distinction from Coldkey Swap

- **Moving stake** — re-points delegation while the controlling coldkey stays the same.
- **Coldkey swap** — migrates the full wallet identity to a new coldkey pair.

## Distinction from Conviction and Locked Stake

- **Moving stake** — relocation of delegated stake between hotkeys or subnets.
- **Conviction and locked stake** — time-based commitment lock that resists balance reduction.

## Distinction from Transfer

- **Moving stake** — relocating a staked position between hotkeys or subnets.
- **Transfer** — sending free, unstaked TAO between coldkey accounts.

## Reader Boundary

This article defines moving stake as a concept. It does not describe movement steps, recommend when
to move stake, or state current fee, slippage, or fee-minimization behavior. Readers should use the
official staking documentation for operational detail
([Staking and delegation overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)).
