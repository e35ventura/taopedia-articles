# Moving Stake

Moving stake is the operation of changing which hotkey holds a staked position. The
[Staking and delegation overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)
lists it alongside transferring stake and direct unstaking as one of the participant-facing ways to
rearrange an existing staked position.

In Taopedia terms, moving stake names a delegation-change operation. It belongs alongside staking
(adding stake), unstaking (withdrawing stake), and transferring stake (reassigning coldkey
ownership) as one of the core stake-management operations.

## Why Moving Stake Exists

A participant may need to redelegate an existing staked position to a different hotkey — for
example, when shifting support to another validator, reorganizing hotkey allocation, or responding
to changing validator performance. The
[Staking and delegation overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)
describes moving stake as a participant-facing alternative to unstaking and restaking under a new
hotkey.

## Relationship to Staking

Staking adds new TAO to a validator's hotkey; moving stake redelegates stake that is already
delegated from one hotkey to another. The
[Glossary: Staking](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#staking) describes staking as
attaching TAO to a validator hotkey, while moving stake re-points that attachment to a different
hotkey.

## Relationship to Unstaking

Unstaking withdraws stake back to the coldkey so it is no longer delegated; moving stake changes
which hotkey the stake is delegated to without withdrawing it. A participant who wants TAO back in
the coldkey must unstake; a participant who wants a different hotkey to hold the position can move.

## Relationship to Transferring Stake

Transferring stake changes which coldkey owns the stake; moving stake changes which hotkey the stake
sits on. The
[Staking and delegation overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)
describes both as stake-rearrangement operations, but they act at different levels of the wallet
model — one changes the owner, the other changes the delegation target.

## Relationship to Hotkey

The hotkey is the delegation identity that moving stake changes. The
[Glossary: Hotkey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#hotkey) describes a hotkey as
the operational key that runs a validator and holds its delegated stake, and moving stake reassigns
a staked position from one hotkey to another.

## Reader Boundary

This article defines moving stake as a concept. It does not describe movement steps, recommend when
to move stake, or state current fee, slippage, or subnet behavior. Readers should use the official
staking documentation for operational detail.
