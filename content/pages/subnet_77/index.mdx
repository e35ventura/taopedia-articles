Liquidity is Bittensor Subnet 77, a subnet that rewards people for providing liquidity on
decentralized exchanges. Its on-chain description sums it up: supply liquidity on external chains
via Uniswap, and let any project be incentivized. The codebase for the subnet is the
`creativebuilds/sn77` repository.

## What Subnet 77 Rewards

Many token projects need deep, reliable liquidity on a decentralized exchange so that people can
trade them without large price swings. Subnet 77 turns that need into an incentive: it pays out to
those who supply liquidity to Uniswap pools, and it lets the community decide which pools deserve
that support. Because token holders choose which pools are favored, any project can earn deeper
liquidity by winning the community's backing, and the rewards follow the liquidity that actually
shows up in the chosen pools.

## Miner and Validator Roles

Miners are the liquidity providers: they supply liquidity to the Uniswap pools the subnet rewards,
and the more they provide to favored pools, the more they earn.

Validators are the scorekeepers: they reward miners in proportion to the liquidity each one supplies
to the pools the community has chosen to back, which is how reward flows through [[Yuma Consensus]].

## On-Chain Identity

Live SN77 subnet data is available on [TaoStats](https://taostats.io/subnets/77), which identifies
the subnet as Liquidity. Readers can reproduce the subnet identity fields directly with the
Bittensor CLI command documented in the
[btcli reference](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/btcli/#btcli-subnets-get-identity):
`btcli subnets get-identity --netuid 77 --network finney`. That Finney identity is the source of
truth for the subnet name and the GitHub repository associated with the subnet.
