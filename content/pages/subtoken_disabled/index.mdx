# Subtoken Disabled

`SubtokenDisabled` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised when subtoken trading is turned
off on a subnet. The standard-errors reference describes it as "SubToken disabled"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#subtokendisabled)).

## What It Means

Each subnet can enable or disable subtoken trading through the `SubtokenEnabled` hyperparameter.
When that setting is false, staking paths that depend on TAO-alpha pool activity are rejected with
`SubtokenDisabled` instead of proceeding to balance or swap checks
([Subnet Hyperparameters: SubtokenEnabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters),
[Subtensor source: lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/lib.rs)).

On add-stake paths, the runtime checks `SubtokenEnabled` before minimum-size, funding, and pool
conversion rules run
([Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

On move-stake and transfer-stake paths, the check applies to both the origin and destination subnet
when those subnets differ, so a disabled setting on either side can block the call
([Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a wallet tries to add stake, remove stake through pool-backed paths, or move
stake through a subnet whose `SubtokenEnabled` setting is false. That can happen after root disables
subtoken trading on a subnet, when a client targets the wrong subnet for a pool-backed action, or
when a move-stake call crosses into a subnet with trading disabled
([Subnet Hyperparameters: SubtokenEnabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters),
[Managing Your Stakes](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-sdk)).

For a reader, the practical meaning is narrow: subtoken trading is off for that subnet. The problem
is not that the subnet does not exist, that free balance cannot fund an add-stake call, or that the
pool lacks liquidity for the conversion
([Subtensor Standard Errors: SubnetNotExists](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#subnetnotexists),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Insufficient Liquidity](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#insufficientliquidity)).

## Distinction from Transfer Disallowed

`SubtokenDisabled` and `TransferDisallowed` both gate stake movement on a subnet, but they test
different switches. `SubtokenDisabled` means the subnet's `SubtokenEnabled` setting has turned off
subtoken trading altogether. `TransferDisallowed` means the subnet's transfer toggle has blocked
stake transfers even though other staking rules may still apply
([Subtensor Standard Errors: TransferDisallowed](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#transferdisallowed),
[Subnet Hyperparameters: SubtokenEnabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters)).

- **SubtokenDisabled** — subtoken trading is disabled on the subnet.
- **TransferDisallowed** — stake transfers are disallowed on the subnet.

## Distinction from Not Enough Balance To Stake

`SubtokenDisabled` and `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` both appear on add-stake calls, but at different
stages. `SubtokenDisabled` is the early subnet-toggle rejection when `SubtokenEnabled` is false.
`NotEnoughBalanceToStake` is the later funding check when free balance cannot cover the requested
TAO amount
([Not Enough Balance To Stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

- **SubtokenDisabled** — subtoken trading is disabled on the subnet.
- **NotEnoughBalanceToStake** — free balance cannot fund the requested stake amount.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the `SubtokenEnabled` gate that raises it. It
does not describe why root disabled trading on a subnet, how to query the live setting for a
specific netuid, or every extrinsic that touches `SubtokenEnabled`; those are documented separately
([Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters),
[Inspecting the Chain with Polkadot.js](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/navigating-subtensor/inspecting-the-chain)).
