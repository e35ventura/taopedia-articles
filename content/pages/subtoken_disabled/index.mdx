# Subtoken Disabled

`SubtokenDisabled` means a pool-backed staking call targeted a subnet where subtoken trading is
turned off. The standard-errors reference describes it as "SubToken disabled"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#subtokendisabled)).

Each subnet can enable or disable subtoken trading through the `SubtokenEnabled` hyperparameter
([Subnet Hyperparameters: SubtokenEnabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#subtokenenabled)).

## What It Means

When `SubtokenEnabled` is false, pool-backed staking paths that depend on TAO-alpha trading on that
subnet are rejected with `SubtokenDisabled`. That includes add-stake and remove-stake calls that
convert through the subnet pool, and move-stake calls that depend on subtoken trading on the origin
or destination subnet
([Staking Implementation](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/navigating-subtensor/swap-stake),
[Staking and Delegation Overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation),
[Subnet Hyperparameters: SubtokenEnabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#subtokenenabled)).

For debugging, treat this as a subnet trading toggle failure, not as proof that the subnet does not
exist, that free balance cannot fund an add-stake call, or that the pool lacks liquidity for the
conversion
([Subtensor Standard Errors: SubnetNotExists](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#subnetnotexists),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: InsufficientLiquidity](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#insufficientliquidity)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a wallet tries to add stake, remove stake, or move stake through a subnet
whose `SubtokenEnabled` setting is false. That can happen after root disables subtoken trading on a
subnet, when a client targets the wrong subnet for a pool-backed action, or when a move-stake call
crosses into a subnet with trading disabled
([Subnet Hyperparameters: SubtokenEnabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#subtokenenabled),
[Managing Your Stakes](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-sdk),
[Staking and Delegation Overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)).

The practical meaning is narrow: subtoken trading is off for that subnet.

## Distinction from Transfer Disallowed

`SubtokenDisabled` and `TransferDisallowed` both gate stake movement on a subnet, but they test
different switches. `SubtokenDisabled` means the subnet's `SubtokenEnabled` setting has turned off
subtoken trading altogether. `TransferDisallowed` means the subnet's transfer toggle has blocked
stake transfers even though other staking rules may still apply
([Subtensor Standard Errors: TransferDisallowed](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#transferdisallowed),
[Subnet Hyperparameters: SubtokenEnabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#subtokenenabled)).

- **SubtokenDisabled** — subtoken trading is disabled on the subnet.
- **TransferDisallowed** — stake transfers are disallowed on the subnet.

## Distinction from Not Enough Balance To Stake

`SubtokenDisabled` and `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` both appear on add-stake calls, but they reject
different conditions. `SubtokenDisabled` means subtoken trading is disabled on the targeted subnet.
`NotEnoughBalanceToStake` means the coldkey's free balance cannot cover the requested TAO amount
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: SubtokenDisabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#subtokendisabled)).

- **SubtokenDisabled** — subtoken trading is disabled on the subnet.
- **NotEnoughBalanceToStake** — free balance cannot fund the requested stake amount.

## Distinction from Insufficient Liquidity

`SubtokenDisabled` and `InsufficientLiquidity` both appear during pool-backed staking, but they
reject different conditions. `SubtokenDisabled` means subtoken trading is turned off on the subnet.
`InsufficientLiquidity` means the pool could not complete the conversion for the action
([Subtensor Standard Errors: InsufficientLiquidity](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#insufficientliquidity),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: SubtokenDisabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#subtokendisabled)).

- **SubtokenDisabled** — subtoken trading is disabled on the subnet.
- **InsufficientLiquidity** — the pool could not support the swap.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the `SubtokenEnabled` condition that raises it.
It does not describe why root disabled trading on a subnet, how to query the live setting for a
specific netuid, or every extrinsic that touches `SubtokenEnabled`; those are separate topics
([Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters),
[Subtensor Storage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/storage)).
