# Subtoken Disabled

`SubtokenDisabled` means a staking call targeted a subnet that has subtoken trading turned off. The
standard-errors reference describes it as "SubToken disabled"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#subtokendisabled)).

Each subnet can enable or disable subtoken trading through the `SubtokenEnabled` hyperparameter
([Subnet Hyperparameters: SubtokenEnabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#subtokenenabled)).

## What It Means

When `SubtokenEnabled` is false for a subnet, staking paths that depend on TAO-alpha pool activity
are rejected with `SubtokenDisabled`
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#subtokendisabled),
[Subnet Hyperparameters: SubtokenEnabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#subtokenenabled)).

For debugging, treat this as a subnet trading toggle, not as proof that the subnet does not exist,
that free balance cannot fund an add-stake call, or that the pool lacks liquidity for a conversion
([Subtensor Standard Errors: SubnetNotExists](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#subnetnotexists),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: InsufficientLiquidity](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#insufficientliquidity)).

On move-stake calls that cross subnets, either the origin or destination netuid can trigger this
error when that side has subtoken trading disabled
([Managing Your Stakes](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-sdk)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a wallet tries to add stake or move stake through a subnet whose
`SubtokenEnabled` setting is false. That can happen after root disables subtoken trading on a
subnet, when a client targets the wrong subnet for a pool-backed action, or when a move-stake call
crosses into a subnet with trading disabled
([Subnet Hyperparameters: SubtokenEnabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#subtokenenabled),
[Managing Your Stakes](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-sdk)).

The practical meaning is narrow: subtoken trading is off for that subnet.

## Distinction from Transfer Disallowed

`SubtokenDisabled` and `TransferDisallowed` both gate stake movement on a subnet, but they test
different switches. `SubtokenDisabled` means the subnet's `SubtokenEnabled` setting has turned off
subtoken trading altogether. `TransferDisallowed` means the subnet's transfer toggle has blocked
stake transfers even though other staking rules may still apply
([Subtensor Standard Errors: TransferDisallowed](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#transferdisallowed),
[Subnet Hyperparameters: SubtokenEnabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#subtokenenabled)).

- **SubtokenDisabled** — subtoken trading is disabled on the subnet.
- **TransferDisallowed** — stake transfers are disallowed on the subnet.

## Distinction from Not Enough Balance To Stake

`SubtokenDisabled` and `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` both appear on add-stake calls, but they reject
different problems. `SubtokenDisabled` means subtoken trading is disabled on the targeted subnet.
`NotEnoughBalanceToStake` means the signing coldkey does not have enough free TAO to fund the
requested stake amount
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake)).

- **SubtokenDisabled** — subtoken trading is disabled on the subnet.
- **NotEnoughBalanceToStake** — free balance cannot fund the requested stake amount.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the disabled `SubtokenEnabled` condition that
raises it. It does not describe why root disabled trading on a subnet, how to query the live setting
for a specific netuid, or every extrinsic that touches `SubtokenEnabled`; those are separate topics
([Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters),
[Subtensor Storage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/storage)).
