# Insufficient Liquidity

`InsufficientLiquidity` means the subnet pool could not simulate or complete the TAO-alpha
conversion needed for the requested stake action. The standard-errors reference describes it as "Not
enough liquidity in the pool"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#insufficientliquidity)).

Staking and unstaking move value through each subnet's TAO and alpha reserves
([Understanding Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-subnets),
[Staking and Delegation Overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)).

## What It Means

Add-stake and remove-stake calls depend on the subnet pool to convert between TAO and alpha.
`InsufficientLiquidity` means that conversion could not be completed for the requested action, so
the extrinsic is rejected
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#insufficientliquidity),
[Understanding Slippage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/slippage)).

For debugging, treat this as a pool-conversion failure, not as proof that the named amount is below
the minimum stake increment, that free balance cannot fund the call, or that a slippage cap blocked
execution
([Subtensor Standard Errors: AmountTooLow](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#amounttoolow),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: SlippageTooHigh](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#slippagetoohigh)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when the subnet pool cannot support the conversion behind a stake or unstake
call. That can happen when reserves are too thin for the trade size, when pool state prevents the
swap from simulating cleanly, or when pricing through the pool fails for the requested amount
([Understanding Slippage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/slippage),
[Managing Your Stakes](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-sdk),
[Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#insufficientliquidity)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the pool could not complete the conversion needed for this staking
action.

## Distinction from Amount Too Low

`InsufficientLiquidity` and `AmountTooLow` both appear during staking validation, but they reject
different failures. `InsufficientLiquidity` means the pool could not simulate or complete the swap
needed for the action. `AmountTooLow` means the named amount is below the chain's minimum stake
increment or is zero on an unstake path
([Subtensor Standard Errors: AmountTooLow](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#amounttoolow),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: InsufficientLiquidity](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#insufficientliquidity)).

- **InsufficientLiquidity** — the pool could not support the swap.
- **AmountTooLow** — the stake amount is below the minimum allowed increment.

## Distinction from Slippage Too High

`InsufficientLiquidity` and `SlippageTooHigh` both relate to subnet pool conversions, but they
reject different conditions. `InsufficientLiquidity` means the pool could not complete the swap
needed for the action. `SlippageTooHigh` means the caller's own slippage ceiling was exceeded with
partial execution disabled
([Subtensor Standard Errors: SlippageTooHigh](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#slippagetoohigh),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: InsufficientLiquidity](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#insufficientliquidity),
[Understanding Slippage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/slippage)).

- **InsufficientLiquidity** — the pool could not support the swap.
- **SlippageTooHigh** — the named amount exceeds the caller's slippage cap.

## Distinction from Not Enough Balance To Stake

`InsufficientLiquidity` and `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` both block add-stake calls, but they compare
different conditions. `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` means the coldkey's free balance cannot cover the
requested TAO amount. `InsufficientLiquidity` means the subnet pool could not complete the
conversion for the action
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: InsufficientLiquidity](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#insufficientliquidity)).

- **InsufficientLiquidity** — the pool could not support the swap.
- **NotEnoughBalanceToStake** — free balance cannot fund the requested stake amount.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label on common add-stake and remove-stake paths. It does
not describe how to size trades against live reserve levels, or every staking path that can return
this error; those are separate topics
([Understanding Slippage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/slippage),
[Managing Your Stakes](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-sdk)).
