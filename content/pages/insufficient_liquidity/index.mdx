# Insufficient Liquidity

`InsufficientLiquidity` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised on staking paths. The
standard-errors reference describes it as "Not enough liquidity in the pool"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#insufficientliquidity)).

## What It Means

Staking and unstaking move value through each subnet's TAO and alpha reserves. Before balances
update, the runtime simulates or executes the pool conversion behind the call.
`InsufficientLiquidity` means that conversion could not be completed in the pool for the requested
action, so the extrinsic stops before stake counters change
([Understanding Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-subnets),
[Understanding Slippage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/slippage),
[Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

On add-stake paths, this can appear when the simulated TAO-to-alpha swap fails, when the swap would
return zero alpha, or when the hotkey stake pool cannot accept the credited alpha amount
([Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

On remove-stake paths, this can appear when the simulated alpha-to-TAO conversion for the unstake
amount cannot be priced through the pool
([Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when the subnet pool cannot support the conversion behind a stake or unstake
call. That can happen when reserves are too thin for the trade size, when pool state prevents the
swap from simulating cleanly, or when the credited alpha amount cannot be applied to the hotkey
stake pool ([Understanding Slippage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/slippage),
[Managing Your Stakes](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-sdk),
[Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

For a reader, the practical meaning is narrow: the pool could not complete the conversion needed for
this staking action. The problem is not that the named amount is below the minimum stake increment,
that free balance cannot fund the call, or that a slippage cap blocked execution
([Amount Too Low](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#amounttoolow),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: SlippageTooHigh](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#slippagetoohigh)).

## Distinction from Amount Too Low

`InsufficientLiquidity` and `AmountTooLow` both appear during staking validation, but they reject
different failures. `InsufficientLiquidity` means the pool could not simulate or complete the swap
needed for the action. `AmountTooLow` means the named amount is below the chain's minimum stake
increment or is zero on an unstake path
([Amount Too Low](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#amounttoolow),
[Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

- **InsufficientLiquidity** — the pool could not support the swap.
- **AmountTooLow** — the stake amount is below the minimum allowed increment.

## Distinction from Not Enough Balance To Stake

`InsufficientLiquidity` and `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` both block add-stake calls, but at different
stages. `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` is the funding check on whether the coldkey's free balance can
cover the requested TAO amount. `InsufficientLiquidity` is the later pool-conversion failure after
the swap path cannot complete
([Not Enough Balance To Stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Subtensor source: stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)).

- **InsufficientLiquidity** — the pool could not support the swap.
- **NotEnoughBalanceToStake** — free balance cannot fund the requested stake amount.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label on common add-stake and remove-stake paths. It does
not describe every move-stake or order-swap path that can also raise `InsufficientLiquidity`, or how
to size trades against live reserve levels; those are documented separately
([Understanding Slippage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/slippage),
[Managing Your Stakes](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/managing-stake-sdk)).
