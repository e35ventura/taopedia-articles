# Subnet 10: Swap

Swap is Bittensor Subnet 10 (SN10), operated by the [Swap-Subnet](https://github.com/Swap-Subnet)
team. The subnet's stated goal is to onboard users to Bittensor by channeling incentives toward real
liquidity provision on [TaoFi](https://www.taofi.com/pool), a decentralized exchange where TAO and
USDC can be swapped. Rather than rewarding computation or data contributions, Swap rewards
participants for the depth and quality of the liquidity they commit to a live on-chain pool.

References: [Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet (GitHub)](https://github.com/Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet),
[taofi.com/pool](https://www.taofi.com/pool)

## How the Mechanism Works

Miners on Swap hold liquidity positions in the TAO/USDC pool on TaoFi, a Uniswap V3-style automated
market maker. Every 24 hours, validators measure how much in trading fees each miner's position
collected during that window. A position earns fees only when the current exchange rate falls within
its chosen tick range — positions set too wide spread capital thin and earn little; positions set
too narrow go out of range and earn nothing when the price moves. Miners must therefore actively
manage their price ranges to maximize fee income.

Scores are assigned proportionally to fee income across all registered miners for that epoch.
Per-miner scores flow into [[Yuma Consensus]], which distributes per-block TAO emissions through
[[Dynamic TAO]].

References: [Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet (GitHub)](https://github.com/Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet)

## Participating as a Miner

A miner's core activity is providing liquidity to the TAO/USDC pool on TaoFi using an EVM-compatible
wallet. After depositing both TAO and USDC into a position with a chosen tick range, the miner
accumulates trading fees from any swaps that cross that range. Rewards from the pool are distributed
daily to the EVM wallet address by default.

Miners who also register a Bittensor hotkey can optionally opt in to receiving SN10 alpha tokens
instead of direct EVM wallet payouts. In that case, the miner links their EVM address to their
hotkey by committing a signed message to the network, then runs the miner process so the subnet can
verify position ownership and attribute fee income to the correct neuron.

References:
[Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet — miner guide](https://github.com/Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet/blob/main/docs/taofi_lp.md)

## Participating as a Validator

Validators on Swap query historical on-chain data to reconstruct each miner's fee income for the
preceding 24-hour period. This requires access to an archive subtensor node capable of serving
historical EVM state — validators connect to a configured RPC endpoint for this purpose. After
computing per-miner scores, validators submit weight vectors to the Bittensor chain. The Wandb
integration is recommended by the operator for monitoring weight submissions and verifying that
scoring behaves consistently across validators.

References:
[Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet — validator guide](https://github.com/Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet/blob/main/docs/validator.md)
