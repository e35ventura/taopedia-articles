Swap (SN10) connects Bittensor's incentive layer to decentralized finance by rewarding participants
who supply liquidity to the TAO/USDC concentrated liquidity pool on [TaoFi](https://www.taofi.com).
The subnet treats liquidity providers as miners: their economic activity on-chain becomes the
primary signal validators use to distribute emissions. This approach is intended to deepen on-chain
liquidity for TAO while directing Bittensor rewards toward participants performing a measurable
financial service, according to the [subnet repository](https://github.com/Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet).

## How the Mechanism Works

Miners in Swap hold active liquidity positions in the TAO/USDC pool on
[TaoFi](https://www.taofi.com/pool). Validators measure each position's performance by tracking the
swap fees it collected over the previous 24 hours — positions that remain in-range and capture more
trading volume earn proportionally higher scores. This fee-based metric creates a direct link
between a miner's capital efficiency and their subnet rewards.

Validators calculate scores independently and submit weights through the [[Yuma Consensus]]
mechanism, which aggregates those scores to determine the final emission distribution among
registered miners. There is no subjective scoring; the fee accrual data is retrieved directly from
on-chain pool state.

## Participating as a Miner

To earn SN10 alpha emissions, a participant first establishes a liquidity position in the
[TaoFi TAO/USDC pool](https://www.taofi.com/pool) using an EVM-compatible wallet such as MetaMask.
Both TAO and USDC are required to open a position, and the participant must select a price range for
their concentrated liquidity allocation. Once a position is active and generating fees, the
participant registers a Bittensor hotkey and runs the subnet's miner software, which associates the
EVM wallet with the on-chain hotkey so validators can attribute the position's earnings to that
identity.

Participants who prefer not to run a miner can still provide liquidity to TaoFi and earn swap fees
directly to their EVM wallet, but will not receive SN10 alpha token emissions. Running a miner is
optional but required for on-chain reward accrual.

## Participating as a Validator

Validators in Swap query on-chain data from the TaoFi pool to retrieve per-position fee accruals
over a rolling 24-hour window. Each registered miner's hotkey is linked to one or more liquidity
positions, and the validator sums those positions' fee earnings to produce a score. Validators are
advised to connect to a local subtensor node to reliably access the historical chain state needed
for accurate scoring, as described in the
[validator documentation](https://github.com/Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet/blob/main/docs/validator.md).
Weights submitted by validators are reconciled through [[Yuma Consensus]] to determine the final
emission allocation.

## On-Chain Identity

Subnet 10 is registered on Bittensor mainnet under the name "Swap" with 256 registered neurons. The
subnet's on-chain record, including the owner coldkey
(`5GbcimKjp17QPUoS568DBSMNqV2pmDetBf3xyC15vh4bTFE1`), current neuron count, and emission parameters,
is viewable at [taostats.io/subnets/10](https://taostats.io/subnets/10). The associated code
repository is [Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet](https://github.com/Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet) and the pool
interface is hosted at [taofi.com/pool](https://www.taofi.com/pool).
