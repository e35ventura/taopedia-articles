Swap (SN10) is a Bittensor subnet operated by [Swap-Subnet](https://github.com/Swap-Subnet) that
channels decentralized incentives toward providing liquidity on [TaoFi](https://www.taofi.com/pool),
the primary TAO/USDC automated market maker built on the Bittensor EVM layer. Rather than rewarding
AI inference or data labor, Swap rewards participants for depositing capital into a Uniswap V3–style
concentrated liquidity pool, effectively bootstrapping on-chain liquidity for the TAO token.

## How the Mechanism Works

Every 24 hours, validators measure the fee revenue earned by each miner's liquidity position in the
TAO/USDC pool. Because Uniswap V3 concentrates liquidity within price ranges set by the depositor,
miners whose positions are in-range and capturing active trading volume earn more fees — and
therefore receive higher scores — than miners whose capital sits out-of-range and contributes
nothing to actual trades. This design tightly aligns subnet rewards with genuine market utility: the
miners most useful to traders are the ones most rewarded by the network.

Validators independently fetch on-chain fee data for each registered miner's position, compute a
score proportional to fee income, and submit weights through [[Yuma Consensus]] as in any other
Bittensor subnet. Because the scoring metric (fees earned) is entirely public and verifiable
on-chain, there is no oracle trust assumption and no off-chain data source that validators must
agree on. Rewards flow in SN10 alpha tokens, which can be staked or sold like any other subnet token
under [[Dynamic TAO]].

## Participating as a Miner

A miner's primary obligation is to hold an active, in-range liquidity position in the TaoFi TAO/USDC
pool. The first step is setting up a compatible EVM wallet such as [MetaMask](https://metamask.io)
and bridging or acquiring both TAO and USDC in that wallet. From the
[TaoFi pool page](https://www.taofi.com/pool), miners deposit both tokens within a chosen price
range and receive a position NFT whose ID they will later need to associate with their Bittensor
hotkey.

Once a position is open and earning fees, a miner registers a Bittensor hotkey on SN10 and runs the
miner software, which links the EVM wallet address to that hotkey. From that point forward, the
validator network tracks fee accumulation against the registered address and routes SN10 alpha
rewards to the hotkey rather than directly to the EVM wallet. Miners who prefer passive income
without running any software can still provide liquidity and receive pool fees directly to their EVM
wallet; running the miner software is only necessary to earn SN10 emissions on top of those fees.

The key operational skill is range management: a position that drifts out of the current TAO price
earns zero fees and therefore zero subnet rewards. Active miners monitor the pool price on
[TaoFi](https://www.taofi.com/pool) and rebalance their positions as needed.

## Participating as a Validator

Validators run the subnet's validation software, which connects to both the Bittensor network and an
archive EVM node to reconstruct 24-hour fee histories for each miner's registered LP position.
Because this historical query requires access to archival blockchain state, validators are expected
to operate against an archive endpoint rather than a pruned full node.

After collecting fee data, the validator converts raw on-chain fee amounts into normalized scores
and submits weight updates for all active miners. The subnet's open-source scoring logic is
available in the [Swap-Subnet GitHub repository](https://github.com/Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet).
Validators are encouraged to enable Weights & Biases (wandb) logging to share transparency reports
with the community.
