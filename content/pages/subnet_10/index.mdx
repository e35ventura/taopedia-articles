# Subnet 10: Swap

Swap is Bittensor Subnet 10 (SN10), operated by Syeam Bin Abdullah. Its purpose is to channel
Bittensor's token emission mechanism toward on-chain liquidity: the subnet rewards participants who
supply capital to the TAO/USDC pool on [TaoFi](https://www.taofi.com/pool), a decentralised exchange
built on an EVM-compatible chain. Where most subnets direct compute toward AI workloads, Swap
targets a purely financial task — deepening the secondary market for TAO.

References: [Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet (GitHub)](https://github.com/Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet),
[taofi.com/pool](https://www.taofi.com/pool)

## How the Mechanism Works

TaoFi's TAO/USDC pool uses a concentrated liquidity model in which participants deposit both tokens
within a self-chosen price range. When a trade occurs and the prevailing market price sits inside a
position's range, that position earns a proportional share of the swap fee. Positions whose range
falls outside the current price accumulate nothing until the market price returns.

Swap's scoring function maps directly onto this structure. Validators query an archive node to
retrieve each registered miner's fee income over the preceding 24 hours, then assign weights
proportional to that income. The measurement window is rolling rather than epoch-aligned, so
positions must maintain continuous in-range placement — not just be present at snapshot time — to
earn rewards. Validator weights flow into [[Yuma Consensus]] to produce per-block emissions,
distributed through [[Dynamic TAO]].

The mechanism is fully transparent: fee income is a verifiable, on-chain quantity, so the scoring
process is reproducible by any party with access to the same archive data.

References: [Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet (GitHub)](https://github.com/Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet),
[taostats.io/subnets/10](https://taostats.io/subnets/10)

## Participating as a Miner

Miners on Swap hold liquidity positions in the TaoFi TAO/USDC pool and register a Bittensor hotkey
that the subnet software links to the EVM address controlling those positions. Validators then
attribute the on-chain fee income of the linked positions to the corresponding neuron.

Earnings are driven by two factors: the capital deployed and the price range chosen. Positions set
closer to the current market price earn fees at a higher rate per unit of capital but go out of
range more readily during price swings. Positions set over a wider range capture fees more
consistently but at a lower rate. This trade-off is the primary strategic dimension for miners, and
it is resolved solely through capital allocation and range selection rather than through any
off-chain computation.

References:
[TaoFi liquidity provider guide](https://github.com/Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet/blob/main/docs/taofi_lp.md)

## Participating as a Validator

Validators on Swap reconstruct each miner's 24-hour fee income by querying historical pool state
from an archive subtensor node. Because two validators querying the same archive data arrive at
identical scores, Swap's validation model is among the more auditable in the Bittensor ecosystem:
disagreements between validators reflect data-access differences, not subjective judgement.

Validators submit per-miner weight sets on each epoch. Those weights, averaged across the validator
set via [[Yuma Consensus]], determine how emissions are distributed across the 256-neuron cohort.

[Swap validator setup guide](https://github.com/Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet/blob/main/docs/validator.md)
