# Subnet 10: Swap

Swap is Bittensor Subnet 10 (SN10), operated by [Swap-Subnet](https://github.com/Swap-Subnet). The
subnet's stated goal is onboarding users and capital to the Bittensor ecosystem by directing
token-incentivized liquidity toward [TaoFi](https://www.taofi.com/pool), a Uniswap V3-style
decentralized exchange that hosts a TAO/USDC trading pool. Participants earn SN10 alpha emissions in
proportion to the fees their liquidity positions generate on-chain.

References: [Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet (GitHub)](https://github.com/Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet),
[taofi.com/pool](https://www.taofi.com/pool)

## How the Mechanism Works

The Swap subnet connects on-chain DeFi activity to Bittensor's incentive layer. Miners deposit TAO
and USDC into the TaoFi TAO/USDC liquidity pool, which operates under a concentrated-liquidity
model: each position covers a price range chosen by the provider, and fees accumulate only while the
market price is within that range. Once per day, validators measure how much fee revenue each
registered position earned during the preceding 24-hour window and translate those figures into
per-miner scores. A miner whose position is out of range or holds less capital earns fewer fees and
therefore receives a lower weight.

Scores flow into [[Yuma Consensus]], which converts validator weight-sets into per-block TAO
emissions distributed through [[Dynamic TAO]]. Because the scoring metric is fee revenue rather than
notional liquidity size, the mechanism rewards active range management and correct market
positioning over passive capital placement.

References: [Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet (GitHub)](https://github.com/Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet)

## Participating as a Miner

Miners on Swap interact with the TaoFi protocol using an EVM-compatible wallet (such as MetaMask)
connected to Bittensor's EVM layer. They supply both TAO and USDC to the TAO/USDC pool on
[TaoFi](https://www.taofi.com/pool), selecting a price range for their concentrated-liquidity
position, and take note of the resulting position ID. Miners then link their EVM wallet to a
registered Bittensor hotkey by committing a signed association on-chain, so that validators can
attribute on-chain fee revenue to a specific subnet participant. Emissions are distributed to the
miner's hotkey rather than directly to the EVM wallet when the hotkey is registered and the
association is established.

Miners whose positions remain in-range for a larger fraction of the day accumulate more fees and
therefore earn higher emissions. Active range management — adjusting position bounds in response to
market movement — is the primary lever available to competitive participants.

References:
[Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet — docs/taofi_lp.md](https://github.com/Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet/blob/main/docs/taofi_lp.md)

## Participating as a Validator

Validators on Swap run a scoring process that queries historical on-chain data to reconstruct the
fee revenue earned by each registered miner's liquidity position over the previous 24 hours. This
requires access to an archive subtensor node that can serve historical state at arbitrary block
heights. Validators read on-chain commitments that associate hotkeys with EVM addresses and position
IDs, then calculate fee revenue for each position, assign proportional scores, and submit weight
vectors to the chain. The Weights & Biases (wandb) integration is available for monitoring validator
output over time.

Validators that submit honest, timely weight-sets earn emissions through [[Yuma Consensus]].
