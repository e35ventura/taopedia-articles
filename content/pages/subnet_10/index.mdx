# Subnet 10: Swap

Swap is Bittensor Subnet 10 (SN10). Rather than coordinating AI inference or model training, the
subnet bridges the Bittensor token economy to decentralized finance: it distributes TAO emissions to
participants who provide real on-chain liquidity to a TAO/USDC pool on
[TaoFi](https://www.taofi.com/pool). The codebase is maintained at
[Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet](https://github.com/Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet).

References: [Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet (GitHub)](https://github.com/Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet),
[taofi.com/pool](https://www.taofi.com/pool)

## How the Mechanism Works

Swap's scoring is grounded in fee revenue rather than subjective output quality. TaoFi uses a
concentrated liquidity model where providers choose a price range within which their capital is
active and earns fees from traders. Every 24 hours, validators measure how much trading fee income
each registered position collected during that window. Miners whose positions generated the highest
fees earn a proportionally larger share of the epoch's emissions through [[Yuma Consensus]] and
[[Dynamic TAO]].

This design aligns miner behavior directly with pool health. Positions that stay within the active
price range, are sized appropriately, and sit in fee tiers that attract real swap volume earn more.
Positions that drift out of range or are placed in low-traffic tiers earn proportionally less.
Scoring is entirely objective and on-chain — validators derive weights solely from fee-collection
records, not subjective judgment.

Validators query fee history for each registered pool position, normalize the results across all
active miners in the epoch, and submit the resulting weight vector to [[Yuma Consensus]].

References: [Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet (GitHub)](https://github.com/Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet)

## Miners

Miners on Swap are liquidity providers. Their economic role is to deploy TAO and USDC into TaoFi's
pool in a way that maximizes fee revenue. The core skill is liquidity management: selecting the
right price range around the current market price, choosing an appropriate fee tier, and keeping the
position funded and in-range as the market moves. A position that consistently earns top-decile fees
earns top-decile subnet emissions. A position that sits out of range earns nothing.

Miners link a Bittensor hotkey to their pool position via a signed on-chain message so that subnet
emissions flow to their registered identity. The association is verified by validators without any
intermediary.

References: [Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet (GitHub)](https://github.com/Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet),
[taofi.com/pool](https://www.taofi.com/pool)

## Validators

Validators on Swap periodically read on-chain state to measure fee income for each registered
position over the prior 24-hour window. The measurement is sourced from both a Bittensor archive
node and a Web3 provider for the EVM-compatible state where the TaoFi pool is deployed. Fee totals
are normalized across the active miner set to produce a weight vector, which is then submitted to
[[Yuma Consensus]]. Because scoring is fully objective, validators on this subnet do not evaluate
subjective output quality — they query chain state and compute a deterministic score.

References: [Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet (GitHub)](https://github.com/Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet)
