Subnet 10, **Swap**, is a Bittensor subnet that bridges decentralised finance and Bittensor's
incentive layer by rewarding on-chain liquidity provisioning. Miners earn TAO alpha emissions in
proportion to the trading fees generated by their positions in the TAO/USDC pool on
[TaoFi](https://www.taofi.com/pool), a decentralised exchange built on an EVM-compatible chain. The
subnet was authored by Syeam Bin Abdullah and is published under the MIT license.

## How the Mechanism Works

TaoFi operates a concentrated liquidity pool modelled on Uniswap V3: liquidity providers deposit TAO
and USDC within a self-chosen price range, and when trades occur inside that range the position
accumulates a share of the swap fees. Unlike a traditional market-making system that spreads capital
uniformly, concentrated liquidity allows providers to focus capital around the current price,
earning more fees per unit of capital when their range stays active.

Swap's validators score miners by querying an archive node to retrieve each registered position's
fee income over the preceding 24 hours. A position that remains in-range — meaning the live TAO/USDC
price sits between the position's lower and upper tick bounds — earns fees continuously. One that
drifts out of range earns nothing until the price returns. Validators submit those fee-income scores
to [[Yuma Consensus]], which converts them into alpha token emissions weighted by each miner's
relative contribution across the epoch.

Because scores derive directly from verifiable on-chain data, the mechanism cannot be gamed by
self-reporting: a miner's reward ceiling is set entirely by actual trading activity in the pool.

## Participating as a Miner

Miners participate by holding active liquidity positions in the TaoFi TAO/USDC pool and linking
those positions to a registered Bittensor hotkey. The subnet software reads on-chain state to match
each hotkey to its associated EVM address and then attributes that address's fee income to the
neuron's score. Participants who do not register a hotkey can still earn swap fees natively through
TaoFi, but those earnings bypass Bittensor's alpha emission mechanism entirely.

Miner strategy centres on range management: a narrow range around the current price maximises fee
yield per dollar deployed but requires more frequent rebalancing as the price moves, while a wider
range demands less active management at the cost of lower capital efficiency.

## Participating as a Validator

Validators maintain a connection to an archive subtensor node capable of serving historical data,
since scoring requires reconstructing fee accrual across a rolling 24-hour window rather than
reading a single block snapshot. Each scoring cycle, the validator resolves every registered miner's
EVM address and aggregates the fees earned by all linked Uniswap V3 positions. The resulting scores
are broadcast to the network and folded into consensus.

The verifiability of on-chain fee data means that two honest validators independently querying the
same archive node will converge on identical scores, making Swap's validation model straightforward
to implement and audit.
