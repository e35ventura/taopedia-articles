# Subnet 10: Swap

Swap is Bittensor Subnet 10 (SN10), operated by Syeam Bin Abdullah. Unlike subnets that coordinate
AI inference or model training, Swap bridges the Bittensor token economy to decentralized finance by
rewarding participants who provide real on-chain liquidity to a TAO<>USDC pool on
[TaoFi](https://www.taofi.com/pool). The project's codebase is maintained at
[Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet](https://github.com/Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet).

References: [Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet (GitHub)](https://github.com/Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet),
[taofi.com](https://www.taofi.com/pool)

## How the Mechanism Works

Swap's incentive design is grounded in fee revenue rather than subjective scoring. Miners hold
liquidity positions in TaoFi's TAO<>USDC pool, which follows a concentrated liquidity model where
providers select a price range within which their capital is active. Every 24 hours, validators
measure how much trading fee income each registered position collected during that window. Miners
whose positions generated the highest fees relative to other participants earn a larger share of
that epoch's emissions through [[Yuma Consensus]].

This design aligns miner behavior directly with pool health: a miner earns more by keeping their
position within the active price range, sizing it appropriately, and selecting a fee tier that
attracts real swap volume. Positions that drift out of range, remain undersized, or sit in
low-traffic fee tiers earn proportionally less. Scoring is entirely on-chain and objective —
validators do not exercise subjective judgment about output quality.

Validators query historical fee-collection records for each miner's registered pool position,
normalize the fee totals across all active miners in the epoch, and submit the resulting weight
vector to [[Yuma Consensus]]. Emissions from [[Dynamic TAO]] then flow to miners in proportion to
their validated fee scores.

References: [Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet (GitHub)](https://github.com/Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet)

## Participating as a Miner

A Swap miner is first and foremost a liquidity provider. The foundational step is depositing TAO and
USDC into TaoFi's concentrated liquidity pool at [taofi.com/pool](https://www.taofi.com/pool),
selecting a price range, and noting the position ID assigned by the pool contract. The position must
remain funded and in-range to collect fees from traders using the pool.

Participants may optionally register a Bittensor hotkey and run the subnet's miner software, which
links the EVM address holding the pool position to a Bittensor identity. This lets subnet emissions
flow to the registered hotkey rather than directly to the EVM wallet. The miner software signs a
message that associates the two addresses on-chain so validators can verify ownership without any
intermediary.

Fee revenue from the pool accrues continuously and can be claimed at any time through the TaoFi
interface. Subnet emissions tied to the hotkey are distributed per epoch based on the validator
weight assignments.

References: [taofi.com/pool](https://www.taofi.com/pool),
[Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet (GitHub)](https://github.com/Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet)

## Participating as a Validator

Validators on SN10 run a process that periodically reads on-chain state to measure fee income for
each registered miner. For each participant, the validator retrieves total fees earned by the
miner's pool position during the preceding 24-hour window from an archive Bittensor node combined
with a Web3 provider for Ethereum-compatible state. Those values are normalized across the active
miner set to produce a weight vector, which is submitted to [[Yuma Consensus]].

Because scoring is objective and derived entirely from on-chain data, validators on Swap do not
evaluate the subjective quality of any outputs. The main operational requirement is access to a
reliable archive subtensor node and a Web3 RPC endpoint. Validators are encouraged to report scoring
telemetry through Weights & Biases (wandb) to support transparency across the network.

References: [Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet (GitHub)](https://github.com/Swap-Subnet/swap-subnet)
