# Not Subnet Owner

`NotSubnetOwner` means a `set_subnet_identity` call on netuid 1 or another named subnet was signed
by a coldkey that does not match `get_subnet_owner` for that submitted netuid. The standard-errors
reference describes it as "Not a subnet owner"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notsubnetowner),
[Subtensor source: identity.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/utils/identity.rs)).

## What It Means

The `set_subnet_identity` extrinsic calls `do_set_subnet_identity`, which updates subnet identity
metadata for a submitted `netuid`. Before validating or storing that metadata, the runtime compares
the signing coldkey to `get_subnet_owner(netuid)` and returns `NotSubnetOwner` when they are not
equal
([Subtensor source: identity.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/utils/identity.rs),
[Subtensor source: dispatches.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/macros/dispatches.rs)).

This ownership check runs before `is_valid_subnet_identity` on the same path. A caller that fails
the ownership comparison is rejected before subnet identity field validation runs
([Subtensor source: identity.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/utils/identity.rs)).

This is an authorization rejection on `set_subnet_identity`, not proof that submitted identity
fields fail byte-limit validation.

## Why It Appears

This error appears when `set_subnet_identity` is signed by a coldkey that does not match
`get_subnet_owner` for the submitted `netuid`. That can happen when tooling uses the wrong wallet,
when ownership changed and credentials were not updated, or when a caller targets a `netuid` they do
not control under the ownership record checked by the runtime
([Subtensor source: identity.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/utils/identity.rs)).

When debugging a failed `set_subnet_identity` on netuid 1, compare against the next rejection on the
same path. `InvalidIdentity` means ownership passed but the submitted subnet identity payload failed
`is_valid_subnet_identity`
([Invalid Identity](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#invalididentity),
[Subtensor source: identity.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/utils/identity.rs)).

## Distinction from Invalid Identity

`NotSubnetOwner` and `InvalidIdentity` both appear on `set_subnet_identity`, but at different
checks. `NotSubnetOwner` is returned when `get_subnet_owner(netuid)` does not equal the signing
coldkey. `InvalidIdentity` is returned later when the subnet identity struct fails validation limits
([Invalid Identity](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#invalididentity),
[Subtensor source: identity.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/utils/identity.rs)).

- **NotSubnetOwner** — signing coldkey does not match `get_subnet_owner(netuid)`.
- **InvalidIdentity** — subnet identity fields fail validation.

## Distinction from Bad Origin

`NotSubnetOwner` and `BadOrigin` are both dispatch failures, but they indicate different conditions.
`NotSubnetOwner` is a signed-caller ownership mismatch on `set_subnet_identity`. `BadOrigin` means
the transaction origin does not satisfy the extrinsic's origin requirement
([Subtensor Standard Errors: BadOrigin](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#badorigin),
[Subtensor source: identity.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/utils/identity.rs)).

- **NotSubnetOwner** — signing coldkey does not match `get_subnet_owner(netuid)`.
- **BadOrigin** — origin type does not satisfy extrinsic requirements.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the ownership comparison in
`do_set_subnet_identity`. It does not report `get_subnet_owner` results for netuid 1 or any other
live `netuid`; those depend on chain state at observation time
([Subtensor source: identity.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/utils/identity.rs),
[Bittensor Networks](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/bittensor-networks)).
