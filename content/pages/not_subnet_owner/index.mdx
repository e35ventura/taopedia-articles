# Not Subnet Owner

`NotSubnetOwner` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised when a subnet identity update is
signed by a coldkey that does not own the targeted subnet. The standard-errors reference describes
it as "Not a subnet owner"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notsubnetowner)).

## What It Means

Subnet owners can publish metadata such as name, contact fields, and repository links for a subnet
they control. `do_set_subnet_identity` loads the owner recorded in `SubnetOwner` storage for the
supplied netuid and compares it against the transaction signer. `NotSubnetOwner` is raised when
those accounts do not match
([Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters),
[Subtensor source: identity.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/utils/identity.rs)).

This is the first check in that extrinsic. The identity field-length validation in
`is_valid_subnet_identity` only runs after the signer has already been confirmed as the subnet owner
([Subtensor source: identity.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/utils/identity.rs)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a wallet other than the recorded subnet owner submits `set_subnet_identity`
for a netuid. That includes a contributor coldkey, a proxy without owner scope, or automation that
targets the wrong netuid on netuid 1 or another named subnet
([Understanding Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-subnets),
[Subtensor source: identity.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/utils/identity.rs)).

For a reader, the practical meaning is narrow: the signing coldkey is not the owner of the subnet
named in the call. The problem is not that the identity fields exceed their byte limits or that the
subnet does not exist
([Invalid Identity](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#invalididentity)).

Many other owner-scoped subnet extrinsics use `ensure_subnet_owner` or `ensure_subnet_owner_or_root`
and return `BadOrigin` instead of `NotSubnetOwner` when the signer lacks permission
([Subtensor source: misc.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/utils/misc.rs)).

## Distinction from Bad Origin

`NotSubnetOwner` and `BadOrigin` both involve subnet-owner authorization, but on different
extrinsics. `NotSubnetOwner` is the explicit rejection in `do_set_subnet_identity` when
`get_subnet_owner(netuid)` does not equal the signer. `BadOrigin` is what `ensure_subnet_owner`
returns on hyperparameter and other owner-scoped paths when the signer is not the recorded owner
([Bad Origin](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#badorigin),
[Subtensor source: misc.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/utils/misc.rs),
[Subtensor source: identity.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/utils/identity.rs)).

- **NotSubnetOwner** — the signer is not the subnet owner on `set_subnet_identity`.
- **BadOrigin** — the signer lacks the required owner or root permission on other privileged paths.

## Distinction from Invalid Identity

`NotSubnetOwner` and `InvalidIdentity` are both checks in `do_set_subnet_identity`, but at different
stages. `NotSubnetOwner` confirms the signer owns the targeted subnet. `InvalidIdentity` is the
later rejection when the submitted identity fields fail `is_valid_subnet_identity`
([Invalid Identity](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#invalididentity),
[Subtensor source: identity.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/utils/identity.rs)).

- **NotSubnetOwner** — the signer is not the subnet owner.
- **InvalidIdentity** — the identity fields fail validation.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the owner check that raises it on
`set_subnet_identity`. It does not describe every privileged subnet extrinsic that can fail for lack
of owner permission through `BadOrigin`; those are documented separately
([Bad Origin](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#badorigin),
[Bittensor CLI: Permissions Guide](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/btcli/btcli-permissions)).
