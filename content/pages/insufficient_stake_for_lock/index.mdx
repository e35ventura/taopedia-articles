# Insufficient Stake For Lock

`InsufficientStakeForLock` means a conviction lock request named more alpha than the coldkey holds
in total on that subnet. The standard-errors reference describes it as "Insufficient stake for lock"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#insufficientstakeforlock)).

Conviction locks bind part of a coldkey's subnet alpha to a commitment signal
([Conviction and Locked Stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).

## What It Means

A conviction lock-stake call either opens a new lock on a hotkey or adds alpha to an existing lock
on that subnet. `InsufficientStakeForLock` means the coldkey's total staked alpha on the netuid is
below the lock amount being requested, so the call is rejected
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#insufficientstakeforlock),
[Conviction and Locked Stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).

For debugging, treat this as a total-alpha shortfall on the lock path, not as proof that the
submitted lock amount is zero, that unlocked alpha is too low for an unstake, or that the hotkey
account does not exist
([Subtensor Standard Errors: AmountTooLow](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#amounttoolow),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: StakeUnavailable](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#stakeunavailable),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyAccountNotExists](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#hotkeyaccountnotexists)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a coldkey tries to lock more alpha on a subnet than it currently holds there
in total. That can happen when the lock request exceeds current holdings or when a top-up would
require more total alpha than the coldkey has staked on the netuid
([Conviction and Locked Stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking),
[Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#insufficientstakeforlock)).

The practical meaning is narrow: total staked alpha on the subnet is below the lock request.

## Distinction from Stake Unavailable

`InsufficientStakeForLock` and `StakeUnavailable` both involve conviction locks, but on opposite
directions. `InsufficientStakeForLock` blocks creating or increasing a lock when total alpha on the
subnet is too low. `StakeUnavailable` blocks remove-stake and move-stake paths when the request
exceeds unlocked alpha after existing locks are excluded
([Subtensor Standard Errors: StakeUnavailable](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#stakeunavailable),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: InsufficientStakeForLock](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#insufficientstakeforlock),
[Conviction and Locked Stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).

- **InsufficientStakeForLock** — total alpha on the subnet is below the lock request.
- **StakeUnavailable** — the request exceeds unlocked alpha on the subnet.

## Distinction from Not Enough Stake To Withdraw

`InsufficientStakeForLock` and `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` both compare stake amounts, but on
different operations. `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` means a hotkey-coldkey pair lacks enough total
staked alpha for a removal request. `InsufficientStakeForLock` means the coldkey's total alpha
across the subnet cannot cover the lock amount being requested
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#notenoughstaketowithdraw),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: InsufficientStakeForLock](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#insufficientstakeforlock)).

- **InsufficientStakeForLock** — total subnet alpha is too low for the lock request.
- **NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw** — total staked alpha on the pair is too low for removal.

## Distinction from Amount Too Low

`InsufficientStakeForLock` and `AmountTooLow` both stop lock-stake calls, but for different reasons.
`AmountTooLow` means the submitted lock amount is below the minimum allowed increment or is zero.
`InsufficientStakeForLock` means the lock amount is positive but total staked alpha on the subnet is
still too low
([Subtensor Standard Errors: AmountTooLow](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#amounttoolow),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: InsufficientStakeForLock](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#insufficientstakeforlock)).

- **AmountTooLow** — the submitted lock amount is zero or below the minimum increment.
- **InsufficientStakeForLock** — total subnet alpha is below the lock request.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the total-alpha condition that raises it. It
does not report a wallet's live locked or total alpha on any subnet; those values depend on chain
state at observation time
([Conviction and Locked Stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).
