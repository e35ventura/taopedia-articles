# Insufficient Stake For Lock

`InsufficientStakeForLock` means a lock-stake call named more alpha than the coldkey currently holds
in total on the subnet. In `do_lock_stake`, the runtime reads `total_coldkey_alpha_on_subnet` and
returns this error when that total is below the lock mass being created or topped up
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

## What It Means

Conviction locks constrain how far a coldkey's subnet alpha can fall while a lock is active
([Conviction and locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).

`do_lock_stake` creates a new lock or tops up an existing one on a specific hotkey for a subnet.
Before writing lock state, the function compares the coldkey's total alpha on that subnet against
the requested lock mass. `InsufficientStakeForLock` means that total is too low to cover the lock
amount
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

For a new lock, the check compares total alpha against the submitted amount. For a top-up, it
compares total alpha against the new combined `locked_mass` after the increase
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a coldkey calls `do_lock_stake` with an amount larger than its total staked
alpha on the subnet can support. That can happen when the lock request exceeds current holdings on
that netuid or when a top-up pushes the combined lock mass above the coldkey's total alpha there
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs),
[Conviction and locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).

When debugging, rule out earlier gates on the same extrinsic. `AmountTooLow` means the submitted
lock amount is zero. `HotKeyAccountNotExists` means the named hotkey account is absent.
`LockHotkeyMismatch` means an existing lock on the subnet is bound to a different hotkey
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

The practical meaning is narrow: total subnet alpha is below the lock mass being requested.

## Distinction from Stake Unavailable

`InsufficientStakeForLock` and `StakeUnavailable` both involve conviction locks, but on opposite
directions. `InsufficientStakeForLock` is raised in `do_lock_stake` when total alpha on the subnet
is too low to create or increase a lock. `StakeUnavailable` is raised on unstake-related paths when
the requested amount exceeds unlocked alpha after existing locks are subtracted
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

- **InsufficientStakeForLock** — total alpha on the subnet is below the lock request.
- **StakeUnavailable** — the request exceeds unlocked alpha on the subnet.

## Distinction from Not Enough Stake To Withdraw

`InsufficientStakeForLock` and `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` both compare stake amounts, but on
different extrinsics and scopes. `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` means a hotkey-coldkey pair lacks enough
total staked alpha for a removal request. `InsufficientStakeForLock` means the coldkey's total alpha
across the subnet cannot cover the lock mass being written in `do_lock_stake`
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughstaketowithdraw),
[Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

- **InsufficientStakeForLock** — total subnet alpha is too low for the lock request.
- **NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw** — total staked alpha on the pair is too low for removal.

## Distinction from Lock Hotkey Mismatch

`InsufficientStakeForLock` and `LockHotkeyMismatch` are both raised in `do_lock_stake`, but at
different checks. `LockHotkeyMismatch` means the coldkey already has a lock on the subnet tied to a
different hotkey. `InsufficientStakeForLock` means the hotkey binding passed but total alpha on the
subnet is still below the requested lock mass
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

- **LockHotkeyMismatch** — the existing lock is bound to another hotkey.
- **InsufficientStakeForLock** — total alpha cannot cover the lock amount.

## Distinction from Amount Too Low

`InsufficientStakeForLock` and `AmountTooLow` both stop lock-stake calls, but for different reasons.
`AmountTooLow` is the zero-amount rejection at the start of `do_lock_stake`.
`InsufficientStakeForLock` is the later total-balance rejection when the submitted amount is
positive but exceeds total subnet alpha
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: AmountTooLow](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#amounttoolow)).

- **AmountTooLow** — the submitted lock amount is zero.
- **InsufficientStakeForLock** — total subnet alpha is below the lock request.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the total-alpha check in `do_lock_stake`. It
does not report a wallet's live locked or total alpha on any subnet; those values depend on chain
state at observation time
([Conviction and locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).
