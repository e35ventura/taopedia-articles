# Insufficient Stake For Lock

`InsufficientStakeForLock` means a conviction lock request named more alpha than the coldkey holds
in total on that subnet. The runtime compares the coldkey's total staked alpha on the netuid against
the lock amount being created or increased, and returns this error when the total is too low
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs),
[Conviction and locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).

## What It Means

Conviction locks bind part of a coldkey's subnet alpha to a commitment signal
([Conviction and locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).

A lock-stake call either opens a new lock on a hotkey or adds alpha to an existing lock on that
subnet. Before the lock is written, the runtime checks that the coldkey's total staked alpha on the
netuid can cover the requested lock mass. `InsufficientStakeForLock` means that total is below the
amount being locked
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

For a first lock, the check compares total alpha against the submitted amount. For a top-up, it
compares total alpha against the combined lock amount after the increase
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a coldkey tries to lock more alpha on a subnet than it currently holds there
in total. That can happen when the lock request exceeds current holdings or when a top-up would push
the combined lock above the coldkey's total staked alpha on the netuid
([Conviction and locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking),
[Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

When debugging, compare against other lock-stake rejections on the same call. `AmountTooLow` means
the submitted lock amount is zero. `HotKeyAccountNotExists` means the named hotkey account is
absent. `LockHotkeyMismatch` means an existing lock on the subnet is already bound to a different
hotkey
([Subtensor Standard Errors: AmountTooLow](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#amounttoolow),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyAccountNotExists](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeyaccountnotexists),
[Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

The practical meaning is narrow: total staked alpha on the subnet is below the lock request.

## Distinction from Stake Unavailable

`InsufficientStakeForLock` and `StakeUnavailable` both involve conviction locks, but on opposite
directions. `InsufficientStakeForLock` blocks creating or increasing a lock when total alpha on the
subnet is too low. `StakeUnavailable` blocks unstake, move-stake, and recycle paths when the request
exceeds unlocked alpha after existing locks are excluded
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

- **InsufficientStakeForLock** — total alpha on the subnet is below the lock request.
- **StakeUnavailable** — the request exceeds unlocked alpha on the subnet.

## Distinction from Not Enough Stake To Withdraw

`InsufficientStakeForLock` and `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` both compare stake amounts, but on
different operations. `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` means a hotkey-coldkey pair lacks enough total
staked alpha for a removal request. `InsufficientStakeForLock` means the coldkey's total alpha
across the subnet cannot cover the lock mass being requested on the lock-stake path
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughstaketowithdraw),
[Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

- **InsufficientStakeForLock** — total subnet alpha is too low for the lock request.
- **NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw** — total staked alpha on the pair is too low for removal.

## Distinction from Account Rejects Locked Alpha

`InsufficientStakeForLock` and `AccountRejectsLockedAlpha` both concern conviction locks, but on
different operations. `InsufficientStakeForLock` is raised when a coldkey tries to lock more alpha
than it holds on the subnet. `AccountRejectsLockedAlpha` is raised when locked alpha would be sent
to a destination coldkey that has not opted in to receive it
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

- **InsufficientStakeForLock** — total alpha is too low for the lock request.
- **AccountRejectsLockedAlpha** — the destination rejects incoming locked alpha.

## Distinction from Amount Too Low

`InsufficientStakeForLock` and `AmountTooLow` both stop lock-stake calls, but for different reasons.
`AmountTooLow` is the zero-amount rejection at the start of the path. `InsufficientStakeForLock` is
the later rejection when the amount is positive but total staked alpha on the subnet is still too
low
([Subtensor Standard Errors: AmountTooLow](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#amounttoolow),
[Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

- **AmountTooLow** — the submitted lock amount is zero.
- **InsufficientStakeForLock** — total subnet alpha is below the lock request.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the total-alpha check on the lock-stake path.
It does not report a wallet's live locked or total alpha on any subnet; those values depend on chain
state at observation time
([Conviction and locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).
