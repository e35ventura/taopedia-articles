# Alpha High Too Low

`AlphaHighTooLow` means a `sudo_set_alpha_values` call submitted an alpha high bound that is below
the chain's minimum allowed value. The standard-errors reference describes it as "Alpha high value
is too low"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#alphahightoolow)).

The check applies only after liquid alpha is enabled on the target subnet and authorization has
passed
([Subnet Hyperparameters: LiquidAlphaEnabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#liquidalphaenabled)).

## What It Means

The `sudo_set_alpha_values` extrinsic writes the `(alpha_low, alpha_high)` pair that bounds liquid
alpha bond smoothing. In `do_set_alpha_values`, the runtime computes a minimum alpha high floor from
`u16::MAX / 40` and returns `AlphaHighTooLow` when the submitted `alpha_high` is strictly below that
floor
([Subtensor source: run_epoch.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/epoch/run_epoch.rs),
[Subnet Hyperparameters: alpha_high](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#alphahigh)).

Alpha high is the upper bound of the liquid alpha range that caps how quickly validator-miner bonds
can smooth when alignment is strong
([Consensus-based Weights](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/consensus-based-weights)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a caller submits `sudo_set_alpha_values` with an `alpha_high` value below
the computed minimum. Mis-scaled units, copied extrinsics with outdated bounds, or automation that
does not read the current floor can hit this error even when liquid alpha is already enabled on the
subnet
([Subtensor source: run_epoch.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/epoch/run_epoch.rs)).

When debugging, rule out earlier gates on the same extrinsic first. `LiquidAlphaDisabled` means
liquid alpha is not enabled on the subnet. `AlphaLowOutOfRange` means the submitted `alpha_low`
value is below the minimum floor or above the submitted `alpha_high` bound
([Subtensor Standard Errors: LiquidAlphaDisabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#liquidalphadisabled),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: AlphaLowOutOfRange](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#alphalowoutofrange)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the submitted alpha high u16 is below the configured minimum.

## Distinction from Alpha Low Out Of Range

`AlphaHighTooLow` and `AlphaLowOutOfRange` are both range checks in `do_set_alpha_values`, but they
guard different submitted values. `AlphaHighTooLow` compares only `alpha_high` against the minimum
floor. `AlphaLowOutOfRange` compares `alpha_low` against the same floor and also requires
`alpha_low` to stay at or below the submitted `alpha_high`
([Subtensor source: run_epoch.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/epoch/run_epoch.rs)).

- **AlphaHighTooLow** — the submitted alpha high is below the minimum floor.
- **AlphaLowOutOfRange** — alpha low is below the floor or above alpha high.

## Distinction from Liquid Alpha Disabled

`AlphaHighTooLow` and `LiquidAlphaDisabled` can both appear on `sudo_set_alpha_values`, but at
different stages. `LiquidAlphaDisabled` is the feature toggle rejection when `LiquidAlphaEnabled` is
false. `AlphaHighTooLow` is the later value-floor rejection after the toggle check has already
passed
([Subtensor Standard Errors: LiquidAlphaDisabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#liquidalphadisabled),
[Subtensor source: run_epoch.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/epoch/run_epoch.rs)).

- **LiquidAlphaDisabled** — liquid alpha is not enabled on the subnet.
- **AlphaHighTooLow** — the submitted alpha high is below the minimum floor.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the alpha high floor check in
`do_set_alpha_values`. It does not describe how liquid alpha varies bonds during an epoch, how to
read live alpha bounds for a subnet, or how bonds moving average behaves when liquid alpha is off;
those are documented separately
([Subnet Hyperparameters: alpha_high](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#alphahigh),
[Yuma Consensus: Bonding mechanics](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus#bonding)).
