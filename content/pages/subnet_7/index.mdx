# Subnet 7: Allways

Allways is Bittensor Subnet 7 (SN7), operated by [entrius](https://github.com/entrius/allways). The
protocol enables trustless native asset swaps across independent blockchains without wrapping or
custodial bridging. Swaps settle on-chain natively on each asset's home chain; the subnet provides
the verification and slashing layer that makes permissionless market-making safe for users. The
protocol launched with BTC ↔ TAO and is designed to extend to any verifiable asset pair.

References: [entrius/allways (GitHub)](https://github.com/entrius/allways),
[all-ways.io](https://all-ways.io/)

## How the Mechanism Works

The Allways mechanism centers on three primitives: collateral, fulfillment, and independent
verification.

**Collateral.** Miners lock capital into a smart contract before quoting rates. This collateral
backs their commitments and is at risk of slashing if they quote rates they cannot or do not honor.
The smart contract manages the full swap lifecycle — from a user posting an intent through matching,
fulfillment, and final settlement.

**Fulfillment.** When a user submits a swap order matching a miner's quoted rate, the miner is
responsible for executing native on-chain transactions on both sides of the pair — for example,
receiving BTC on the Bitcoin chain and releasing TAO on Bittensor, or the reverse. The miner then
reports the outcome to the subnet.

**Independent verification.** Validators do not rely on the miner's report. Each validator
independently monitors the blockchains involved, verifies the on-chain state for both sides of the
claimed transaction, and votes on whether the outcome is valid. Consensus across validators
determines whether a fulfillment is accepted. Accepted fulfillments release miner collateral;
rejected fulfillments trigger slashing. This structure removes the need for any trusted intermediary
between the two chains.

Validator scores feed into [[Yuma Consensus]], which translates consensus-weighted results into
per-block TAO emissions.

Reference: [entrius/allways (GitHub)](https://github.com/entrius/allways#architecture)

## Miners

Miners on Allways are market makers. Their economic role is to quote competitive exchange rates,
back those quotes with on-chain collateral, and reliably execute the native transactions that
fulfill matched orders. A miner who quotes rates they cannot fulfill, or who executes a swap
incorrectly, is slashed by the contract. A miner who fulfills accurately and at competitive rates
earns subnet emissions proportional to their validated output.

The collateral mechanism creates a natural selection pressure: only miners with reliable
infrastructure, real asset liquidity, and accurate pricing can participate sustainably. Undercutting
on rate while failing on execution is directly penalized.

Reference: [entrius/allways (GitHub)](https://github.com/entrius/allways)

## Validators

Validators are the verification layer. Their job is not to execute swaps but to independently
confirm that miners did. For each claimed fulfillment, validators check the on-chain state of both
blockchains involved and submit a binary vote on validity. The aggregate vote across validators
determines whether a fulfillment is slashed or settled.

Because validators operate independently and each verifies directly from chain data, the protocol's
security relies on validator diversity and honest reporting. Validators that consistently vote with
the accurate outcome receive dividends through [[Yuma Consensus]].

Reference: [entrius/allways (GitHub)](https://github.com/entrius/allways)
