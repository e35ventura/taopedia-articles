# Coldkey Swap Announcement Not Found

`ColdkeySwapAnnouncementNotFound` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised by
`swap_coldkey_announced`, the extrinsic that finalizes a previously announced
[coldkey swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/coldkey-swap). The standard-errors reference
describes it as "Coldkey swap announcement not found"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#coldkeyswapannouncementnotfound)).

## What It Means

`swap_coldkey_announced` looks up a pending announcement record for the calling coldkey before doing
anything else. `ColdkeySwapAnnouncementNotFound` is the rejection when that lookup finds nothing:
the coldkey has no announcement on record to finalize
([Subtensor source: dispatches.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/macros/dispatches.rs),
[Coldkey Swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/coldkey-swap)).

This is the first of three checks the extrinsic runs in sequence, ahead of the checks that compare
the supplied new coldkey against the announced hash and confirm the pending window has elapsed. A
caller cannot reach either of those later checks without an announcement record to read in the first
place
([Subtensor source: dispatches.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/macros/dispatches.rs)).

## Why It Appears

The most direct cause is calling `swap_coldkey_announced` for a coldkey that never called
`announce_coldkey_swap`. It can also happen for a coldkey whose announcement already finalized
successfully, since a completed swap consumes the announcement record rather than leaving it in
place for a repeat finalize call ([Coldkey Swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/coldkey-swap),
[Subtensor source: dispatches.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/macros/dispatches.rs)).

## Distinction from Coldkey Swap Too Early

`ColdkeySwapAnnouncementNotFound` and `ColdkeySwapTooEarly` are both raised by
`swap_coldkey_announced`, but they sit at opposite ends of the same check sequence.
`ColdkeySwapAnnouncementNotFound` means there is no announcement record to work with at all.
`ColdkeySwapTooEarly` is the later, separate rejection for a call that does have a valid
announcement, but arrives before that announcement's pending window has elapsed
([Coldkey Swap Too Early](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#coldkeyswaptooearly),
[Subtensor source: dispatches.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/macros/dispatches.rs)).

- **ColdkeySwapAnnouncementNotFound** — no announcement record exists for the caller.
- **ColdkeySwapTooEarly** — an announcement exists, but its pending window hasn't elapsed.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the lookup that raises it. It does not describe
how an announcement is created or disputed; those are documented separately
([Coldkey Swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/coldkey-swap),
[Subtensor source: dispatches.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/macros/dispatches.rs)).
