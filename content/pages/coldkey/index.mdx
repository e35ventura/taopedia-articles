# Coldkey

A coldkey is the ownership root in Bittensor wallet design, protecting the TAO and stake held under
a wallet identity. The coldkey private key can be recovered using a seed phrase
([Glossary: Coldkey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#coldkey),
[Wallets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/wallets)).

Coldkey is ownership vocabulary. It is not an operational key for daily subnet participation. Those
daily interactions are handled by the hotkey, which is controlled by the coldkey.

## Ownership Role

The coldkey holds ownership authority over the wallet's TAO and stake. In Bittensor wallet design,
the coldkey is the root from which hotkeys are controlled, and it represents the ultimate ownership
position in the wallet hierarchy ([Wallets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/wallets),
[Working with Keys](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/working-with-keys)).

This makes coldkey different from the hotkey. A hotkey is used for operational subnet participation;
the coldkey is kept secure as the ownership anchor for the wallet.

## Seed Phrase Recovery

A coldkey private key can be restored using a seed phrase. The seed phrase is the human-readable
word sequence that recovers the coldkey's key material when access is lost
([Glossary: Seed Phrase](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#seed-phrase),
[Wallets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/wallets)).

This recovery path is why the coldkey and seed phrase are treated as sensitive material. Losing
access to the seed phrase means losing the ability to restore the coldkey.

## Hotkey Relationship

Coldkeys and hotkeys are the two key types in Bittensor wallet design. The coldkey is the ownership
root; the hotkey is the operational key used for active subnet tasks
([Working with Keys](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/working-with-keys)).

Keeping them separate is a security principle. High-value ownership material stays in the coldkey,
while the hotkey handles the frequent actions needed for network participation.

## Reader Boundary

Coldkey is concept vocabulary for the ownership root in Bittensor wallet design. It should not be
read as an operational key, a network address, or a balance display
([Glossary: Coldkey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#coldkey)).

The coldkey and its seed phrase are security-sensitive material. Their role is ownership protection,
not daily interaction. For operational key vocabulary, hotkey is the correct term
([Wallets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/wallets)).
