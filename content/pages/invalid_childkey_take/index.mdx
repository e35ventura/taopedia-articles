# Invalid Childkey Take

`InvalidChildkeyTake` means a `set_childkey_take` call submitted a take value outside the allowed
range for that subnet. The standard-errors reference describes it as "Childkey take is invalid"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#invalidchildkeytake)).

[Childkey take](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys) is the share of validation
dividends a child hotkey keeps when validating on behalf of a parent on one subnet. Official
documentation describes it as subnet-specific and bounded within a documented 0% to 18% range
([Child Hotkeys](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys)).

## What It Means

`set_childkey_take` lets the owning coldkey set that rate for a hotkey on a selected subnet.
`InvalidChildkeyTake` means the submitted take value does not fit the permitted bounds, so the
update is rejected
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#invalidchildkeytake),
[Child Hotkeys](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys)).

This is a range-bound rejection on the submitted value. It applies whether the caller is trying to
raise or lower take relative to the current setting.

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a `set_childkey_take` call submits a take outside the permitted bounds for
the selected subnet. Mis-scaled percentages, copied extrinsics, or values above the documented 18%
cap can hit this error.

When debugging, compare range and timing failures on childkey-take updates.
`TxChildkeyTakeRateLimitExceeded` means a take increase was retried before the childkey-take
cooldown cleared. That is a separate timing rejection on the increase path, not an out-of-range
value
([Subtensor Standard Errors: TxChildkeyTakeRateLimitExceeded](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#txchildkeytakeratelimitexceeded),
[Chain Rate Limits: Child key take rate limit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits#child-key-take-rate-limit)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the submitted take does not fit the allowed range for that subnet.

## Distinction from Tx Childkey Take Rate Limit Exceeded

`InvalidChildkeyTake` and `TxChildkeyTakeRateLimitExceeded` both appear on childkey-take updates,
but they reject different problems. `InvalidChildkeyTake` means the submitted take is outside the
allowed range. `TxChildkeyTakeRateLimitExceeded` means a take increase was attempted before the
childkey-take cooldown expired
([Subtensor Standard Errors: TxChildkeyTakeRateLimitExceeded](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#txchildkeytakeratelimitexceeded),
[Chain Rate Limits: Child key take rate limit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits#child-key-take-rate-limit)).

- **InvalidChildkeyTake** — the submitted take is outside the allowed range.
- **TxChildkeyTakeRateLimitExceeded** — a take increase was attempted before the cooldown expired.

## Distinction from Validator Take Errors

Childkey take and validator take share a similar percentage shape, but they belong to different
extrinsics and error families. `InvalidChildkeyTake` applies only to childkey-take updates.
Delegate-take bound errors such as `DelegateTakeTooHigh` and `DelegateTakeTooLow` apply to validator
commission changes instead
([Child Hotkeys](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: DelegateTakeTooHigh](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#delegatetaketoohigh),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: DelegateTakeTooLow](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#delegatetaketoolow)).

- **InvalidChildkeyTake** — childkey-take value is outside the allowed range.
- **DelegateTakeTooHigh / DelegateTakeTooLow** — validator commission change fails a delegate-take
  bound.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the out-of-range condition that raises it. It
does not describe how to assign child hotkeys through `set_children`, minimum parent stake
requirements, or the live child list on a subnet; those are separate topics
([Child Hotkeys](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys),
[Chain Rate Limits: Child hotkey operations rate limit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits#child-hotkey-operations-rate-limit)).
