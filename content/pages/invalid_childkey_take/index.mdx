# Invalid Childkey Take

`InvalidChildkeyTake` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised by childkey-take update calls.
The standard-errors reference describes it as "Childkey take is invalid"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#invalidchildkeytake)).

## What It Means

[Childkey take](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys) is the share of validation
dividends a child hotkey keeps when validating on behalf of a parent on one subnet.
`do_set_childkey_take` lets the owning coldkey set that rate for a hotkey on a selected subnet.
`InvalidChildkeyTake` means the submitted take value is outside the allowed range, so the update is
rejected before any increase cooldown check runs
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#invalidchildkeytake),
[Child Hotkeys](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys),
[Subtensor source: set_children.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/set_children.rs)).

In `set_children.rs`, `do_set_childkey_take` compares the submitted take against
`get_effective_min_childkey_take(netuid)` and `get_max_childkey_take()`, returning
`InvalidChildkeyTake` when the value is below that minimum or above the maximum
([Subtensor source: set_children.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/set_children.rs)).

Official child-hotkey documentation describes childkey take as subnet-specific and bounded within a
documented 0% to 18% range
([Child Hotkeys](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a `set_childkey_take` call submits a take outside the permitted bounds for
the selected subnet. A value above the maximum or below the effective minimum fails here regardless
of whether the caller is trying to raise or lower take relative to the current setting
([Child Hotkeys](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys),
[Subtensor source: set_children.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/set_children.rs)).

For a reader, the practical meaning is narrow: the submitted take does not fit the allowed range for
that subnet. The problem is not that an increase was attempted too soon; that is a separate cooldown
rejection on the increase path
([Subtensor Standard Errors: TxChildkeyTakeRateLimitExceeded](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#txchildkeytakeratelimitexceeded),
[Chain Rate Limits: Child key take rate limit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits#child-key-take-rate-limit)).

## Distinction from Tx Childkey Take Rate Limit Exceeded

`InvalidChildkeyTake` and `TxChildkeyTakeRateLimitExceeded` both appear on childkey-take updates,
but they reject different problems. `InvalidChildkeyTake` is the range check on the submitted value.
`TxChildkeyTakeRateLimitExceeded` is the separate cooldown rejection when a take increase is retried
before the childkey-take rate limit has elapsed
([Subtensor Standard Errors: TxChildkeyTakeRateLimitExceeded](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#txchildkeytakeratelimitexceeded),
[Chain Rate Limits: Child key take rate limit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits#child-key-take-rate-limit),
[Subtensor source: set_children.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/set_children.rs)).

The bound check runs first. The cooldown check applies only when the new take is higher than the
stored take
([Subtensor source: set_children.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/set_children.rs)).

- **InvalidChildkeyTake** — the submitted take is outside the allowed range.
- **TxChildkeyTakeRateLimitExceeded** — a take increase was attempted before the cooldown expired.

## Distinction from Validator Take Errors

Childkey take and validator take share a similar percentage range, but they belong to different
extrinsics and error families. `InvalidChildkeyTake` applies only to childkey-take updates through
`do_set_childkey_take`. Delegate-take bound errors such as `DelegateTakeTooHigh` and
`DelegateTakeTooLow` apply to validator commission changes instead
([Childkey Take](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys),
[Delegate Take Too High](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#delegatetakehigh),
[Delegate Take Too Low](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#delegatetoolow)).

- **InvalidChildkeyTake** — childkey-take value is outside the allowed range.
- **DelegateTakeTooHigh / DelegateTakeTooLow** — validator commission change fails a delegate-take
  bound.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the range check that raises it. It does not
describe how to assign child hotkeys through `set_children`, minimum parent stake requirements, or
the live child list on a subnet; those are documented separately
([Child Hotkeys](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys),
[Chain Rate Limits: Child hotkey operations rate limit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits#child-hotkey-operations-rate-limit)).
