# Trust

Trust is a Bittensor consensus metric for the miner side of evaluation. It describes how much miner
support remains after validator weights are clipped by consensus
([Glossary: Trust](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#trust),
[Yuma Consensus](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus)).

The term belongs to Yuma Consensus vocabulary because it is read after validator weight signals have
been filtered and aggregated. It is not a standalone miner reputation label
([Glossary: Trust](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#trust)).

## What It Represents

Trust answers how much validator support a miner retained after consensus clipping removed outlier
weight signals. Raw validator weight totals count support before filtering; trust reflects the
miner-side support consensus considers reliable after comparing validator signals across the set
([Yuma Consensus](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus)).

On netuid 1, trust examples illustrate one subnet's clipping outcome. They should not be read as
representing production behavior on another network
([Bittensor Networks](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/bittensor-networks)).

## Pre-Rank Before Clipping

Trust compares two miner-side standings inside the same Yuma pass. **Pre-rank** is the
stake-weighted sum of validator weights for a miner before consensus clipping removes outlier
values. Documentation describes it as summing each validator's submitted weight scaled by that
validator's stake ([Glossary: Trust](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#trust),
[Yuma Consensus: Miner emissions](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus#miner-emissions)).

**Final rank** is the miner-side standing after clipping and aggregation. Rank vocabulary names that
post-filter result; pre-rank names the support level that existed before the consensus benchmark
trimmed excess weight ([Glossary: Rank](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#rank),
[Yuma Consensus: Clipping](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus#clipping)).

## Trust as a Clipping Survival Ratio

For each miner `j`, trust is the ratio of final rank to pre-rank: `T_j = R_j / P_j`. The glossary
describes this as the proportion of original validator support that survived consensus filtering
([Glossary: Trust](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#trust)).

A value near `1.0` means clipping barely changed the miner's standing relative to pre-rank support.
Lower values mean more of that pre-rank support was removed when weights were compared against the
[consensus score](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#consensus-score) benchmark. A
value of `0.0` means no consensus-aligned support remained after clipping
([Glossary: Trust](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#trust)).

Trust therefore reports clipping impact on one miner, not total emission share. Miner
[incentives](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#incentives) are credited later from
normalized rank inside the same pass
([Yuma Consensus: Miner emissions](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus#miner-emissions)).

## Miner Trust Versus Validator Trust

Trust and [validator trust](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-trust) are
read from the same clipping pass but on opposite sides of evaluation. Trust is a per-miner ratio
showing how much pre-rank support survived filtering. Validator trust sums each validator's clipped
weights across miners, measuring how much influence that validator contributed after consensus
([Glossary: Validator Trust](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-trust),
[Glossary: Trust](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#trust)).

A miner can read low trust when validators disagreed sharply about its work, while a validator can
still read high validator trust when its clipped contributions sum large across miners—even when
individual miners show low survival ratios. The two metrics answer different reader questions on
opposite sides of the same Yuma settlement
([Yuma Consensus](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus)).

## Distinction from Rank

Rank is the miner-side aggregate standing after Yuma Consensus filters and combines validator
weights. Trust is the ratio of final rank to pre-rank that shows how much support survived clipping
([Glossary: Rank](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#rank),
[Glossary: Trust](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#trust),
[Yuma Consensus](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus)).

- **Rank** — miner-side standing after aggregation.
- **Trust** — ratio showing how much pre-rank support survived filtering.

## Distinction from Consensus Score

Consensus score is the per-miner clipping benchmark used to cap outlier validator weights. Trust is
the miner-side ratio of final rank to pre-rank that shows how much support survived after that
benchmark was applied on the selected subnet
([Glossary: Consensus Score](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#consensus-score),
[Glossary: Trust](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#trust),
[Yuma Consensus: Clipping](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus#clipping)).

- **Consensus score** — per-miner agreement benchmark used for clipping.
- **Trust** — miner-side clipping survival ratio after that benchmark applies.

## Distinction from Validator Trust

Validator trust measures validator-side clipped influence across neurons. Trust measures miner-side
support preservation between pre-rank and final rank
([Glossary: Validator Trust](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-trust),
[Glossary: Trust](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#trust)).

- **Validator trust** — validator-side clipped influence measure.
- **Trust** — miner-side support preservation through clipping.

## Distinction from Weight Vector

A weight vector is one validator's submitted evaluation signals. Trust is interpreted after the full
validator set is filtered and aggregated into miner-side outcomes
([Glossary: Weight Vector](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#weight-vector),
[Glossary: Trust](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#trust)).

- **Weight vector** — one validator's input signal before aggregation.
- **Trust** — miner-side clipping impact after those signals are processed.

## Distinction from Weight Matrix

A weight matrix stacks validator weight vectors into the grouped input Yuma Consensus processes.
Trust is read after that grouped input is clipped and aggregated into miner-side rank outcomes on
the selected subnet
([Glossary: Weight Matrix](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#weight-matrix),
[Glossary: Trust](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#trust),
[Yuma Consensus](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus)).

- **Weight matrix** — assembled validator input entering consensus.
- **Trust** — how much miner-side support survived clipping after processing.

## Distinction from Incentives

Incentives name miner-side emission outcomes credited after rank is normalized. Trust names the
pre-rank to final-rank ratio that shows clipping impact inside the same pass
([Glossary: Incentives](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#incentives),
[Glossary: Trust](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#trust),
[Yuma Consensus: Miner emissions](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus#miner-emissions)).

- **Trust** — support preservation through clipping in the same pass.
- **Incentives** — credited miner emission outcomes after normalization.

## Distinction from Weight Copying

Weight copying concerns whether a validator derived its submission from independent miner evaluation
or from another validator's visible scores. Trust is a miner-side ratio read after Yuma Consensus
clips and aggregates validator weights into rank
([Weight Copying Problem](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/weight-copying-in-bittensor),
[Glossary: Trust](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#trust),
[Yuma Consensus](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus)).

- **Weight copying** — reuse of another validator's published scores.
- **Trust** — miner-side measure of how much pre-rank support survived clipping.

## Distinction from Yuma Consensus

Trust is the miner-side clipping-survival ratio `T_j = R_j / P_j`. Yuma Consensus is the full
on-chain pass that clips the weight matrix, computes rank and trust, and credits emission shares at
the epoch boundary ([Glossary: Trust](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#trust),
[Yuma Consensus](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus)).

- **Trust** — miner-side support preserved through Yuma clipping.
- **Yuma Consensus** — tempo step that turns submitted weights into emission shares.

## Reader Boundary

Trust is a miner-side consensus metric after clipping. It is not rank itself, validator trust, or a
reward guarantee ([Glossary: Trust](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#trust),
[Yuma Consensus](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus)).
