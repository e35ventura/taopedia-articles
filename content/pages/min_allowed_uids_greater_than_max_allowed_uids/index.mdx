# Min Allowed UIDs Greater Than Max Allowed UIDs

`MinAllowedUidsGreaterThanMaxAllowedUids` is an admin-utils pallet error raised by
`sudo_set_min_allowed_uids`. The pallet defines it as "The minimum allowed UIDs must be less than
the maximum allowed UIDs"
([Subtensor source: admin-utils/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/admin-utils/src/lib.rs)).

## What It Means

Each subnet stores both a minimum and maximum allowed UID count. The `sudo_set_min_allowed_uids`
extrinsic compares the submitted min-UID value against the subnet's stored `get_max_allowed_uids`
value and returns `MinAllowedUidsGreaterThanMaxAllowedUids` when the proposed minimum is not below
that maximum
([Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters),
[Subtensor source: admin-utils/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/admin-utils/src/lib.rs)).

The check requires the submitted minimum to be strictly less than the stored maximum for that
netuid. A proposed min-UID value at or above that maximum is rejected before the new minimum is
written
([Subtensor source: admin-utils/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/admin-utils/src/lib.rs)).

This is a min-max consistency rejection on the min-UID setter, not proof that the submitted netuid
has no subnet record or that the proposed minimum is not below the current filled UID count.

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a root-signed `sudo_set_min_allowed_uids` call names a min-UID value at or
above the subnet's stored maximum allowed UIDs. That can happen when a client raises the minimum
floor before checking the current maximum for the same netuid, or when the two bounds are edited in
the wrong order
([Subtensor source: admin-utils/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/admin-utils/src/lib.rs)).

When debugging, compare against other rejections on the same call. `SubnetDoesNotExist` means the
submitted netuid has no subnet record. `MinAllowedUidsGreaterThanCurrentUids` means the proposed
minimum is not below the current filled UID count. `MaxAllowedUidsLessThanMinAllowedUids` is the
separate rejection on `sudo_set_max_allowed_uids` when the proposed maximum is below the stored
minimum
([Subnet Does Not Exist](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#subnetdoesnotexist),
[Min Allowed UIDs Greater Than Current UIDs](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#minalloweduidsgreaterthancurrentuids),
[Max Allowed UIDs Less Than Min Allowed UIDs](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#maxalloweduidslessthanminalloweduids),
[Subtensor source: admin-utils/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/admin-utils/src/lib.rs)).

The practical meaning is narrow: lower the proposed min-UID value below the stored maximum before
retrying `sudo_set_min_allowed_uids`.

## Distinction from Subnet Does Not Exist

`MinAllowedUidsGreaterThanMaxAllowedUids` and `SubnetDoesNotExist` both appear on
`sudo_set_min_allowed_uids`, but at different checks. `SubnetDoesNotExist` means the submitted
netuid has no subnet record. `MinAllowedUidsGreaterThanMaxAllowedUids` means the netuid exists but
the proposed minimum is not below the stored maximum allowed UIDs
([Subnet Does Not Exist](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#subnetdoesnotexist),
[Subtensor source: admin-utils/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/admin-utils/src/lib.rs)).

- **MinAllowedUidsGreaterThanMaxAllowedUids** — proposed min UIDs are at or above the stored
  maximum.
- **SubnetDoesNotExist** — the submitted netuid has no subnet record.

## Distinction from Min Allowed UIDs Greater Than Current UIDs

`MinAllowedUidsGreaterThanMaxAllowedUids` and `MinAllowedUidsGreaterThanCurrentUids` both appear on
`sudo_set_min_allowed_uids`, but at different checks. `MinAllowedUidsGreaterThanMaxAllowedUids`
means the proposed minimum is not below the stored maximum allowed UIDs.
`MinAllowedUidsGreaterThanCurrentUids` means the proposed minimum is not below the current filled
UID count
([Min Allowed UIDs Greater Than Current UIDs](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#minalloweduidsgreaterthancurrentuids),
[Subtensor source: admin-utils/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/admin-utils/src/lib.rs)).

- **MinAllowedUidsGreaterThanMaxAllowedUids** — proposed min UIDs are at or above the stored
  maximum.
- **MinAllowedUidsGreaterThanCurrentUids** — proposed min UIDs are at or above the current filled
  UID count.

## Distinction from Max Allowed UIDs Less Than Min Allowed UIDs

`MinAllowedUidsGreaterThanMaxAllowedUids` and `MaxAllowedUidsLessThanMinAllowedUids` both protect
consistency between min and max UID bounds, but on opposite setters.
`MinAllowedUidsGreaterThanMaxAllowedUids` is raised by `sudo_set_min_allowed_uids` when the proposed
minimum is not below the stored maximum. `MaxAllowedUidsLessThanMinAllowedUids` is raised by
`sudo_set_max_allowed_uids` when the proposed maximum is below the stored minimum
([Max Allowed UIDs Less Than Min Allowed UIDs](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#maxalloweduidslessthanminalloweduids),
[Subtensor source: admin-utils/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/admin-utils/src/lib.rs)).

- **MinAllowedUidsGreaterThanMaxAllowedUids** — proposed min UIDs are at or above the stored
  maximum.
- **MaxAllowedUidsLessThanMinAllowedUids** — proposed max UIDs are below the stored minimum.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the min-versus-max comparison on
`sudo_set_min_allowed_uids`. It does not report live min or max allowed-UID values for any netuid on
any network; those are chain state readers can query separately
([Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters),
[Bittensor Networks](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/bittensor-networks)).
