# Not Enough Balance To Stake

`NotEnoughBalanceToStake` is a documented Subtensor standard error that means an add-stake request
asked to stake more than the coldkey account holds. The standard-errors reference describes it as
"Not enough balance in coldkey account," and the Subtensor source defines the variant as the caller
requesting adding more stake than there exists in the coldkey account
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Subtensor source: errors.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/macros/errors.rs)).

## What It Means

This error is about funds, not about ownership or timing. When an add-stake call arrives, the
runtime checks that the signing coldkey account can cover the amount being staked before it moves
anything into a staking position. The Subtensor source expresses this as a balance guard, raising
the error when the coldkey account cannot supply the requested stake amount
([Subtensor source: errors.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/macros/errors.rs),
[Staking and Delegation](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)).

So when this error appears, the immediate meaning is narrow: the coldkey that signed the request did
not have enough free balance in its account to fund the stake it asked to add. It does not, by
itself, say anything about whether the coldkey owns the targeted hotkey or whether the hotkey exists
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Glossary: Coldkey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#coldkey)).

## Why It Appears

Staking moves balance out of a coldkey account and into a staking position. Because the funds come
from the coldkey, the runtime requires the coldkey account to hold at least the amount being staked,
and it rejects the call when the account cannot cover it. The Subtensor source confirms this: the
variant is documented as the caller requesting adding more stake than there exists in the coldkey
account
([Subtensor source: errors.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/macros/errors.rs),
[Staking and Delegation](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)).

In practice this typically surfaces when a stake amount is set higher than the coldkey's spendable
balance, or when other costs have already drawn the account down so that the requested stake no
longer fits. The chain treats the coldkey balance as the source of the staked funds and refuses the
operation when that balance is short
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Glossary: Stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#stake)).

## What It Does Not Mean

This error should not be read as an ownership or existence failure. Bittensor documents separate
errors for those conditions, such as `NonAssociatedColdKey` when a coldkey acts on a hotkey it does
not own and `HotKeyAccountNotExists` when the targeted hotkey is not present on chain.
`NotEnoughBalanceToStake` is a funds rejection: it concerns the amount the coldkey can supply, not
who owns the hotkey or whether the hotkey exists
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#nonassociatedcoldkey),
[Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeyaccountnotexists)).

It also should not be read as a per-subnet condition. The check is on the coldkey account balance,
which is account-level state for the key, and the Subtensor source raises this error from that
balance guard rather than from any subnet-specific stored value, so the meaning does not depend on a
particular netuid
([Subtensor source: errors.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/macros/errors.rs),
[Glossary: Coldkey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#coldkey)).

## Distinction from Not Enough Stake To Withdraw

`NotEnoughBalanceToStake` and `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` sit on opposite sides of a staking
operation. The standard-errors reference describes `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` as not enough balance
in the coldkey account, while it describes `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` as not enough stake to
withdraw, and the Subtensor source defines the latter as the caller requesting removing more stake
than exists in the staking account
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Subtensor source: errors.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/macros/errors.rs)).

- **NotEnoughBalanceToStake** — too little coldkey balance to add the requested stake.
- **NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw** — too little existing stake to remove the requested amount.

## Distinction from Non Associated Cold Key

`NotEnoughBalanceToStake` is a funds check, while `NonAssociatedColdKey` is an ownership check. The
standard-errors reference describes `NonAssociatedColdKey` as a request made by a coldkey that is
not associated with the hotkey account, so it names a mismatch between the signer and the hotkey
rather than a shortfall of funds
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#nonassociatedcoldkey),
[Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake)).

- **NotEnoughBalanceToStake** — right coldkey, but insufficient coldkey balance.
- **NonAssociatedColdKey** — the signing coldkey does not own the targeted hotkey.

## Distinction from Hot Key Account Not Exists

`NotEnoughBalanceToStake` assumes a valid target and fails on funds, while `HotKeyAccountNotExists`
fails because the target is missing. The standard-errors reference describes
`HotKeyAccountNotExists` as the specified hotkey not existing, so it names an absent account rather
than a balance shortfall
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeyaccountnotexists),
[Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake)).

- **NotEnoughBalanceToStake** — the hotkey is fine, but the coldkey balance is short.
- **HotKeyAccountNotExists** — the hotkey is not present on chain at all.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label. It does not tell a reader how much balance a given
coldkey holds on a live network, or how large a stake it can fund; that depends on the current
on-chain balance of the coldkey account. The documented place to read the error itself is the
standard-errors reference, and the funds it draws on are part of the chain's account state for the
coldkey
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Glossary: Coldkey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#coldkey)).
