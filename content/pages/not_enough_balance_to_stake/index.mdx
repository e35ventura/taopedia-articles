# Not Enough Balance To Stake

`NotEnoughBalanceToStake` means the signing coldkey does not have enough free TAO to fund the amount
named in an add-stake call. The standard-errors reference describes it as "Not enough balance in
coldkey account"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake)).

Add-stake calls are signed by the coldkey and name a hotkey, subnet, and TAO amount to stake. This
error compares that requested TAO against the coldkey's spendable free balance, not against staked
alpha already held on the subnet.

## What It Means

After minimum-amount and slippage checks pass, the chain asks whether the coldkey can withdraw the
requested TAO from its free balance. `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` means that withdrawal test failed, so
the extrinsic stops before the subnet swap and stake counters update
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake)).

The check uses the TAO amount the caller requested, not the alpha that would be credited after the
swap.

## Why It Appears

This error fires when the coldkey's spendable TAO balance is below the amount named in the
extrinsic, such as when fees or prior transfers reduced free balance after the client built the
call, when the amount includes swap fees the wallet did not reserve, or when the call simply names
more TAO than the account holds.

When debugging, compare add-stake and remove-stake failures before assuming a hotkey or subnet
problem. `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` means the pair lacks enough staked alpha to remove on the
remove- stake path. `HotKeyAccountNotExists` and `SubtokenDisabled` are separate rejections on other
checks
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughstaketowithdraw),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyAccountNotExists](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeyaccountnotexists),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: SubtokenDisabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#subtokendisabled)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the coldkey cannot fund the requested stake from free balance.

## Distinction from Not Enough Stake To Withdraw

`NotEnoughBalanceToStake` and `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` both block staking extrinsics, but they
apply to opposite directions and different balances. `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` is checked on
add-stake calls against the coldkey's free TAO balance. `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` is checked on
remove-stake and move-stake paths against staked alpha for the hotkey-coldkey pair on the subnet
([Subtensor Standard Errors: Not Enough Stake To Withdraw](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughstaketowithdraw)).

- **NotEnoughBalanceToStake** — not enough free TAO to add stake.
- **NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw** — not enough staked alpha to remove.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the coldkey balance check that raises it. It
does not describe minimum stake thresholds, swap liquidity failures, or hotkey registration
requirements; those are separate checks in the same validation path.
