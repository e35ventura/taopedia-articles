# Not Enough Balance To Stake

`NotEnoughBalanceToStake` is a documented Subtensor standard error that means an add-stake request
asked to stake more than the signing coldkey account holds. The standard-errors reference describes
it as "Not enough balance in coldkey account," and the Subtensor source defines the variant as the
caller requesting adding more stake than there exists in the coldkey account
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Subtensor source: errors.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/macros/errors.rs)).

## What It Means

When an add-stake call arrives, the runtime checks that the signing coldkey account holds at least
the amount being staked before moving anything into a staking position, and raises this error when
it does not. The funds for a stake come from the coldkey account, so the rejection is specifically
about that account's free balance
([Subtensor source: errors.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/macros/errors.rs),
[Glossary: Coldkey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#coldkey)).

## Why It Appears

The error appears when a stake amount is larger than the coldkey's spendable balance, whether
because the amount was set too high or because other costs have already drawn the account down. The
chain treats the coldkey balance as the source of the staked funds and refuses the operation when
that balance is short
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Staking and Delegation](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)).

## What It Does Not Mean

This error reports only that the coldkey balance was insufficient. It does not establish anything
about the targeted hotkey: the balance guard runs before the runtime checks ownership or existence,
so this error can be raised without proving that the coldkey owns the hotkey or that the hotkey is
present on chain. It is also account-level rather than per-subnet, drawn from the coldkey balance
rather than any subnet-specific stored value
([Subtensor source: errors.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/macros/errors.rs),
[Glossary: Coldkey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#coldkey)).

## Distinction from Not Enough Stake To Withdraw

`NotEnoughBalanceToStake` and `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` sit on opposite sides of a staking
operation. The standard-errors reference describes the first as not enough balance in the coldkey
account, and the Subtensor source defines the second as the caller requesting removing more stake
than exists in the staking account
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Subtensor source: errors.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/macros/errors.rs)).

- **NotEnoughBalanceToStake** — too little coldkey balance to add the requested stake.
- **NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw** — too little existing stake to remove the requested amount.

## Distinction from Non Associated Cold Key

`NotEnoughBalanceToStake` is a funds check, while `NonAssociatedColdKey` is an ownership check. The
standard-errors reference describes `NonAssociatedColdKey` as a request made by a coldkey not
associated with the hotkey account, so it names a mismatch between the signer and the hotkey rather
than a shortfall of funds
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#nonassociatedcoldkey),
[Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake)).

- **NotEnoughBalanceToStake** — the signing coldkey does not hold enough balance for the stake.
- **NonAssociatedColdKey** — the signing coldkey is not associated with the targeted hotkey.

## Distinction from Hot Key Account Not Exists

`NotEnoughBalanceToStake` is a funds rejection, while `HotKeyAccountNotExists` is an absent-target
rejection. The standard-errors reference describes `HotKeyAccountNotExists` as the specified hotkey
not existing, naming a missing account rather than a balance shortfall
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeyaccountnotexists),
[Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake)).

- **NotEnoughBalanceToStake** — the call is rejected on coldkey balance, checked before the target
  hotkey.
- **HotKeyAccountNotExists** — the targeted hotkey is not present on chain at all.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label. It does not report how much balance a given
coldkey holds on a live network or how large a stake it can fund; that depends on the current
on-chain balance of the coldkey account, read through the standard-errors reference and the chain's
account state
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake),
[Glossary: Coldkey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#coldkey)).
