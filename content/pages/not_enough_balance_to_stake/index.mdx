# Not Enough Balance To Stake

`NotEnoughBalanceToStake` means the signing coldkey does not have enough free TAO to fund the amount
named in an add-stake call. The standard-errors reference describes it as "Not enough balance in
coldkey account"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#notenoughbalancetostake)).

Add-stake calls are signed by the coldkey and name a hotkey, subnet, and TAO amount to stake. This
error compares that requested TAO against the coldkey's spendable free balance, not against staked
alpha already held on the subnet
([Staking and Delegation Overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)).

## What It Means

`NotEnoughBalanceToStake` means the coldkey cannot cover the TAO amount named in the extrinsic from
its free balance
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#notenoughbalancetostake)).

For debugging, treat this as a funding failure on the coldkey account, not as proof that the hotkey
already lacks staked alpha on the subnet. Staked alpha on the remove-stake path is a separate
balance question
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#notenoughstaketowithdraw)).

## Why It Appears

This error fires when the coldkey's spendable TAO balance is below the amount named in the
extrinsic, such as when fees or prior transfers reduced free balance after the client built the
call, when the amount includes swap fees the wallet did not reserve, or when the call simply names
more TAO than the account holds.

When debugging, compare add-stake and remove-stake failures before assuming a hotkey or subnet
problem. `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` means the pair lacks enough staked alpha to remove on the
remove-stake path. `HotKeyAccountNotExists` and `SubtokenDisabled` are separate rejections on other
checks
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#notenoughstaketowithdraw),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyAccountNotExists](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#hotkeyaccountnotexists),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: SubtokenDisabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#subtokendisabled)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the coldkey cannot fund the requested stake from free balance.

## Distinction from Not Enough Stake To Withdraw

`NotEnoughBalanceToStake` and `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` both block staking extrinsics, but they
apply to opposite directions and different balances. `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` is raised on
add-stake calls when the signing coldkey does not have enough free balance to fund the requested TAO
amount. `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` is raised on remove-stake and move-stake paths when the requested
alpha removal exceeds the pair's existing staked alpha on the subnet
([Subtensor Standard Errors: Not Enough Stake To Withdraw](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#notenoughstaketowithdraw)).

- **NotEnoughBalanceToStake** — not enough free TAO to add stake.
- **NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw** — not enough staked alpha to remove.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the coldkey free-balance condition that raises
it. It does not describe minimum stake amounts, swap slippage, or hotkey registration requirements;
those are separate topics
([Staking and Delegation Overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)).
