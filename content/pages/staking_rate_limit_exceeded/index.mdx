# Staking Rate Limit Exceeded

`StakingRateLimitExceeded` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised when a staking or
unstaking call is retried before its per-account cooldown has cleared. The standard-errors reference
describes it as "Staking rate limit exceeded"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#stakingratelimitexceeded),
[Subtensor API Errors: StakingRateLimitExceeded](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#stakingratelimitexceeded)).

## What It Means

Bittensor paces staking and unstaking operations through a per-account cooldown to prevent rapid
stake churning. The chain rate limits reference documents this pacing alongside other rate-limited
extrinsic families ([Chain Rate Limits](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits)).

`StakingRateLimitExceeded` is the rejection when a stake or unstake call is submitted before that
cooldown has elapsed. The error names a timing breach on the staking path, not proof that the signer
lacks delegation permission, that the hotkey is unregistered, or that the requested amount exceeds
the staked balance.

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a wallet or automation retries a staking or unstaking call within the same
cooldown window. Common cases include submitting multiple add-stake or remove-stake transactions in
quick succession, retrying a failed stake attempt without waiting for the block cooldown to clear,
or running automation that issues staking calls faster than the chain allows
([Chain Rate Limits](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits),
[Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#stakingratelimitexceeded)).

The practical meaning is narrow: wait for the cooldown period to pass before resubmitting the
staking or unstaking call.

## Distinction from Tx Rate Limit Exceeded

`StakingRateLimitExceeded` and `TxRateLimitExceeded` both enforce per-account transaction pacing,
but on different extrinsic families. `TxRateLimitExceeded` is the default label for subnet-scoped
cooldowns on many transaction types. `StakingRateLimitExceeded` is the dedicated rejection for
staking and unstaking calls specifically
([Subtensor Standard Errors: TxRateLimitExceeded](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#txratelimitexceeded),
[Chain Rate Limits](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits)).

- **StakingRateLimitExceeded** — the staking-specific cooldown has not cleared.
- **TxRateLimitExceeded** — a different transaction type's cooldown has not cleared.

## Distinction from Delegate Tx Rate Limit Exceeded

`StakingRateLimitExceeded` and `DelegateTxRateLimitExceeded` both pace staking-related operations,
but on different calls. `DelegateTxRateLimitExceeded` specifically gates delegate registration and
take-change operations. `StakingRateLimitExceeded` gates stake and unstake calls on the main staking
path
([Subtensor Standard Errors: DelegateTxRateLimitExceeded](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#delegatetxratelimitexceeded)).

- **StakingRateLimitExceeded** — staking or unstaking retried too soon.
- **DelegateTxRateLimitExceeded** — a delegate registration or take update retried too soon.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the staking cooldown that raises it. It does
not report the live cooldown length in blocks or the current block height; those depend on chain
state at the time of the call
([Chain Rate Limits](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits),
[Bittensor Networks](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/bittensor-networks)).
