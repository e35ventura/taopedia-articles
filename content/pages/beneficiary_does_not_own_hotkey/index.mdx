# Beneficiary Does Not Own Hotkey

`BeneficiaryDoesNotOwnHotkey` is a documented Subtensor standard error. The standard-errors
reference describes it as "Beneficiary does not own hotkey"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#beneficiarydoesnotownhotkey)).

## What It Means

A leased subnet names a beneficiary who is allowed to take ownership of the subnet after the lease
ends. Terminating a lease moves ownership to that beneficiary using a hotkey the caller supplies
([Subnet Crowdloans](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans)).

`BeneficiaryDoesNotOwnHotkey` means the runtime accepted the caller as the lease beneficiary but
then found that the beneficiary's coldkey does not own the hotkey the caller passed in to receive
the subnet. The termination is refused and the lease state does not change
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#beneficiarydoesnotownhotkey),
[Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

## Why It Appears

The check is the last precondition in `do_terminate_lease`, just before ownership transfer. By the
time the runtime reaches the ownership step, it has already verified that the lease exists
(`LeaseDoesNotExist`), that the caller's account matches the lease's beneficiary
(`ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin`), and that the lease's end block has already passed
(`LeaseHasNotEnded`). The runtime then asks whether the beneficiary's coldkey owns the hotkey the
caller passed in. If that relationship does not hold, `BeneficiaryDoesNotOwnHotkey` is raised and
the termination fails before any ownership writes happen
([Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

For a reader, the practical meaning is narrow: the caller is the beneficiary of a lease that has
ended, but the hotkey they submitted for the new subnet owner is not one they control. The runtime
refuses the transfer rather than write a subnet ownership relationship the beneficiary could not
later prove
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#beneficiarydoesnotownhotkey),
[Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

## Distinction from Expected Beneficiary Origin

`BeneficiaryDoesNotOwnHotkey` and `ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin` are both raised from the early
preconditions of `do_terminate_lease`, but they check different things about the caller.
`ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin` fires before any beneficiary-side check, when the caller is not the
account recorded as the lease's beneficiary at all. `BeneficiaryDoesNotOwnHotkey` fires later, after
the runtime has accepted that the caller is the beneficiary, when the beneficiary's coldkey does not
own the submitted hotkey
([Subtensor Standard Errors: ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#expectedbeneficiaryorigin),
[Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

- **ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin** — the caller is not the lease's beneficiary.
- **BeneficiaryDoesNotOwnHotkey** — the caller is the lease's beneficiary, but does not own the
  submitted hotkey.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the `BeneficiaryDoesNotOwnHotkey` error label and the
beneficiary-versus-hotkey check inside `do_terminate_lease`. It does not describe lease creation,
the crowdloan that backs a lease, the conditions that make a lease eligible for termination, or the
broader subnet-ownership transfer flow; those are separate conditions with their own error labels
and their own articles ([Subnet Crowdloans](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans),
[Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).
