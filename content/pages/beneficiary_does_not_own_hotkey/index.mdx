# Beneficiary Does Not Own Hotkey

`BeneficiaryDoesNotOwnHotkey` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised in
`do_terminate_lease`, the helper called by the `terminate_lease` extrinsic when a
[subnet lease](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans)'s beneficiary claims ownership
once the lease has ended. The error reference describes it as "Beneficiary does not own hotkey"
([Subtensor API: Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors)).

## What It Means

Terminating a lease transfers the leased subnet's ownership to the beneficiary. The caller supplies
a hotkey to become the subnet's registered owner hotkey after the transfer. Before that transfer
runs, `do_terminate_lease` confirms the beneficiary's coldkey actually owns the supplied hotkey and
raises `BeneficiaryDoesNotOwnHotkey` when it does not
([Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

This is one of the later checks in the termination sequence: it runs after the lease has been found,
the caller has been confirmed as the beneficiary, and the end-block timing has been validated, but
before the subnet ownership is actually written
([Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

## Why It Appears

The hotkey argument is supplied by the caller at the time of the termination call, separate from the
beneficiary identity recorded at registration. A hotkey that belongs to a different coldkey than the
lease's beneficiary, or one that is not registered at all, fails this ownership check and prevents
the transfer
([Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

## Distinction from Expected Beneficiary Origin

`BeneficiaryDoesNotOwnHotkey` and `ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin` both gate the same termination path by
checking account associations, but at different stages. `ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin` is an earlier
check confirming the transaction signer is the beneficiary recorded on the lease.
`BeneficiaryDoesNotOwnHotkey` is a later check confirming the hotkey argument the beneficiary
supplied for the ownership transfer is actually owned by that same beneficiary
([Subtensor Standard Errors: ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#expectedbeneficiaryorigin),
[Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

- **ExpectedBeneficiaryOrigin** — the transaction signer is not the lease's beneficiary.
- **BeneficiaryDoesNotOwnHotkey** — the beneficiary's supplied hotkey isn't owned by them.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the ownership check that raises it. It does not
describe the rest of the lease termination flow; that is documented separately
([Subnet Lease](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans)).
