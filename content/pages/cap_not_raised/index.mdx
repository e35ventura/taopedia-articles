# Cap Not Raised

`CapNotRaised` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised by the `finalize` extrinsic in the
crowdloan pallet. The Subtensor API error reference describes it as "The crowdloan cap has not been
raised" ([Subtensor API: Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors)).

## What It Means

Finalizing a [crowdloan](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans) requires the total
amount raised to exactly equal the crowdloan's funding cap. The `finalize` extrinsic checks whether
`raised == cap` and raises `CapNotRaised` when the raised amount is still below the cap, meaning the
crowdloan has not yet collected its full funding target
([Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

This check runs after confirming the crowdloan exists and the caller is its creator, but before the
finalization is carried out
([Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

## Distinction from Cap Raised

`CapNotRaised` and `CapRaised` are conceptual opposites that operate on different extrinsics.
`CapRaised` fires in the `contribute` extrinsic when the crowdloan's raised amount is already at or
above its cap, blocking further contributions. `CapNotRaised` fires in the `finalize` extrinsic when
the raised amount has not yet reached the cap, blocking premature finalization
([Subtensor Standard Errors: CapRaised](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

- **CapRaised** — contributions blocked because the cap has been met.
- **CapNotRaised** — finalization blocked because the cap has not been met.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the equality check that raises it. It does not
describe the rest of the crowdloan finalization flow; that is documented separately
([Crowdloans](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans)).
