# Cap Not Raised

`CapNotRaised` is a crowdloan pallet error raised by the `finalize` extrinsic. The pallet defines it
as "The crowdloan cap has not been raised"
([Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

## What It Means

Each [crowdloan](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans) campaign names a funding cap —
the total amount that must be collected before finalization can execute the stored call or transfer.
Before marking a campaign finalized, the `finalize` extrinsic compares the recorded total raised
against that cap and returns `CapNotRaised` when the two amounts are not equal
([Crowdloans: Who can finalize a crowdloan and when?](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans#who-can-finalize-a-crowdloan-and-when),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

This is a funding-target rejection on the finalize path, not proof that the contribution deadline
has passed, that the caller is not the campaign creator, or that the campaign was already finalized.

## Why It Appears

This error appears when the crowdloan creator calls `finalize` while `raised` is still below `cap`.
That can happen when automation triggers finalization early, when contributions stalled before the
last funding gap closed, or when a client misreads partial funding as full funding
([Crowdloans: What happens if the cap is not reached?](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans#what-happens-if-the-cap-is-not-reached),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

When debugging, compare against other finalize rejections on the same call. `InvalidOrigin` means
the signer is not the crowdloan creator. `AlreadyFinalized` means the campaign has already completed
finalization. `CapRaised` is the separate rejection on the `contribute` path when no additional
funding is accepted because the cap is already full
([Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs),
[Cap Raised](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#capraised)).

The practical meaning is narrow: wait until total raised equals the cap, or follow the refund path
documented for campaigns that never reach their target.

## Distinction from Cap Raised

`CapNotRaised` and `CapRaised` both compare total raised against the campaign cap, but on different
extrinsics. `CapNotRaised` blocks `finalize` when funding is still short of the cap. `CapRaised`
blocks `contribute` when the cap is already full and no additional deposits are accepted
([Cap Raised](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#capraised),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

- **CapNotRaised** — finalize arrived before the cap was fully raised.
- **CapRaised** — contribute arrived after the cap was already full.

## Distinction from Already Finalized

`CapNotRaised` and `AlreadyFinalized` both appear on `finalize`, but at different checks.
`CapNotRaised` means the campaign is not yet fully funded. `AlreadyFinalized` means the campaign has
already completed finalization and cannot be finalized again
([Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

- **CapNotRaised** — raised total still differs from the cap.
- **AlreadyFinalized** — the campaign was already finalized.

## Distinction from Invalid Origin

`CapNotRaised` and `InvalidOrigin` both block `finalize`, but at different checks. `InvalidOrigin`
means the signing account is not the crowdloan creator. `CapNotRaised` means the creator signed the
call but the raised total still does not equal the cap
([Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

- **CapNotRaised** — the cap has not been fully raised.
- **InvalidOrigin** — the signer is not the crowdloan creator.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the total-raised comparison on `finalize`. It
does not report a particular crowdloan's live raised amount or cap on any network; those are
campaign state readers can query separately
([Crowdloans](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans),
[Bittensor Networks](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/bittensor-networks)).
