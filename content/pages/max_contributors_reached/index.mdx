# Max Contributors Reached

`MaxContributorsReached` is a crowdloan pallet error raised by the `contribute` extrinsic. The
pallet defines it as "The maximum number of contributors has been reached"
(([Subtensor API Extrinsics: crowdloan.contribute](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/extrinsics#contributecrowdloan_id-crowdloanid-amount-u128),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

## What It Means

Each [crowdloan](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans) tracks how many accounts have
contributed to it. The `contribute` extrinsic checks that count against `MaxContributors`, a fixed
runtime constant set to 500, and raises `MaxContributorsReached` when the count has already reached
that limit — blocking any `contribute` call, including from accounts that have already contributed
before
([Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

The limit is a fixed constant, not per-crowdloan state. Any crowdloan with 500 contributors already
recorded fails this check for any caller regardless of how much of its funding cap remains
([Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

## Why It Appears

This check runs after confirming the contribution period has not ended and the crowdloan has not yet
raised its full cap, but before the contribution itself is processed. A popular crowdloan that fills
its contributor slots before filling its funding cap will reject any `contribute` call here —
including from accounts with existing contributions — even though additional funding would still be
accepted per the cap
([Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

## Distinction from Cap Raised

`MaxContributorsReached` and `CapRaised` both block new contributions, but they reflect different
limits. `CapRaised` fires when the crowdloan's total raised amount has reached its funding cap.
`MaxContributorsReached` fires when the number of contributing accounts has reached 500,
independently of how much total funding has been raised
([Subtensor Standard Errors: CapRaised](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#capraised),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

- **CapRaised** — the crowdloan's total funding has hit its cap.
- **MaxContributorsReached** — the crowdloan already has 500 contributors.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the count check that raises it. It does not
describe how crowdloans are created, funded, or finalized; those are documented separately
([Crowdloans](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans)).
