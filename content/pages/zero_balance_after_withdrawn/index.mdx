# Zero Balance After Withdrawn

`ZeroBalanceAfterWithdrawn` means a coldkey withdrawal was rejected because completing the move
would leave the account with zero balance in a way the chain does not allow. The standard-errors
reference describes it as "Balance would be zero after withdrawal"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#zerobalanceafterwithdrawn)).

## What It Means

Coldkey balance moves on Subtensor follow the underlying account preservation rules for TAO. Some
withdrawals must leave enough balance for the account to remain valid after the move.
`ZeroBalanceAfterWithdrawn` is the rejection when the requested withdrawal would violate that rule
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#zerobalanceafterwithdrawn),
[Glossary: Coldkey](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#coldkey)).

This is an account-preservation failure, not a subnet pool failure and not proof that the coldkey
lacks any TAO at all. The account may still hold balance, but not enough to cover the requested
withdrawal while satisfying the chain's post-withdrawal constraints.

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a transfer or withdrawal names an amount that would zero out the coldkey
account under the path's preservation mode. That can happen when a client requests the full visible
balance without reserving the portion the chain must keep, when fees are deducted after the amount
was chosen, or when a partial withdrawal still overshoots what the account can spend safely
([Glossary: Transfer](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#transfer),
[Transaction Fees in Bittensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/fees)).

When debugging, compare against other coldkey funding errors on the same extrinsic.
`InsufficientBalance` means the account cannot fund the requested amount under the path's spend
rules at all. `BalanceWithdrawalError` means the withdrawal step failed for a balances-layer reason
after earlier checks passed. `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` is the add-stake rejection when free balance
cannot cover the named stake amount
([Subtensor Standard Errors: InsufficientBalance](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#insufficientbalance),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: BalanceWithdrawalError](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#balancewithdrawalerror),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the withdrawal would leave the coldkey with zero balance in a way
the chain rejects.

## Distinction from Insufficient Balance

`ZeroBalanceAfterWithdrawn` and `InsufficientBalance` both block coldkey TAO moves, but they name
different rejection reasons. `InsufficientBalance` means the account cannot fund the requested
amount under the operation's spend rules. `ZeroBalanceAfterWithdrawn` means the specific problem is
that the withdrawal would zero out the account
([Subtensor Standard Errors: InsufficientBalance](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#insufficientbalance)).

- **ZeroBalanceAfterWithdrawn** — the withdrawal would zero out the account.
- **InsufficientBalance** — the coldkey cannot fund the requested amount.

## Distinction from Balance Withdrawal Error

`ZeroBalanceAfterWithdrawn` and `BalanceWithdrawalError` both involve failed coldkey withdrawals,
but they describe different failure modes. `ZeroBalanceAfterWithdrawn` is the preservation rejection
when the post-withdrawal balance would be zero. `BalanceWithdrawalError` is the documented failure
when the runtime cannot complete the withdraw step from the coldkey account
([Subtensor Standard Errors: BalanceWithdrawalError](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#balancewithdrawalerror)).

- **ZeroBalanceAfterWithdrawn** — the withdrawal would zero out the account.
- **BalanceWithdrawalError** — the coldkey withdrawal step failed.

## Distinction from Not Enough Balance To Stake

`ZeroBalanceAfterWithdrawn` and `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` both appear on value-moving paths, but on
different extrinsic families. `ZeroBalanceAfterWithdrawn` is a coldkey withdrawal preservation
rejection. `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` is the add-stake rejection when the coldkey cannot fund the
requested stake amount
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughBalanceToStake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetostake)).

- **ZeroBalanceAfterWithdrawn** — the withdrawal would zero out the coldkey account.
- **NotEnoughBalanceToStake** — the coldkey cannot fund the requested add-stake amount.

## Distinction from Cannot Afford Lock Cost

`ZeroBalanceAfterWithdrawn` and `CannotAffordLockCost` both involve coldkey TAO on
registration-related paths, but they test different conditions. `CannotAffordLockCost` is the
subnet-registration affordability rejection when the coldkey cannot fund the quoted lock cost.
`ZeroBalanceAfterWithdrawn` is a withdrawal-preservation rejection on balance-move paths
([Create a Subnet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/create-a-subnet)).

- **CannotAffordLockCost** — the coldkey cannot afford the subnet lock cost.
- **ZeroBalanceAfterWithdrawn** — the withdrawal would zero out the coldkey account.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the account-preservation rejection it
represents. It does not report a wallet's live free balance, fee reserve, or the exact amount that
can be withdrawn safely on a network; those depend on chain state at observation time
([Glossary: Transfer](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#transfer),
[Transaction Fees in Bittensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/fees)).
