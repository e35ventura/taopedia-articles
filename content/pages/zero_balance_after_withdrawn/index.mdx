# Zero Balance After Withdrawn

`ZeroBalanceAfterWithdrawn` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised when a withdrawal would
bring the account balance to zero. The standard-errors reference describes it as "Unsuccessfully
withdraw, balance could be zero (can not make account exist) after withdrawal"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#zerobalanceafterwithdrawn),
[Subtensor API Errors: ZeroBalanceAfterWithdrawn](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#zerobalanceafterwithdrawn)).

## What It Means

`ZeroBalanceAfterWithdrawn` is the rejection when a withdrawal would reduce the account balance to
zero. The documented description frames this as an account-existence constraint: a zero balance
cannot make the account exist after the withdrawal
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#zerobalanceafterwithdrawn)).

This is a resulting-balance check, not proof that the account has insufficient stake to cover the
requested amount or that the withdrawal operation encountered a runtime error.

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a withdrawal is sized to remove the full remaining balance of the account
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#zerobalanceafterwithdrawn)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the withdrawal amount must leave a non-zero balance after the
operation completes.

## Distinction from Balance Withdrawal Error

`ZeroBalanceAfterWithdrawn` and `BalanceWithdrawalError` both block withdrawal operations, but they
reject at different conditions. `BalanceWithdrawalError` fires when the withdrawal operation itself
cannot be completed. `ZeroBalanceAfterWithdrawn` fires when the withdrawal amount would bring the
account balance to zero
([Subtensor Standard Errors: BalanceWithdrawalError](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#balancewithdrawalerror)).

- **ZeroBalanceAfterWithdrawn** — withdrawal would leave zero balance.
- **BalanceWithdrawalError** — the withdrawal operation itself cannot be executed.

## Distinction from Not Enough Stake to Withdraw

`ZeroBalanceAfterWithdrawn` and `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` both block stake removal, but they check
different conditions. `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` fires when the staked amount is less than the
requested withdrawal. `ZeroBalanceAfterWithdrawn` fires when the staked amount covers the request
but completing it would leave the account at zero balance
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughstaketowithdraw)).

- **ZeroBalanceAfterWithdrawn** — stake covers the request, but resulting balance would be zero.
- **NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw** — staked amount is less than the requested withdrawal.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the resulting-balance check that raises it. It
does not report the live account balance for any specific account; that depends on chain state at
the time of the call
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#zerobalanceafterwithdrawn)).
