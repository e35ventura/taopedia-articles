# Subnet 3: Deprecated

Subnet 3 (netuid 3) is a retired subnet on the Bittensor network. It is listed on-chain with a
"deprecated" identity and carries no active mechanism, operator, or working GitHub repository. The
subnet still holds registered neurons and an alpha token pool but no meaningful validation or mining
work is being performed.

References: [taostats.io/subnets/3](https://taostats.io/subnets/3)

## How the Mechanism Works

No active incentive mechanism exists on Subnet 3. Validators are not issuing tasks and miners are
not being rewarded for work. Any emissions directed to this subnet via [[Yuma Consensus]] accrue
without a functioning scoring loop, effectively making participation unrewarding for new
registrants.

## Participating as a Miner

Participation in Subnet 3 is not recommended. With no active tasks being issued by validators and no
operator maintaining the codebase, miners who register would receive no meaningful work and would be
competing for emissions without a viable performance signal.

## Participating as a Validator

There is no active validator infrastructure on Subnet 3. A validator registered here would have no
reference mechanism to score miners against, making it impossible to emit meaningful weights.

## On-Chain Identity

Subnet 3 is recorded on-chain with the name "deprecated" and placeholder contact information. Its
alpha token pool and neuron slots remain technically active on the Bittensor network but serve no
operational purpose. The full on-chain record is available at
[taostats.io/subnets/3](https://taostats.io/subnets/3).
