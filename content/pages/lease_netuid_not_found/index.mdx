# Lease Netuid Not Found

`LeaseNetuidNotFound` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised by
`do_register_leased_network`, the extrinsic that finalizes a
[crowdloan](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans) into a registered
[subnet lease](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans). The standard-errors reference
describes it as "Couldn't find the lease netuid"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#leasenetuidnotfound)).

## What It Means

Leased-network registration creates a dedicated lease coldkey and hotkey, registers a new subnet
under that coldkey, and then must record which subnet ID was created. After the registration step
completes, `do_register_leased_network` looks up the subnet whose owner matches the lease coldkey.
`LeaseNetuidNotFound` is the rejection when that lookup returns nothing
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#leasenetuidnotfound),
[Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

This check runs only after earlier registration-path validations have already passed, including the
crowdloan-creator identity match and any optional end-block freshness check. It is the step that
confirms the newly registered subnet can be tied back to the lease coldkey before lease storage,
beneficiary proxy setup, and contributor refunds proceed
([Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

## Why It Appears

The error means the registration call reached the post-registration lookup but could not find a
subnet owner record for the lease coldkey that was just used to register the network. Without that
mapping, the chain cannot attach the lease record to a specific subnet ID, so the extrinsic stops
before writing lease storage or distributing refunds
([Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

For a reader, the practical meaning is narrow: the leased-network registration failed at the
subnet-ID resolution step, not at beneficiary identity or end-block validation. The problem is not
that the caller is the wrong crowdloan creator, that the end block is in the past, or that a lease
record is missing during termination
([Invalid Lease Beneficiary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#invalidleasebeneficiary),
[Lease Cannot End In The Past](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#leasecannotendinthepast),
[Lease Does Not Exist](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#leasedoesnotexist)).

## Distinction from Invalid Lease Beneficiary

`LeaseNetuidNotFound` and `InvalidLeaseBeneficiary` both appear in leased-network registration, but
at different stages. `InvalidLeaseBeneficiary` is the first check and rejects callers who are not
the crowdloan creator. `LeaseNetuidNotFound` is a later post-registration lookup reached only after
creator validation and subnet registration have already succeeded
([Invalid Lease Beneficiary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#invalidleasebeneficiary),
[Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

- **InvalidLeaseBeneficiary** — the caller is not the crowdloan creator.
- **LeaseNetuidNotFound** — the lease coldkey cannot be mapped to a registered subnet ID.

## Distinction from Lease Does Not Exist

`LeaseNetuidNotFound` and `LeaseDoesNotExist` both involve a missing lease-related record, but on
different extrinsics. `LeaseNetuidNotFound` blocks registration when the chain cannot resolve the
new subnet ID for a lease coldkey. `LeaseDoesNotExist` blocks termination when the supplied lease ID
has no stored lease record
([Lease Does Not Exist](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#leasedoesnotexist),
[Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).

- **LeaseNetuidNotFound** — registration cannot map the lease coldkey to a subnet ID.
- **LeaseDoesNotExist** — termination cannot find a lease record for the supplied lease ID.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the post-registration subnet lookup that raises
it. It does not describe crowdloan contribution rules, lease dividend distribution, or lease
termination after creation; those are documented separately
([Subnet Lease](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans),
[Subtensor source: leasing.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/leasing.rs)).
