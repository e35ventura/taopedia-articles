# Not Enough Balance To Pay Swap Coldkey

`NotEnoughBalanceToPaySwapColdKey` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised through
`charge_swap_cost`, the shared helper that collects a swap fee from a coldkey. The standard-errors
reference describes it as "Not enough balance to pay for the swap"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetopayswapcoldkey)).

## What It Means

A [coldkey swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/coldkey-swap) charges an announcement fee from
the source coldkey the first time a swap is announced for that account, not on every reannouncement.
`NotEnoughBalanceToPaySwapColdKey` is the rejection when that coldkey's balance cannot cover the fee
at the moment of the first announcement
([Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs),
[Coldkey Swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/coldkey-swap)).

This is the same shared fee-charging helper used by a separate, root-only path that can force a
coldkey swap for any account without going through the announcement flow at all. Both paths can fail
with this error if the targeted coldkey's balance is insufficient, though the ordinary path any
wallet owner uses is the announcement flow
([Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs)).

## Why It Appears

Because the fee is only charged on a coldkey's first swap announcement, this error specifically
means that first announcement attempt failed for lack of balance; a reannouncement of an
already-announced swap does not trigger a second fee charge and so cannot fail this way
([Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs),
[Coldkey Swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/coldkey-swap)).

## Distinction from Coldkey Swap Reannounced Too Early

`NotEnoughBalanceToPaySwapColdKey` and `ColdkeySwapReannouncedTooEarly` both relate to the
announcement step of a coldkey swap, but they apply to different attempts. The balance error applies
only to a coldkey's first announcement, when the fee is actually charged. The reannouncement error
applies to a later attempt to re-announce a swap that is already pending, before the required delay
since the prior announcement has passed
([Coldkey Swap Reannounced Too Early](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#coldkeyswapreannouncedtooearly),
[Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs)).

- **NotEnoughBalanceToPaySwapColdKey** — the first announcement's fee charge failed.
- **ColdkeySwapReannouncedTooEarly** — a reannouncement arrived before the required delay.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the fee charge that raises it. It does not
describe the rest of the coldkey swap process, such as the pending window, dispute path, or
finalization step; those are documented separately
([Coldkey Swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/coldkey-swap),
[Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs)).
