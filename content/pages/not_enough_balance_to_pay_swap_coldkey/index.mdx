# Not Enough Balance To Pay Swap Coldkey

`NotEnoughBalanceToPaySwapColdKey` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised through
`charge_swap_cost`, the shared helper that collects a swap fee from a coldkey. The standard-errors
reference describes it as "Not enough balance to pay for the swap"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughbalancetopayswapcoldkey)).

## What It Means

A [coldkey swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/coldkey-swap) uses the shared
`charge_swap_cost` helper to collect the required swap cost from the source coldkey.
`NotEnoughBalanceToPaySwapColdKey` is the rejection when that coldkey's balance cannot cover that
charge
([Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs),
[Coldkey Swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/coldkey-swap)).

Current source and tests show that this same helper is used on both charged coldkey-swap paths:
`announce_coldkey_swap` and `swap_coldkey`. That means the error is not limited to the first
announcement path; it also appears when the charged `swap_coldkey` path cannot collect the same cost
([Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs),
[Subtensor test: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/tests/swap_coldkey.rs)).

## Why It Appears

The current test coverage shows this error on both charged entry points. One test fails on
`swap_coldkey` when the targeted coldkey has no balance or only enough balance to fall below the
existential-deposit requirement after the charge. Another test fails on `announce_coldkey_swap`
under the same insufficient-balance conditions
([Subtensor test: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/tests/swap_coldkey.rs)).

The first-announcement rule still matters for the ordinary announcement flow: a reannouncement of an
already-announced swap does not trigger a second fee charge and so does not fail with this error on
that later attempt
([Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs),
[Coldkey Swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/coldkey-swap)).

## Distinction from Coldkey Swap Reannounced Too Early

`NotEnoughBalanceToPaySwapColdKey` and `ColdkeySwapReannouncedTooEarly` both relate to the
announcement step of a coldkey swap, but they apply to different attempts. The balance error applies
when an announcement path actually tries to collect the swap charge. The reannouncement error
applies to a later attempt to re-announce a swap that is already pending, before the required delay
since the prior announcement has passed
([Coldkey Swap Reannounced Too Early](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#coldkeyswapreannouncedtooearly),
[Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs)).

- **NotEnoughBalanceToPaySwapColdKey** — the charged swap-cost collection failed.
- **ColdkeySwapReannouncedTooEarly** — a reannouncement arrived before the required delay.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the fee charge that raises it. It does not
describe the rest of the coldkey swap process, such as the pending window, dispute path, or
finalization step; those are documented separately
([Coldkey Swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/coldkey-swap),
[Subtensor source: swap_coldkey.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/swap/swap_coldkey.rs)).
