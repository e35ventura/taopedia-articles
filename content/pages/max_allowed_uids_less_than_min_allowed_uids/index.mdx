# Max Allowed UIDs Less Than Min Allowed UIDs

`MaxAllowedUidsLessThanMinAllowedUids` is an admin-utils pallet error raised by
`sudo_set_max_allowed_uids`. The pallet defines it as "The maximum allowed UIDs must be greater than
the minimum allowed UIDs"
([Subtensor source: admin-utils/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/admin-utils/src/lib.rs)).

## What It Means

Each subnet stores both a minimum and maximum allowed UID count. The `sudo_set_max_allowed_uids`
extrinsic compares the submitted max-UID value against the subnet's stored `get_min_allowed_uids`
value and returns `MaxAllowedUidsLessThanMinAllowedUids` when the proposed maximum is lower
([Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters),
[Subtensor source: admin-utils/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/admin-utils/src/lib.rs)).

The check requires the submitted maximum to be greater than or equal to the stored minimum for that
netuid. A proposed max-UID value strictly below that minimum is rejected before the new maximum is
written
([Subtensor source: admin-utils/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/admin-utils/src/lib.rs)).

This is a min-max consistency rejection on the max-UID setter, not proof that the submitted netuid
has no subnet record or that the proposed maximum is below the subnet's current UID count.

## Why It Appears

This error appears when `sudo_set_max_allowed_uids` is called with a max-UID value below the
subnet's stored minimum allowed UIDs. That can happen when a client lowers the maximum before
checking the current minimum for the same netuid, or when the two bounds are edited in the wrong
order
([Subtensor source: admin-utils/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/admin-utils/src/lib.rs)).

When debugging, compare against other rejections on the same call. `SubnetDoesNotExist` means the
submitted netuid has no subnet record. `MaxAllowedUIdsLessThanCurrentUIds` means the proposed
maximum is below the subnet's current UID count. `MinAllowedUidsGreaterThanMaxAllowedUids` is the
separate rejection on `sudo_set_min_allowed_uids` when the proposed minimum is not below the stored
maximum
([Subnet Does Not Exist](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#subnetdoesnotexist),
[Subtensor source: admin-utils/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/admin-utils/src/lib.rs)).

The practical meaning is narrow: raise the proposed max-UID value or lower the stored minimum before
retrying `sudo_set_max_allowed_uids`.

## Distinction from Subnet Does Not Exist

`MaxAllowedUidsLessThanMinAllowedUids` and `SubnetDoesNotExist` both appear on
`sudo_set_max_allowed_uids`, but at different checks. `SubnetDoesNotExist` means the submitted
netuid has no subnet record. `MaxAllowedUidsLessThanMinAllowedUids` means the netuid exists but the
proposed maximum is below the stored minimum allowed UIDs
([Subnet Does Not Exist](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#subnetdoesnotexist),
[Subtensor source: admin-utils/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/admin-utils/src/lib.rs)).

- **MaxAllowedUidsLessThanMinAllowedUids** — proposed max UIDs are below the stored minimum.
- **SubnetDoesNotExist** — the submitted netuid has no subnet record.

## Distinction from Max Allowed UIDs Less Than Current UIDs

`MaxAllowedUidsLessThanMinAllowedUids` and `MaxAllowedUIdsLessThanCurrentUIds` both appear on
`sudo_set_max_allowed_uids`, but at different checks. `MaxAllowedUidsLessThanMinAllowedUids` means
the proposed maximum is below the stored minimum allowed UIDs. `MaxAllowedUIdsLessThanCurrentUIds`
means the proposed maximum is below the subnet's current UID count
([Subtensor source: admin-utils/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/admin-utils/src/lib.rs)).

- **MaxAllowedUidsLessThanMinAllowedUids** — proposed max UIDs are below the stored minimum.
- **MaxAllowedUIdsLessThanCurrentUIds** — proposed max UIDs are below the current UID count.

## Distinction from Min Allowed UIDs Greater Than Max Allowed UIDs

`MaxAllowedUidsLessThanMinAllowedUids` and `MinAllowedUidsGreaterThanMaxAllowedUids` both protect
consistency between min and max UID bounds, but on opposite setters.
`MaxAllowedUidsLessThanMinAllowedUids` is raised by `sudo_set_max_allowed_uids` when the proposed
maximum is below the stored minimum. `MinAllowedUidsGreaterThanMaxAllowedUids` is raised by
`sudo_set_min_allowed_uids` when the proposed minimum is not below the stored maximum
([Subtensor source: admin-utils/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/admin-utils/src/lib.rs)).

- **MaxAllowedUidsLessThanMinAllowedUids** — proposed max UIDs are below the stored minimum.
- **MinAllowedUidsGreaterThanMaxAllowedUids** — proposed min UIDs are not below the stored maximum.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the max-versus-min comparison on
`sudo_set_max_allowed_uids`. It does not report live min or max allowed-UID values for any netuid on
any network; those are chain state readers can query separately
([Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters),
[Bittensor Networks](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/bittensor-networks)).
