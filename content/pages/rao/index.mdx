# RAO

RAO is a small-denomination unit for TAO amounts in Bittensor. The
[Glossary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#rao) defines RAO as one billionth of a
TAO, while [Introduction to Bittensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/introduction) describes
TAO as the token used to incentivize subnet activity.

## Denomination Role

RAO is useful when Bittensor material needs to discuss small TAO-denominated amounts without losing
precision. Instead of changing what the token is, RAO changes the scale used to express the amount.
The [Glossary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#rao) gives the base conversion,
and [Introduction to Bittensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/introduction) places TAO in
the broader incentive context of miners, validators, subnet creators, and stakers.

## Relationship to TAO

RAO and TAO are not separate token systems. RAO is a denomination of TAO, so the concept belongs
where Bittensor documentation discusses TAO-denominated incentives, emissions, staking context, or
subnet activity. [Introduction to Bittensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/introduction)
describes TAO as the incentive token for subnet activity, and
[Emission](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions) describes emission as distributing newly
created TAO and subnet-specific alpha tokens across network roles.

## Emission Context

Emission articles and references sometimes need to distinguish the token being distributed from the
unit used to express an amount. RAO helps with that second part: it is a smaller unit for
TAO-denominated quantities, not a different reward category. The
[Glossary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#rao) links RAO to emission context,
and [Emission](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions) provides the broader distribution
frame for TAO and subnet-specific alpha tokens.

## Reader Cautions

RAO should be read as a denomination, not as a separate token or subnet role. It also should not be
confused with subnet-specific alpha tokens: those are separate subnet-context tokens, while RAO is a
denomination of TAO. This distinction follows the
[Glossary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#rao) definition and the
[Emission](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions) distinction between TAO and
subnet-specific alpha tokens.

## Relationship to Subtensor

RAO and Subtensor are related but different parts of Bittensor chain vocabulary. Subtensor names
Bittensor's layer 1 blockchain and system of record, while RAO names a small-denomination unit for
expressing TAO amounts recorded through chain-layer tokenomics accounting. The
[Glossary: Subtensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#subtensor) describes the
blockchain layer, and the [Glossary: RAO](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#rao)
defines RAO as one billionth of a TAO.

For readers, Subtensor names the chain layer, while RAO names a denomination scale used when reading
TAO-denominated amounts on that layer.

References: [Glossary: Subtensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#subtensor),
[Glossary: RAO](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#rao)

## Relationship to Netuid

RAO and a netuid are related but different parts of Bittensor vocabulary. A netuid identifies a
subnet on the network, while RAO names a denomination unit for TAO amounts that can appear in
subnet-level incentive and emission context. The
[Glossary: Netuid](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#netuid) places netuid at the
subnet level, and the [Glossary: RAO](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#rao) links
RAO to emission and incentive context for TAO-denominated amounts.

For readers, a netuid selects which subnet context is in scope, while RAO names the denomination
scale used when expressing TAO amounts in that broader network context.

References: [Glossary: Netuid](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#netuid),
[Glossary: RAO](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#rao)

## Relationship to Issuance

RAO and issuance are related but different parts of Bittensor supply vocabulary. Issuance names the
record of TAO circulating in the network, while RAO names a smaller denomination unit for expressing
TAO quantities within that supply context. The
[Glossary: Issuance](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#issuance) describes issuance
as TAO circulating in the network, and the
[Glossary: RAO](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#rao) defines RAO as one billionth
of a TAO.

For readers, issuance names the circulating supply record, while RAO names a denomination scale for
reading TAO amounts within that record.

References: [Glossary: Issuance](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#issuance),
[Glossary: RAO](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#rao)

## Relationship to Halving

RAO and halving are related but different parts of Bittensor tokenomics vocabulary. Halving names
the scheduled reduction in TAO emission rate, while RAO names a denomination unit for expressing
TAO-denominated amounts affected by that emission schedule. The
[Glossary: Halving](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#halving) describes halving as
a reduction in the rate at which new TAO is emitted, and the
[Glossary: RAO](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#rao) defines RAO as one billionth
of a TAO.

For readers, halving names an emission-rate event, while RAO names the denomination scale used when
reading TAO amounts in emission context.

References: [Glossary: Halving](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#halving),
[Glossary: RAO](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#rao)
