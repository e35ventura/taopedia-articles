# Subnet Registration Disabled

`SubNetRegistrationDisabled` means a `do_register` call targeted a subnet whose registration gate is
closed. The standard-errors reference describes it as "Registration is disabled for this subnet"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#subnetregistrationdisabled)).

The check runs after root-subnet and subnet-existence validation and before hotkey membership,
burn-price, and balance checks
([Subtensor source: registration.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/registration.rs)).

## What It Means

Neuron registration on a subnet is gated by the `NetworkRegistrationAllowed` hyperparameter. In
`do_register`, the runtime reads that setting through `get_network_registration_allowed` and returns
`SubNetRegistrationDisabled` when it is false
([Subnet Hyperparameters: NetworkRegistrationAllowed](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#networkregistrationallowed),
[Subtensor source: registration.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/registration.rs)).

When registration is disabled, new neuron entry through the burn-based `do_register` path is
rejected even if the subnet exists and the caller holds enough balance for the current burn price
([Network Registration Allowed](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#networkregistrationallowed)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a coldkey signs a registration call on a subnet whose
`NetworkRegistrationAllowed` setting is false. That can happen after a subnet owner or root closes
registration, when automation targets the wrong netuid, or when a client still uses an outdated
registration flow after the gate was flipped
([Subtensor source: registration.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/registration.rs)).

When debugging, rule out earlier gates on the same extrinsic first.
`RegistrationNotPermittedOnRootSubnet` means the call targeted netuid 0. `SubnetNotExists` means the
named subnet is not present. `HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet` means the hotkey already holds a UID
on that subnet
([Subtensor Standard Errors: RegistrationNotPermittedOnRootSubnet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#registrationnotpermittedonrootsubnet),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: SubnetNotExists](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#subnetnotexists),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#hotkeyalreadyregisteredinsubnet)).

The practical meaning is narrow: neuron registration is disabled on the targeted subnet.

## Distinction from Registration Not Permitted On Root Subnet

`SubNetRegistrationDisabled` and `RegistrationNotPermittedOnRootSubnet` both block registration
calls, but they test different conditions. `RegistrationNotPermittedOnRootSubnet` rejects any
`do_register` attempt on the root netuid. `SubNetRegistrationDisabled` applies to non-root subnets
whose `NetworkRegistrationAllowed` setting is false
([Subtensor source: registration.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/registration.rs)).

- **RegistrationNotPermittedOnRootSubnet** — registration was attempted on the root subnet.
- **SubNetRegistrationDisabled** — registration is disabled on the targeted subnet.

## Distinction from Hotkey Already Registered In Subnet

`SubNetRegistrationDisabled` and `HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet` are both early checks in
`do_register`, but they reject different problems. `SubNetRegistrationDisabled` is the subnet-wide
registration toggle. `HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet` means the signing hotkey already occupies a
UID on that subnet
([Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#hotkeyalreadyregisteredinsubnet),
[Subtensor source: registration.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/registration.rs)).

- **SubNetRegistrationDisabled** — new registration is disabled on the subnet.
- **HotKeyAlreadyRegisteredInSubNet** — the hotkey already has a UID there.

## Distinction from No Neuron Id Available

`SubNetRegistrationDisabled` is a policy gate that closes registration before capacity logic runs.
`NoNeuronIdAvailable` is a later capacity rejection when the subnet is full and no UID can be pruned
for replacement
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NoNeuronIdAvailable](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#noneuronidavailable),
[Subtensor source: registration.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/registration.rs)).

- **SubNetRegistrationDisabled** — registration is turned off on the subnet.
- **NoNeuronIdAvailable** — the subnet has no free or pruneable UID slot.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the `NetworkRegistrationAllowed` gate in
`do_register`. It does not describe burn pricing, pruning rules, or how to reopen registration on a
subnet; those are documented separately ([Register](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/miners),
[Subnet Hyperparameters: NetworkRegistrationAllowed](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters#networkregistrationallowed)).
