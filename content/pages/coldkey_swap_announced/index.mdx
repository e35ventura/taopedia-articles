# Coldkey Swap Announced

`ColdkeySwapAnnounced` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised while a
[coldkey swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/coldkey-swap) announcement is active. The
standard-errors reference describes it as "A coldkey swap has been announced for this account"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#coldkeyswapannounced)).

This page covers the error label returned when a blocked call is rejected. It is separate from the
successful `ColdkeySwapAnnounced` event that `announce_coldkey_swap` emits after a valid
announcement is recorded
([Subtensor source: dispatches.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/macros/dispatches.rs)).

## What It Means

After a coldkey swap is announced, the chain keeps a pending announcement record for that coldkey.
The `CheckColdkeySwap` dispatch extension inspects signed calls against that state before they run.
If the signer has an active, undisputed announcement and the call is not on the swap-related allow
list, the extension rejects it with `ColdkeySwapAnnounced`
([Subtensor source: check_coldkey_swap.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/guards/check_coldkey_swap.rs),
[Coldkey Swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/coldkey-swap)).

The guard exists to enforce the pending-window lock the coldkey swap concept article describes:
while a swap is announced, the wallet is restricted against transfers, staking, and most other
ordinary operations until the swap finalizes, is cleared, or enters dispute
([Coldkey Swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/coldkey-swap)).

Calls that remain allowed through an undisputed announcement are the swap flow itself — announcing,
finalizing, disputing, or clearing the pending swap — plus MEV-protected submission through
`submit_encrypted`
([Subtensor source: check_coldkey_swap.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/guards/check_coldkey_swap.rs)).

## Why It Appears

The most direct cause is attempting an ordinary signed operation — such as adding stake, removing
stake, setting weights, or sending a remark — while the caller still has a pending swap announcement
on record. The extension runs for the signing account on each dispatch, including proxied calls
resolved to that account, so the block applies even when the transaction is submitted through a
proxy delegate
([Subtensor source: check_coldkey_swap.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/guards/check_coldkey_swap.rs)).

Because the check is tied to announcement storage rather than to a single extrinsic, the same error
label can surface on many unrelated calls until the pending announcement is finalized, cleared after
the required delay, or disputed
([Subtensor source: check_coldkey_swap.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/guards/check_coldkey_swap.rs)).

## Distinction from Coldkey Swap Disputed

`ColdkeySwapAnnounced` and `ColdkeySwapDisputed` are both raised by the same `CheckColdkeySwap`
extension, but they correspond to different announcement states. `ColdkeySwapAnnounced` applies when
a pending announcement exists and is not disputed: most calls are blocked, but swap-related calls
can still pass. `ColdkeySwapDisputed` applies once a dispute record exists and blocks all signed
calls for that coldkey, including swap-related ones
([Coldkey Swap Disputed](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#coldkeyswapdisputed),
[Subtensor source: check_coldkey_swap.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/guards/check_coldkey_swap.rs)).

- **ColdkeySwapAnnounced** — pending announcement active; most calls blocked, swap flow still
  allowed.
- **ColdkeySwapDisputed** — swap under dispute; all signed calls blocked.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the dispatch guard that raises it. It does not
list every call the extension blocks or allows, describe the announcement or finalization steps, or
state the length of the pending window; those are documented separately
([Coldkey Swap](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/coldkey-swap),
[Subtensor source: check_coldkey_swap.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/guards/check_coldkey_swap.rs)).
