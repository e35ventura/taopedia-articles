# UID Map Could Not Be Cleared

`UidMapCouldNotBeCleared` means a subnet owner's UID trim on netuid 1 or another named subnet could
not finish resetting the subnet's hotkey-to-UID map during survivor compression. The Subtensor API
errors reference summarizes it as "The UID map for the subnet could not be cleared"
([Subtensor API Errors: UidMapCouldNotBeCleared](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#uidmapcouldnotbecleared)).

[UID trimming](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/uid-trimming) removes low emitters, keeps
protected immune neurons, and compresses survivors into consecutive indices. The guide also states
that associated storage is migrated and cleaned up as part of that process
([UID Trimming](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/uid-trimming)).

## What It Means

After a trim selects which UIDs to remove, the chain reindexes the survivors and updates the maps
that tie hotkeys to UID slots on the subnet. `UidMapCouldNotBeCleared` means that reset step for the
subnet's hotkey-to-UID map did not complete, so the trim aborts before the new compressed layout is
written
([Subtensor API Errors: UidMapCouldNotBeCleared](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#uidmapcouldnotbecleared),
[UID Trimming](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/uid-trimming)).

This is a storage-reset failure on the trim path. It does not, by itself, say anything about immune
share limits, trim timing cooldowns, or whether the submitted target count is outside the documented
minimum and maximum UID bounds.

## Why It Appears

This error appears late in a UID trim, after earlier trim checks have passed and the chain has begun
compressing survivors. The API label points to a failure while clearing the subnet's UID map rather
than while choosing which low emitters to remove
([UID Trimming](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/uid-trimming)).

When debugging a failed trim on netuid 1, compare against earlier trim rejections before treating
this as a routine owner-configuration problem. `TrimmingWouldExceedMaxImmunePercentage` means the
protected immune share at the target would violate the documented 80% ceiling. `InvalidValue` means
the submitted target count falls outside the allowed minimum or maximum UID bounds.
`HotKeySetTxRateLimitExceeded` means a prior trim has not cleared the documented trimming cooldown
yet
([Subtensor API Errors: TrimmingWouldExceedMaxImmunePercentage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#trimmingwouldexceedmaximmunepercentage),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: InvalidValue](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#invalidvalue),
[Chain Rate Limits: UID trimming rate limit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits#uid-trimming-rate-limit)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the trim reached the map-reset step and could not finish clearing
the subnet's UID map.

## Distinction from Trimming Would Exceed Max Immune Percentage

`UidMapCouldNotBeCleared` and `TrimmingWouldExceedMaxImmunePercentage` both appear on UID trimming
calls, but at different stages. `TrimmingWouldExceedMaxImmunePercentage` is the immune-share
rejection on the requested target count before deregistrations proceed. `UidMapCouldNotBeCleared` is
the later failure while resetting the hotkey-to-UID map during survivor compression
([Subtensor API Errors: TrimmingWouldExceedMaxImmunePercentage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#trimmingwouldexceedmaximmunepercentage)).

- **UidMapCouldNotBeCleared** — the subnet UID map could not be cleared during compression.
- **TrimmingWouldExceedMaxImmunePercentage** — immune neurons would exceed the allowed share at the
  target count.

## Distinction from Invalid Value

`UidMapCouldNotBeCleared` and `InvalidValue` both block UID trims, but they reject different
problems. `InvalidValue` means the submitted target UID count is outside the subnet's allowed
minimum or maximum bounds. `UidMapCouldNotBeCleared` means earlier bounds and immune checks passed,
but the map-reset step during compression failed
([Subtensor Standard Errors: InvalidValue](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#invalidvalue)).

- **UidMapCouldNotBeCleared** — the hotkey-to-UID map could not be cleared on the trim path.
- **InvalidValue** — the submitted target UID count is out of allowed bounds.

## Distinction from Hotkey Set Tx Rate Limit Exceeded

`UidMapCouldNotBeCleared` and `HotKeySetTxRateLimitExceeded` both stop a subnet-owner trim, but at
different checks. `HotKeySetTxRateLimitExceeded` is the timing rejection when another trim was
attempted before the documented trimming cooldown cleared. `UidMapCouldNotBeCleared` is a later
storage-reset failure after trim execution has begun
([Chain Rate Limits: UID trimming rate limit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits#uid-trimming-rate-limit),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeySetTxRateLimitExceeded](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#hotkeysettxratelimitexceeded)).

- **UidMapCouldNotBeCleared** — the subnet UID map could not be cleared during compression.
- **HotKeySetTxRateLimitExceeded** — the trim was attempted too soon after the last trim.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the map-reset failure it represents. It does
not predict whether a particular trim on netuid 1 will succeed on retry or which live storage state
caused the clear step to fail; those depend on chain state at observation time
([UID Trimming](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/uid-trimming),
[Bittensor Networks](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/bittensor-networks)).
