# Bonds Reset Enabled

Bonds reset enabled is a per-subnet Bittensor hyperparameter that controls whether accumulated
validator-miner bonds may be cleared so standing can rebuild from scratch. It is a boolean flag
stored per netuid, not a value that shapes scoring on its own
([Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters),
[BondsResetOn storage](https://subtensor.com/reference/subtensormodule/storage/BondsResetOn)).

The flag belongs to the Yuma Consensus bond path. It decides whether the bond-reset capability is
available on a subnet; it does not change how validators evaluate miners or how weights are
submitted ([Yuma Consensus](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus)).

## What It Controls

A validator-miner bond is the smoothed record of how much a validator has backed a miner over time,
and that bond history is what pairs validators into the dividend split. Bonds reset enabled answers
one narrow question: may that accumulated history be wiped for a neuron on this subnet
([Glossary: Validator-Miner Bonds](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-miner-bonds),
[Yuma Consensus](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus)).

When the flag is off, the reset path is a no-op and bonds keep accumulating through their normal EMA
smoothing. When the flag is on, the reset path is allowed to run and clear the bonds directed at a
neuron, so its standing has to rebuild from the next epoch onward
([BondsResetOn storage](https://subtensor.com/reference/subtensormodule/storage/BondsResetOn)).

The trade-off is continuity versus responsiveness. Keeping bonds produces more historical continuity
and stable ranking; allowing resets produces more dynamic ranking but discards the bond history that
would otherwise carry forward
([BondsResetOn storage](https://subtensor.com/reference/subtensormodule/storage/BondsResetOn)).

## How the Reset Works

The on-chain reset is gated by this flag. The `do_reset_bonds` routine first reads the subnet's
bonds-reset setting and returns immediately when it is disabled, so no bonds change unless the
subnet has opted in
([`do_reset_bonds`](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/epoch/run_epoch.rs)).

When the flag is enabled, the routine resolves the target neuron's UID and then walks every
validator's stored bond vector, removing the entries that point at that UID. The effect is that the
bond mass other validators had built toward that neuron is dropped, while bonds toward other neurons
are left intact
([`do_reset_bonds`](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/epoch/run_epoch.rs)).

Because the clearing is column-scoped to one UID rather than a subnet-wide wipe, a reset targets a
single neuron's accumulated standing. Validators then rebuild bonds toward that neuron through the
normal per-epoch EMA update
([Yuma Consensus](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus)).

## Default and Configuration

The flag defaults to `False`, so a new subnet keeps bonds unless an owner turns the capability on.
It is stored per netuid in the `BondsResetOn` map and read back as a boolean
([BondsResetOn storage](https://subtensor.com/reference/subtensormodule/storage/BondsResetOn),
[Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters)).

It is set with the `sudo_set_bonds_reset_enabled` extrinsic, callable by the subnet owner or root,
which updates the flag and emits a `BondsResetToggled` event. The call carries the netuid and the
boolean to apply
([sudo_set_bonds_reset_enabled](https://subtensor.com/reference/adminutils/calls/sudo_set_bonds_reset_enabled),
[Extrinsics: sudoSetBondsResetEnabled](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/extrinsics)).

Different subnets can choose different bond-reset policies, so an observed reset on one subnet
should not be read as network-wide behavior
([BondsResetOn storage](https://subtensor.com/reference/subtensormodule/storage/BondsResetOn)).

## Distinction from Validator-Miner Bonds

Validator-miner bonds name the smoothed standing a validator accumulates toward miners. Bonds reset
enabled names whether that accumulated standing may be cleared for a neuron on the subnet
([Glossary: Validator-Miner Bonds](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-miner-bonds),
[Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters)).

- **Validator-Miner Bonds** — the bond state that accumulates through EMA smoothing.
- **Bonds reset enabled** — the toggle deciding whether that state may be wiped.

The bond is the thing being tracked; the flag governs whether the tracking can be zeroed. See
[[Validator-Miner Bonds]] for the underlying mechanic.

## Distinction from Bonds Moving Average

Bonds moving average sets how quickly bond history reacts to new weights under normal operation.
Bonds reset enabled does not tune that smoothing speed; it governs whether the accumulated result
can be discarded entirely
([Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters),
[Exponential Moving Averages](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/ema)).

- **Bonds moving average** — how fast bonds smooth toward new weights.
- **Bonds reset enabled** — whether bonds can be cleared back to nothing.

See [[Bonds Moving Average]] for the smoothing window itself.

## Distinction from Bonds Penalty

Bonds penalty sets how much weight is subtracted for exceeding the subnet consensus benchmark before
bonds update. Bonds reset enabled acts on the stored bond result, not on the per-epoch penalty
applied to out-of-consensus weights
([Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters),
[Yuma Consensus](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus)).

- **Bonds penalty** — the penalty magnitude on out-of-consensus weights each epoch.
- **Bonds reset enabled** — whether accumulated bonds can be cleared.

See [[Bonds Penalty]] for the penalty control.

## Distinction from Liquid Alpha

Liquid alpha varies the bond-smoothing factor by a pair's consensus alignment within configured
bounds. Bonds reset enabled is a separate switch that allows the bond state to be cleared rather
than smoothed
([Consensus-Based Weights](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/consensus-based-weights),
[Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters)).

- **Liquid alpha** — varies how fast bonds smooth, by alignment.
- **Bonds reset enabled** — whether bonds can be reset to zero.

See [[Liquid Alpha]] for the responsiveness control.

## Distinction from Immunity Period

Immunity period protects a newly registered neuron from deregistration for a number of blocks. Bonds
reset enabled is unrelated to deregistration protection; it governs whether bond standing toward a
neuron may be cleared
([Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters),
[Glossary: Immunity Period](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#immunity-period)).

- **Immunity period** — temporary shield against deregistration.
- **Bonds reset enabled** — toggle for clearing accumulated bonds.

See [[Immunity Period]] for the protection window.

## Distinction from Deregistration

Deregistration removes a neuron from its UID slot when capacity is needed. Bonds reset enabled does
not remove a neuron; it clears the bonds pointing at a UID while the neuron keeps its slot
([Glossary: Deregistration](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#deregistration),
[Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters)).

- **Deregistration** — removal of a neuron from its slot.
- **Bonds reset enabled** — clearing of bond standing without removing the neuron.

See [[Deregistration]] for slot removal.

## Distinction from Yuma Consensus

Yuma Consensus is the epoch-boundary settlement that aggregates validator weights, applies clipping
and bonding, and turns the result into incentive and dividend shares. Bonds reset enabled is one
bond-path switch that can clear bond standing before that settlement reads it
([Yuma Consensus](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus),
[Emission](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions)).

- **Yuma Consensus** — settlement from weights to emissions each epoch.
- **Bonds reset enabled** — toggle for clearing bond standing on the path into settlement.

See [[Yuma Consensus]] for the settlement step and [[Dividends]] for the validator-side payout that
bonds feed.

## Reader Boundary

Bonds reset enabled is a per-subnet boolean on the bond path, read after a neuron's bonds are
targeted for clearing. It is not a scoring metric, a validator recommendation, or a guarantee about
ranking outcomes; exact behavior depends on the subnet's setting and consensus data
([BondsResetOn storage](https://subtensor.com/reference/subtensormodule/storage/BondsResetOn),
[Yuma Consensus](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus)).
