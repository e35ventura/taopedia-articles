# Objective Function

An objective function is the goal a Bittensor subnet is designed to optimize through its incentive
mechanism. The [Glossary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#objective-function)
defines the term in the context of machine learning and subnet operations, where the subnet
continuously optimizes for a goal through its incentive mechanism.

## Role in a Subnet

The objective function gives a subnet a target for useful work. It is not the same thing as a miner
response, a validator score, or the full subnet protocol. It is the goal that gives those pieces a
shared direction.
[Understanding Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-subnets) describes
subnets as specialized markets where miners produce work and validators measure that work.

## Relationship to Incentive Mechanisms

An incentive mechanism translates the subnet's goal into a structure miners and validators can
interact with.
[Understanding Incentive Mechanisms](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/anatomy-of-incentive-mechanism)
describes subnet design in terms of the task, the protocol between miners and validators, and the
scoring model used to evaluate responses. The objective function is the goal those design choices
are meant to serve.

## Relationship to Scoring

A scoring model evaluates miner responses, while the objective function names the goal those
evaluations are trying to encourage. Keeping those ideas separate helps readers distinguish the
subnet's intended outcome from the method used to evaluate individual responses.
[Understanding Incentive Mechanisms](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/anatomy-of-incentive-mechanism)
places scoring inside the broader incentive-mechanism design.

## Interpretation Boundaries

Objective function should be read as a high-level subnet design concept, not as an equation, a local
setting, or a complete explanation of any particular subnet's evaluation method. The official
glossary defines the term at the concept level, and the incentive-mechanism documentation provides
the broader context for how subnet goals are translated into miner-validator interaction.
