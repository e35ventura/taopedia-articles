An objective function is the goal a Bittensor subnet is trying to optimize through its incentive
mechanism. The official
[Glossary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#objective-function) defines it in the
context of machine learning and subnet operations.

## Why It Matters

The term helps readers focus on the desired outcome of a subnet, rather than on one particular
measurement method. The official
[Understanding Incentive Mechanisms](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/anatomy-of-incentive-mechanism)
page describes incentive mechanisms as the structures that guide participant behavior and the
production of a subnet's digital commodity.

For Taopedia readers, the useful takeaway is simple: the objective function is the goal being
optimized. Details about how a specific subnet measures progress toward that goal belong to that
subnet's own documentation.
