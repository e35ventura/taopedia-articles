# Hotkey Not Registered In Network

`HotKeyNotRegisteredInNetwork` means a serving call named a hotkey that does not hold a UID on the
submitted netuid. The standard-errors reference describes it as "The hotkey is not registered in the
specified subnet"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#hotkeynotregisteredinnetwork)).

The check runs before IP, port, and serving rate-limit validation on axon publish paths
([Subtensor source: serving.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/serving.rs)).

## What It Means

Axon and prometheus serving extrinsics publish a hotkey's network endpoint for a specific subnet. In
`validate_serve_axon`, the runtime calls `is_hotkey_registered_on_network(netuid, hotkey_id)` and
returns `HotKeyNotRegisteredInNetwork` when that lookup fails
([Subtensor source: serving.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/serving.rs),
[Glossary: Axon](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#axon)).

The same membership check appears on prometheus serving paths that publish validator endpoint data
for a subnet
([Subtensor source: serving.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/serving.rs)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a caller tries to serve axon or prometheus information for a hotkey on a
netuid where that hotkey has no UID slot. That can happen when automation targets the wrong netuid,
when serving is attempted before registration completes, or when a hotkey was pruned from the subnet
but serving calls still use the old mapping
([Subtensor source: serving.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/serving.rs)).

When debugging, rule out later gates on the same extrinsic. `InvalidIpType` and `InvalidIpAddress`
mean the endpoint fields failed format checks after membership passed. `ServingRateLimitExceeded`
means the hotkey republished serving data too soon
([Subtensor Standard Errors: InvalidIpType](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#invalidiptype),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: InvalidIpAddress](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#invalidipaddress),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: ServingRateLimitExceeded](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#servingratelimitexceeded)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the hotkey is not registered on the subnet named in the serving
call.

## Distinction from Hotkey Not Registered In Subnet

`HotKeyNotRegisteredInNetwork` and `HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet` share the same standard-errors
wording, but they are raised on different extrinsic families. `HotKeyNotRegisteredInNetwork` is the
serving-path rejection in `validate_serve_axon` and related prometheus checks.
`HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet` is the rejection on other subnet-scoped extrinsics such as weight
submission when the signing hotkey lacks a UID on the targeted netuid
([Hotkey Not Registered In Subnet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#hotkeynotregisteredinsubnet),
[Subtensor source: serving.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/serving.rs)).

- **HotKeyNotRegisteredInNetwork** — the hotkey lacks a UID on the netuid for a serving call.
- **HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet** — the hotkey lacks a UID on the netuid for another subnet-scoped
  extrinsic.

## Distinction from Hotkey Account Not Exists

`HotKeyNotRegisteredInNetwork` and `HotKeyAccountNotExists` both involve hotkey validity, but at
different scopes. `HotKeyNotRegisteredInNetwork` means the hotkey account is present but has no UID
on the named subnet. `HotKeyAccountNotExists` means the hotkey account itself is absent from chain
state
([Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyAccountNotExists](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#hotkeyaccountnotexists),
[Subtensor source: serving.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/serving.rs)).

- **HotKeyNotRegisteredInNetwork** — the hotkey exists but is not registered on the subnet.
- **HotKeyAccountNotExists** — the hotkey account does not exist.

## Distinction from Serving Rate Limit Exceeded

`HotKeyNotRegisteredInNetwork` and `ServingRateLimitExceeded` both appear on axon serving paths, but
at different stages. `HotKeyNotRegisteredInNetwork` is the membership check before endpoint fields
are accepted. `ServingRateLimitExceeded` is the later pacing rejection when the hotkey republishes
too frequently
([Subtensor Standard Errors: ServingRateLimitExceeded](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#servingratelimitexceeded),
[Subtensor source: serving.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/serving.rs)).

- **HotKeyNotRegisteredInNetwork** — the hotkey is not registered on the subnet.
- **ServingRateLimitExceeded** — the hotkey served axon data too soon.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the subnet membership check on serving paths.
It does not describe how to register a neuron, how axon TLS fields are validated, or the live
serving rate limit for a subnet; those are documented separately
([Glossary: Axon](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#axon),
[Chain Rate Limits: Axon serve rate limit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/chain-rate-limits)).
