# Proposal Hash

A proposal hash is the identifier used to refer to a proposal in Bittensor governance. The
[Glossary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#proposal-hash) defines it as a unique
identifier for a proposal used in the voting process.

## Governance Context

The [Senate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/governance/senate) documentation places proposals in
the Senate review process, while the
[Governance Overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/governance) explains that proposals are
created by the Triumvirate and reviewed by the Senate. A proposal hash is the reference for the
proposal, not the proposal body itself.

## Relationship to Multiple Mechanisms

A proposal hash identifies a governance item that may change privileged subnet settings. The
[Glossary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#subnet-creator) notes that subnet
creators can configure multiple incentive mechanisms to distribute emissions across different types
of work or evaluation criteria.

For readers, a proposal hash still names an on-chain governance reference. The proposal it points to
may affect subnet design with more than one incentive mechanism.

References:
[Multiple Incentive Mechanisms Within Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-multiple-mech-subnets),
[Glossary: Subnet Creator](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#subnet-creator)

## Reader Boundary

This article does not list active proposal hashes or describe any proposal outcome. Readers should
use official governance references for proposal records and review context.
