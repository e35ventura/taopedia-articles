# Subnet Metagraph

A subnet metagraph is a snapshot-style view of one Bittensor subnet at a particular block. It brings
together the information needed to understand which participants are represented on the subnet, how
stake and activity are reflected, and what consensus-related metrics are visible for that moment in
time.

References: [The Subnet Metagraph](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/metagraph),
[Subtensor metagraph source](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/rpc_info/metagraph.rs)

## Why it matters

The metagraph is useful because Bittensor subnets are not just named projects; they are active
markets of miners, validators, stake, weights, bonds, and performance signals. A reader who wants to
understand a subnet needs a way to inspect that shared state without turning every metric into a
permanent claim about the subnet.

References: [The Subnet Metagraph](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/metagraph),
[Understanding Neurons](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/neurons)

## What it shows

At a high level, the metagraph shows participant and subnet state. Participant data can include
UIDs, keys, activity, stake, ranks, trust, consensus, incentives, dividends, emissions, weights,
bonds, and network connection details. Subnet data can include name and symbol, timing values,
registration settings, weight settings, Commit Reveal settings, and selected Dynamic TAO values.

References: [The Subnet Metagraph](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/metagraph),
[Subtensor metagraph source](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/rpc_info/metagraph.rs)

## Relationship to Yuma Consensus

The metagraph helps readers see the data around consensus, but it is not the consensus algorithm
itself. Validators submit weights for miners, Yuma Consensus aggregates those signals, and the
resulting ranks, bonds, dividends, incentives, and emissions are visible through metagraph-related
data.

References: [The Subnet Metagraph](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/metagraph),
[Yuma Consensus](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus)

## Reading it carefully

Metagraph values are tied to a subnet, a network, and a block. That makes the metagraph a good
inspection view, but a poor place to make timeless claims about who is participating or how they are
performing. For operational decisions, readers should query current data directly and interpret the
fields using official documentation or implementation sources.

References: [The Subnet Metagraph](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/metagraph),
[Subtensor metagraph source](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/rpc_info/metagraph.rs)
