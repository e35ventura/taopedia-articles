# Max Immune Uids Percentage

Max immune uids percentage is a subtensorModule runtime constant, listed in the chain's constants
metadata as maxImmuneUidsPercentage, with the value 80 and the summary "Maximum percentage of immune
UIDs"
([Subtensor API Constants: maxImmuneUidsPercentage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/constants#maximmuneuidspercentage-percent)).
It caps the fraction of a subnet's UIDs that may be immune at any one time.

## What It Means

An immune UID is a neuron slot that is protected from removal while its protection applies. Max
immune uids percentage sets the ceiling on how much of a subnet can hold that protected status at
once: no more than 80% of a subnet's UIDs may be immune. The UID trimming guide documents the same
80% maximum for immune UIDs ([UID Trimming](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/uid-trimming)).
Because the cap is 80%, at least one fifth of a subnet's UIDs remain non-immune and therefore
eligible for removal. On a subnet such as netuid 1, at most 80% of UIDs may be immune at the same
time.

## Relation to UID Trimming

UID trimming reduces a subnet's live UID count by removing the lowest emitters and compacting the
survivors, and it skips immune UIDs while doing so
([UID Trimming](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/uid-trimming)). Max immune uids percentage
bounds that protection: a trim cannot leave more than 80% of the subnet immune. When an operation
would push the immune share past the cap, the runtime raises the
`TrimmingWouldExceedMaxImmunePercentage` error
([Subtensor API Errors: TrimmingWouldExceedMaxImmunePercentage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#trimmingwouldexceedmaximmunepercentage)).

## Distinction from Immunity Period

Immunity period and max immune uids percentage protect neurons along different axes. Immunity period
is a per-subnet hyperparameter measured in blocks that determines how long a newly registered neuron
stays immune
([Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters)). Max
immune uids percentage does not measure time; it caps how many UIDs may carry immune status at once,
expressed as a share of the subnet. One is a duration, the other is a population ceiling.

## Distinction from Immune Owner Uids Limit

Immune owner uids limit and max immune uids percentage are also distinct caps. Immune owner uids
limit is a per-subnet hyperparameter that caps how many neurons an owner may mark as owner-immune
([Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters)). Max
immune uids percentage is a network-wide constant that caps the total immune share of a subnet
regardless of why each UID is immune. One counts owner-marked neurons; the other bounds the overall
immune fraction.

## Reader Boundary

Max immune uids percentage is a single network-wide constant, not a per-subnet hyperparameter, and
it reflects the current runtime configuration rather than a fixed protocol rule; the value could
change in a future runtime upgrade. It does not describe which specific UIDs are immune or how
pruning scores are computed
([Subtensor API Constants: maxImmuneUidsPercentage](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/constants#maximmuneuidspercentage-percent)).
