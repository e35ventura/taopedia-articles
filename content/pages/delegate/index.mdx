# Delegate

A delegate is a subnet validator that receives staked TAO from delegators and performs validation
tasks in one or more subnets
([Glossary: Delegate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#delegate),
[Glossary: Subnet Validator](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#subnet-validator)).

## What It Represents

Delegate names the receiving validator role in a staking relationship. A
[nominator](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominator) supplies TAO support; the
delegate performs validation work and may keep
[validator take](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-take-) from
delegated-stake emissions before the remainder returns to supporters
([Staking and delegation overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)).

Readers should separate four "delegate" ideas that sound similar:

- **Delegate** — the subnet validator role that receives nominated stake.
- **Delegation** — the support relationship created when TAO is staked behind a validator hotkey.
- **Nominate** — the action a staker takes to assign that support.
- **Staking proxy** — a wallet pattern that grants scoped authority to act for a coldkey; not the
  validator role that receives stake
  ([Glossary: Delegation](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#delegation),
  [Glossary: Nominate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominate),
  [Proxies overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/keys/proxies)).

Delegate stake is TAO the validator stakes from its own balance.
[Effective stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#effective-stake) is the
combined measure after self-stake and nominated support are counted. Stake weight is the downstream
consensus influence derived from that staking context
([Glossary: Delegate Stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#delegate-stake),
[Glossary: Stake Weight](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#stake-weight)).

On a subnet such as netuid 1, delegate vocabulary names the receiving validator role inside that
subnet's incentive mechanism. A
[root validator](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#root-subnetsubnet-zero) is
associated with Subnet Zero rather than validation inside one task subnet
([Staking and delegation overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation)).

A delegate may also authorize
[child hotkeys](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys) to validate on its behalf
on a selected netuid. That routes signing work without transferring ownership of the underlying
stake ([Child Hotkeys](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys)).

Delegate examples from one network environment should not be read as representing production staking
relationships on another
([Bittensor Networks](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/bittensor-networks)).

## Delegate Role Versus Validator Permit

Being a delegate means receiving stake and performing validation work. Validator permit is a
separate on-chain flag recording whether that subnet UID may submit counting weights
([Glossary: Validator Permit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-permit),
[Glossary: Subnet Validator](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#subnet-validator)).

Nominate assigns TAO support to a delegate hotkey but does not make the staker a delegate or grant
validator permit on a subnet UID. The nominator supplies support; the delegate receives it; permit
vocabulary names weight-submission rights separately
([Glossary: Nominator](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominator),
[Glossary: Nominate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominate)).

## Default Validator Take

The staking and delegation documentation describes an 18% default validator take: the percentage of
emissions from delegated stake that a delegate retains before the remainder is distributed to
stakers. That take applies to the delegated-stake portion of returns, separate from emissions
proportional to the delegate's own self-stake
([Staking and Delegation Overview](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/delegation),
[Glossary: Validator Take %](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-take-)).

For nominators, net returns from supporting a delegate therefore depend on this take as well as on
validator performance and the subnet's emission context at distribution time
([Emission: Distribution](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions#distribution)).

## Distinction from Delegation

Delegation names the support relationship attaching nominated TAO to a hotkey. Delegate names the
receiving validator role on the other side of that relationship
([Glossary: Delegation](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#delegation),
[Glossary: Delegate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#delegate)).

- **Delegation** — support relationship attaching nominated TAO to a hotkey.
- **Delegate** — receiving validator role that performs validation work.

## Distinction from Nominator

A delegate receives staked TAO from delegators. A nominator is the TAO-holder role that stakes
behind a delegate
([Glossary: Delegate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#delegate),
[Glossary: Nominator](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominator)).

- **Delegate** — receiving validator role that performs validation and earns validator take.
- **Nominator** — supplying role that delegates TAO behind a chosen validator.

## Distinction from Nominate

A delegate is the validator that accepts stake from others. To nominate is the action a staker takes
to assign stake to that delegate
([Glossary: Delegate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#delegate),
[Glossary: Nominate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominate)).

- **Delegate** — the validator that accepts and validates with others' stake.
- **Nominate** — the staker's action of assigning stake to a delegate.

## Distinction from Effective Stake

Effective stake is the combined total behind a delegate hotkey after self-stake and nomination are
counted. Delegate names the receiving validator role, not that measured total
([Glossary: Effective Stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#effective-stake),
[Glossary: Delegate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#delegate)).

- **Delegate** — receiving validator role in the staking relationship.
- **Effective stake** — combined self-stake plus nominated support behind the hotkey.

## Distinction from Validator Take

Validator take names the share a delegate keeps from delegated-stake emissions. Delegate names the
receiving validator role, not that split
([Glossary: Validator Take %](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-take-),
[Glossary: Delegate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#delegate)).

- **Delegate** — receiving validator role in the staking relationship.
- **Validator take** — commission on delegated-stake returns at distribution.

## Distinction from Stake Weight

Stake weight names the consensus influence measure derived from stake behind a delegate hotkey.
Delegate names the validator role that receives support and submits weights
([Glossary: Stake Weight](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#stake-weight),
[Glossary: Delegate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#delegate)).

- **Delegate** — receiving validator role behind the hotkey.
- **Stake weight** — consensus influence derived from stake behind that role.

## Distinction from Autostaking

Autostaking routes a miner's earned emissions into a validator-staking position. Delegate names the
receiving validator role that accumulated stake sits behind
([Auto Staking for Miners](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/miners/autostaking),
[Glossary: Delegate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#delegate)).

- **Autostaking** — automatic routing of miner emissions into validator stake.
- **Delegate** — receiving validator role that holds the resulting support.

## Distinction from Root Validator

A delegate on a subnet such as netuid 1 receives nominated stake and performs validation inside that
subnet's incentive mechanism. A root validator is associated with Subnet Zero
([Glossary: Delegate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#delegate),
[Glossary: Root Subnet/Subnet Zero](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#root-subnetsubnet-zero)).

- **Delegate** — receiving subnet validator role on a selected netuid.
- **Root validator** — validator role on Subnet Zero without task-subnet mining.

## Distinction from Child Hotkeys

Delegate names the parent validator role that holds stake on a subnet. A child hotkey is a separate
authorized key the parent uses to validate with re-delegated stake on that subnet
([Glossary: Delegate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#delegate),
[Child Hotkeys](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators/child-hotkeys)).

- **Delegate** — parent validator role that holds stake on the selected netuid.
- **Child hotkey** — authorized worker key that signs validation using routed stake.

## Distinction from Validator Permit

A delegate is the receiving validator role in a staking relationship. Validator permit records
whether that subnet UID may submit counting weights
([Glossary: Delegate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#delegate),
[Glossary: Validator Permit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-permit)).

- **Delegate** — receiving validator role that holds delegated stake.
- **Validator permit** — flag recording whether subnet weights may count.

## Distinction from Yuma Consensus

A delegate is the subnet validator role that receives staked TAO and performs validation. Yuma
Consensus converts submitted validator weights into emission shares on a subnet
([Glossary: Delegate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#delegate),
[Yuma Consensus](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/yuma-consensus)).

- **Delegate** — validator role that receives delegated stake and performs validation.
- **Yuma Consensus** — on-chain mechanism that uses validator weights to determine emission shares.

## Reader Boundary

Delegate names the receiving validator role in a staking relationship. It is not the nominator role
that supplies TAO support, nor a balance report or dividend quote
([Glossary: Delegate](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#delegate),
[Glossary: Nominator](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#nominator)).
