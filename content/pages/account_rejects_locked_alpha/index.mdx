# Account Rejects Locked Alpha

`AccountRejectsLockedAlpha` means a call tried to move locked alpha to a coldkey that has not opted
in to receive it. Subtensor coldkeys reject incoming locked alpha by default, and this error is
returned when a transfer would send locked mass to such a destination
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs),
[Conviction and locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).

## What It Means

Conviction locks bind part of a coldkey's subnet alpha to a commitment signal
([Conviction and locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).

When stake moves between coldkeys on the same subnet, freely available alpha can transfer first and
any remainder can carry lock state with it. Before that locked portion is accepted, the runtime
checks whether the destination coldkey allows incoming locked alpha. `AccountRejectsLockedAlpha`
means that destination has not opted in
([Conviction and locked stake: Transferring locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking#transferring-locked-stake),
[Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

The same rejection applies when a coldkey swap would move existing locks to a new coldkey that still
rejects incoming locked alpha
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a stake transfer or coldkey swap would deliver locked alpha to a coldkey
that rejects it. Coldkeys reject incoming locked alpha by default unless they opt in through the
chain's reject-locked-alpha setting
([Subtensor source: dispatches.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/macros/dispatches.rs)).

When debugging, compare against other lock-related rejections on transfer paths.
`LockHotkeyMismatch` means the destination coldkey already has a lock bound to a different hotkey.
`StakeUnavailable` means the source coldkey cannot free enough unlocked alpha for an unstake or move
request
([Conviction and locked stake: Transferring locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking#transferring-locked-stake),
[Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the destination coldkey will not accept incoming locked alpha.

## Distinction from Lock Hotkey Mismatch

`AccountRejectsLockedAlpha` and `LockHotkeyMismatch` both block same-subnet stake transfers that
carry lock state, but they test different conditions. `LockHotkeyMismatch` means the destination
coldkey already has a lock tied to another hotkey. `AccountRejectsLockedAlpha` means the destination
coldkey rejects incoming locked alpha regardless of hotkey binding
([Conviction and locked stake: Transferring locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking#transferring-locked-stake),
[Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

- **AccountRejectsLockedAlpha** — the destination coldkey rejects incoming locked alpha.
- **LockHotkeyMismatch** — the destination lock points at a different hotkey.

## Distinction from Stake Unavailable

`AccountRejectsLockedAlpha` and `StakeUnavailable` both involve locked stake, but on different sides
of a move. `StakeUnavailable` means the source coldkey cannot supply enough unlocked alpha for the
request. `AccountRejectsLockedAlpha` means the destination coldkey will not accept the locked
portion that would follow the transfer
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

- **StakeUnavailable** — the source lacks enough unlocked alpha for the request.
- **AccountRejectsLockedAlpha** — the destination rejects incoming locked alpha.

## Distinction from Transfer Disallowed

`AccountRejectsLockedAlpha` and `TransferDisallowed` both stop stake moves, but at different layers.
`TransferDisallowed` is the subnet transfer toggle that blocks coldkey-to-coldkey stake transfers on
a netuid. `AccountRejectsLockedAlpha` is the destination-account rejection when locked alpha would
be included in an otherwise permitted transfer
([Subtensor Standard Errors: TransferDisallowed](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#transferdisallowed),
[Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

- **TransferDisallowed** — stake transfers are disabled on the subnet.
- **AccountRejectsLockedAlpha** — the destination coldkey rejects locked alpha.

## Distinction from Insufficient Stake For Lock

`AccountRejectsLockedAlpha` and `InsufficientStakeForLock` both concern conviction locks, but on
different operations. `InsufficientStakeForLock` is raised when a coldkey tries to create or
increase a lock without enough total alpha on the subnet. `AccountRejectsLockedAlpha` is raised when
locked alpha would be sent to a coldkey that has not opted in to receive it
([Subtensor source: lock.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/lock.rs)).

- **InsufficientStakeForLock** — total alpha is too low for the lock request.
- **AccountRejectsLockedAlpha** — the destination rejects incoming locked alpha.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the destination opt-in check for incoming
locked alpha. It does not report whether a particular coldkey has opted in on a live network; that
setting depends on chain state at observation time
([Conviction and locked stake](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking)).
