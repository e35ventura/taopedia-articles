# Account Not Allowed Commit

`AccountNotAllowedCommit` is a commitments pallet error raised by the `set_commitment` extrinsic
when the calling account is not eligible to commit. The pallet defines it as "Account is not allowed
to make commitments to the chain"
([Subtensor source: commitments/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/commitments/src/lib.rs)).

## What It Means

`set_commitment` is the extrinsic used to write a commitment — structured metadata — to the chain
for a given subnet. Before accepting the commitment, it runs a `can_commit` eligibility check as its
first guard. `AccountNotAllowedCommit` fires when that check returns false, blocking the submission
before any further validation or state changes occur
([Subtensor source: commitments/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/commitments/src/lib.rs)).

## Distinction from Space Limit Exceeded

`AccountNotAllowedCommit` and `SpaceLimitExceeded` both block `set_commitment`, but at different
checks. `AccountNotAllowedCommit` fires at the first guard, before the commitment content is
inspected, because the caller is not eligible to commit at all. `SpaceLimitExceeded` fires later,
after eligibility passes, when the caller has already committed enough data in the current epoch to
exhaust their per-epoch space allowance
([Subtensor source: commitments/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/commitments/src/lib.rs)).

- **AccountNotAllowedCommit** — the account is not eligible to commit.
- **SpaceLimitExceeded** — the account is eligible but has consumed its per-epoch space allowance.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the eligibility check that raises it. It does
not describe how commitment fields are structured or the rest of the commitment submission flow
([Subtensor source: commitments/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/commitments/src/lib.rs)).
