# Protocol Alpha

Protocol alpha is alpha accumulated by the Bittensor protocol itself rather than by an individual
staker. The official
[Glossary: Protocol Alpha](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#protocol-alpha)
describes it as alpha that results from chain-side TAO buys during reserve injection, is cached per
subnet instead of being immediately recycled, and later participates in subnet dissolution
settlement.

## Protocol vs Participant Alpha

Subnet alpha vocabulary names several different holders and flows. Alpha tokens in general describe
subnet-specific assets used in Dynamic TAO tokenomics, while alpha outstanding names alpha set aside
for participant distribution. Protocol alpha is different again: it names alpha the protocol itself
accumulates during chain-side reserve activity, not alpha already attributed to miners, validators,
stakers, or a subnet owner.

For readers, the useful boundary is ownership. Participant-facing alpha terms describe rewards and
staking context; protocol alpha describes a protocol-held alpha balance tied to subnet reserve
injection.

## Reserve Injection Context

Protocol alpha arises during the injection stage of Bittensor emissions. The official
[Emission: Injection](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/emissions#injection) documentation
explains that, when the chain performs excess TAO swaps into a subnet pool during reserve injection,
the alpha acquired through those chain buys is cached per subnet rather than immediately recycled.
That cached alpha gives the protocol an explicit ownership stake in the subnet's tokenomics context.

This makes protocol alpha part of the reserve-injection story, not a separate reward label for
validators or nominators. It follows chain-side liquidity activity during emission injection rather
than a participant payout schedule.

## Subnet Dissolution Context

Protocol alpha also matters when a subnet ends. The
[Glossary: Protocol Alpha](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#protocol-alpha) states
that, when a subnet is dissolved, the protocol's alpha participates in the pro-rata TAO settlement
calculation and reduces staker payouts proportionally, while the protocol's corresponding TAO share
is returned to the chain. The glossary also notes that the protocol alpha balance is cleared when a
subnet dissolves.

For readers, protocol alpha therefore has both an ongoing reserve-injection meaning and a
dissolution-settlement meaning. It is not only a label for everyday staking balances.

## Reader Boundary

Protocol alpha should not be read as a live balance report, a staking recommendation, or a complete
explanation of alpha emissions math. It is a tokenomics term for protocol-held alpha tied to reserve
injection and dissolution settlement. Current balances, injection amounts, and settlement outcomes
belong in official emission references and live chain data.
