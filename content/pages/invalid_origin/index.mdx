# Invalid Origin

`InvalidOrigin` is a crowdloan pallet error raised when a creator-only call is signed by an account
other than the crowdloan creator. The pallet defines it as "The origin of this call is invalid"
([Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

## What It Means

Several [crowdloan](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans) management extrinsics
require the signing account to be the campaign creator. After loading the crowdloan record, those
calls compare the signer against `crowdloan.creator` and return `InvalidOrigin` when the two
accounts differ
([Crowdloans: Crowdloan Lifecycle](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans#crowdloan-lifecycle),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

In the current source, that creator check appears on `finalize`, `refund`, `dissolve`,
`update_min_contribution`, `update_end`, `update_cap`, and `set_max_contribution`
([Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

This is a creator-permission rejection on crowdloan management paths, not proof that the crowdloan
id is invalid, that the campaign was already finalized, or that a contribution amount was too small
on `contribute`.

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a non-creator account signs one of the creator-only crowdloan extrinsics
listed above. That can happen when a contributor wallet retries a creator action, when automation
uses the wrong signing key, or when a client confuses creator management calls with open contributor
calls such as `contribute` or `withdraw`
([Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

When debugging, compare against other rejections on the same call. `InvalidCrowdloanId` means the
submitted id does not resolve to stored campaign state. `AlreadyFinalized` means the campaign
already completed finalization. On `finalize`, `CapNotRaised` means the creator signed the call but
total raised still differs from the cap
([Invalid Crowdloan Id](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#invalidcrowdloanid),
[Already Finalized](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#alreadyfinalized),
[Cap Not Raised](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#capnotraised),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

The practical meaning is narrow: sign creator-only crowdloan calls with the campaign creator
account.

## Distinction from Invalid Crowdloan Id

`InvalidOrigin` and `InvalidCrowdloanId` both block crowdloan calls, but at different stages.
`InvalidCrowdloanId` means the submitted crowdloan id does not resolve to stored campaign state.
`InvalidOrigin` means the id resolved to a campaign, but the signer is not its creator on a
creator-only path
([Invalid Crowdloan Id](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#invalidcrowdloanid),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

- **InvalidOrigin** — the signer is not the crowdloan creator.
- **InvalidCrowdloanId** — the crowdloan id does not resolve to stored state.

## Distinction from Bad Origin

`InvalidOrigin` and `BadOrigin` both signal authorization failures, but on different pallets and
calls. `InvalidOrigin` is the crowdloan pallet error for creator-only management extrinsics.
`BadOrigin` is the broader subtensor rejection when a privileged extrinsic lacks required root or
subnet-owner permission
([Bad Origin](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#badorigin),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

- **InvalidOrigin** — the signer is not the crowdloan creator.
- **BadOrigin** — the signer lacks required privileged permission on a subtensor call.

## Distinction from Cap Not Raised

`InvalidOrigin` and `CapNotRaised` both appear on `finalize`, but at different checks.
`InvalidOrigin` means the signer is not the crowdloan creator. `CapNotRaised` means the creator
signed the call but total raised still differs from the cap
([Cap Not Raised](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#capnotraised),
[Subtensor source: crowdloan/lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/crowdloan/src/lib.rs)).

- **InvalidOrigin** — the signer is not the crowdloan creator.
- **CapNotRaised** — raised total still differs from the cap.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the creator-origin comparison on crowdloan
management extrinsics. It does not report which account created a particular crowdloan on any
network; that is campaign state readers can query separately
([Crowdloans](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/crowdloans),
[Bittensor Networks](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/concepts/bittensor-networks)).
