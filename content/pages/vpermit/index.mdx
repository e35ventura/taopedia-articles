# VPermit

VPermit is a Bittensor validation term for the subnets where a delegate is authorized to validate.
The [Glossary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#vpermit) defines VPermit as a list
of subnet IDs that indicate which subnets a delegate is authorized to validate on.

## Delegate Context

A delegate can validate in more than one subnet. VPermit is the delegate-level view of that
authorization, while a
[validator permit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-permit) is the
subnet-level permission flag for validation rights.

## Relationship to Validator Permits

The glossary describes VPermits as aggregating individual validator permits across multiple subnets.
At Taopedia level, the useful distinction is scope: a validator permit answers whether validation
rights apply inside one subnet, while VPermit summarizes the subnets where those rights apply for a
delegate.

## Stake Weight Context

Individual validator permits are tied to stake-weight position inside each subnet. The
[Glossary: Validator Permit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-permit)
describes those permits as awarded to the top neurons by stake weight within a subnet. VPermit is
the delegate-level summary of which subnets carry that authorization, so a delegate's VPermit
reflects where those stake-weight-based subnet permits apply across the subnets they validate on.

References: [Glossary: VPermit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#vpermit),
[Glossary: Validator Permit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#validator-permit),
[Glossary: Stake Weight](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#stake-weight)

## Relationship to Multiple Mechanisms

VPermit summarizes the subnets where a delegate is authorized to validate. The
[Glossary](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#multiple-incentive-mechanisms) notes
that validators must evaluate miners separately for each mechanism.

For readers, VPermit still names delegate-level subnet authorization. Validation rights on a subnet
that runs more than one incentive mechanism still span separate evaluation contexts inside that
netuid.

References:
[Multiple Incentive Mechanisms Within Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-multiple-mech-subnets),
[Glossary: Multiple Incentive Mechanisms](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#multiple-incentive-mechanisms)

## Reader Boundary

This article defines the term at a high level. It does not list live permissions, rank validators,
describe staking or registration steps, or restate validator requirements. Readers should use
official validator references for those details.

## Relationship to Coinbase

VPermit and coinbase are related but different parts of how delegate validation rights connect to
emissions. VPermit is the delegate-level summary of which subnets a delegate is authorized to
validate on. Coinbase is the per-block protocol mechanism that drives TAO emission, injects TAO into
emitting subnets' pool reserves, accumulates pending emissions, and checks epoch boundaries to
trigger Yuma Consensus rounds. The
[Glossary: VPermit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#vpermit) describes the
delegate-level permission summary, and the
[Coinbase Implementation](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/navigating-subtensor/emissions-coinbase)
documentation describes the per-block emission operation.

A delegate's VPermit lists the subnets where it holds validation rights, and those rights are part
of what lets it participate in — and earn validator dividends from — the coinbase-triggered epoch
rounds on each listed subnet. Coinbase produces and distributes those dividends each epoch on a
per-subnet basis, while VPermit records, across subnets, where a delegate is authorized to take
part. VPermit answers where a delegate can validate; coinbase answers how each of those subnets'
emissions are generated and paid out each epoch.
