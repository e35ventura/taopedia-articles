# Not Enough Stake To Set Weights

`NotEnoughStakeToSetWeights` means the signing hotkey's stake weight on the targeted subnet is below
the global `StakeThreshold`. The standard-errors reference describes it as "Less than minimum stake
required to set weights"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#notenoughstaketosetweights)).

`StakeThreshold` is a global root parameter that sets the minimum stake weight a neuron must hold
before it can submit weights
([Stake Threshold](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters),
[Validating in Bittensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators)).

## What It Means

Weight submission requires the signing hotkey to carry enough stake weight on the targeted subnet.
`NotEnoughStakeToSetWeights` means that stake weight is below the global threshold, so the call is
rejected
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#notenoughstaketosetweights),
[Stake Threshold](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters)).

For debugging, treat this as a stake-weight floor on the weight path, not as proof that the hotkey
lacks a UID on the subnet, that list shapes are invalid, or that the neuron lacks a validator permit
for non-self weights
([Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#hotkeynotregisteredinsubnet),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: WeightVecNotEqualSize](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#weightvecnotequalsize),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NeuronNoValidatorPermit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#neuronnovalidatorpermit)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a registered hotkey tries to submit weights but its stake weight on the
subnet falls below the global minimum. That can happen after partial unstaking, when delegated stake
has not yet reached the threshold, or when the hotkey is registered but still under the documented
minimum for weight submission
([Validating in Bittensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators),
[Stake Threshold](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters)).

The practical meaning is narrow: the hotkey does not meet the stake-weight floor for submitting
weights on that subnet.

## Distinction from Neuron No Validator Permit

`NotEnoughStakeToSetWeights` and `NeuronNoValidatorPermit` both appear in weight submission, but
they reject different requirements. `NotEnoughStakeToSetWeights` means stake weight is below the
global threshold. `NeuronNoValidatorPermit` means non-self weights were submitted without a
validator permit
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NeuronNoValidatorPermit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#neuronnovalidatorpermit),
[Validating in Bittensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators)).

- **NotEnoughStakeToSetWeights** — stake weight is below the global threshold for weight submission.
- **NeuronNoValidatorPermit** — non-self weights were submitted without a validator permit.

## Distinction from Hotkey Not Registered In Subnet

`NotEnoughStakeToSetWeights` and `HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet` both appear during weight
troubleshooting, but they reject different conditions. `HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet` means the
hotkey has no UID on the targeted netuid. `NotEnoughStakeToSetWeights` means the hotkey is below the
stake-weight floor for weight submission on that subnet
([Subtensor Standard Errors: HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#hotkeynotregisteredinsubnet)).

- **HotKeyNotRegisteredInSubNet** — the hotkey has no UID on the subnet the call targets.
- **NotEnoughStakeToSetWeights** — stake weight is below the global threshold.

## Distinction from Not Enough Stake To Withdraw

`NotEnoughStakeToSetWeights` and `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` both involve stake balances, but on
different extrinsics. `NotEnoughStakeToSetWeights` blocks weight submission when total stake weight
is too low to meet the global threshold. `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` blocks unstaking when the
requested removal exceeds the staked alpha held by a hotkey-coldkey pair on a subnet
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subtensor-api/errors#notenoughstaketowithdraw)).

- **NotEnoughStakeToSetWeights** — stake weight is too low to submit weights.
- **NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw** — unstake amount exceeds available staked alpha.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the stake-threshold condition that raises it.
It does not describe how validator permits are awarded each epoch, how stake weight is computed in
full, or the live `StakeThreshold` value on a network; those are separate topics
([Stake Threshold](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters),
[Validating in Bittensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators),
[Yuma Consensus Epoch](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/navigating-subtensor/epoch)).
