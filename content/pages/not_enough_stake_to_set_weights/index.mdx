# Not Enough Stake To Set Weights

`NotEnoughStakeToSetWeights` is a documented Subtensor standard error raised on weight-setting
paths. The standard-errors reference describes it as "Less than minimum stake required to set
weights"
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughstaketosetweights)).

## What It Means

Weight submission requires the signing hotkey to carry enough stake weight on the targeted subnet.
Before version-key, rate-limit, or validator-permit checks run, `internal_set_weights` calls
`check_weights_min_stake` and returns `NotEnoughStakeToSetWeights` when the hotkey's stake weight on
that subnet is below the global `StakeThreshold` value
([Subtensor Standard Errors](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughstaketosetweights),
[Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters),
[Subtensor source: weights.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/weights.rs),
[Subtensor source: lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/lib.rs)).

In `lib.rs`, `check_weights_min_stake` compares the hotkey's stake weight from
`get_stake_weights_for_hotkey_on_subnet` against `get_stake_threshold()`, except for the subnet
owner hotkey, which is allowed through regardless of stake
([Subtensor source: lib.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/lib.rs)).

## Why It Appears

This error appears when a registered hotkey tries to submit weights but its stake weight on the
subnet falls below the global minimum. That can happen after partial unstaking, when delegated stake
has not yet reached the threshold, or when the hotkey is registered but still under the documented
minimum for weight submission
([Validating in Bittensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators),
[Subtensor source: weights.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/weights.rs)).

For a reader, the practical meaning is narrow: the hotkey does not meet the stake-weight floor for
submitting weights on that subnet. The problem is not that the UID and value lists have different
lengths, that the weights version key is stale, or that the neuron lacks a validator permit for
non-self weights
([Weight Vec Not Equal Size](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#weightvecnotequalsize),
[Incorrect Weight Version Key](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#incorrectweightversionkey),
[Subtensor Standard Errors: NeuronNoValidatorPermit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#neuronnovalidatorpermit)).

## Distinction from Neuron No Validator Permit

`NotEnoughStakeToSetWeights` and `NeuronNoValidatorPermit` both appear in weight submission, but
they reject different requirements. `NotEnoughStakeToSetWeights` is the stake-weight floor checked
early in `internal_set_weights`. `NeuronNoValidatorPermit` is the later check that blocks non-self
weight assignments when the neuron does not hold a validator permit
([Subtensor Standard Errors: NeuronNoValidatorPermit](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#neuronnovalidatorpermit),
[Subtensor source: weights.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/weights.rs)).

A hotkey can fail the stake threshold even though permit questions have not been reached yet. It can
also clear the stake threshold and still fail later on permit rules for non-self weights.

- **NotEnoughStakeToSetWeights** — stake weight is below the global threshold for weight submission.
- **NeuronNoValidatorPermit** — non-self weights were submitted without a validator permit.

## Distinction from Not Enough Stake To Withdraw

`NotEnoughStakeToSetWeights` and `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` both involve stake balances, but on
different extrinsics. `NotEnoughStakeToSetWeights` blocks weight submission when total stake weight
is too low to meet the global threshold. `NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw` blocks unstaking when the
requested removal exceeds the staked alpha held by a hotkey-coldkey pair on a subnet
([Not Enough Stake To Withdraw](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/errors/subtensor#notenoughstaketowithdraw),
[Subtensor source: weights.rs](https://github.com/opentensor/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/weights.rs)).

- **NotEnoughStakeToSetWeights** — stake weight is too low to submit weights.
- **NotEnoughStakeToWithdraw** — unstake amount exceeds available staked alpha.

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the meaning of the error label and the stake-threshold check that raises it. It
does not describe how validator permits are awarded each epoch, how stake weight is computed in
full, or the live `StakeThreshold` value on a network; those are documented separately
([Subnet Hyperparameters](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/subnet-hyperparameters),
[Validating in Bittensor](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/validators),
[Yuma Consensus Epoch](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/navigating-subtensor/epoch)).
