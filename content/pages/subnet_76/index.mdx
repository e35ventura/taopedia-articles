Byzantium is Bittensor Subnet 76. Its live Finney identity registers the subnet name as Byzantium,
the description as "Leverage marketing with AI Swarm," and the project site as
[byzantiumai.net](https://www.byzantiumai.net). No public GitHub repository is currently listed in
the on-chain identity.

The Byzantium website describes the project as the largest decentralized AI marketing agency
coordinated on Bittensor, with AI agents acting as marketers across X (formerly Twitter) and
Farcaster. The site carries a "coming soon" notice, indicating the subnet has not yet launched
publicly as of the current identity state.

## How the Mechanism Works

Based on the registered identity and public site, Byzantium is designed to coordinate a swarm of AI
agents that perform marketing tasks. The registered description, "Leverage marketing with AI Swarm,"
and the site description of "a swarm of AI marketers" both point to an incentive design where miners
run AI agents that produce marketing outputs, and validators score those outputs for quality and
reach.

Because the subnet has not yet published a public repository or mechanism specification, the exact
miner task, validator scoring function, and weight-setting logic are not confirmed in on-chain
identity or public documentation. Operators should verify current documentation against the
[Byzantium site](https://www.byzantiumai.net) and live chain state before participating.

## Participating as a Miner

No public miner repository or setup documentation is currently listed in the Byzantium on-chain
identity or on the Byzantium website. When the subnet publishes its miner documentation, the process
for registering on SN76, acquiring a hotkey slot, and running the required AI marketing agent
software will be defined there. Operators should treat current participation guidance as preliminary
until the project publishes its miner setup instructions.

## Participating as a Validator

Validator responsibilities for Byzantium are not yet documented in the public on-chain identity.
Based on the declared swarm marketing purpose, validators are expected to evaluate the quality and
effectiveness of AI marketing outputs produced by miners and submit weights accordingly into
[[Yuma Consensus]]. When the subnet publishes its validator documentation, the scoring method for
evaluating AI marketing tasks will be described there.

## On-Chain Identity

Live SN76 data is available on [TaoStats](https://taostats.io/subnets/76). Readers can reproduce the
registered identity fields with the Bittensor CLI command documented in the
[btcli reference](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/btcli/#btcli-subnets-get-identity):
`btcli subnets get-identity --netuid 76 --network finney`.

That Finney identity reports the subnet name as Byzantium, the description as "Leverage marketing
with AI Swarm," the subnet URL as byzantiumai.net, and the Discord field as linked. No GitHub
repository is registered.
