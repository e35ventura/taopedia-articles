Byzantium is Bittensor Subnet 76, a decentralized AI marketing subnet. Its registered on-chain
description is "Leverage marketing with AI Swarm." The project website describes Byzantium as
building the largest decentralized AI marketing agency coordinated on Bittensor, with distribution
targeting X/Twitter and Farcaster. The project website is
[byzantiumai.net](https://www.byzantiumai.net).

The subnet's on-chain identity lists a Discord community in the Bittensor Discord server. No public
GitHub repository is registered in the identity fields.

Because no public source repository appears in the identity record, this article describes what the
subnet's published materials state about the intended mechanism rather than citing validator source
code. Readers should verify the current mechanism against any documentation the operator publishes.

## How the Mechanism Works

Based on Byzantium's published project description, the subnet tasks an AI swarm with producing and
distributing marketing content across social platforms. Miners are expected to generate or route
marketing material, and validators score the output according to criteria defined by the subnet
operator. Coordination relies on the Bittensor incentive layer and [[Yuma Consensus]] to rank miner
contributions.

The website describes the network as approaching launch, so the full scoring specification may be
published after the subnet's initial registration.

## Participating as a Miner

Miners on Byzantium are expected to produce or facilitate marketing work — content generation,
distribution, or campaign execution — targeted at social channels such as X/Twitter and Farcaster.
Because no public repository or specification is registered in the on-chain identity, prospective
miners should monitor [TaoStats](https://taostats.io/subnets/76) and the Byzantium Discord for
current participation requirements.

## Participating as a Validator

Validators on Byzantium evaluate the quality and reach of the marketing work miners produce and
submit weights that feed into [[Yuma Consensus]]. Scoring criteria are not described in the current
public identity record. Operators should consult any documentation published by the subnet owner
before running a validator.

## On-Chain Identity

Live SN76 data is available on [TaoStats](https://taostats.io/subnets/76). The registered Finney
identity reports the subnet name as Byzantium, description "Leverage marketing with AI Swarm,"
website [byzantiumai.net](https://www.byzantiumai.net), and a Discord link. The GitHub field is
blank.
