# Subnet 76: Byzantium

Byzantium is Bittensor's Subnet 76, a network positioned around AI-powered marketing automation. Its
registered on-chain description — "Leverage marketing with AI Swarm" — describes a system where AI
agents collaborate as a swarm to perform marketing-related tasks. The project maintains a
public-facing website at [byzantiumai.net](https://www.byzantiumai.net).

The subnet currently has 44 registered neurons and no public GitHub repository for its validator or
miner code. On-chain identity details are available at
[taostats.io/subnets/76](https://taostats.io/subnets/76).

## How the Mechanism Works

Based on the subnet's stated description, Byzantium coordinates multiple AI agents — collectively
referred to as a swarm — to carry out marketing tasks. In the Bittensor context, miners would
perform marketing-related work such as content generation, campaign analysis, audience targeting, or
similar tasks, while validators assess the quality and effectiveness of that work to assign rewards.

Because no public miner or validator codebase has been released, the precise scoring logic and task
format are not independently verifiable from public sources.

## Participating as a Miner

The subnet has not published a public GitHub repository with miner setup instructions. Prospective
miners should consult [byzantiumai.net](https://www.byzantiumai.net) or the project's community
channels for participation details and any registration requirements.

## Participating as a Validator

Validator participation details are similarly not publicly documented through an open-source
codebase. Validators interested in running on SN76 should reach out through the project's official
channels for guidance on scoring criteria and operational requirements.

## On-Chain Identity

Subnet 76 is registered on the Bittensor Finney network with the identity name Byzantium. Its
on-chain description states "Leverage marketing with AI Swarm." The subnet's public website is
[byzantiumai.net](https://www.byzantiumai.net). No GitHub repository is listed in the on-chain
identity.

Live neuron count, emissions, and pool data are tracked on
[taostats.io/subnets/76](https://taostats.io/subnets/76).
